# Dire wolf

![Dire wolf](mob:dire_wolf)

A wolf of the Ice Age, **heavier than any wolf of today**, with a broad head, a deep chest and amber eyes. It
is an ancient relative of the [dog](24), and **it is a hunter: it runs at you and bites**. It is an
[extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself, it has to be brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Look for its fossil.** It lies far down, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:fossil_rock_rare)
   `fossil_rock_rare`: a dark rock with a three-toed footprint. The page on [fossils](262) says how deep to dig.
2. **Break the block** with a [pickaxe](68). Which animal is inside depends on the place: if it was a dire
   wolf, you take ![](item:fossil_dire_wolf) `fossil_dire_wolf` with you.
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45 seconds**. It is
   a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived dire wolf: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back. **Think
before you revive one next to your house.**

## Behaviour

- While nobody is near, **it strolls** around its place, looks about and flicks its ears.
- **It is hostile, by day and by night.** When a player comes within **16 blocks**, it goes after them: the
  nearest one, and the same one for everybody who is watching.
- **It runs at 2.4 blocks a second.** You walk at 4.5: you can always get away on foot.
- When it is just over a block from you **it bites: 3 damage**, at most once every 1.2 seconds. You have 20
  health: that is 7 bites.
- **It lets go** when you are more than 24 blocks from it, or more than 32 blocks from its home. Then it walks
  back. It never goes further from its home than that.
- If it defeats you, it leaves you alone for 10 seconds.

There is one colour only: grey-brown, darker along the back, with pale cheeks and legs and a black tip to its tail.

## Tips

- **Walk away and it gives up.** It is slower than you, and it does not leave the surroundings of its home.
- A fence stops it: build its pen **before** you start the machine, with the machine inside.
- With a [shield](252) in your left hand, and facing it, its bite does half the damage.
- Put it next to a dog to see the difference. In the [Bestiary](5), the sheet of both shows them together
  under the title **Evolution**.

## The real dire wolf

- **When and where.** It lived in **the Americas**, above all in North America, from about **125,000 to
  10,000 years ago**, in the last Ice Age.
- **Its name.** Scientists call it *Aenocyon dirus*, which means something like "terrible wolf".
- **Size.** It was about as long and as tall as the biggest grey wolves of today, around **80 centimetres**
  at the shoulder, but much **heavier**: between about **60 and 70 kilos**. Its head was broader and its teeth
  were bigger, with a bite strong enough to crack bones.
- **What it ate.** Big animals: horses, bison, camels and giant sloths. Scientists think it hunted **in
  packs**, as wolves do.
- **A trap of tar.** In the tar pits of La Brea, in Los Angeles, the bones of more than **4,000** dire wolves
  have been found. Animals got stuck in the sticky tar, the wolves came to eat them, and they got stuck too.
- **Why it died out.** When the Ice Age ended, most of the big animals it hunted disappeared, and the dire
  wolf went with them. The grey wolf, which eats smaller prey too, is still here.
- **Is it the dog's grandfather?** No. Dogs come from grey wolves. In 2021 scientists read the DNA of dire
  wolf bones and found that it was only a distant cousin: its branch had split from that of wolves and
  dogs nearly **6 million years ago**. They looked alike because they lived alike.
