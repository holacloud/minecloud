# Bee and beehive

![Bee](mob:bee)

Bees live in **colonies** around a hive hanging under the canopy of a tree. They are peaceful as long as nobody
bothers them… but strike one and **the whole swarm comes after you**.

## Where it lives

- In **forests** and on the **plains**, in a hive hanging under the leaves of a tree, with two free blocks below.
  Forests have twice as many hives as the plains.
- Each colony has **5 to 7 bees**.
- There are at most **3 hives** at a time.
- They come out **by day** in fair weather. At night and in the rain they shelter inside the hive and cannot be
  seen.

## Behaviour

- They hover around the hive's entrance in a nervous zig-zag.
- Now and then they fly to a nearby **flower** (up to about 10 blocks away), **sip** from it for a few seconds and
  come back.
- Near the colony you hear the **buzz**, louder the more bees there are.
- **When angry:** the buzz rises in pitch and they all swarm around your head. Each bee **stings only once**
  (1 damage), tumbles to the ground and vanishes.
- The anger lasts about **20 seconds**, and wears off much sooner if you get more than 28 blocks away.
- If you go **under water**, they cannot sting you.
- The colony **replaces its bees**: a new one is born every 25 s while they are calm.

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit a bee or the hive | A bee **dies at the first blow** ("You swatted a bee"), with a **10 %** chance of a `honeycomb`; the hive cannot be destroyed. Either way **you anger the whole colony** ("You angered the bees!"), and the hive shakes. |
| Right click the hive | 1–2 ![honeycomb](item:honeycomb) `honeycomb`. Then the hive needs **120 s** to refill. |
| … with a `torch` in your hand | The **smoke calms** the bees: you take the honeycomb without being attacked, and it also soothes them if they were already angry. |
| … without a torch | You get the honeycomb, but the colony gets angry. |
| Net (`bug_net`) | You catch one bee and get a `bee_egg`. |
| Bait | `bait_honey`: bees sip the bait instead of flowers. |
| Tame them | You can't: it is a colony. |
| Egg | `bee_egg`: makes a colony in the nearest tree (up to 12 blocks away) or, if there is none, a hive on the ground. |
| Command | `/mob bee` |

## Tips

- **Always bring a torch:** take the honeycomb with a `torch` in your hand and you won't be stung.
- `honeycomb` and `glass` make a ![honey bottle](item:honey_bottle) `honey_bottle` (+5 health, gives the glass
  back). See [Mob resources](2).
- Honeycomb also tames [pigs](19) and [horses](34), and makes `bait_honey`.
- Chased without a torch? Jump into water and wait.

## Trivia

- Real honeybees also die after stinging: their stinger is barbed and stays stuck.
- Beekeepers have used **smoke** to calm bees for thousands of years. It masks the alarm scent that bees release
  when they feel attacked.
- A bee visits hundreds of flowers in a single trip, and to tell the others where they are it performs a
  **dance**.
- The hive in the game is made of **five tiers** of comb, with a dark entrance on the widest one.
- If the hive despawns as you walk away, its bees go with it.
