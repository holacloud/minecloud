# Chairs and sofas

![Oak chair](item:oak_chair)

Right click to **sit**. Your view drops to the height of the seat, you can look around but not walk, and **Sneak** (Shift, or the ⇣ Sneak button on a phone) stands you up. Other players see you sitting.

If someone breaks the seat, or a [motor ram](217) pushes it away, you stand up where you were.

## Pieces that join

- **Long benches** placed side by side and facing the same way become one bench: the legs in the middle go away.
- A **sofa** on its own is an armchair. Put another next to it, facing the same way, and the arms between them disappear: as long a sofa as you like.

A chair looks at you when you place it, so stand on the side where you want to sit down to the table.
