# Plesiosaur

![Plesiosaur](mob:plesiosaur)

A reptile of the sea with a **very long neck**, a small head full of needle teeth, a broad flat body, a short
tail and **four flippers**. It does not paddle: it **flies through the water**. And since it was a reptile, now
and then it comes up to **put its head out and breathe**. It died out 66 million years ago: in MineCloud you
only see one if you gather its skeleton and bring it back to life. It is one of the hardest animals to get, and it is
**5 blocks** long.

## What it was

A **reptile of the sea**, not a dinosaur, though it lived in their time. Its ancestors walked on land, like
those of the whales; in time its four legs turned into flippers and it never went back to the shore. It went on
breathing **air**, with lungs, as sea turtles and dolphins do today.

## How to get one

1. **Its skeleton, in three pieces.** You need the skull, the spine and the tail: `fossil_plesiosaur_skull`,
   `fossil_plesiosaur_spine` and `fossil_plesiosaur_tail`. They come out of the three blocks of a **skeleton**
   (`fossil_bones_skull`, `fossil_bones_spine` and `fossil_bones_tail`), which lie in a straight line inside the
   stone, between y = −11 and y = −4, and only in 1 chunk out of 30. The three blocks of a skeleton always belong
   to one animal, but not every skeleton is a plesiosaur's. See [Fossils](262).
2. **A lot of water.** The revival machine has to stand next to **120 blocks of water** joined together, or
   more: a lake, the sea, or a big pool that you dig and fill with a [bucket](111).
3. **The [revival machine](264).** With the three pieces inside it takes 120 seconds, two whole minutes. The plesiosaur
   appears in the water.

## The pool it needs

With 120 blocks of water it comes back to life, but to swim at its ease it wants more:

- It is **5 blocks long** and about **3 from flipper tip to flipper tip**. In a pool of less than about
  12 × 10 blocks it can hardly go forwards: it turns slowly on the spot and hangs still at times.
- It needs water **2 blocks deep** at the least, and better 3 or 4, to go up and down.
- Leave **air over the water**. With a roof right on the surface it cannot put its head out. It does not drown
  for that, but you miss the best of it.
- The walls may be [glass](51): it does not go through it, and you can watch it swim from outside.

## Behaviour

- **It swims in long curves** in mid water, at a little over 1 block a second, going up and down unhurriedly.
- **It beats its four flippers** up and down, like wings; the two at the back come a little after the two at
  the front.
- **Its neck goes first**: before a turn it bends its neck to that side, and then its body follows, leaning
  into the curve.
- **It comes up to breathe** about every 35 to 60 seconds: it rises until its back touches the surface, **lifts
  its head out of the water** for some 3 seconds, its mouth half open, and dives again. You will see the rings
  on the water and hear it puff.
- **It looks where it is going**: it slows down and turns before it reaches a wall, the bed or the shore, and it
  never leaves the water.
- **It does not go further** than about 8 blocks from where it was revived.
- **It is peaceful**: it does not bite you even if you swim beside it.
- **If its water goes away**, it lies on the ground, waiting. It does not die: as soon as the water is back, it
  swims again.

There are **2 colours**: greyish blue and olive green, both with a dark back and a pale belly.

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit it | It shoots off away from you, at more than twice its speed, for a couple of seconds. |
| Defeat it | **10 hits** bare-handed (5 with a gold [sword](71)). It leaves nothing: it is far better alive. |
| Tame it | You can't. |
| Bait | None interests it. |
| Cage | It does not fit. |
| Egg | `plesiosaur_egg`: use it next to water. |
| Command | `/mob plesiosaur`: it appears in the nearest water where it fits; if there is none near, the game tells you. |

## The real science

- **When they lived.** From about **200 million years ago** to **66 million** years ago, in seas all over the
  world. They disappeared in the same extinction as the dinosaurs, when the asteroid fell.
- **How big they were.** They came in many sizes. The first ones found were about 3 and a half metres long.
  Those with the longest necks reached about 12 metres, and more than half of that was neck. The one in the
  game is 5.
- **A neck with more than 70 bones.** You have 7 bones in your neck, and a giraffe has 7 too. Some plesiosaurs
  had **more than 70**.
- **It did not hold it up like a swan.** Old drawings show it with its neck stiff in the air, but the neck was
  rather rigid: it carried it stretched out ahead and could only lift its head a little, as the one in the game
  does to breathe.
- **It flew under water.** Penguins and sea turtles swim by beating two flippers as if they were wings. The
  plesiosaur did it with **all four**, and no living animal swims like that.
- **What it ate.** Fish, squid and ammonites too. Its long, thin teeth fitted between one another like a cage,
  so that nothing slippery got away.
- **It swallowed stones.** Smooth stones have been found inside their skeletons, where the stomach was. They may
  have helped to grind its food or to keep it from floating too much: scientists still argue about it.
- **It laid no eggs.** It could not come out on land, so its young were born in the water, fully formed. We know
  from a fossil of a mother with her young inside.
- **Who found it.** Mary Anning, who had looked for fossils on the cliffs of England since she was a girl, dug
  out the first complete skeleton of a plesiosaur in 1823. It was so strange that at first some people thought
  it was a fake.
- **Its name** means "near to the lizards".
- **Who is left of its family.** Nobody: it left no descendants.
