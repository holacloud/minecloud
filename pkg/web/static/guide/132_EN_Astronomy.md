# Astronomy

![Telescope](item:telescope)

When the sun goes down and the sky darkens, the **stars** come out. They are not just decoration: the MineCloud
sky has its own **constellations**, two **planets** wandering among them, a moon with **phases** and, once in a
while, visitors you will not want to miss.

The sky is **the same for every player**: it only depends on the world clock, which the server keeps. If your
friend sees the comet, so do you.

## The night sky

- The stars **come out little by little at dusk** and fade at dawn.
- They **turn slowly** through the night around one fixed star, **The Nail**, which sits in the **north**,
  half-way up the sky. Lost at night? Find it: north is right below it.
- Each night the sky has turned a little further than the night before, so **different constellations show** at
  the same time of night. After about four weeks it starts over.
- In the **rain**, clouds hide almost every star.
- With the naked eye you see the bright ones; through the [telescope](133) many more come out.

With the telescope and a [star chart](134) you can **record** them all.

## The planets

Two bright dots that **do not twinkle** and **move** among the constellations from one day to the next. They
always travel along the same band of the sky.

| Planet | Colour | One lap of the sky |
|---|---|---|
| **Ferro** | rusty red, like iron ore | 9 days |
| **Aurum** | golden | 31 days |

## The phases of the moon

The moon changes phase **every day**, in an **8-day** cycle. The phase changes right at the darkest point of the
night. The telescope tells you the name of that night's phase.

| Day of the cycle | Phase |
|---|---|
| 0 | Full Moon |
| 1 | Waning Gibbous |
| 2 | Last Quarter |
| 3 | Waning Crescent |
| 4 | New Moon |
| 5 | Waxing Crescent |
| 6 | First Quarter |
| 7 | Waxing Gibbous |

## Shooting stars

On clear nights a **shooting star** crosses the sky about every half minute. They follow the world clock, so
**every player sees the same ones**: if you shout "look!", your friend saw it too.

## Rare events

- **The comet.** One night in **13**, a comet with a long pale tail appears. It only lasts that night. That night
  also brings **many more shooting stars**: a meteor shower.
- **The red moon.** One night in **24**, at **full moon**, the world's shadow crosses the moon and turns it **rust
  red**: a **lunar eclipse**. It comes after the darkest point of the night and lasts less than a minute. Do not
  miss it.

> The old astronomers used to say that whoever watches a rare event **through a telescope** reads something
> written in the sky. Nobody believed them.

## Trivia

- Real stars are so far away that their light takes years to reach us: some of the ones you see may no longer
  exist.
- Our world's Pole Star plays the same part as The Nail: it barely moves and it marks the north.
- Real lunar eclipses turn the moon red too: it is the light of every sunrise and sunset on Earth at once, leaking
  through its shadow.
