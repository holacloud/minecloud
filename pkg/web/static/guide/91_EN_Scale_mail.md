# Scale mail

![Scale mail](item:scale_mail)

Armour of overlapping green scales sewn onto leather. It is **the best protection** against creatures: every blow
takes 2 less health.

## How to get it

It is crafted (C key):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `scale_mail` | **6** [Scale](83) `scale` + **2** [Leather](76) `leather` |

| Scales from… | How |
|---|---|
| ![Lizard](mob:lizard) [Lizard](30) | strike it: 35 % per strike, 10 s wait |
| ![Snake](mob:snake) [Snake](42) | defeat it: 1–2 |

## How to use it

**There is nothing to put on:** just **carry it in your inventory**. If you select it and press G, the chat reminds
you what it does.

**Effect:** −2 damage on every creature attack, **but never below 1**.

| Attacker | Without mail | With mail |
|---|---|---|
| ![Cave monster](mob:cave_monster) [Cave monster](21) | 4 | **2** |
| ![Wandering shadow](mob:shadow) [Wandering shadow](37) | 3 | **1** |
| ![Storm golem](mob:storm_golem) [Storm golem](43) (punch) | 2–3 | **1** |
| ![Spider](mob:spider) [Spider](20) | 2 | **1** |
| ![Giant mole](mob:mole) [Giant mole](47) | 2 | **1** |
| Bee, crab, goat, scorpion, snake | 1 | 1 |

**It does not protect from:** poison, falls, lightning or the void.

If you also carry the [warm coat](90), only the better of the two counts: the mail.

## Tips

- **Against the wandering shadow** it makes a huge difference: from 3 down to 1 per claw strike.
- Against the cave monster, half the damage.
- Against 1-damage critters (bees, scorpions) it does not help: for venom, carry [antivenom](89).
- Pair it with the [feather cape](92): together they cover creatures and falls.

## Trivia

- The Romans had armour like this, the *lorica squamata*: metal scales sewn onto cloth or leather, like those of a
  fish or a reptile.
- Some animals wear their own mail: the pangolin is covered in hard keratin scales.
