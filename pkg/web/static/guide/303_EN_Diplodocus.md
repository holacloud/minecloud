# Diplodocus

![Diplodocus](mob:diplodocus)

The **biggest animal of the game**: a dinosaur that eats plants, **14 blocks** from its nose to the tip of its
tail. Its back is **5 blocks** up at the hips, and its small head, on a very long neck held forward and up,
reaches more than **6 blocks** over the ground: high enough to **eat from the treetops**. It has four legs like
pillars and a tail that ends in a whip. It is peaceful, and once tamed **you can ride it**, high over the
ground. It is an [extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself, it has to be brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Its skeleton, in three pieces.** You need the skull, the spine and the tail: `fossil_diplodocus_skull`,
   `fossil_diplodocus_spine` and `fossil_diplodocus_tail`. They come out of the three blocks of a **skeleton**
   (`fossil_bones_skull`, `fossil_bones_spine` and `fossil_bones_tail`), which lie in a straight line inside the
   stone, deep down. The three blocks of a skeleton always belong to one animal. It is a **legendary** fossil:
   its weight against the other skeletons is 2, against 6 of the mammoth, 2 of the plesiosaur and 1 of the
   tyrannosaurus. The page on [fossils](262) says how deep to dig and how to find the other two pieces once you
   have one.
2. **Break the blocks** with a [pickaxe](68).
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264), with **the three pieces** inside. It takes **120
   seconds**. It is a land animal: it needs no water.

**Make room first.** The machine puts it on a free block next to itself and knows nothing of its size: build the
machine in an open place, with some **15 blocks** of flat ground around it and nothing lower than **6 blocks**
over it, or its neck and tail will stick into the walls and the trees.

The server keeps a revived diplodocus: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back.

## Behaviour

- **It walks very slowly** near its place, swinging its long tail from side to side and turning its neck to look
  around. Its steps are long and heavy.
- **It eats from the treetops.** When it stands still with leaves at the height of its head, it raises its
  neck towards them and **chews** for a few seconds: you can hear it. It does not break the leaves.
- It is **peaceful**: it attacks nobody. If you hit it, it gives a start.
- **Its body takes room.** It stops before its chest reaches a wall, a fence, a ledge more than 3 blocks deep or
  a roof lower than its back: it looks **2 blocks** ahead of its middle. It cannot go through a door. Its neck
  and its tail are higher, and pass over low things.
- **It pushes you out of its legs.** You cannot stand inside it, but you can walk **under its belly**, between
  its legs: there are 2.4 blocks of room under it.

There is one colour only: grey-green, darker along the back with a row of soft spines, faint bands on the
flanks and a pale belly.

| Bait | None. |
| Cage | It is far too big for the cage. |

## Riding it

| | Diplodocus | [Horse](34) |
|---|---|---|
| Your eyes, above the ground | **6.4 blocks** | 2.3 blocks |
| Speed, blocks per second | **3** | 9 |
| Galloping (double tap **W**) | **3.8** | 11.25 |
| Jump (**Space**) | about **1 block** | about 2.7 blocks |

- You sit high on its back, behind the shoulders, and see over the bushes and the low trees.
- It is the slowest mount of the game: a ride on it is a walk, not a race.
- It walks up 1-block steps by itself, but it cannot jump any higher.
- **It needs 7 free blocks of height**: under a roof or a tree you cannot get on, and riding under the leaves of
  a tree stops you.
- It does not let you push its head into a wall: it stops with its chest 2 blocks short of it, and with its
  hips when you ride it backwards.
- Other players see you riding it.

## Tips

- Leaves come from trees: break some leaves with your hand and offer them to it one at a time until it stays
  with you.
- Do not carry its food in your hand when you want to get on: with leaves the right click feeds it.
- Let it stand near tall trees and watch it eat.
- In a forest its neck goes through the leaves of the trees around it. It is happier on open plains.

## The real Diplodocus

- **When and where.** It lived in the west of **North America**, in the **Jurassic**, about **154 to 152
  million years ago**, on plains with rivers and forests of conifers, ferns and horsetails.
- **Its name** means "double beam": under its tail it had small bones shaped like two beams side by side.
- **Size.** It was one of the **longest animals** that ever walked on land: about **24 to 26 metres** from nose
  to tail, as long as two buses, and it weighed between about **10 and 16 tonnes**. Some bones of its family show
  even longer ones. In the game it is shorter, so that it fits.
- **The neck and the tail.** Its neck was about **6 metres** long and its tail even longer, about **13
  metres**, thinning to a whip. Scientists still argue about how high it held its neck: probably not straight up,
  but forward, raising it to reach leaves higher up. Some think it could crack the tip of its tail like a whip.
- **What it ate.** Plants. Its teeth were thin pegs at the front of the mouth only: it did not chew, it stripped
  the leaves off the branches and swallowed them. Its teeth wore out fast and a new one grew in about **every
  month**.
- **A small head.** Its skull was only about **60 centimetres** long, on an animal of more than 20 metres.
- **In museums.** Casts of one skeleton, *Diplodocus carnegii*, were given to museums of many countries more than
  a hundred years ago: one stands in Madrid, at the National Museum of Natural Sciences, and others in London,
  Paris and Berlin.
- **Its family.** It was a **sauropod**: the four-legged plant eaters with long necks, the biggest land animals
  of all time. Its family died out with the other dinosaurs, and left no descendants: the birds come from
  another branch, that of Tyrannosaurus.
