# Honey bottle

![Honey bottle](item:honey_bottle)

A glass bottle full of golden honey. It heals a lot and **gives the bottle back** when you drink it.

## How to get it

It is crafted (C key):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `honey_bottle` | 1 [Honeycomb](79) `honeycomb` + 1 `glass` |

Honeycomb comes from ![Bee](mob:bee) [bee](35) hives (with a torch in your hand so they do not attack). Glass comes
from 3 `sand` → 2 `glass`.

## Drinking (G key)

| Health | Effect |
|---|---|
| **+5** | gives the `glass` back |

At full health you do not drink it.

## Tips

- **The glass comes back:** with a single glass you can bottle honey over and over. Only the honeycomb is used up.
- A hive gives 1–2 honeycombs every 2 minutes: a few hives near home are an endless larder.
- It does not cure poison: for that, [milk](78) or [antivenom](89).

## Trivia

- Well-sealed honey **hardly ever spoils**: jars of honey thousands of years old have been found in Egyptian tombs.
- A real bee visits hundreds of flowers to gather a single drop of nectar.
