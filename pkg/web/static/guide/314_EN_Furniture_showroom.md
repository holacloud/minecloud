# Furniture showroom

![Sofa](item:green_sofa)

A low, wide building of one floor, **56 by 52 blocks**, with a yellow line painted on its corridors: a **furniture showroom**. It is like a big furniture store, where a one-way path takes you past room after room, each one furnished. Here **every piece of furniture, decoration and mirror** of the game stands in a scene, and next to each a sign tells you how to use it. It is the best place to discover what you can put in your own home.

## How to visit it

- Go in by the **entrance** and **follow the yellow line**. It goes round a ring of corridors, past rooms open towards you, and brings you back to the **exit**, next to the entrance.
- **Right click a sign** to read all of it, in Spanish and English: the board only shows the first line.
- Try everything: sit on the chairs, lie on the beds, draw the curtains, open the chests.

## The route

| # | Room | What you find and learn |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Living room** | Sofas and curtains. Right click a sofa to sit, sneak to stand up; right click a curtain to draw it |
| 2 | **Dining room** | Chairs, a bench, stools and tables: you can sit on all of them |
| 3 | **Kitchen** | An ![](item:oven) oven that cooks by itself when you put in food and coal, a ![](item:fridge) fridge and a freezer that work on a generator of their own, sinks, and a workbench for recipes |
| 4 | **Bedroom** | Beds, a wardrobe, chests and bedside tables. A wardrobe and a chest keep things; on a bed you lie down, and at night you sleep |
| 5 | **Bathroom** | A ![](item:toilet) toilet you can sit on, a ![](item:bathtub) bathtub you can step into, sinks, and ![](item:wall_mirror) mirrors to look at yourself |
| 6 | **Study** | Desks, a chair, a chest and bookshelves (the shelves are decoration) |
| 7 | **Stained glass gallery** | The 16 colours of [stained glass](229), as blocks and as panes, against the sky |
| 8 | **Light room** | Dark on purpose: bays with one light each, and stripes on the floor to measure how far each light reaches |
| 9 | **Joins** | Which pieces join with their neighbours and which do not |
| 10 | **Hall of mirrors** | A long room to see how far a mirror really reflects |
| 11 | **Storage corner** | A chest, a barrel and a wardrobe with boards, wool and dyes, to try with another player |
| 12 | **Sample wall** | All 16 curtains and all 16 carpets |
| 13 | **Workshop** | A [foundry](235) and an [electric arc furnace](236), and an [incubator](253) and an autoclave |
| 14 | **Microscope** | A [microscope](238) by a strip of daylight, with water and moss to sample; and a row of ![](item:glow_block) glow blocks |

## Experiments to try

### The light room

Every bay has one source of light and a floor of black and white stripes to count how far its light reaches. The signs give each one's **light level**:

| Light | Level |
|---|---|
| ![](item:candle) Candle | 9 |
| ![](item:lantern) Lantern, standing or hanging | 13 |
| ![](item:table_lamp) Table lamp | 12 |
| ![](item:floor_lamp) Floor lamp | 14 |
| ![](item:chandelier) Chandelier | 14 |
| ![](item:oven) Oven, while it burns | 11 |
| Nothing | This is how dark it is |

Which one would you hang in a big room? And by a bed? See [Lights without electricity](228) and [Light and darkness](105).

### Glass and colour

- **Red behind blue**: very little light gets through. Each glass lets through only its own colour, and red glass stops what blue glass let pass.
- **A cyan pane in front of a mirror**: the stained glass also tints the reflection.
- **A piece under water**: look at it through the glass side of the pool, and from the step above.

### Mirrors

In the hall of mirrors, walk backwards away from a mirror: it **really reflects up to 14 blocks**; further away it is only a shiny texture. There are mirror cubes and two mirror walls, and sofas of every colour to reflect. See [Mirrors](232).

### Joins

**Sofas** of the same colour join into one long sofa; a red and a blue one do not. **Benches** join too, if they face the same way. **Tables** join in a row. Use that when you furnish: one long table for a feast, or two sofas of different colours that stay separate.

### Electricity

The kitchen's fridge and freezer touch one ![](item:rtg) generator: **60 W of its 80 W**. In the workshop the arc furnace has **two generators** of its own, because it asks for **150 W** while it works; the incubator (20 W) and the autoclave (40 W) share another. Each little [network](200) is enough for what it feeds.

### Sharing

Take something out of the chest of the **storage corner**, or put something in, and ask a friend to look: what a chest holds lives on the server, so everybody sees the same.

## What the chests hold

Each room's storage holds what that room would: frozen food in the freezer, cold milk, water, cheese and soda in the fridge, bread, wheat, salt, plates and a jug in the kitchen barrel; coats, wool, leather and a sewing basket in the bedroom; books, an inkwell, scrolls and blueprints in the study. The storage corner is the shop's back room: planks of three woods, glass and torches in the chest, wool of eight colours in the barrel, and pigments, gold leaf and cobalt glass in the wardrobe. The ovens and furnaces are cold until you bring fuel and something to cook or melt.

## Curiosities

- The light room is in the very middle of the building, under a thick roof, with a long lane in, so that no daylight leaks into it.
- At night the corridors are lit by lanterns hanging from the ceiling in a pattern of diamonds that leaves no dark gaps.
