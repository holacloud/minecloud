## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize HE Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_he` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

It takes part in **1** reaction you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *This gas combines with nothing, but alone in the flask it floats away.*

## In the real world

- It was discovered in **1868** in the light of the Sun, before it was found on Earth (*helios*, "sun").
- It is the second most abundant element in the universe.
- Liquid helium, at −269 °C, cools the magnets of MRI scanners.
