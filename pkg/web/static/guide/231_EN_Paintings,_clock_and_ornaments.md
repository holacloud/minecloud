## Wall clock

![](item:wall_clock) `wall_clock`: 2 oak planks, 1 copper block, 1 iron ore. It hangs on a wall and its hands show **the time in the game**: morning starts at 6 o'clock, as the sun comes up. A whole day lasts four minutes, so you can actually watch the big hand move. Look at it and the block panel tells you the exact time too.

## Flower pot and vase

| | In-game name | Id | Made from |
|---|---|---|---|
| ![](item:flower_pot) | Flower Pot | `flower_pot` | 2 brick, 1 dirt, 1 red flower |
| ![](item:vase) | Vase | `vase` | 2 brick, 1 sand, 1 cobalt block |

Clay pots hold water for plants because baked clay is full of tiny holes that let the soil breathe. The blue of the vase is the same [cobalt](163) as blue glass: painted on before the kiln, it comes out deep blue, as on old Chinese porcelain.

Everything here stays where you put it even if the wall behind it goes, as [signs](64) do.
