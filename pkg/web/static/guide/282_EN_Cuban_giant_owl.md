# Cuban giant owl

![Cuban giant owl](mob:giant_owl)

**The biggest owl there has ever been**: over a metre tall, on **long, strong legs**. It could hardly fly, so
it ran. It is an ancient relative of the [owl](46). It minds its own business, but **if you hit it, it
spreads its wings and kicks**. It is an [extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself, it has to be
brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Look for its fossil.** It lies far down, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:fossil_rock_rare)
   `fossil_rock_rare`: a dark rock with a three-toed footprint. The page on [fossils](262) says how deep to dig.
2. **Break the block** with a [pickaxe](68). Which animal is inside depends on the place: if it was a
   Cuban giant owl, you take ![](item:fossil_giant_owl) `fossil_giant_owl` with you.
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45 seconds**. It is
   a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived Cuban giant owl: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back.

## Behaviour

- **It walks about** near its place with long strides, as fast as a goat, and turns its flat face to look
  around. It never flies and never perches in a tree.
- Its card says it is a night animal, as it was in life. In the game it walks by day too, and its eyes do
  not glow as those of the owl of today do.
- It is **neutral**: it attacks nobody who has not attacked it.
- **If you hit it, it warns you.** It hisses, turns on you and **spreads its wings** for 0.5 seconds, to look
  bigger.
- Then **it kicks**, if you are still within 2 blocks of it: **2 damage**, and it throws you back. After that
  it stands still for a second.
- It only kicks whoever hit it, and only that player sees the warning.

There is one colour only: brown with dark bars, a pale barred belly and orange eyes.

## Tips

- **Step back as soon as it spreads its wings.** From more than 2 blocks away the kick does not reach you.
- With a [shield](252) in your left hand, and facing it, the kick does half the damage.
- Do not fight it at the edge of a drop: the kick throws you back.
- Put it next to an owl of today: that one sits in a tree and waits; this one stands on the ground on legs
  as long as its body.
- In the [Bestiary](5), its sheet and the owl's show the two together under the title **Evolution**.

## The real Cuban giant owl

- **When and where.** It lived on the island of **Cuba** during the last ice ages, and died out about
  **10,000 years ago**, perhaps a little later. Its bones have been found in caves all over the island.
- **Its name.** Scientists call it *Ornimegalonyx*, which means "bird with a giant claw".
- **Size.** It stood about **1.1 metres** tall and weighed some **9 kilos**: more than twice the biggest owl
  alive today.
- **Its legs.** Very long and very strong, with huge talons. The first bones found, in 1954, were so big
  that they were taken for those of another kind of bird, a giant runner. They were an owl's.
- **Could it fly?** Hardly. The bone of its breast, where the muscles that beat the wings are fixed, was
  small. Scientists think it ran and jumped on its prey, and at most fluttered a short way, as a turkey does.
- **What it ate.** The animals of Cuba at that time: big rodents called hutias, and the young of ground
  sloths, some as heavy as itself or heavier.
- **Islands make giants.** There were no big mammal hunters in Cuba, such as wolves or big cats. An owl took
  their place, and grew.
- **Why it died out.** Nobody knows for sure. The climate changed at the end of the ice ages, the animals it
  hunted became rare, and by then the first people had reached the island.
- **Its relatives.** The wood owls: owls of the forest with a round head and no ear tufts. That is why the
  one of the game has none.
