## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize AR Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_ar` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

It takes part in **2** reactions you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *Argon with a drop of the liquid metal, in glass, glows blue.*
- *A filament of the hardest metal to melt, in glass full of a lazy gas, gives warm light.*

## In the real world

- It is almost **1 %** of the air: the third most abundant gas.
- Its name comes from the Greek *argos*, "lazy", because it hardly reacts.
- It fills light bulbs and shields welds from the oxygen in the air.
