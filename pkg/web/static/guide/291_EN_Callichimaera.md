# Callichimaera

![Callichimaera](mob:callichimaera)

The **beautiful chimera**: a tiny crab with **huge eyes** and **legs like oars**, that swam instead of running
sideways. It is an ancient relative of the [crab](39), and it looks like a baby crab that never grew up. It is
an [extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself, it has to be brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Look for its fossil.** It lies far down, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:fossil_rock_rare)
   `fossil_rock_rare`: a dark rock with a three-toed footprint. The page on [fossils](262) says how deep to dig.
2. **Break the block** with a [pickaxe](68). Which animal is inside depends on the place: if it was a
   Callichimaera, you take ![](item:fossil_callichimaera) `fossil_callichimaera` with you.
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45 seconds**. In
   the game it lives on land: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived Callichimaera: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back.

## Behaviour

- **It strolls** near its place, **rowing with its four oars** as it goes, and its tail sways behind it.
- **It walks forwards.** It does not run sideways as the crab does, and it never buries itself.
- It is **peaceful**: it comes up to nobody and attacks nobody. If you hit it, it gives a start.

There are **2 colours**: coral orange and grey-green.

## Tips

- It is small: look for it on the ground, near the machine that revived it.
- Look at it from the front: its two eyes are nearly as wide as its whole body.
- Put it next to a crab. The crab is wide and runs sideways; the Callichimaera is long and goes forwards.
- In the [Bestiary](5), its sheet and the crab's show the two together under the title **Evolution**.

## The real Callichimaera

- **When and where.** It lived between about **95 and 90 million years ago**, in the Cretaceous, the last age
  of the dinosaurs. Its fossils were found in the mountains of **Colombia**, in rock that was then the bottom
  of a warm sea, and also in the United States.
- **Its name** means "beautiful chimera". In the Greek myths the Chimera was a monster made of pieces of
  several animals. This crab looks like that too: the eyes of a larva, the mouth of a shrimp, the claws of one
  kind of crab and the shell of a lobster. Its scientists gave it a second name, *perplexa*: "the puzzling one".
- **Size.** Tiny: about as big as a **coin**. In the game it is many times bigger, so that you can see it.
- **The eyes.** They were enormous, and it had nowhere to fold them away: no sockets, nothing to protect them.
  Among the crabs of today only the larvae, which drift in open water, have eyes like that.
- **The oars.** Its legs were wide and flat, like paddles. Scientists think it was a good **swimmer**, and
  that with those eyes it hunted smaller animals in the water.
- **A baby that never grew up.** A young crab changes a lot as it grows: its eyes shrink, its tail folds under
  its body and its shell widens. The Callichimaera grew up and kept the body of a larva. It happens in other
  animals too.
- **Why it matters.** The shape of a crab is such a good idea that nature has come up with it several times.
  The Callichimaera shows the opposite: a crab can also stop looking like one.
- **How we know all this.** Dozens of them were found, some so well kept that the eyes can still be seen.
  Scientists described it in **2019**.
- **Why it died out.** Nobody knows.
- **Nobody knows its colour.** The colours of the game are a guess.
