## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize CO Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_co` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

It takes part in **2** reactions you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *Glass (Si + 2 O) turns deep blue with a touch of cobalt.*
- *Lithium, cobalt and two oxygens store your phone's energy.*

## In the real world

- **Cobalt blue** has coloured glass and pottery for thousands of years.
- Its name comes from the German *Kobold*, a goblin of the mines.
- It is in many lithium batteries.
