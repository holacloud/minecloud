# Macaw

![Macaw](mob:macaw)

A brilliantly coloured parrot that flutters a little above the ground in noisy flocks. Harmless and very
showy.

## Where it lives

- **Forests:** it is one of the most common animals there.
- By day and by night, in groups of **1 to 3**.

## Behaviour

- **It flies low:** it keeps about 2 blocks above the ground, bobbing gently up and down as it flaps.
- It roams the forest without straying far from where it was born, following the shape of the land.
- If you hit it, it bounces in the air, squawks and turns towards you.
- It preens: if you are within 24 blocks, it sometimes **drops a feather** on its own.

## How to interact

- **Hitting it:** it has **12 health** and falls in **3 blows** bare-handed (2 with a stone sword or better, see
  [Swords](71)); defeated, it drops **1 to 3** `feather`. Besides, each hit has a **40 %** chance of dropping a `feather`
  ![Feather](item:feather) (at most one every 8 seconds per macaw).
- **Taming it** (see [Taming and breeding](3)):

| Food | Chance per try |
|---|---|
| ![Red flower](item:flower_red) `flower_red`, ![Yellow flower](item:flower_yellow) `flower_yellow`, ![Cherry leaves](item:cherry_leaves) `cherry_leaves` | 1/3 |

- **Bait:** it comes to `bait_seeds` (see [Baits and traps](4)).
- **Cage:** it does **not** fit: flyers do not go into the cage.
- **Egg:** `macaw_egg`, or the command `/mob macaw`.

## Tips

- Red and yellow flowers grow in the same forest where it lives, and so do cherry trees: everything you need to
  tame it is close at hand.
- It is the only flyer that can be tamed: it follows you, fluttering by your side.
- Together with the [Duck](18), it is the best source of feathers without harming anyone.

## Trivia

- There are **4 plumages**, and each macaw gets its own at birth, so a flock can mix them:
  - green body and yellow head,
  - yellow body, orange wings and green head,
  - red body, yellow wings and blue head,
  - blue body and yellow head, with a red tail.
- Real macaws can live for more than 50 years.
- They eat clay from riverbanks to protect themselves from the toxins in some seeds.
- Their beak is so strong that it cracks nuts with rock-hard shells.
