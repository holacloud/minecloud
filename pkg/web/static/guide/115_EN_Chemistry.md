# Chemistry

![Lab bench](item:lab_bench)

The 36 [element blocks](60) are not just decoration: at a **lab bench** they combine, atom by atom, into
**compounds**, **alloys**, crystals and devices. Each element block counts as **one atom**.

## How the lab works

1. Craft a [lab bench](117) (C key) and place it.
2. **Right click** it: the lab opens, with a **periodic table** of the elements.
3. Each tile shows the **atomic number** (top) and how many blocks of that element you carry (bottom, in yellow).
4. **Click** an element to put one atom in the flask and see its details under the table; **right click** takes it
   out. Or pick a known reaction in the list on the right and the flask fills itself.
5. When the mix matches a reaction **exactly**, its equation appears. Press **React**: the flask bubbles and you
   get the product.
6. The **📖** buttons close the lab and open this guide at the element's or the product's page. Closing the book
   takes you back to the game.

Proportions matter: 2 H + O gives water, but 1 H + 1 O gives nothing. The flask holds 8 atoms.

## Reactions to discover

There are **30 reactions**. At first the lab knows only 4; the rest show in the list as **???**
(with how many different elements they take) until you **mix the right atoms in the flask**. Then a fanfare plays,
the reaction is **discovered for good**, and you can pick it from the list and open its page.

The reactions known from the start:

| Reaction | Kind | Product |
|---|---|---|
| `2 H + O → H₂O (water)` | compound | 1 `water_bucket` (if you carry a `bucket`) or 1 `water_flask` (uses 1 `glass`) |
| `Na + Cl → NaCl (salt)` | compound | 4 `salt` |
| `Si + 2 O → SiO₂ (glass)` | compound | 4 `glass` |
| `Fe + C → steel` | alloy | 2 `steel_ingot` |

And the rest? Every element page (from [Hydrogen](139) to [Lead](174)) gives **hints** about the reactions it
takes part in. A few ideas to start: noble gases shine alone, metals rust, and gems are oxides with a pinch of
another metal.

## Kinds of products

- A **compound** is a new substance: its atoms are held together by chemical bonds in a fixed proportion. That is
  why it has a **formula**: H₂O means "two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom".
- An **alloy** is a mixture of metals (or of a metal and carbon) melted together. It has no fixed formula.
- The **noble gases** (He, Ne, Ar) hardly react: they are bottled.
- A **pure metal**, such as gold or silver, is worked without combining it with anything.
- A **coloured crystal** is a compound with a pinch of another element that gives it colour (that is what rubies
  are).
- A **device** puts materials together to do something: give light, store energy...

## Tips

- Every element block costs 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore`: water costs three.
- After a reaction, the mix stays in the flask if you have enough for another round.
- Reactions you can make right now are shown in green in the list.
- In creative mode, reactions are also discovered by mixing them.

## Trivia

- In the 18th century Antoine Lavoisier showed that in a reaction **mass is neither created nor destroyed**: atoms
  only change partners. That is why equations must balance.
