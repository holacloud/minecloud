## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize I Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_i` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

It takes part in **1** reaction you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *Iodine atoms always go in pairs; in a flask, they heal wounds.*

## In the real world

- When heated it gives off a **violet** vapour: its name comes from the Greek for "violet".
- Iodised salt has iodine because the thyroid gland needs it.
- Tincture of iodine has disinfected wounds since the 19th century.
