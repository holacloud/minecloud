# Baits

![Seed bait](item:bait_seeds)

Little piles of food that **draw animals** to a spot. They gather a herd, fill a [cage trap](96) and, two of them,
make fish bite sooner on the [rod](94). There are five.

When you set it down, the chat tells you what it draws ("Lures: …").

## Step by step

1. With a bait in your hand, **right click the top of a block**. Not in water, and not right next to another bait or
   a [jar](97).
2. A little pile appears. **It lasts 2 minutes.**
3. The animals that like it, within **16 blocks** (and no more than 6 blocks higher or lower), head for it. They
   check roughly once a second.
4. When they arrive they **eat with their heads down** and you hear little nibbles. The pile shrinks: a single animal
   finishes it in about **14 seconds**; several, sooner.
5. Afterwards they stay around for **25 to 40 seconds**: their "home" becomes the bait spot.

**Taking it back:** right click the pile. You only get the bait back if **nobody has touched it**; once nibbled, it
is lost.

## Special cases

- **Frogs, lizards and turtles** move in their own way (hops, dashes, swimming), and the bait nudges them towards it
  little by little; a bait inside a [cage](96) draws them right to its middle.
- **Bees:** the honey bait must be within about 20 blocks of their hive. Bees on their way to a flower go to the bait
  instead and sip it, more slowly than an animal eats.
- **Animals with a will of their own:** some have their own behaviour and do not always heed it.
- Pets following you and animals with a rider ignore it.
- **Inside a [cage trap](96):** only the animals that fit in it come.

## On the fishing rod

If you carry `bait_worms` or `bait_fish` in your inventory, one goes on the hook when you cast (worms first) and
**fish bite almost twice as fast**. One is used up per cast. See [Fishing rod](94).

## Tips

- **Seeds and worms are dirt cheap:** tall grass and dirt are everywhere.
- For taming, a bait brings the animal to your feet without a chase.
- Baits **are not kept** when the page reloads: use them there and then.
- Careful with meat bait: it also draws spiders, snakes and cave monsters.

## Trivia

- Real anglers also scatter food in the water to gather fish: it is called **chumming**.
- Earthworms are the oldest and most widely used fishing bait in the world.
