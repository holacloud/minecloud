# Iron shears

![Iron shears](item:iron_shears)

A pair of shearing scissors. Use them to **take wool and fur from animals without hurting them** and to cut
**leaves** and **wool** in a flash.

| In-game name | Id |
|---|---|
| Iron Shears | `iron_shears` |

They only come in iron.

## How to get them

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `iron_shears` | 2 `iron_ore` |

It is the only tool with no wood in it.

## On animals (right click)

| Animal | Gives | Wait |
|---|---|---|
| ![Sheep](mob:sheep) [Sheep](17) | **1–3 `white_wool`**; the sheep stays shorn until its wool grows back | 150 s |
| ![](mob:goat) goat, ![](mob:camel) camel, ![](mob:horse) horse, ![](mob:dog) dog, ![](mob:cat) cat, ![](mob:fox) fox, ![](mob:rabbit) rabbit, ![](mob:mole) mole | **1 `fur`** | 120 s per animal |

If you try too soon, the chat tells you it has not grown back yet. The animal is not hurt and does not get angry.

## For mining

Shears count as an iron tool (×2.6) for:

| Block | Bare hand | With shears |
|---|---|---|
| Leaves (all nine) | 0.2 s | 0.08 s |
| Wool (all 16 colours) | 0.35 s | 0.13 s |

On every other block they speed things up ×1.43, like any iron tool.

## Tips

- A pen with a few sheep plus shears = **endless white wool** for [nets](95), [feather capes](92) and [beds](63).
- Fur can be tanned into leather: 2 `fur` + 1 `wood` = 1 `leather` (see [Fur](77)).
- Shears are the most useful thing you can make with your **first 2 iron ore**.

## Trivia

- The oldest shears, one-piece U-shaped blades, were already used in ancient Egypt.
- A merino sheep can give over 5 kilos of wool a year.
- Chris the sheep, lost for years in Australia without a shearing, ended up carrying **41 kilos** of wool.
