| Result | Ingredients |

## In the lab

Reactions the lab knows from the start:

- [Water](118): `2 H + O → H₂O (water)`
- [Glass](51): `Si + 2 O → SiO₂ (glass)`

It takes part in **11** reactions you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *Shells and chalk hold calcium, carbon and plenty of oxygen.*
- *Painters' whitest white comes from titanium hugging two oxygens.*
- *What you breathe out puts bubbles into a water flask.*
- *They say iron rusts when it meets oxygen: two of iron for three of oxygen.*
- *Corundum (two aluminium, three oxygen) turns red with a pinch of chromium.*
- *The same corundum turns blue with a little iron and titanium.*
- *Copper, sulfur and four oxygens form deep blue crystals.*
- *Sodium, hydrogen, carbon and three oxygens make bread rise.*
- *One calcium with a single oxygen: the builders' lime.*
- *Glass (Si + 2 O) turns deep blue with a touch of cobalt.*
- *Lithium, cobalt and two oxygens store your phone's energy.*

## In the real world

- It is **21 %** of the air, and almost every living thing needs it to breathe.
- It is the most abundant element in the Earth's crust (almost half its mass) and in your body (about two thirds).
- Liquid oxygen is pale blue.
