## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize H Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_h` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

Reactions the lab knows from the start:

- [Water](118): `2 H + O → H₂O (water)`

It takes part in **2** reactions you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *A nitrogen surrounded by hydrogens smells strong... and plants love it.*
- *Sodium, hydrogen, carbon and three oxygens make bread rise.*

## In the real world

- It is the **lightest** element and the most abundant in the universe: about three quarters of normal matter.
- The Sun shines because its core fuses hydrogen into helium.
- Its name means "water maker": when it burns, it makes water.
