# Gold leaf

![Gold leaf](item:gold_leaf)

Sheets of beaten **gold**, so thin that light shines through.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:gold_leaf) | Gold Leaf | `gold_leaf` |

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *Gold reacts with nothing, but alone in the flask it is beaten paper-thin.*

## What it is for

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `gold_block` (*Press Gold Leaf into a Block*) | 4 `gold_leaf` |

## Trivia

- Gold leaf is about a ten-thousandth of a millimetre thick: roughly a thousand times thinner than paper.
- It gilds altarpieces and frames, and even some cakes: gold does not react, so it is harmless.

## Solution

If you would rather discover it yourself, stop reading here.

| Reaction | Kind | Product |
|---|---|---|
| `Au → gold leaf` | pure metal | 8 `gold_leaf` |
