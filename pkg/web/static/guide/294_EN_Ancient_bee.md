# Ancient bee

![Ancient bee](mob:ancient_bee)

**One of the first bees**: still half wasp, with a narrow waist and thin legs, and already **dusted with
pollen**. It is an ancient relative of the [bee](35), but it lived alone, with no hive and no honey, and **it
stings nobody**. It is an [extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself, it has to be brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Look for amber.** It is under the **forest**, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:amber_rock)
   `amber_rock`: stone with honey-coloured drops that glow a little in the dark. The page on [amber](263) says
   how deep to dig.
2. **Break the block** with a [pickaxe](68). Which insect is inside depends on the place: if it was an ancient
   bee, you take ![](item:fossil_ancient_bee) `fossil_ancient_bee` with you: a piece of amber with the bee in it.
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45 seconds**. It is
   a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived ancient bee: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back.

It is **one bee**, not a colony: it has no hive, it makes no honey and no swarm comes to defend it.

## Behaviour

- **It flies low**, 1 block above the ground, from one place to another near its home. Its wings never stop,
  and from close by you hear it hum.
- **It lands.** When it has had nowhere to go for 4 seconds, it comes down to the ground and folds its wings
  along its back. It takes off again as soon as it moves on.
- It is **peaceful**: it comes up to nobody and it never stings. If you hit it, it takes off and stays in
  the air for a while.

There is one colour only: a brown, hairy body speckled with yellow pollen, and a dark abdomen with thin
golden rings.

## Tips

- Wait for it to land to look at it closely: the narrow waist, the long feelers, the pollen on its hind legs.
- It is as small as a bee of today: look for it at the height of your knees, near the machine that revived it.
- You can stand next to it without fear. It is not like the bees of the [hive](35), which chase whoever
  bothers them.
- In the [Bestiary](5), its sheet and the bee's show the two together under the title **Evolution**.

## The real ancient bee

- **When and where.** It lived about **100 million years ago**, in the Cretaceous, the last age of the
  dinosaurs. It was found in the amber of **Myanmar**, in Asia.
- **Half wasp, half bee.** Two bees are known from that amber. One, *Melittosphex*, is only **3 millimetres**
  long. It has branched hairs, like feathers, which is what bees use to carry pollen; but its hind legs are
  thin, like those of a wasp. The other, *Discoscapa*, was found with grains of **pollen** still stuck to
  its body.
- **Where bees come from.** From wasps. Wasps hunt insects to feed their young. Some of them began to bring
  **pollen** instead of prey, and their descendants are the bees: wasps that turned vegetarian.
- **Bees and flowers.** In those days plants with flowers were spreading over the world. Flowers feed bees,
  and bees carry pollen from flower to flower. Each has changed the other ever since.
- **No hive.** It lived alone. Even today most of the 20,000 kinds of bees live alone, in a hole in the ground
  or in a stem. The honey bee, with its hive, is the exception.
- **Size.** In the game it is more than a hundred times bigger than it was, so that you can see it.
- **Is it the bee's grandmother?** Close. Scientists still argue whether *Melittosphex* was already a true bee
  or the nearest thing to one. It shows what the first bees must have looked like.
- **Why it died out.** Nobody knows. Its relatives carried on, and changed into the bees of today.
- **In amber.** [Amber](263) keeps an insect whole, down to its hairs and the pollen on them. Its colour is
  not so sure: the colours of the game are a guess.
