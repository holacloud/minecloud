# Sheep

![Sheep](mob:sheep)

A calm, woolly sheep that grazes in small flocks. It never attacks, and it is the game's main source of **white
wool**.

## Where it lives

- **Plains:** it is the most common animal of the meadows.
- **Rocky hills:** it shows up there too, less often.
- **Forests:** a few, now and then.
- Out by day and by night, in flocks of **2 to 4**.

## Behaviour

- It strolls slowly without wandering far from where it was born.
- It is a **grazer**: when it stands still, it lowers its head to nibble the grass.
- It also looks around, flicks its ears and breathes slowly.
- If you hit it, it hops, gives a short bleat and turns towards you. It does not fight back.

## How to interact

- **Hitting it:** it cannot be hurt or defeated.
- **Shearing it:** right click with `iron_shears` ![Shears](item:iron_shears): it drops **1 to 3** `white_wool`.
  - It becomes slimmer and pinkish grey, without its little woolly cap.
  - The wool grows back in **150 seconds**. Until then it says "Its wool is still growing back".
- **Taming it** (see [Taming and breeding](3)):

| Food | Chance per try |
|---|---|
| ![Tall grass](item:tall_grass) `tall_grass`, ![Yellow flower](item:flower_yellow) `flower_yellow`, ![Leaves](item:leaves) `leaves` | 1/3 |

- **Bait:** it comes to `bait_seeds` (see [Baits and traps](4)).
- **Cage:** it **does** fit in the `cage_trap`.
- **Egg:** `sheep_egg`. The command `/mob sheep` puts one in front of you.

## Tips

- Tame two sheep and breed them: you get a wool flock next to your house that never despawns.
- Wool goes into the `feather_cape` and the feather bed (see [Mob resources](2) and [Coloured wool](58)).
- Tamed sheep can sit (right click with an empty hand) so they do not follow you while you shear the others.

## Trivia

- Real sheep grow wool all their lives without stopping: that is why they need shearing every year.
- Sheep recognise faces: they can remember other sheep and people for years.
- In the game there is only one coat: white wool, with a black face and legs.
- Shearing makes a "snip, snip" from the shears and a bleat.
