# Day, night and weather

## The world clock

- A full MineCloud day lasts **4 real minutes**.
- **Night** runs from **18:00 to 6:00**: about 2 minutes.
- The server keeps the clock, so **it is the same time for every player**.
- Type **/time** in the chat to see the time, for example "Current world time: 21:30". The compass and the
  clock on screen help you too.

The sun and the moon are cubes that cross the sky. The moon changes **phase** every day, in an 8-day cycle.

## When night falls

- The sky and the fog grow dark and you cannot see as far.
- **Torch** light stands out much more.
- Under a roof or in a cave everything is darker still.

The night changes the animals too:

| Creature | What it does at night |
|---|---|
| [Wandering shadow](37) | rises out of the dark, away from light, and stalks you |
| [Wisp](41) | a flame floating far away that tries to lead you into danger |
| [Fireflies](36) | clouds of tiny lights over the grass and ponds, from dusk to dawn |
| [Bats](38) | wake at dusk and flutter about chasing fireflies |
| [Owl](46) | wakes up, hoots, its eyes glow and it hunts |
| [Fox](45) | yips to other foxes and **steals items lying on the ground** |
| [Scorpion](31) | gets more dangerous: by day it only attacks if you come very close |
| [Rabbits](44) | go into their burrow at dusk and come out at dawn |
| [Bees](35) | shelter in their hive |

**Tip:** do not leave items lying around at night if there are foxes nearby. If you strike the fox, it
drops what it carries in its mouth.

## Rain

- The weather changes on its own: every **one or two minutes** it goes from clear to rain, or back.
- When it rains, the sun dims a little and the fog turns grey.
- Bees shelter in their hive.
- Rain brings [storm golems](43): they are born from a lightning strike and crumble when the rain stops.

Rain happens in your browser: another player may not see the same rain at the same time as you.

## Sleeping

- At night, aim at a **bed** and press **F** to sleep until dawn.
- If you play with others, **dawn comes for everyone**, and the chat says who slept.
- You cannot sleep by day ("You can only sleep at night").
- A bed you place becomes your **respawn point**. See [Bed](63).
