# Honeycomb

![Honeycomb](item:honeycomb)

A chunk of golden wax comb, dripping with honey. It comes from **beehives** and makes **honey bottles**, **honey
bait**, and wins over pigs and horses.

## How to get it

**Collect honey:** right click a ![Bee](mob:bee) [bee](35) hive, whatever you are holding. **1–2 `honeycomb`**
fall to the ground. Then that hive needs **120 seconds** to fill up again ("The hive has no honeycomb left").

| Held item | What happens |
|---|---|
| `torch` | **The smoke calms the bees.** You take the comb in peace, and it even soothes them if they were already angry. |
| anything else | The colony **gets angry and chases you** to sting you (1 health per sting). |

| 2 [honey baits](93) `bait_honey` | **1** `honeycomb` |

## Tips

- **Always bring a torch** to the hive: it is free and saves you a swarm.
- Raw honeycomb **is not food**: turn it into a honey bottle (+5 health).
- One honeycomb makes 2 honey baits: set near the hive, the bees go and sip them instead of visiting flowers.

## Trivia

- Comb cells are **hexagons**: the shape that holds the most honey with the least wax.
- Real beekeepers use **smoke** too: it masks the bees' alarm scent and calms them.
- The game's loot list has honeycomb (10 %) for defeating a bee, but bees cannot be defeated: striking them only
  angers the colony.
