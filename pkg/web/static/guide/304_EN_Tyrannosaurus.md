# Tyrannosaurus

![Tyrannosaurus](mob:tyrannosaurus)

The **great hunter of the last dinosaurs**, and the rarest prize of the game: **8 blocks** long, **4.2 blocks**
tall at the hips, its body level over two strong legs, a **huge head full of teeth**, two **tiny arms** with two
fingers each and a heavy tail held out behind for balance. **It is hostile: it comes for you, it roars, and it
charges.** It is an [extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself, it has to be brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Its skeleton, in three pieces.** You need the skull, the spine and the tail: `fossil_tyrannosaurus_skull`,
   `fossil_tyrannosaurus_spine` and `fossil_tyrannosaurus_tail`. They come out of the three blocks of a
   **skeleton** (`fossil_bones_skull`, `fossil_bones_spine` and `fossil_bones_tail`), which lie in a straight line
   inside the stone, deep down. The three blocks of a skeleton always belong to one animal. It is a
   **legendary** fossil and **the rarest skeleton of all**: its weight against the others is 1, against 6 of the
   mammoth, 2 of the plesiosaur and 2 of the diplodocus. The page on [fossils](262) says how deep to dig and how
   to find the other two pieces once you have one.
2. **Break the blocks** with a [pickaxe](68).
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264), with **the three pieces** inside. It takes **120
   seconds**. It is a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived tyrannosaurus: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back. **Build
its pen before you start the machine**, with the machine inside, in an open place: some **10 blocks** of flat
ground around the machine, and nothing lower than **5 blocks** over it. The machine puts it on a free block
next to itself and knows nothing of its size.

## Behaviour

- While nobody is near, **it walks slowly** around its place, swaying its tail.
- **It is hostile, by day and by night.** When a player is within **16 blocks**, it goes after them: the
  nearest one, and the same one for everybody who is watching. It roars when it starts.
- **It walks after you at 2.2 blocks a second.** You walk at 4.5: on foot you can always get away from its walk.
- **It is slow to turn**: its body turns about **80 degrees a second** at most. Go round to its side and it
  needs time to face you.
- **It bites with its jaws**, which are nearly 3 blocks in front of its middle: it stops **3 blocks** from you
  and bites whoever is at its jaws, **6 damage**, at most once every 1.5 seconds. You have 20 health.
- **It charges.** When you are in front of it, between **5 and 12 blocks** away, it **roars** with its jaws wide
  open for **1.2 seconds**. Then **it runs in a straight line** at where you were, at **7.5 blocks a second**,
  for up to 1.6 seconds: faster than you, but it cannot turn while it runs. If it gets within **3.6 blocks** of
  you, it does **7 damage** and throws you back. Then it stands still for 2 seconds, and it does not charge again
  for 4 more.
- Only the player it is after sees it charge. If you hit it, it charges you too.
- **It lets go** when you are more than 24 blocks from it, or more than 32 blocks from its home. Then it walks
  back. If it defeats you, it leaves you alone for 10 seconds.
- **Its body takes room.** It stops before its chest reaches a wall, a fence, a deep drop or a roof lower than
  its back: it looks **2.2 blocks** ahead of its middle, and 3.2 blocks while it charges. It cannot go through a
  door.

There is one colour only: brown with darker stripes across the back, a pale belly and gold eyes.

## Tips

- **When it roars, run to one side.** It charges at where you were when it set off, not at where you are: a few
  long steps sideways and it goes past.
- **Do not stand in front of its head.** Its jaws are where it bites; at its side you have time.
- **Defeating it is a feat: do it with friends.** Its health is the server's, so the blows of everybody add up,
  and it only goes after one of you at a time while the others strike.
- Wear armour, and carry a [shield](252) in your left hand: facing it, its bite and its charge do half the
  damage.
- A wall or a fence stops it, but its head is high: stand a few blocks back from the wall, or its jaws reach
  over it.
- Put it next to a [triceratops](297): they lived at the same time and in the same place.

## The real Tyrannosaurus

- **When and where.** *Tyrannosaurus rex* lived in the west of **North America**, between about **68 and 66
  million years ago**: it was one of the very last dinosaurs.
- **Its name** means "tyrant lizard king".
- **Size.** It was about **12 to 13 metres** long and almost **4 metres** tall at the hips, and weighed between
  about **6 and 9 tonnes**, more than an elephant. Its skull was about **1.5 metres** long. In the game it is
  smaller, so that it fits.
- **Teeth and bite.** It had about 60 teeth, thick and serrated like knives, the biggest about **30
  centimetres** long counting the root: the size of a banana. Its bite was one of the strongest of any animal that
  ever lived on land: it crushed bones. Bones of Triceratops have been found with its tooth marks.
- **The tiny arms.** Its arms were only about **1 metre** long, with two fingers, but they were strong.
  Scientists still argue about what it used them for.
- **Senses.** The part of its brain for smell was very big: it smelled very well, and its eyes faced forward a
  little, so it could judge distances.
- **Was it fast?** Probably not very: an animal so heavy would break its legs running like a horse. It walked
  fast, perhaps at **15 to 25 kilometres an hour**. In the game it charges only for a moment.
- **Growing up.** A young one grew slowly and then, as a teenager, gained up to about **2 kilos a day**. The most
  complete skeleton, "Sue", in a museum in Chicago, belonged to one that lived about **28 years**.
- **Why it died out.** About **66 million years ago** a huge rock from space, an asteroid, crashed into the
  Earth, where Mexico is today, and the dinosaurs died out, all but one branch: the **birds**.
- **Its family.** It was a **theropod**, the branch of dinosaurs that walked on two legs and ate meat. The birds
  come from that branch: Tyrannosaurus is not their grandfather, but a distant cousin of the hen and the eagle.
  Some smaller relatives of it had feathers; the skin of Tyrannosaurus that has been found has small scales.
