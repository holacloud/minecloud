# Baking soda

![Baking soda](item:baking_soda)

**Sodium bicarbonate**: a white powder that releases CO₂ bubbles when heated. It makes bread rise.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:baking_soda) | Baking Soda | `baking_soda` |

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *Sodium, hydrogen, carbon and three oxygens make bread rise.*

## What it is for

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 2 `bread` (*Bake Soda Bread*) | 2 `wheat` + 1 `baking_soda` |

The usual recipe turns 3 wheat into 1 bread: with baking soda, 1 wheat makes 1 bread.

## Trivia

- Irish **soda bread** has no yeast: baking soda makes it rise.
- Baking soda with vinegar fizzes: it is the science fair "volcano".

## Solution

If you would rather discover it yourself, stop reading here.

| Reaction | Kind | Product |
|---|---|---|
| `Na + H + C + 3 O → NaHCO₃ (baking soda)` | compound | 4 `baking_soda` |
