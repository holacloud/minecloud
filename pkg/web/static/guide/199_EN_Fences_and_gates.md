# Fences and gates

![Oak fence](item:oak_fence)

A **fence** is a post that reaches out with rails to its neighbours: with them you close in pens and gardens. A
**gate** is the piece of fence that opens.

## How to get them

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| **3** fences | **2** planks + **1** log of the same wood |
| **1** gate | **2** planks + **2** logs of the same wood |

## Fences

- They join by themselves with the fences next to them (of any wood), with solid blocks and with gates set in line
  with them. Remove one and the others pull their rails back.
- **They are a block and a half tall** for collisions: nobody jumps them. You jump a little over one block and
  animals only climb one block, so whatever you pen in, wild or [tamed](3), stays in.
- Riding, a [horse](7) does clear a fence: its jump is much higher than yours.
- You see through them, and light passes between the posts.

## Gates

- When placed it goes **across in front of you**, in line with the fence.
- **Right click** opens or closes it; every player sees it and the world saves it. It always opens **to the far
  side**, away from whoever opens it.
- Open, you walk through without bumping into it (so do animals wandering by). Closed, it is just more fence.
- **Animals cannot open it.**
- **Crouching (Shift)**, a right click places the block you hold instead of opening it.

## Tips

- Fence the whole pen and leave a gate: walk in with the animals following you and close it behind you.
- An open gate does not stop a horse: open it to ride out at a gallop.
- A row of fences along a cliff edge prevents falls.

## Trivia

- Dry-stone walls in the countryside are built without mortar: the stones lock together and stand for centuries.
- A *cattle grid* is a grille across a road: cars drive over it, but cows will not step on it.
