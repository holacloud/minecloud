# Bronze

![Bronze](item:bronze_ingot)

Copper with tin: the first great alloy in history. In the game it is cast into **bells**.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:bronze_ingot) | Bronze Ingot | `bronze_ingot` |

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *5,000 years ago someone melted plenty of copper with a little tin...*

## What it is for

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 2 `sound_block` (*Cast Bronze Bells*) | 1 `bronze_ingot` + 1 `planks` |

It is the cheapest way to get many [sound blocks](66).

## Trivia

- Real bronze has about **12 %** tin by mass: more than ten copper atoms for every tin atom. The lab uses 3 to 1 so you spend fewer blocks.
- The **Bronze Age** began over 5,000 years ago in the Near East.
- Bells are cast from a bronze with more tin (about 20 %), which rings clearer.

## Solution

If you would rather discover it yourself, stop reading here.

| Reaction | Kind | Product |
|---|---|---|
| `3 Cu + Sn → bronze` | alloy | 2 `bronze_ingot` |
