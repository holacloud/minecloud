# Fur

![Fur](item:fur)

A tuft of soft, warm fur. It is trimmed with shears without hurting the animal, and it is used to **tan leather**
and for the **warm coat**.

## How to get it

**Trim** with `iron_shears` ([Iron shears](73), 2 `iron_ore`) in your hand: right click the animal. You get **1
fur**, and that same animal needs **120 seconds** before it can be trimmed again ("was trimmed recently").

## What it is for

| Recipe | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| [Leather](76) `leather` (tanning) | **2** `fur` + 1 `wood` |
| [Warm coat](90) `warm_coat` | 3 `leather` + **2** `fur` |

## Tips

- The wait is **per animal**: with several tamed dogs, goats or horses ([Taming and breeding](3)) you can trim them
  one after another without waiting.
- Tamed animals follow you, so your fur herd travels with you.

## Trivia

- Some goats give luxury fibres: **cashmere** and **mohair**. Camel hair is spun into coats too.
- Trimming does not hurt or anger anyone: not even the fox or the giant mole.
