# Snake

![Snake](mob:snake)

An ambush hunter that hides **buried in the sand** with only its eyes showing. Before it strikes it warns you: it
coils up, **hisses** and, if it is a rattlesnake, shakes its tail. Its bite is **venomous**.

## Where it lives

- In the **desert** and, a little less often, on **rocky hills and mountains**.
- Always **alone**. There are at most **3 snakes** at a time.
- By day and by night. At night it moves about a little more often.

## Behaviour

- **Lying in wait:** it is still most of the time. On soft ground (sand, dirt, grass, gravel or clay) **it buries
  itself** with only its head and eyes showing; on rock it lies **coiled** and flat.
- **Warning:** if you come within **5 blocks** (2.5 when sneaking), it rears up into an S facing you, opens its
  mouth and **hisses**. Rattlesnakes **shake their tail**.
- **Bite:** if you come within **2.2 blocks**, it lunges forward and bites: **1 damage + venom** (3 more hits of 1
  damage, every 1.5 s).
- **If you back off**, it calms down: it uncoils, slithers away in waves and buries itself somewhere else.
- It flicks its **forked tongue** often, especially when alert, even poking out of the sand.
- It throws up a cloud of sand as it buries itself or bursts out.

There are **3 kinds**:

| Kind | Looks like | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Sandy diamondback | sand coloured with dark diamonds and a banded tail, **with a rattle** | desert |
| Green snake | green with a dark stripe along the back and a yellowish belly, no rattle | desert and rocks |
| Rock rattlesnake | speckled grey with dark crossbands, **with a rattle** | rocks |

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit it | It recoils and gets angry: **it strikes back almost at once**. Badly hurt, it flees. |
| Defeat it | **3 hits.** It leaves 1–2 ![scale](item:scale) `scale` and, 60 % of the time, a ![venom sac](item:venom_sac) `venom_sac`. |
| Tame it | No: it is hostile. |
| Bait | It is listed with `bait_meat`, but it follows its own behaviour and hardly ever comes. |
| Cage | It fits, but you will only catch it if it wanders in by chance. |
| Egg | `snake_egg` |
| Command | `/mob snake` ("Keep your distance") |

## Tips

- **Sneak through the desert:** the snake warns you much later, and you can walk around it.
- If it hisses, **back off**: as soon as you move away, it calms down and leaves.
- Carry `milk` or, better, an `antivenom`: it cures the venom and gives 3 minutes of immunity. Antivenom is made
  with `venom_sac` + `milk`. See [Antivenom](89).
- 6 `scale` and 2 `leather` make the `scale_mail`, which reduces damage from creatures. See [Scale mail](91).
- If you hit it, keep going: after the first blow it bites back right away.

## Trivia

- Rattlesnakes shake their tail as a **warning**: they would rather scare you off than waste venom.
- The **forked tongue** is how they "smell": it picks up particles from the air and carries them to an organ in
  the roof of the mouth.
- The game's snake body is a **chain of 9 segments** that follow the path of the head: that is why it moves in
  waves, like a real one.
- On the model, the **fangs** only show when it opens its mouth.
- Many desert snakes bury themselves in the sand to hide from the heat and wait for prey.
