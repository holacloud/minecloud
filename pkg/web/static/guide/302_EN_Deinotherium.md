# Deinotherium

![Deinotherium](mob:deinotherium)

A **relative of the elephants**, taller than any elephant of today: grey wrinkled skin, long legs, big ears,
a short trunk and, what no other animal has, **two tusks in its lower jaw that curve down and back** towards
its chest. It stands **3.5 blocks** tall, more than twice a [horse](34). It is peaceful. It is an
[extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself, it has to be brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Look for its fossil.** It lies far down, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:fossil_rock_rare)
   `fossil_rock_rare`: a dark rock with a three-toed footprint. The page on [fossils](262) says how deep to dig.
2. **Break the block** with a [pickaxe](68). Which animal is inside depends on the place: if it was a
   deinotherium, you take ![](item:fossil_deinotherium) `fossil_deinotherium` with you.
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45 seconds**. It is
   a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived deinotherium: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back.

## Behaviour

- **It strolls** near its place with long, slow strides, swaying its short trunk.
- It is a grazer: when it stands still, **it lowers its head to eat**.
- It turns its head to look around and flicks its big ears.
- It is **peaceful**: it comes up to nobody and attacks nobody. If you hit it, it gives a start.

There is one colour only: grey with darker wrinkles, pinkish inside the ears, and ivory tusks.

## Tips

- It is very tall: in a forest its head goes through the leaves. Bring it back in an open place.
- Put it next to a mammoth: the mammoth's tusks grow from the upper jaw and curl up, the deinotherium's from the
  lower jaw and curl down.

## The real Deinotherium

- **When and where.** It lived in **Europe, Asia and Africa**, from about **15 million** to about **1 million
  years ago**. The last ones lived in Africa, at the same time as some of the first humans.
- **Its name** means "terrible beast".
- **Size.** The biggest stood **3.5 to 4 metres** at the shoulder and weighed about **10 tonnes**: it is one of
  the biggest **mammals** (animals with hair that feed their young on milk) that ever walked on land.
- **The tusks.** They grew from the lower jaw and pointed down and back. Nobody knows for sure what it used them
  for: to pull branches down to its mouth, to strip the bark off trees, or to dig. Its trunk was probably shorter
  than an elephant's.
- **What it ate.** Leaves of trees and bushes. Its teeth were made for cutting leaves, not for grinding grass.
- **Its family.** It belonged to the same big group as elephants and mammoths, but to a branch that split off
  very early and left no descendants. It is not an ancestor of the elephants: rather a very distant cousin.
- **Why it died out.** Nobody knows for sure. While it lived, the climate of Africa grew drier, and the forests
  it fed in gave way to grasslands, where the elephants of today, which eat grass, did better.
