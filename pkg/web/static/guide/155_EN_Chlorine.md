## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize CL Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_cl` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

Reactions the lab knows from the start:

- [Salt](119): `Na + Cl → NaCl (salt)`

It takes part in **1** reaction you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *Potassium, joined to a halogen, also feeds the fields.*

## In the real world

- It is a **greenish**, poisonous gas.
- It disinfects swimming pools and tap water.
- It is half of table salt.
