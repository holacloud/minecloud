# Sapphire

![Sapphire](item:sapphire)

A blue gem: the same corundum as [ruby](175), with traces of iron and titanium.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:sapphire) | Sapphire | `sapphire` |

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *The same corundum turns blue with a little iron and titanium.*

## What it is for

It is a **collectible**: the G key tells you what it is.

## Trivia

- Ruby and sapphire are the same mineral: only the impurities change.
- Sapphires come in almost every colour; the red ones are called rubies.
- The glass of many watches is synthetic sapphire, almost impossible to scratch.

## Solution

If you would rather discover it yourself, stop reading here.

| Reaction | Kind | Product |
|---|---|---|
| `2 Al + 3 O + Fe + Ti → Al₂O₃ : Fe, Ti (sapphire)` | coloured crystal | 1 `sapphire` |
