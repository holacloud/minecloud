## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize LI Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_li` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

It takes part in **1** reaction you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *Lithium, cobalt and two oxygens store your phone's energy.*

## In the real world

- It is the **lightest** metal: it even floats on oil.
- It is in the rechargeable batteries of phones, laptops and electric cars.
- It is one of the three elements made in the Big Bang, with hydrogen and helium.
