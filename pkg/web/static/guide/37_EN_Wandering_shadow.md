# Wandering shadow

![Wandering shadow](mob:shadow)

A tall hooded figure with **two eyes of light** under its hood, which rises out of the darkness in the dead of
night and glides after you. Light is its only weakness. Few have seen it: it is **a legend**, and many players
never meet it at all.

## Where it lives

- **Anywhere, but only in the dead of night** (from 22:00 to 4:00), **under a clear sky**. Never by day, and
  never in the rain: storms belong to the [storm golems](43).
- It only comes for those who wander **far from home**: more than 48 blocks from your respawn point, with **no
  torch** in your hand and none placed within 10 blocks of you.
- It is **very, very rare**. After one appears, none rises again for at least **3 days**, and even then the
  night has to be right. On average, **one every 10 nights or so** spent out in the wild.
- There is only ever **one**, and never while a storm golem walks.
- **An omen comes first:** the night falls silent and the chat warns you. The first time you will know
  something special is coming. A few seconds later it **rises from the ground in a cloud of smoke**, 20–30
  blocks away from you.
- It never rises over water or within 4 blocks of a **placed torch**.

## Behaviour

- If you are within **24 blocks**, it glides towards you, floating and barely moving its legs, its cloak
  billowing and its claws raised.
- **If you stare at it from afar** (more than 7 blocks), it stops and stares back. As soon as you look away, it
  moves on.
- If something blocks its way for a while, **it fades out and reappears** in smoke about 5 blocks from you.
- Within 1.4 blocks **it strikes with its claws**: **3 damage**, every 1.6 s.
- When it is close (within 14 blocks), you hear **whispers**.
- When it is not chasing you, it wanders aimlessly, sometimes standing still.
- **Light undoes it:** dawn, a `torch` in your hand within 5 blocks, or a placed torch within 4 blocks of it make
  it **dissolve into smoke**, shrinking and fading away.

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit it | It reels back in smoke. **3 hits** banish it ("You banished the wandering shadow") and it leaves 1–2 ![shadow essence](item:shadow_essence) `shadow_essence`. |
| Chase it off with light | It flees ("The shadow fled from the light"). If you are within 16 blocks, there is a **50 %** chance it leaves 1 `shadow_essence`. |
| Tame, bait, cage or net | No. |
| Egg | `shadow_egg` |
| Command | `/mob shadow` (summoned ones last a minute in daylight, so you can see them). |

## Tips

- **Always carry a torch at night.** With one in your hand, no shadow gets within 5 blocks of you.
- **Ring your house with torches:** shadows are not born near them, and the ones that come dissolve.
- Staring freezes it, but only from afar: once it is close, it strikes anyway.
- `shadow_essence` with 2 `stone` makes 2 `obsidian`. See [Mob resources](2).

## Trivia

- It is the creature with the deepest voice in the game: its base tone is only **70 Hz**.
- In **Ultra** mode its eyes glow so brightly that they have a halo in the dark.
- It is like the figures of many legends: beings that only move when you are not looking. In MineCloud, it is
  one of those legends: if you have seen one, you can tell the tale.
- In earlier versions it appeared almost every night, two at a time, and the world was full of shadows. It
  has since withdrawn to the deepest hours.
- **At dawn**, a shadow dissolving near you sometimes **whispers something** as the sun rises. It is worth
  keeping an eye on the chat and writing it down.
