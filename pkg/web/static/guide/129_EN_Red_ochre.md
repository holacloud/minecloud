# Red ochre

![Red ochre](item:rust_pigment)

A red powder of **iron oxide**: plain rust, and humanity's oldest pigment.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:rust_pigment) | Red Ochre | `rust_pigment` |

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *They say iron rusts when it meets oxygen: two of iron for three of oxygen.*

## What it is for

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 2 `red_wool` (*Dye Wool with Red Ochre*) | 1 `rust_pigment` + 2 `white_wool` |

## Trivia

- The paintings of the **Altamira** cave use iron oxide ochres.
- Mars is red because its dust is full of iron oxide: it is a rusty planet.

## Solution

If you would rather discover it yourself, stop reading here.

| Reaction | Kind | Product |
|---|---|---|
| `2 Fe + 3 O → Fe₂O₃ (red ochre)` | compound | 2 `rust_pigment` |
