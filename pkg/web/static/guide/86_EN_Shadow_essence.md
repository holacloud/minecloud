# Shadow essence

![Shadow essence](item:shadow_essence)

A wisp of darkness held in an invisible flask, with violet sparks inside. It is what remains of a **wandering
shadow**, and it hardens stone into **obsidian**.

## How to get it

Only from the ![Wandering shadow](mob:shadow) [wandering shadow](37), which appears at night away from the light:

| How the shadow ends | Essence |
|---|---|
| **Banished with blows** (3 blows) | **1–2**, always |
| **Flees from the light**: a `torch` in your hand within 5 blocks, or one placed near it | **50 %** chance of 1 |
| **Dissolves at dawn** | **50 %** chance of 1 |

In the last two cases you must be **within 16 blocks** when it dissolves.

## What it is for

| Recipe | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| **2** `obsidian` | **1** `shadow_essence` + 2 `stone` |

The usual obsidian recipe is 2 `stone` + 1 `sand` → 2 `obsidian`: the essence takes the place of the sand. See
[Fine stones and metals](59).

## Tips

- **Blows are sure, but they hurt:** each claw strike of the shadow takes 3 health. With the
  [scale mail](91) it only takes 1.
- **Light is safer:** draw your torch when it comes near. Half of the time it leaves essence without a fight.
- At dawn, stay close to the shadows following you: they may leave you a gift.

## Trivia

- Real obsidian is **volcanic glass**: lava that cooled so fast it had no time to crystallise. It is black and
  glossy, as if made of shadow.
- Sometimes, as it dissolves in the sun, a shadow whispers something in the chat. Pay attention.
