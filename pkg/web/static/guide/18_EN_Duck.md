# Duck

![Duck](mob:duck)

A white duck with an orange bill and feet that waddles about in little groups. It is harmless and gives away
**feathers** without needing to be hurt.

## Where it lives

- **Plains:** fairly common.
- **Desert:** it shows up among the dunes too.
- By day and by night, in groups of **2 to 4**.

## Behaviour

- It walks around unhurried near where it was born.
- Now and then it ruffles its wings a little, looks around and breathes.
- If you hit it, it hops, gives a high quack and turns towards you.
- It preens: if you are within 24 blocks, it sometimes **drops a feather** on its own.

## How to interact

- **Hitting it:** it has **12 health** and falls in **3 blows** bare-handed (2 with a stone sword or better, see
  [Swords](71)); defeated, it drops **1 to 3** `feather`. Besides, each hit has a **40 %** chance of dropping a `feather`
  ![Feather](item:feather) (at most one every 8 seconds per duck).
- **Taming it** (see [Taming and breeding](3)):

| Food | Chance per try |
|---|---|
| ![Tall grass](item:tall_grass) `tall_grass`, ![Wheat seeds](item:wheat_seeds) `wheat_seeds`, ![Raw fish](item:raw_fish) `raw_fish`, ![Yellow flower](item:flower_yellow) `flower_yellow` | 1/3 |

- **Baits:** it comes to `bait_seeds` and `bait_worms` (see [Baits and traps](4)).
- **Cage:** it **does** fit in the `cage_trap`.
- **Egg:** `duck_egg`, or the command `/mob duck`.

## Tips

- Feathers go into the `feather_cape` (6 feathers + 2 wool), which softens fall damage, and into the feather
  bed. See [Feather](75).
- A couple of tamed ducks next to your house give you feathers little by little without you doing anything.
- It is one of the few animals that can be tamed with both fish and grass.

## Trivia

- Real ducks have a gland near the tail with an oil they spread over their feathers to make them waterproof.
- Ducklings follow the first creature they see after hatching: this is called **imprinting**.
- Ducks' feet do not freeze on ice because the blood flowing to them cools down and warms up again on the way.
- In the game there are no colour variants: they are all white.
