# Trilobite

![Trilobite](mob:trilobite)

An armoured animal of the sea bed, with **three lobes** along its back and a great many little legs underneath.
It has been extinct for 252 million years: in MineCloud you only see one if you find its fossil and bring it back
to life.

## What it was

An **arthropod**: a distant relative of crabs, spiders and insects, its body covered by a hard armour made of
pieces. Its name means "three lobes", and they are not its head, its body and its tail: they are the three
strips that run **along** it, a raised one in the middle and one on each side. Look at one from above and you
will see them.

## How to get one

1. **Its fossil.** It comes out of common fossil rock, `fossil_rock`, the stone with a coiled shell drawn on it:
   it is inside the stone, between y = −11 and y = 2. Breaking it gives you the fossil of the animal it held, and
   that may be a `fossil_trilobite`. See [Fossils](262).
2. **Water.** It is a sea animal: the revival machine has to stand next to **4 blocks of water** joined
   together, or more. A puddle that you dig and fill with a [bucket](111) will do, and so will the sea.
3. **The [revival machine](264).** With its fossil inside it takes 20 seconds. The trilobite appears in the water.

## Behaviour

- **It walks on the bed** on its little legs, slowly, and often stops to rest and to feel about with its
  antennae.
- **It goes up and down steps** of the bed one block high.
- **It never leaves the water**: at the shore or at a wall, it turns round.
- **It does not go further** than about 3 and a half blocks from where it was revived.
- **If its water goes away**, it stays still on the ground, waiting. It does not die: as soon as the water is
  back, it walks on.

It is almost **half a block** long. There are **3 colours**: chestnut, slate grey and ochre.

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit it | Six times out of ten **it rolls into a ball** and stays still for 3 to 5 and a half seconds. The other times, it runs off. |
| Defeat it | **2 hits** bare-handed. It leaves nothing. |
| Tame it | You can't. |
| Bait | None interests it. |
| Cage | No. |
| Egg | `trilobite_egg`: use it next to water. |
| Command | `/mob trilobite`: it appears in the nearest water; if there is none near, the game tells you. |

## The real science

- **When they lived.** They appeared about **521 million years ago** and the last ones died about
  **252 million** years ago. That is almost 270 million years in the seas: long before the dinosaurs, and for
  much longer than them.
- **How many there were.** More than **20,000 species** are known. That is why their fossil is one of the
  commonest.
- **How big they were.** Most were between 3 and 10 centimetres long; some, little more than a millimetre. The biggest one
  known was more than **70 centimetres** long. The one in the game is one of the giants, so that you can see
  it well.
- **Their eyes.** They are among the first complex eyes we know of. Each had many small lenses, like the eyes of
  a fly, and those lenses were made of **calcite**, a clear mineral: eyes of rock crystal. That is why they
  have been kept in the stone.
- **The ball.** Many trilobites rolled up to protect their soft belly, just as woodlice do today. Fossils have
  been found like that, rolled up.
- **They changed their armour.** To grow they had to shed the old armour and make another, as crabs do. Many
  trilobite fossils are not the animal: they are the armour it left behind.
- **What they ate.** It depended on the species: some stirred through the mud of the bottom, some hunted worms
  and some filtered the water.
- **Why they died out.** 252 million years ago came the greatest extinction in the history of the Earth.
  Enormous volcanoes, in what is Siberia today, changed the air and the sea for thousands of years, and most
  of the species of the sea disappeared. The last trilobites were among them.
- **Who is left of their family.** Nobody: they left no descendants. Their living relatives are the other
  arthropods.
