# Shell

![Shell](item:shell)

A ridged pink seashell. Ground up with sand it turns into **limestone**, and it is the cheapest way to get it.

## How to get it

| Source | How | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| ![Turtle](mob:turtle) [Turtle](28) | Strike it (without killing it): **15 %** per strike, with a **60 s** wait per turtle | 1 |
| ![Crab](mob:crab) [Crab](39) | Defeat it (3 blows) | **1–2** |
| ![Scorpion](mob:scorpion) [Scorpion](31) | Defeat it (2 blows): **25 %** | 1 |
| [Fishing rod](94) | **12 %** of catches | 1 |

## What it is for

| Recipe | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| **4** `limestone` | **1** `shell` + 1 `sand` |

Compare it with the usual limestone recipe: 2 `cobblestone` + 1 `sand` → only 2 `limestone`. With a shell you get
**twice as much** and save the stone. See [Fine stones and metals](59).

## Tips

- **Fishing** is the calmest source: roughly one catch in eight is a shell.
- Striking turtles gives little (15 % and a minute's wait); besides, they hide in their shell and flee.
- **Crabs** come in groups of 2 to 4 along the beach: a good harvest, but they pinch (1 health).

## Trivia

- Real limestone is largely made of the **shells and skeletons** of sea creatures piled up over millions of
  years. The game's recipe is almost geology.
- The crab's list of foods includes shells, but today crabs cannot be tamed.
