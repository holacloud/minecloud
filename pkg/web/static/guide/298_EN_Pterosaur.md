# Pterosaur

![Pterosaur](mob:pterosaur)

**A flying reptile** of the age of the dinosaurs: **not a dinosaur, and not a bird**. Its wings are skin, its
beak is long and has no teeth, and a crest of bone points back from its head. In the game its wings measure
**5 blocks** from tip to tip. It circles high over its home and harms nobody. It is an
[extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself, it has to be brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Look for its fossil.** It lies far down, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:fossil_rock_rare)
   `fossil_rock_rare`: a dark rock with a three-toed footprint. The page on [fossils](262) says how deep to dig.
2. **Break the block** with a [pickaxe](68). Which animal is inside depends on the place: if it was a
   pterosaur, you take ![](item:fossil_pterosaur) `fossil_pterosaur` with you.
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45 seconds**. It is
   a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived pterosaur: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back. It
appears on the ground next to the machine, and a few seconds later it takes off.

## Behaviour

- **It circles over its home**, about **9 blocks** over the ground, in wide turns, gliding with its wings
  held out. Every 7 to 14 seconds it beats them a few times. It leans into its turns.
- **It never goes more than 14 blocks from its home**, the place where it came back to life.
- **It comes down to perch.** After 50 to 100 seconds in the air it glides down to the highest place it sees
  near its home (a treetop, a roof, or the ground), folds its wings and sits there for 12 to 25 seconds,
  looking about. Then it rises **2 blocks** straight up, spreads its wings and goes back to its circles.
- **It never flies into anything.** It flies over what is low and round what is high.
- **With a roof over it, it does not fly.** On the ground it flutters out from under the roof, if there is
  open sky within 4 blocks; if not, it stays where it is. A roof put over it while it flies brings it down,
  slowly, after 4 seconds.
- It is **peaceful**: it dives at nobody. The real one caught fish; the one of the game does not fish.
- Each player sees it fly its own way round the same home.

There are **2 colours**: sand with an orange crest, and grey with a blue crest.

## Tips

- **To see it up close, wait for it to perch.** Look at its wing then: it is one piece of skin, with no
  feathers, and there are three small claws half way along it. Those are its fingers.
- **Revive it out of doors**, with room around it. Indoors it cannot take off, and it stays on the floor.
- It likes high places: put a wide roof or a platform near its home, lower than it flies, and it will often
  sit there.
- Compare it with the [Argentavis](281): the same size, but one is a bird and the other a reptile.

## The real pterosaur

- **Which one.** There were many kinds of pterosaur, some as small as a sparrow. The one of the game is drawn
  after *Pteranodon*, one of the best known.
- **When and where.** Pteranodon lived about **85 million years ago** in North America, on the coasts of a
  shallow sea that then split the continent in two. Its fossils come from the chalk of what was the bottom
  of that sea, in Kansas and the states around it; the first were found in 1870.
- **Its name** means "wing without teeth". "Pterosaur" means "winged lizard".
- **Not a dinosaur.** Pterosaurs were reptiles, cousins of the dinosaurs, but a different branch of the
  tree. And they are **not the ancestors of birds**: birds come from small feathered dinosaurs.
- **The first to fly.** Of all the animals with a backbone, pterosaurs were the first that flew by beating
  their wings: more than 200 million years ago, long before the birds and the bats.
- **A wing of skin.** Its wing was a sheet of skin and muscle stretched from the body to the tip of **one
  finger**, the fourth, which was enormously long. The other three fingers were small and had claws. A bat's
  wing is skin too, but stretched between four fingers; a bird's is made of feathers.
- **Size.** The biggest males had wings of up to **7 metres** from tip to tip, though most were smaller, and
  the females were about half that. For all those wings it weighed little, perhaps as much as a big dog:
  its bones were hollow, with walls as thin as card.
- **The crest.** The males had a long crest of bone, the females a small one. It was probably for showing
  off to one another.
- **What it ate.** Fish: fish bones have been found inside its fossils. It soared over the sea as an
  albatross does.
- **On the ground** it walked on all fours, with its wings folded and its weight on its hands. The one of
  the game sits up like a bird.
- **It was not bald.** Pterosaurs had a kind of short fuzz on the body. That is why the one of the game is
  furry.
- **Why it died out.** The last pterosaurs disappeared 66 million years ago, in the same great extinction
  as the dinosaurs, when an asteroid hit the Earth.
- **Nobody knows its colour**, and the colours of the game are a guess.
