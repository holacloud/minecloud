# Chat commands

Press **Enter** to open the chat, type, and press **Enter** again to send. **Escape** closes it without
sending.

Anything starting with **/** is a command: other players do not see it, the game runs it.

## All the commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/help` | shows the list of commands |
| `/guide` | opens this guide where you left it |
| `/guide 12` | opens the guide at page 12 (a secret code works too) |
| `/spawn` | takes you to your respawn point: your bed or, if you have none, the last safe spot |
| `/mob horse` | spawns an animal in front of you (see below) |
| `/time` | tells the world time, for example "21:30" |
| `/rtx` | turns RTX mode on or off (see [Graphics modes](14)) |
| `/sayhere text` | leaves a message floating at your spot for 30 s (up to 80 letters) |
| `/ping` | raises a beam of light with your name at your spot for 10 s |
| `/react heart` | throws hearts over the player you aim at |
| `/react clap` | applause over the player you aim at |
| `/react confetti` | confetti over the player you aim at |
| `/laugh` | a laugh that nearby players hear |
| `/cheer` | a cheer that nearby players hear |
| `/boo` | a boo that nearby players hear |

- `/sayhere`, `/ping` and `/react` are seen by **every player**.
- For `/react` you must aim at a player within 7 blocks.
- `/laugh`, `/cheer` and `/boo` carry about 16 blocks, louder the closer you are.

## Mentioning someone

**Left click** another player: the chat opens with `@name` already typed.
