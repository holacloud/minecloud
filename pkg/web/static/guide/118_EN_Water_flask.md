# Water flask

![Water flask](item:water_flask)

Water made from scratch in the lab: two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:water_flask) | Water Flask | `water_flask` |
| ![](item:water_bucket) | Water Bucket | `water_bucket` |

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117):

| Reaction | Formula | Product |
|---|---|---|
| 2 H + O | **H₂O** | 1 `water_bucket` if you carry an empty [bucket](111) (it is used up) |
| | | otherwise, 1 `water_flask` (uses 1 `glass`) |

If you carry a `bucket`, the lab fills it first: the bucket's water can be poured as a [water](52) block, far away
from any lake.

## Drinking (G key)

| Health | Effect |
|---|---|
| **+2** | you get the `glass` back |

At full health you do not drink it. It is also used to make [soda water](123).

## Trivia

- Water covers about **71 %** of the Earth's surface, but nearly all of it is salty: fresh water is only about 3 %
  of the total.
- Water is one of the few substances that **expand when they freeze**: that is why ice floats and lakes freeze
  from the top, sheltering the fish below.
- Burning hydrogen gives only water: it is one of the cleanest fuels there is.

See also: [Chemistry](115), [Bucket](111), [Water](52).

A [fridge](234) chills it to `cold_water`, and a freezer makes it `ice`.
