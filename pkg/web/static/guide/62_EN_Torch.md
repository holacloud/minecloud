# Torch

![Torch](item:torch)

A stick with fire on top. It is the game's **light**: it brightens caves and nights, **drives shadows away** and
**calms bees**.

| In-game name | Id | Break |
|---|---|---|
| Torch | `torch` | 0.1 s |

## How to get it

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| **4** `torch` | 1 `wood` + 1 `coal_ore` |
| **4** `torch` | 1 `glow_dust` + 1 `wood` |

Coal comes from veins in the stone ([Ores](53)); glow dust from ![Fireflies](mob:firefly) [fireflies](36) and
![Wisp](mob:wisp) [wisps](41).

## How to use it

**Placed** (right click): it stands on the ground or on any block, and is not solid.

- It gives **dynamic light**: the glow spreads through the air for about **13 blocks**, fading a little with each
  block. Solid blocks stop it; glass, leaves, water and [slabs](56) let it through.
- In bright daylight you hardly notice it; it really shines **at night and in caves**. In Ultra mode it also
  glows.

**In your hand:** just having it selected **lights up your surroundings** as you walk, like a lantern.

## Against shadows

The ![Shadow](mob:shadow) [wandering shadow](37) hates light:

- It **never appears** within 4 blocks of a placed torch.
- It **dissolves into smoke** if it comes within **5 blocks** of you while you hold a torch, or within **4 blocks**
  of a placed torch.

## With bees

If you take honey from a hive with a torch **in your hand**, the smoke **calms the bees** (even if they were
already angry) and they do not chase you. Without a torch, the colony attacks. See [Bee](35).

## Tips

- Ring your house with torches: shadows cannot be born close by.
- Always carry a handful at night and select them when you see glowing eyes.
- A torch inside a glass box makes a lovely lamp: the light shines through the glass.
- With glow dust you save your coal.

## Trivia

- Before lamps, people lit caves with torches and fat lamps: that is how the cave paintings of Altamira and Lascaux
  were made.
- The Olympic torch is lit at Olympia (Greece) with sunlight and a concave mirror.
- Real beekeepers use **smoke** to calm bees: it masks the alarm scent they use to warn each other.
