# Portal network

![Red portal](item:portal_red)

The world of MineCloud is enormous, and everybody plays in the same one. To get far away without walking for days there is a **network of plazas joined by portals**. Nobody owns it, and nobody can break it.

## The plazas

A **plaza** is a square of **31 × 31 blocks**. In the middle is the **core**, 17 × 17 of light stone, edged with a line of the plaza's **colour**; round it lies the **margin**, 7 blocks of darker paving on each side: room to build shops. Its floor is perfectly flat, at the height of the **highest ground** under it, so a plaza on a hill or a peak sits on top instead of being dug into it. Where the ground is lower it stands on a foundation of cobblestone; over the sea it is a deck on the water. Above it there is nothing at first: the sky is open.

- The **central plaza** is at the very middle of the world, at 0, 0. **New players appear there.** Nobody builds on it.
- The other plazas are **network plazas**: you can build on top of them, core and margin, as anywhere else, but no block of the plaza itself can be broken or changed.

## What a plaza has

- **The compass rose**, at the centre, in the floor: you arrive standing on it. It shows **N** (north), **E** (east), **S** (south) and **O** (west, *oeste* in Spanish), each on its own side, and its red arm points north. **Right click** it and the plaza's legend opens (see below).
- **Four posts**, one at each **corner of the core**, just inside its coloured line; the middle of the plaza is left free. Read from top to bottom they spell the plaza's **address** (see below): the first letter at the top. The central plaza has no address, so its posts carry a **star**.
- **The colour** of the plaza, on the line round the core and on the top of each post: **white** in the central plaza, then the colour of the jump that brings you there: ![](item:portal_trim_red) red, ![](item:portal_trim_orange) orange, ![](item:portal_trim_yellow) yellow, ![](item:portal_trim_green) green. It is also the colour of the portal back.
- **The legend**: the ![](item:portal_legend) block with an **i** at the foot of each post. **Right click** it (tap it on a tablet) and a panel explains where you are and how the plazas work. A letter or the star of a post does the same, and so does the rose you arrive on. To build against one, crouch.

## The portals

A portal is a frame of plaza stone, 5 blocks wide and 4 tall, standing in the middle of each side of the core, with a glowing **pane** of colour inside, 3 × 3. On top of the frame there is a **letter**, ![](item:portal_letter_n) N, E, S or O: the way the portal takes you. Right click it and the chat tells you where it goes, how far, and which plaza you will reach.

**Walk into the pane** and you cross it. The screen fills with rings of the portal's colour, **blocks rush past you** out of the middle of the screen as stars do in a jump to hyperspace, and you read the name of the plaza you are going to, until that place has loaded and is drawn: at least half a second, and never more than 30 seconds. Then you are at the centre of the other plaza, on its rose, looking the way you were going. If you were riding or sitting, you get off first.

A portal never changes direction: the north portal takes you north, the east one east. What changes is **how far**, and the colour says it, like a rainbow:

| Colour | Jump | New plazas |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:portal_red) Red | 1,000 blocks | 4 |
| ![](item:portal_orange) Orange | 10,000 blocks | 12 |
| ![](item:portal_yellow) Yellow | 100,000 blocks | 28 |
| ![](item:portal_green) Green | 1,000,000 blocks | 60 |

Each colour jumps **ten times further** than the one before: 1,000 × 10 = 10,000, × 10 = 100,000, × 10 = 1,000,000. That is a power of ten, and it is why a few jumps take you so far.

## Going back

Every plaza but the central one also has a **portal back**. It stands on the side across from the way you were going, so when you arrive it is **behind you**, and it has the colour of the portal you came through: it jumps as far, but backwards, and leaves you in the plaza you left.

Example: in the central plaza you walk into the red portal on the north side and arrive at plaza N. There you find three orange portals, which go on, and a red one behind you, which goes back to the centre.

To get back to the central plaza from anywhere in the network, cross the portal back of each plaza: four times at most.

## Addresses

Each plaza has an **address**: the letters of the portals you crossed to get there from the centre, **N** (north), **S** (south), **E** (east) and **O** (west, *oeste* in Spanish). The central plaza has no letters.

- The first letter moves you 1,000 blocks, the second 10,000, the third 100,000, the fourth 1,000,000.
- The portals that go on **never undo the way**: once you went north none jumps south, and once east none west. So the central plaza has **4 portals**, a plaza reached along one line (N, NN) has **3** to go on, and the others (NE) have **2**. The only one that goes backwards is the portal back.
- Example: **NE** is 1,000 north and then 10,000 east. **EN** is 1,000 east and then 10,000 north: another place altogether. Two different addresses never land in the same spot.

There are **105 plazas** (1 + 4 + 12 + 28 + 60) and **208 portals**: 104 out, one to each plaza but the central one, and 104 back. The plazas of four letters are the ends of the network: they only have the portal back. The farthest, **OOOO**, is 1,111,000 blocks west of the centre.

When you arrive, the chat tells you the address of the plaza and how far it is from the centre, for example "Plaza NE: 10,000 east, 1,000 north", or "Central plaza" when you are back in it.

## Who builds it

The network is official: players can't make portals nor take them away. The world's administrator builds it once, and building it again changes nothing nor removes what anybody built on a plaza.
