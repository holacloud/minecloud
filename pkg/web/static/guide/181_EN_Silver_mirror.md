# Silver mirror

![Silver mirror](item:mirror)

A glass block with a very thin layer of **silver** behind it, in a dark frame.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:mirror) | Silver Mirror | `mirror` |

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *Silver alone, spread on a glass, looks back at you.*

## What it is for

- Place it like any block. Breaking: **0.25 s**.
- It is a real [mirror](232): up close, its six sides reflect the world and you.
- With 1 plank it makes 2 wall mirrors, to hang in a bathroom or a hall.

## Trivia

- In 1835 the chemist Justus von Liebig found how to deposit silver on glass: modern mirrors were born.
- Silver reflects more visible light than any other metal.
- Many mirrors today use aluminium, which is cheaper.

## Solution

If you would rather discover it yourself, stop reading here.
