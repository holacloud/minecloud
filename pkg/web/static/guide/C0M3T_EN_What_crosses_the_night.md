# What crosses the night

> You found a hidden page. It was written in light, in a comet's tail or in the shadow of a red moon. Few have
> read it: you have to be awake, looking up, on the right night.

![Telescope](item:telescope)

The MineCloud sky is a **clock**. Everything that happens in it is decided by the world clock, the same for
everyone. That is why it can be **predicted**. This is what those who looked long enough know.

## How the nights are counted

Every night has a number. Night **0** is the first night of the world, and the number goes up by one every day, at
midday: that way a whole night, from dusk to dawn, keeps the same number.

## The comet

- It comes back **every 13 nights**. It first crossed on **night 6**; then on 19, 32, 45...
- Its head and tail show **in a different place each time**, but in the same place for every player.
- That night **shooting stars** fall almost **three times as often** as usual: from one every half minute to one
  every fifteen seconds.
- You must keep it in the telescope for **two seconds** in a row to read what it carries.

## The red moon

- It comes **every 24 nights**. The first was **night 16**; then 40, 64...
- It always falls on a **full moon**: 24 is a multiple of 8, and night 16 starts the moon's cycle.
- It begins **right after** the darkest point of the night and lasts about **38 seconds**. In the middle, for
  about fifteen seconds, the moon is fully red: that is the moment to look at it through the telescope.
- Since both dates repeat, **the comet and the red moon meet** every 312 nights. Nobody has waited that long.

## The shooting stars

They are not random. The world clock is cut into **5-second** slices, and in each slice the slice's own number
decides whether a star falls, where and which way. That is why everyone sees **the same one**. If someone says
"I saw one" and you did not, you were looking the other way.

## The planets are in different hurries

**Ferro** runs: it goes round the sky in 9 days. **Aurum** is slow: 31 days. About every 13 days Ferro catches up
with Aurum and the two pass very close. The old astronomers called it "the embrace of iron and gold".

## The figures that never sleep

The Owl and The Hive **never set**: they circle The Nail all night long. Legends say the owl in the sky and the
owl in the trees keep watch on each other, and that is why neither of them sleeps at night.

> Some say the comet does not come from far away but from far below: a spark from a storm golem's heart that rose
> too high and could not find its way back.
