# Fluorite

![Fluorite](item:fluorite)

A purple crystal of **calcium fluoride**.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:fluorite) | Fluorite | `fluorite` |

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *One calcium with two fluorines makes purple crystals.*

## What it is for

It is a **collectible**: the G key tells you what it is.

## Trivia

- The word **fluorescence** comes from fluorite: many glow under ultraviolet light.
- England has a famous purple-and-yellow banded fluorite, the *Blue John*.

## Solution

If you would rather discover it yourself, stop reading here.

| Reaction | Kind | Product |
|---|---|---|
| `Ca + 2 F → CaF₂ (fluorite)` | compound | 1 `fluorite` |
