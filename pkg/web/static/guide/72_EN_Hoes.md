# Hoes

![Iron hoe](item:iron_hoe)

The gardener's tool. It quickly clears **leaves** and the **grass block**.

| | Hoe | Id | Speed on its material |
|---|---|---|---|
| ![](item:wood_hoe) | Wood Hoe | `wood_hoe` | ×1.45 |
| ![](item:stone_hoe) | Stone Hoe | `stone_hoe` | ×1.9 |
| ![](item:iron_hoe) | Iron Hoe | `iron_hoe` | ×2.6 |
| ![](item:gold_hoe) | Gold Hoe | `gold_hoe` | **×3.2** |

## How to get them

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| `wood_hoe` | 3 `planks` + 1 `wood` |
| `stone_hoe` | 3 `cobblestone` + 1 `wood` |
| `iron_hoe` | 3 `iron_ore` + 1 `wood` |
| `gold_hoe` | 3 `gold_ore` + 1 `wood` |

## How to use them

Select it and break with a left click. **It never wears out.** It speeds up the leaves of all nine trees and the
grass block (`grass`).

| Block | Bare hand | Wood | Stone | Iron | Gold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Leaves | 0.2 s | 0.14 s | 0.11 s | 0.08 s | 0.08 s |
| Grass | 0.35 s | 0.24 s | 0.18 s | 0.13 s | 0.11 s |

0.08 s is the fastest any block can break: with iron or gold, leaves are already at the limit.

**On other blocks** it helps less: ×1 for wood, ×1.05 for stone, ×1.43 for iron and ×1.76 for gold.

In this version the hoe **does not till the soil**: there are no crops.

## Tips

- Clearing tree crowns with a hoe is lightning fast, and every broken leaf may drop a [sapling](55) (8 %) or an
  [egg](74).
- An iron hoe is enough for leaves: a gold one does not break them any faster.
- [Iron shears](73) do the same with leaves and cut wool too.

## Trivia

- The hoe is one of the oldest farming tools: it already appears in ancient Egyptian paintings.
- A hoe pulls up weeds and loosens the soil so roots can breathe.
