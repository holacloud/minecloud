## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize SI Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_si` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

Or as it is really done, in the [electric arc furnace](236), which needs 150 W:

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| **3** `element_si` | 1 `sand` + 2 `coal_ore`, in 20 s |

Sand is silicon and oxygen, and at about 2000 °C carbon carries the oxygen off: SiO₂ + 2 C → Si + 2 CO. The C recipe stays, and is instant.

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

Reactions the lab knows from the start:

- [Glass](51): `Si + 2 O → SiO₂ (glass)`

It takes part in **2** reactions you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *Two silicons, with boron on one side and phosphorus on the other, turn light into electricity.*
- *Glass (Si + 2 O) turns deep blue with a touch of cobalt.*

## In the real world

- It is the second most abundant element in the Earth's crust, after oxygen.
- Computer **chips** and solar panels are made of very pure silicon.
- Silicon Valley, in California, is named after it.
