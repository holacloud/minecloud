# Glow dust

![Glow dust](item:glow_dust)

A handful of yellow sparks that shine on their own. It replaces coal for making **torches**.

## How to get it

| Source | How | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| ![Fireflies](mob:firefly) [Fireflies](36) | Strike the cloud (at night): **30 %**, with a **20 s** wait per cloud | 1 |
| ![Wisp](mob:wisp) [Wisp](41) | Catch up with it and touch or strike it: it bursts into sparks | 1 (35 %: **2**) |
| ![Wisp](mob:wisp) Wisp | Catch it with the [bug net](95) | 1, straight into your inventory |
| ![Cave monster](mob:cave_monster) [Cave monster](21) | Defeat it (4 blows): **50 %** | 1 |
| ![Storm golem](mob:storm_golem) [Storm golem](43) | Defeat it (5 blows) | **2–3** (and 1–2 `iron_ore`) |

## What it is for

| Recipe | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| **4** `torch` | **1** `glow_dust` + 1 `wood` |

It is the same amount as the usual recipe (1 `wood` + 1 `coal_ore` → 4 `torch`), but **without coal**. See
[Torch](62).

## Tips

- **Torches without mining:** one night swatting fireflies gives you dust for plenty of torches.
- Torches protect you from [wandering shadows](37): one in your hand or placed nearby dissolves them.
- The wisp is fast, but it **tires** after a long flight: sprint after it. Careful, it leads you towards deep water
  or a cliff.

## Trivia

- Real fireflies glow thanks to a chemical reaction (**luciferin** and **luciferase**). It is "cold light": it gives
  off almost no heat.
- The glow dust of fireflies and that of the wisp are the same item, even though one is an insect and the other a
  mysterious flame.
