# Mongolarachne

![Mongolarachne](mob:mongolarachne)

**The biggest fossil spider ever found**: a long abdomen marked with gold and eight very long, thin legs, the
front ones the longest. It is an ancient relative of the [spider](20). It minds its own business, but **if
you hit it, it lifts its front legs and bites**. It is an [extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself,
it has to be brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Look for its fossil.** It lies far down, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:fossil_rock_rare)
   `fossil_rock_rare`: a dark rock with a three-toed footprint. The page on [fossils](262) says how deep to dig.
2. **Break the block** with a [pickaxe](68). Which animal is inside depends on the place: if it was a
   Mongolarachne, you take ![](item:fossil_mongolarachne) `fossil_mongolarachne` with you.
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45 seconds**. It is
   a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived Mongolarachne: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back.

## Behaviour

- **It strolls slowly** near its place, on the tips of its legs, and looks about.
- It is **neutral**: it attacks nobody who has not attacked it.
- **If you hit it, it warns you.** It hisses, turns on you and **lifts its front legs** for 0.6 seconds.
- Then **it bites**, if you are still within 1.7 blocks of it: **1 damage**, with no poison. After that it
  stands still for a little over a second.
- It only bites whoever hit it, and only that player sees the warning.

There is one colour only: dark brown with golden spots, and legs banded in orange and black.

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit it | **8 health**: 2 hits bare-handed or with a [sword](71); 1 with a golden sword. Every hit that does not defeat it makes it warn, unless it was warning already. |
| Defeat it | It leaves **1 or 2** ![white wool](item:white_wool) `white_wool`: its silk. |
| Tame it | You can't. |

## Tips

- **Step back as soon as it lifts its legs.** From more than 1.7 blocks away the bite does not reach you.
- With a [shield](252) in your left hand, and facing it, the bite does half the damage.
- Leave it alone and it is the calmest neighbour: it never starts anything.
- Put it next to the spider of today. That one is a monster, bigger than any real spider; the Mongolarachne
  is a real animal, even if the game shows it about 4 times as big as it was.
- In the [Bestiary](5), its sheet and the spider's show the two together under the title **Evolution**.

## The real Mongolarachne

- **When and where.** It lived about **165 million years ago**, in the Jurassic, when the dinosaurs ruled. Its
  fossils come from the north of **China**, from a place that was then a lake in a forest, with volcanoes
  near it. The ash of the volcanoes covered the animals that sank to the bottom, and kept them whole.
- **Its name** means "spider of Mongolia": the region where it was found is called Inner Mongolia.
- **Size.** Its body was about **2.5 centimetres** long and its front legs more than **5 centimetres** each:
  about **15 centimetres** from the tip of one leg to the tip of the opposite one. It would cover your hand.
  No bigger fossil spider is known.
- **A case of mistaken identity.** The first one found was a female, and it looked so much like the golden
  orb-weavers of today that scientists put it in their family. Two years later a male turned up, and it did
  not fit: the Mongolarachne got a family of its own, one that no longer exists.
- **Hairy legs.** On its legs it had tufts of hairs, like leg warmers. The females of golden orb-weavers have
  them too.
- **What it ate.** Probably insects, like nearly every spider. Its living relatives catch them in webs, but no
  web of a Mongolarachne has been found: silk almost never turns to stone.
- **Its living look-alikes.** Golden orb-weavers live in warm countries. Their webs can be more than 1 metre
  across, and their silk shines like gold in the sun.
- **Why it died out.** Nobody knows. From the whole of its family only a few fossils are left.
- **Nobody knows its colour.** The gold and the orange of the game are borrowed from the golden orb-weavers.
