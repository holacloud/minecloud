# Cooked fish

![Cooked fish](item:cooked_fish)

A golden, crispy fish roasted over the coals. It is one of the **most filling** foods in the game.

## How to get it

It is crafted (C key):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `cooked_fish` | 1 [Raw fish](80) `raw_fish` + 1 `coal_ore` |

Coal comes from [ores](53); the fish from a [fish school](26) or the [fishing rod](94).

## Eating (G key)

| Health | Effect |
|---|---|
| **+6** | — |

At full health you do not eat it.

## Tips

- **Six times better** than raw for a single `coal_ore`: always worth it.
- Carry a good stack before exploring caves or the desert. With 20 maximum health, three fish leave you almost as
  good as new.
- It is not a bait and does not tame anything: keep raw fish for that.

## Trivia

- In the game you need no furnace or fire: the coal in the crafting panel (C key) is enough.
- Roasting fish over embers is one of the oldest ways of cooking known.
