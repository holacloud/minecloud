# Salt

![Salt](item:salt)

Table salt: **sodium chloride**. It cures fish, which then feeds you more.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), a reaction known from the start:

| Reaction | Kind | Product |
|---|---|---|
| `Na + Cl → NaCl (salt)` | compound | 4 `salt` |

## What it is for

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 [`salted_fish`](127) | 1 `raw_fish` + 1 `salt` |

## Trivia

- **Sodium** is a metal so soft you can cut it with a knife, and it bursts into flame in water. **Chlorine** is a greenish, poisonous gas. Together they make something you eat every day.
- Sea water holds about **3.5 %** dissolved salts, mostly sodium chloride.
- The word "salary" comes from the Latin *salarium*, linked to salt, which was very valuable in ancient Rome.
