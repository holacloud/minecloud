# Argentavis

![Argentavis](mob:argentavis)

**One of the biggest birds that ever flew**: black, with a bare red head in a white ruff and **wings of 5
blocks** from tip to tip. It is an ancient relative of the [vulture](40), and it looks like a giant condor. It
circles slowly, high over its home, and harms nobody. It is an [extinct animal](261): it never appears by
itself, it has to be brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Look for its fossil.** It lies far down, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:fossil_rock_rare)
   `fossil_rock_rare`: a dark rock with a three-toed footprint. The page on [fossils](262) says how deep to dig.
2. **Break the block** with a [pickaxe](68). Which animal is inside depends on the place: if it was an
   Argentavis, you take ![](item:fossil_argentavis) `fossil_argentavis` with you.
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45 seconds**. It is
   a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived Argentavis: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back. It
appears on the ground next to the machine, and a few seconds later it takes off.

## Behaviour

- **It circles over its home**, about **10 blocks** over the ground, in wide slow turns: with wings like
  those it cannot turn sharply. It leans into its turns.
- **It hardly beats its wings**: a few beats every 10 to 20 seconds, and otherwise it glides with them held
  out and the feathers at their tips spread like fingers.
- **It never goes more than 14 blocks from its home**, the place where it came back to life.
- **It comes down to perch.** After 60 to 120 seconds in the air it glides down to the highest place it sees
  near its home (a treetop, a roof, or the ground), folds its wings and sits there for 15 to 30 seconds,
  looking about. Then it rises **2 blocks** straight up, beating hard, spreads its wings and goes back to its
  circles.
- **It never flies into anything.** It flies over what is low and round what is high.
- **With a roof over it, it does not fly.** On the ground it flutters out from under the roof, if there is
  open sky within 4 blocks; if not, it stays where it is. A roof put over it while it flies brings it down,
  slowly, after 4 seconds.
- It is **peaceful**: it dives at nobody, and in the game it does not come to carrion as the vulture does.
- Each player sees it fly its own way round the same home.

There is one colour only: black, with a white band along the back of each wing, a white ruff and a bare red head.

## Tips

- **To see it up close, wait for it to perch.** It comes down every couple of minutes. It likes high places:
  put a wide roof or a platform near its home, lower than it flies, and it will often sit there.
- **Revive it out of doors**, with room around it. Indoors it cannot take off, and it stays on the floor.
- Stand under it when it glides over you: 5 blocks from one wingtip to the other.
- Put it next to a vulture of today, which has wings half as long.
- In the [Bestiary](5), its sheet and the vulture's show the two together under the title **Evolution**.

## The real Argentavis

- **When and where.** It lived between about **9 and 7 million years ago** in what is now **Argentina**, over
  dry plains at the foot of the Andes.
- **Its name** means "magnificent bird of Argentina": *Argentavis magnificens*. It was given it in 1980.
- **Size.** It weighed about **70 kilos**, as much as a grown person: it is the heaviest flying bird known.
  Its wings measured between **5 and 7 metres** from tip to tip, depending on who works it out: only a few of
  its bones are known. Standing, it was as tall as a person.
- **How it flew.** It was too heavy to beat its wings for long. It **soared**: it let itself be lifted by
  the warm air that rises over the plains and by the wind that climbs the slopes, in circles, as condors do
  today, and so it could travel very far without tiring. That is why the one of the game hardly beats its wings.
- **Taking off was the hard part.** Scientists think it needed to run downhill, or into the wind, to get
  into the air. In the game it rises straight up: there is no room for a runway.
- **What it ate.** Probably dead animals that it found from the air, and perhaps small prey that it
  swallowed whole.
- **Its relatives.** It belonged to a family of giant birds of America that has died out, the teratorns,
  which looked like condors and vultures.
- **Not the widest.** A sea bird of long ago, *Pelagornis*, had wings as long or longer, but it weighed
  much less.
- **Why it died out.** Nobody knows. Perhaps the climate changed, and with it the winds and the animals of
  the plains.
- **Nobody knows its colour.** The black and white of the game are those of the Andean condor.
