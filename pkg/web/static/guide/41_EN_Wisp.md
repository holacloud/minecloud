# Wisp

![Wisp](mob:wisp)

A **little blue-green flame** that floats in the night, pulsing and chiming as if it were calling you. It never
attacks: it only **deceives**. Follow it and it will lead you to deep water or to the edge of a cliff.

## Where it lives

- **Anywhere, at night**, but not every night. Every 30 seconds there is a **20 %** chance that one appears
  **25–40 blocks** away from you: about **one every other night**.
- It prefers dangerous places: the **water's edge** or next to a **ravine**, where the ground drops 4 blocks or
  more.
- There is at most **1 wisp** at a time.
- **At dawn it goes out** and vanishes, and also if you get more than 72 blocks away.

## Behaviour

- **From afar** it floats and bobs, with slow pulses of light and a **two-note chime** to catch your attention.
  If you are very far, it drifts closer so you can see it.
- **When you come** within about 9 blocks, it drifts away and stays **6–8 blocks** ahead of you, leading you
  towards danger: it hovers over deep water or over the drop of the ravine.
- Now and then it **flickers out and reappears** further ahead.
- If you fall for it (you end up in the water or at the bottom of the ravine), **it giggles** ("Somewhere, a wisp
  giggles") and slips away to call you from somewhere else.
- If you are within 6 blocks, **it flees fast**… but **it tires** after a few seconds of fleeing, and then slows
  down.
- It leaves a trail of **little sparks** that rise and fade. In **Ultra** mode it glows with a halo.

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Touch it (within 1.2 blocks) | **You catch it:** it bursts into sparks and leaves 1–2 ![glow dust](item:glow_dust) `glow_dust` (35 % chance of 2). |
| Hit it | It bursts just as if you caught it, with the same dust. |
| Net (`bug_net`) | Gives you 1 `glow_dust` straight away. |
| Tame, bait or cage | No. |
| Egg | `wisp_egg` |
| Command | `/mob wisp` (summoned ones last a minute in daylight, so you can see them). |

## Tips

- **Sprint at it:** it flees faster than you walk, but it tires quickly. When it slows down, catch it.
- **Don't follow it blindly:** watch your step, especially near rivers and ravines at night.
- Its `glow_dust` with `wood` makes 4 `torch`.
- Those who catch a wisp with their own hands (touching or hitting it) sometimes hear it **whisper a code** as it
  fades. Read the chat carefully.

## Trivia

- In real life, **will-o'-the-wisps** are faint lights sometimes seen at night over marshes and graveyards. They
  are thought to be gases such as **methane** catching fire on their own as they seep out of the ground.
- In legends all over Europe they were spirits that **led travellers astray** into the bogs, which is exactly
  what the one in the game does.
- The name means "Will with the wisp": a *wisp* was a bundle of burning straw used as a torch.
- Its notes come from a very high **pentatonic scale**, which is why it sounds like a music box.
