# Nuralagus

![Nuralagus](mob:nuralagus)

The **giant rabbit of Menorca**: it weighed six times as much as a wild rabbit and **could not hop**. It has a
stiff back, short legs, and small ears and eyes. It is an ancient relative of the [rabbit](44). It is an
[extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself, it has to be brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Look for its fossil.** It lies far down, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:fossil_rock_rare)
   `fossil_rock_rare`: a dark rock with a three-toed footprint. The page on [fossils](262) says how deep to dig.
2. **Break the block** with a [pickaxe](68). Which animal is inside depends on the place: if it was a
   Nuralagus, you take ![](item:fossil_nuralagus) `fossil_nuralagus` with you.
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45 seconds**. It is
   a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived Nuralagus: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back.

## Behaviour

- **It walks**, slowly, near its place. **It never hops, not once.**
- It does nothing a rabbit of today does: no burrow, no zig-zag runs, and it hides from nobody.
- It is a grazer: when it stands still, **it lowers its head to eat**.
- It looks around and flicks its ears.
- It is **peaceful**: it attacks nobody and runs from nobody.

There are **2 coats**: brown and grey, both with a pale belly and a white tail.

## Tips

- There is no need to sneak up on it, as on a rabbit: nothing startles it.
- `tall_grass` is everywhere: take a good handful and offer it one at a time until it stays with you.
- Watch it walk next to a rabbit: one goes in hops and the other step by step.
- In the [Bestiary](5), its sheet and the rabbit's show the two together under the title **Evolution**.

## The real Nuralagus

- **When and where.** It lived only on the island of **Menorca**, between about **5 and 3 million years ago**.
  Its ancestors walked there when the Mediterranean Sea dried up almost completely, more than 5 million years
  ago. Then the sea came back, and they were cut off.
- **Its name**, *Nuralagus rex*, means "the king of the hares of Menorca": Nura is a very old name of the
  island. It was given in **2011**.
- **Size.** It stood half a metre tall and weighed between **8 and 12 kilos**: about **six times** as much as
  a wild rabbit. It is one of the biggest rabbits there have ever been.
- **It could not hop.** A rabbit has a long, supple back: it bends and stretches like a spring at every hop.
  The back of the Nuralagus was **short and stiff**, and its hind legs were short. It walked, and it is
  thought to have dug for roots to eat.
- **Small eyes and ears.** Its eye sockets and the bones of its ear were small for an animal of its size: it
  saw and heard worse than a rabbit. Ears are not kept in fossils, but they are thought to have been short.
- **Why it was like that.** Nothing hunted it on its island. With nobody to run from there is no need to
  sprint or to keep watch, and the body of a runner and sharp senses use up a lot of energy. It did not grow
  fast, either: it grew big by growing for more years.
- **The rule of islands.** On islands, small animals tend to become big and big ones small. The Nuralagus is
  the rabbit that grew; the [Myotragus](275), on the island next door, is the goat that shrank.
- **Why it died out.** Nobody knows for sure. It vanished at about the time when the sea fell, Menorca and
  Mallorca were joined, and the animals of Mallorca, the Myotragus among them, crossed to its island. There
  may not have been room or food for all.
- **Is it the rabbit's grandfather?** No: it belongs to the same family, that of rabbits and hares, but to a
  branch that stayed on its island and left no descendants.
- **Nobody knows its colour.** Fossils keep bones and teeth, not hair: the coats of the game are those of a
  rabbit of today.
