# Salted fish

![Salted fish](item:salted_fish)

Fish cured with [salt](119): it needs no fire and feeds you more than cooked fish.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:salted_fish) | Salted Fish | `salted_fish` |

## How to get it

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `salted_fish` (*Cure Fish with Salt*) | 1 `raw_fish` + 1 `salt` |

## What it is for

| Food (G key) | Health |
|---|---|
| `raw_fish` | 1 |
| `cooked_fish` | 6 |
| `salted_fish` | **8** |

At full health you do not eat it.

## Trivia

- Salt preserves food because it **draws the water out**: without water, bacteria cannot grow.
- Salt **cod** keeps for months without a fridge: sailors crossed oceans with it.
