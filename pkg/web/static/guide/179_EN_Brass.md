# Brass

![Brass](item:brass_ingot)

Copper with zinc: a **golden** metal that is easy to shape.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:brass_ingot) | Brass Ingot | `brass_ingot` |

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *Two parts copper and one part zinc give the golden metal of trumpets.*

## What it is for

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `telescope` (*Craft Telescope (Brass)*) | 2 `glass` + 1 `brass_ingot` + 2 `planks` |

The brass telescope is the same as the usual one: see [Telescope](133).

## Trivia

- Trumpets, trombones and tubas are **brass** instruments... made of brass.
- Brass doorknobs help against germs: copper slowly kills them.
- The game's proportions (2 copper to 1 zinc) are those of real brass.

## Solution

If you would rather discover it yourself, stop reading here.

| Reaction | Kind | Product |
|---|---|---|
| `2 Cu + Zn → brass` | alloy | 2 `brass_ingot` |
