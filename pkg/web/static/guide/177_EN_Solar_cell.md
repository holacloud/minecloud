# Solar cell

![Solar cell](item:solar_cell)

A plate of **doped silicon** (boron on one side, phosphorus on the other) that turns light into electricity.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:solar_cell) | Solar Cell | `solar_cell` |

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *Two silicons, with boron on one side and phosphorus on the other, turn light into electricity.*

## What it is for

For now it is a **collectible**: keep it for when machines and electricity arrive.

## Trivia

- **Doping** silicon means adding a tiny amount of another element; that creates a boundary where light pushes electrons along.
- The first practical solar cells were made in 1954, at Bell Labs.
- Satellites and the International Space Station run on solar panels.
