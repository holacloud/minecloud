# Megalodon

![Megalodon](mob:megalodon)

The **biggest shark that ever lived**, and the biggest animal of MineCloud's seas: **9 blocks long**, with
a **wide mouth full of triangular teeth**, a tall fin on its back and a tail shaped like a crescent moon. It
is a hunter: **it bites whoever swims in its water**. On the shore you are safe. It died out more than 3
million years ago: in MineCloud you only see one if you gather its skeleton and bring it back to life. It is
one of the hardest animals of the game to get.

## What it was

A **shark**, shaped like a great white but much bigger and heavier. Like every shark, it breathed with
**gills**: it never comes up for air. And like every shark, its skeleton was not made of bone but of
**cartilage**, the bendy stuff of your nose and ears.

## How to get one

1. **Its skeleton, in three pieces.** You need `fossil_megalodon_skull`, `fossil_megalodon_spine` and
   `fossil_megalodon_tail`. They come out of the three blocks of a **skeleton** (`fossil_bones_skull`,
   `fossil_bones_spine` and `fossil_bones_tail`), which lie in a straight line inside the stone, between
   y = −11 and y = −4, and only in 1 chunk out of 14. The three blocks of a skeleton always belong to one
   animal, but not every skeleton is a megalodon's. See [Fossils](262).
2. **A great deal of water.** The revival machine has to stand next to **300 blocks of water** joined
   together, or more: in practice the sea or a lake.
3. **The [revival machine](264).** With the three pieces inside it takes 120 seconds, two whole minutes. The
   megalodon appears in the water.

A real megalodon hardly ever left a skeleton: cartilage rots away, and what is found of it are its **teeth**
and, now and then, its **vertebrae**, the rings of its backbone. In the game its three pieces are the same as
for every other skeleton: think of the "skull" as its jaws full of teeth.

The server keeps a revived megalodon: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back. Think
twice about which water you put it in: after that, it is its water, and nobody will swim there in peace.

## The water it needs

With 300 blocks of water it comes back to life, but to swim at its ease it wants much more:

- It is **9 blocks long**, about **2.5 tall** with its fin and almost **4 from one fin of its chest to the
  other**.
- In a lake of about 30 × 20 blocks, or in the sea, it **cruises** in long curves. In one hardly bigger than
  itself it never stops: it **circles slowly**, as well as it can.
- It needs water **3 blocks deep** for its fin to be under the surface. In water 2 deep it still swims, with
  its fin out, as a shark in the shallows.
- The walls may be [glass](51): it does not go through it.

## Behaviour

- **It swims in long curves**, a little below mid water, at a little over 1 block a second, beating its tail
  from side to side.
- **It is hostile, in the water.** If you are in the water within **14 blocks** of it, it comes for you at
  **2.2 blocks a second**. You swim a little faster than that.
- **The bite.** When its mouth reaches you it bites: **5 damage**, at most once every 1.2 seconds. Then it
  swims on past you for a moment, and turns back. Standing still does not save you: close by, it slows down
  and turns its head in.
- **On the shore you are safe.** It never leaves the water, and it does not go for a player who is out of
  it. Standing in water up to your knees counts as being in it.
- **Smaller animals.** With nobody in the water near it, it chases the [archelons](286),
  [ammonites](267) and [trilobites](266) of its water and snaps at them; they get away with a fright.
- **It does not go further** than about 12 blocks from where it was revived, unless it is after somebody:
  then it follows up to about 26 blocks from there, and turns back.
- **If its water goes away**, it lies on the ground, waiting. It does not die: as soon as the water is back,
  it swims again.

There are **2 colours**: slate grey, a little blue, and the brown grey of a shark of warm, muddy water. Both
have a white belly.

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit it | It shoots off away from you, at more than twice its speed, for a couple of seconds. Then, if you are still in the water, it comes back. |
| Defeat it | **80 health**: 20 hits bare-handed, 14 with a stone [sword](71), 12 with an iron one, 10 with a golden one. It leaves **3 to 6** ![raw fish](item:raw_fish) `raw_fish`. |
| Tame it | You can't. |
| Bait | None interests it. |
| Cage | It does not fit. |
| Egg | `megalodon_egg`: use it next to water. |
| Command | `/mob megalodon`: it appears in the nearest water where it fits; if there is none near, the game tells you. |

## Tips

- **Get out of the water.** One step on to the shore and it forgets you.
- **Hit it from the edge**, with your feet dry: it cannot answer. It takes a long while.
- With a [shield](252) in your left hand, and facing it, its bite does half the damage.
- Watch it from a tank of [glass](51) built into its lake: you will see the whole of it go past.
- Put a [Dunkleosteus](288) in the same sea to compare two great hunters, 360 million years apart.

## The real science

- **When it lived.** From about **23 to 3.6 million years ago**, long after the dinosaurs and before the
  first people.
- **Where.** In warm seas all over the world: its teeth turn up on every continent but Antarctica.
- **Its name** means "big tooth". Its teeth were triangles with edges like a saw, and the biggest are **as
  long as a hand**, more than 15 centimetres.
- **So many teeth.** A shark loses its teeth all its life, and new ones grow in rows behind them: one shark
  could drop thousands. Teeth are hard and last; that is why there are so many fossil teeth, and hardly any
  bones.
- **How big was it?** Nobody has seen a whole one, so its size is worked out from its teeth and its
  vertebrae. Most scientists say **15 to 18 metres**, perhaps more: three times a great white. The one in the
  game is 9 blocks, so that it fits in a lake.
- **What it ate.** Big prey: **whales**, seals, sea cows and sea turtles. Fossil whale bones have been found
  with the marks of its teeth.
- **Why it died out.** Probably several things at once: the seas grew colder, many of the warm shallow places
  where its young grew up dried out, the whales it ate moved to cold waters, and new hunters appeared, the
  great white shark and the killer whale.
- **Is it the great white's ancestor?** No. They look alike and are cousins, but the megalodon belonged to
  another family of sharks, which has nobody left today.
