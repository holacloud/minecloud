|---|---|

| In-game name | Phosphorus Block |
| Id | `element_p` |

## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize P Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_p` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

It takes part in **1** reaction you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *Two silicons, with boron on one side and phosphorus on the other, turn light into electricity.*

## In the real world

- The alchemist Hennig Brand discovered it in **1669**... by boiling urine.
- White phosphorus glows in the dark: its name means "light bearer".
- It is in your bones, your teeth and your DNA.
