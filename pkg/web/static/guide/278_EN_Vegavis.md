# Vegavis

![Vegavis](mob:vegavis)

A **diving bird the size of a duck** that lived when the last dinosaurs did, in Antarctica. It is an ancient
relative of the [duck](18): slimmer, with its legs set far back and **a pointed bill** instead of a flat one.
It is an [extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself, it has to be brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Look for its fossil.** It lies far down, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:fossil_rock_rare)
   `fossil_rock_rare`: a dark rock with a three-toed footprint. The page on [fossils](262) says how deep to dig.
2. **Break the block** with a [pickaxe](68). Which animal is inside depends on the place: if it was a
   Vegavis, you take ![](item:fossil_vegavis) `fossil_vegavis` with you.
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45 seconds**. It is
   a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived Vegavis: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back.

## Behaviour

- **It waddles about** near its place, a little slower than a duck.
- When it stands still **it lowers its head to dabble** at the ground, and it wags its tail.
- Now and then it drops a feather by itself.
- It is **peaceful**: it comes up to nobody and attacks nobody. If you hit it, it gives a start.
- In the game it does not dive: it is a bird of the shore, on foot.

There are **2 colours**: brown with a rusty cheek, and grey with a white cheek.

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit it | **8 health**: 2 hits bare-handed. Each hit has a **40 %** chance of dropping 1 ![feather](item:feather) `feather` (at most one every 8 seconds). |

| Tame it | Yes, with what tames a duck: `tall_grass`, `wheat_seeds`, `raw_fish` or `flower_yellow`, with **1 in 3** per try. Then it follows you and sits when you ask. See [Taming and breeding](3). |
| Breed | Two tamed ones in love have a chick. |
| Bait | None. |
| Cage | **Yes**, it fits in the `cage_trap`, if it walks in. |

| Command | `/mob vegavis` |

## Tips

- `tall_grass` is everywhere: take a good handful and offer it one at a time until it stays with you.
- Put it next to a duck: the duck is rounder and has a flat bill for sifting mud; the Vegavis is long and low,
  with a bill for catching.
- In the [Bestiary](5), its sheet and the duck's show the two together under the title **Evolution**.

## The real Vegavis

- **When and where.** It lived between about **69 and 66 million years ago**, at the very end of the age of
  the dinosaurs, on the coast of **Antarctica**. Antarctica had no ice then: it was green, with forests, and
  the sea was not frozen.
- **Its name** means "bird of Vega": its bones were found in 1992 on Vega Island, next to Antarctica, and it
  was given its name in 2005.
- **Size.** About that of a wild duck.
- **How it lived.** Its leg bones are those of a bird that **swims under water pushing with its feet**, as
  loons and grebes do today. A skull described in 2025 has a narrow, pointed beak, good for catching fish,
  and not the flat bill of a duck.
- **Its voice.** One fossil keeps its **voice box**, the oldest known of any bird. It is like that of ducks
  and geese: Vegavis could probably honk.
- **Why it matters.** Scientists argued for years about whether it was really a cousin of ducks and geese;
  the skull of 2025 says that it was. That means that **birds like those of today already lived alongside
  Tyrannosaurus**. They are the only dinosaurs that came through the great extinction of 66 million years ago.
- **Why it died out.** Nobody knows what became of Vegavis itself. Its relatives went on, and today there are
  ducks and geese on every continent.
- **Nobody knows its colour.** Fossils keep bones, not feathers: the colours of the game are those of diving
  birds of today.
