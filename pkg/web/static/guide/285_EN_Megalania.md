# Megalania

![Megalania](mob:megalania)

The biggest lizard that ever walked on land: **4 blocks long**, with a heavy tail, legs that stick out
sideways and a **forked tongue** that it flicks out to smell the air. It is an ancient relative of the
[lizard](30), and it is a hunter: **it comes for you**. It is an [extinct animal](261): it never appears by
itself, it has to be brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Look for its fossil.** It lies far down, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:fossil_rock_rare)
   `fossil_rock_rare`: a dark rock with a three-toed footprint. The page on [fossils](262) says how deep to dig.
2. **Break the block** with a [pickaxe](68). Which animal is inside depends on the place: if it was a
   Megalania, you take ![](item:fossil_megalania) `fossil_megalania` with you.
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45 seconds**. It is
   a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived Megalania: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back. Think
twice about where you put the machine: what comes out of it is a hunter.

## Behaviour

- **It strolls slowly** near its place, swinging its tail, and stays within **8 blocks** of where it came
  back to life.
- **It flicks its tongue** out for half a second every few seconds. That is how it smells.
- **It is hostile.** It goes for the nearest player within **12 blocks**, at **1.5 blocks a second**: you walk
  three times as fast. While it hunts, its tongue goes in and out all the time.
- **The bite.** It stops when its mouth reaches you and bites: **3 damage**, at most once every 1.2 seconds.
  It has no venom in the game.
- **It lets go** when you are more than 18 blocks away from it, or more than 26 from its home. Then it goes
  back to its place.
- **One player at a time.** A revived one goes after the same player on everybody's screen: the server picks
  who. If that player dies, it leaves them alone for 10 seconds.

There are **2 colours**: earth grey, like the Komodo dragon, and dark brown with rows of cream spots and a
ringed tail, like the perentie of Australia.

## Tips

- **Walk away.** It cannot catch a player who keeps walking, and it never goes further than 26 blocks from
  its home.
- **Keep off its front end.** Its bite is in its mouth: its tail only swings.
- With a [shield](252) in your left hand, and facing it, its bite does half the damage.
- A fence stops it, as it stops any animal: build one round the machine before you start it.
- Put it next to a lizard to see the difference in size. In the [Bestiary](5), the sheet of both shows them
  together under the title **Evolution**.

## The real Megalania

- **When and where.** It lived in **Australia** during the ice ages, and died out about **50,000 years ago**.
  The first people to reach Australia may have seen it.
- **Its name** was given to it in 1859 and means "great roamer". Today scientists put it with the monitor
  lizards and call it *Varanus priscus*, but everybody still says Megalania.
- **Size.** Nobody has ever found a whole skeleton, only loose bones, so the answer depends on who works it
  out: from about **3.5 to 7 metres** long. Even the smallest of those numbers makes it the biggest lizard
  that ever lived on land. The biggest lizard alive, the Komodo dragon, reaches 3 metres.
- **What it ate.** The giant animals of the Australia of its time: kangaroos taller than a person, and
  *Diprotodon*, a relative of the wombat as big as a rhinoceros.
- **The forked tongue.** Monitor lizards and snakes smell with their tongue. It picks up tiny bits of scent
  from the air and the ground and carries them to the roof of the mouth, where they are tasted. The two tips
  tell the animal which side the smell is stronger on: that is how it follows a trail.
- **Was it venomous?** The Komodo dragon has venom in its lower jaw, and some scientists think Megalania had
  it too. If so, it was the biggest venomous animal there has ever been. We cannot be sure: venom leaves no
  fossil.
- **Why it died out.** Nobody knows for sure. The giant animals it ate vanished at about the same time. Some
  scientists blame the climate, which grew drier, and others the arrival of people.
- **Its family.** Its nearest relatives alive are the big monitor lizards: the perentie of Australia and the
  Komodo dragon.
