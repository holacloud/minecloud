## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize PB Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_pb` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

It does not react with anything in the lab yet: for now it is a building block... or maybe someone will discover something with it in a future version?

## In the real world

- It is very **dense** and soft, and it stops X-rays: X-ray aprons contain lead.
- The Romans made lead pipes (*plumbum*); hence the English word *plumber*.
- It is toxic, and it was removed from petrol and paints.
