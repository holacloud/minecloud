# Beaver

![Beaver](mob:beaver)

A chubby brown rodent with **big orange incisors**, webbed hind feet and a **flat, scaly tail** shaped like a
paddle. It waddles slowly on land but **swims beautifully**, with only its head and back above the water. It is
the only creature that **changes the world**: it fells trees and builds dams and lodges.

## Where it lives

- On the **banks of rivers and lakes** in **forests** and on the **plains** (on grass, dirt or sand next to the
  water).
- In families of **1 to 3**. There are at most **4 beavers** at a time.
- Active by day and by night.

## Behaviour

- It spends its time between the bank and long swims, **grooming** its face with its front paws when it rests.
- **It fells trees:** every few minutes it walks to a **small tree near the water**, sits at the foot of the trunk
  and **gnaws** for about 7 seconds while wood chips fly. Then the trunk **creaks and topples** away from it, and
  the leaves left without a tree fall a few at a time.
- **It builds:** it carries up to **4 logs in its mouth** (you can see them) to a narrow stretch of shallow water
  and builds a **dam** and a **lodge**, one block at a time. See [Beaver dams](138).
- **Alarm:** if you come near while it swims (7 blocks, 3.5 when sneaking), it **slaps its tail** on the water
  with a loud crack and a burst of spray, and **dives**. All the beavers within 24 blocks dive with it. If it has
  a finished lodge, it swims underwater into it and stays inside for a while.
- On land it lets you get a little closer (4.5 blocks, 2.5 sneaking) and then **runs to the water**, slaps and
  dives. While it is gnawing or building it is so busy that you can come within 3.5 blocks (1.8 sneaking).

There are **2 coats**: dark brown and chestnut.

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit it | It hisses and grumbles, drops what it was gnawing and flees: into the water, tail slap and dive. |
| Defeat it | **3 hits** bare-handed (2 with a stone [sword](71) or better). It leaves 1–2 ![fur](item:fur) `fur` and, half the time, 1 ![wood](item:wood) `wood`. |
| Tame it | You can't: it is too busy with its trees and dams. |
| Bait | `bait_seeds`: it comes over to eat while it is wandering. |
| Cage | **Yes**: put `bait_seeds` inside, on the bank. |
| Egg | `beaver_egg` |
| Command | `/mob beaver` (beavers from an egg or the command start looking for work after a few seconds) |

## Tips

- **Sneak** along the bank to watch it gnaw without frightening it.
- To find beavers, look for **stumps of missing trees** and little rows of logs and mud across a stream.
- **Your builds are safe**: a beaver never touches a tree with planks, bricks, glass, torches or any other
  non-natural block within 3 blocks. See [Beaver dams](138) for how to protect a tree you like.
- Everything a beaver breaks or places is an ordinary block: you can mine its dam for `wood` and `dirt`.

## Trivia

- Beavers are a **keystone species** and **ecosystem engineers**: their dams turn streams into ponds and
  **wetlands** where fish, frogs, insects, birds and many plants thrive.
- Beaver ponds **store water** through droughts, slow down floods and trap mud, which leaves the water downstream
  cleaner.
- Their teeth are **orange** because the enamel contains **iron**, which makes it harder. They never stop
  growing, so beavers must keep gnawing.
- The **tail** is a rudder when swimming, a prop when sitting up to gnaw, a fat store for the winter and an
  alarm: the slap on the water warns the whole family.
- They can stay underwater for about **15 minutes**. Transparent eyelids protect their eyes, and their ears and
  nostrils close when they dive.
- The **lodge** is entered from underwater, so predators cannot get in, while the family stays dry in a chamber
  above the waterline.
- The longest known beaver dam, in **Wood Buffalo National Park** (Canada), is about **850 metres** long.
- After the capybara, the beaver is the **largest rodent** in the world.
