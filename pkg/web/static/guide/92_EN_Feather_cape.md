# Feather cape

![Feather cape](item:feather_cape)

A light cape of white feathers with a golden clasp. It breaks your falls: **fall damage drops by 60 %**.

## How to get it

It is crafted (C key):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `feather_cape` | **6** [Feather](75) `feather` + **2** `white_wool` |

| Ingredient | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| `feather` | strike ![Duck](mob:duck) ducks, ![Macaw](mob:macaw) macaws, ![Owl](mob:owl) owls, ![Vulture](mob:vulture) vultures (40 %) or ![Eagle](mob:eagle) eagles (50 %); or wait for them to moult |
| `white_wool` | shear a ![Sheep](mob:sheep) [sheep](17) (1–3), or craft it: 2 `leaves` + 1 `flower_red` → 4 wool |

## How to use it

**There is nothing to put on:** just **carry it in your inventory**. If you select it and press G, the chat reminds
you what it does.

**Effect:** the damage of every fall is multiplied by 0.4 and rounded **down**.

| Fall (approx.) | Without cape | With cape |
|---|---|---|
| 5 blocks | 2 | **0** |
| 6 blocks | 4 | **1** |
| 8 blocks | 8 | **3** |
| 10 blocks | 11 | **4** |
| 12 blocks | 15 | **6** |
| 15 blocks or more | 20 (deadly) | **8** |

With 20 maximum health, **without the cape a fall of about 15 blocks kills you; with it, it takes 8 at most**.

**It does not protect from:** creatures, poison, lightning or falling into the void.

## Tips

- Falls of up to about 5 blocks **do nothing** with the cape: you can jump off the top of a house.
- A must for mountains, cliffs, and for chasing the [wisp](41), which leads you to the edge of drops.
- It goes along with the [coat](90) or the [mail](91): each one guards against something different.
- Landing in water also softens the blow, with or without the cape.

## Trivia

- In Hawaii, chiefs wore feather cloaks (*ʻahu ʻula*) made of hundreds of thousands of tiny bird feathers.
- In the Greek myth, Icarus flew on wings of feathers and wax… and fell. With this cape it would not have gone so
  badly.
