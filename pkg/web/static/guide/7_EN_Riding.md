# Riding

![Horse](mob:horse)

The [horse](34) is the only animal you can ride. It lives on the plains in herds of 2 to 4.

## Getting on and off

- **Get on:** walk up to it (within 4 blocks) and **right click** with an empty hand or any block. It does
  not need to be tamed: wild horses let you ride them too.
- **Get off:** press **Shift**. You step off on whichever side of the horse has room.
- If there is no room for horse and rider (a low ceiling), you cannot get on.
- Foals cannot be ridden until they grow up ("too young to ride").

> Careful: if you hold its favourite food (`tall_grass`, `flower_yellow` or `honeycomb`), the right click
> tries to **tame** it instead of riding it.

## Speeds

| How you travel | Blocks per second |
|---|---|
| On foot | 4.5 |
| On foot, sprinting (double tap **W**) | 6 |
| On horseback | **9** |
| On horseback, galloping (double tap **W**) | **11.25** |
| On horseback, in water | 3.15 |

The horse walks, trots or gallops with its speed, and you hear its hooves.

## Jumping

- **Space** jumps. A horse jumps much higher than you: about **2.7 blocks**, against 1.3 on foot.
- **Auto-jump:** if you run into a 1-block step, the horse hops up by itself, without pressing anything.
- In the air, the horse tucks its legs in.
- Falls from great heights still hurt you, even on horseback.

## In the water

The horse swims, but slowly. To get out, look for a low bank: auto-jump does not work in water, so press
Space.

## What others see

Other players see you **riding your horse**, with the same coat colour, running and jumping with you.

A horse you have ridden no longer disappears when you walk away during that session. To keep it **for
good** (across reloads too), tame it: see [Taming and breeding](3).

## Tamed horses

- A **right click** still rides it.
- **Shift + right click** (crouching) makes it **sit** and stay. Another Shift + click has it follow you
  again.
- You can breed them by feeding two tamed adults.
