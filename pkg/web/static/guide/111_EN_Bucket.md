# Bucket

![Bucket](item:bucket)

An iron pail. With it you can **carry water**: scoop up a block from a lake and pour it wherever you like.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:bucket) | Bucket | `bucket` |
| ![](item:water_bucket) | Water Bucket | `water_bucket` |

## How to get it

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `bucket` | 3 `iron_ore` |

Iron ore comes from veins in the stone: see [Ores](53). The water bucket is not crafted: you fill the empty one.

## How to use it

1. **Fill it:** with the empty bucket in your hand, aim at [water](52) within reach and right click. That water
   block disappears (for every player, as if you had broken it) and the bucket becomes a `water_bucket`.
2. **Pour it:** with the water bucket in your hand, right click the face of a block. A water block appears on that
   side, in an empty space, and you get the empty bucket back.

Each bucket holds **one block** of water. Carry several to move more at once.

## Good to know

- Water **does not flow** in MineCloud: the poured block stays exactly where you put it, and the hole you leave in
  a lake stays a hole.
- Breaking water with your hand or a tool still gives you nothing: only the bucket keeps it.
- You can aim through the surface: the bucket takes the first water block in front of you, but never through solid
  blocks.
- Water is not solid, so you can pour it right where you stand.

## Tips

- **A pool at the foot of a tower:** a couple of buckets of water below break a long fall. See [Water](52).
- **A fishing hole:** pour water into a pit near your house and fish in it with the [fishing rod](94).
- **Draining a pond:** scoop the water out block by block to reach the bottom, or to build under water.

## Trivia

- Some of the oldest buckets were made of wood held together with hoops, like small barrels.
- A real bucket of water weighs about 10 kg: one litre of water weighs one kilogram.
- A cubic metre of water, the size of one block, weighs a whole tonne. Good thing the game's buckets are magic!
