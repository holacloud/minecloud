# Furniture

A house is not finished until you can sit down in it. Everything here can be crafted (the **C** key) from materials you already know, and placed like any block.

![Chest](item:chest)

## Families

- ![](item:oak_chair) [Chairs and sofas](224): chairs, stool, long bench and sofas. You can **sit** on them.
- ![](item:table) [Tables](225): table, bedside table and desk.
- ![](item:chest) [Chests and wardrobes](226): chest, barrel and wardrobe **really store items**; bookshelf and wall shelf.
- ![](item:oven) [Kitchen and bath](227): workbench, sink, toilet and bathtub, an [oven that cooks](233), and a [fridge and chest freezer](234) that run on electricity.
- ![](item:lantern) [Lights without electricity](228): candle, lantern, table lamp, floor lamp and chandelier.
- ![](item:blue_stained_glass) [Stained glass](229): 16 colours, as blocks and panes.
- ![](item:red_carpet) [Carpets and curtains](230): 16 colours each.
- ![](item:painting_sunset) [Paintings, clock and ornaments](231): pictures, a clock that tells the game's time, a flower pot and a vase.
- ![](item:wall_mirror) [Mirrors](232): a wall mirror and a mirror cube that reflect the world.

## Good to know

- Pieces that have a front **look at you** when you place them: stand where you want them to face.
- **Right click** uses a piece: sit, open, light. To build something against it, **crouch** while you click.
- Things placed on walls attach to the **wall you click**, and their back stays against it.
- Like [torches](62) and [signs](64), furniture does not fall when you take away what is under or behind it.
- Most of it can be walked around, but small things (candles, curtains, paintings) can be walked through.
