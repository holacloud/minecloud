# Lights without electricity

![Lantern](item:lantern)

None of these needs a wire or a [generator](201). They shine on their own, day and night, and light up the world around them like a [torch](62).

| | In-game name | Id | Light | Made from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ![](item:candle) | Candle | `candle` | 9 | 1 [honeycomb](79), 1 white wool (makes 4) |
| ![](item:table_lamp) | Table Lamp | `table_lamp` | 12 | 1 white wool, 1 copper block, 1 torch |
| ![](item:lantern) | Lantern | `lantern` | 13 | 2 iron ore, 1 glass, 1 torch |
| ![](item:floor_lamp) | Floor Lamp | `floor_lamp` | 14 | 2 white wool, 2 iron ore, 1 torch |
| ![](item:chandelier) | Chandelier | `chandelier` | 14 | 2 gold ore, 4 torches |

Light goes down by one with each block it travels: a candle reaches 8 blocks or so, a chandelier 13. A torch gives 14.

## How to place them

- **Lantern**: on a floor or table it stands; click the **underside** of a block and it hangs from a chain.
- **Chandelier**: it only goes **under a block**, to hang from it. Look up at the ceiling and click.
- **Floor lamp**: two blocks tall, so it needs the room.

## How a candle burns

A candle looks like a stick of wax with a string, but the string is not really what burns. The heat of the flame **melts** the wax, and the liquid climbs up the wick, the way water climbs up a paper towel. Near the flame the liquid wax turns into **vapour**, and that vapour is what burns, by mixing with the oxygen ([O](146)) in the air.

The wax is made mostly of carbon ([C](144)) and hydrogen ([H](139)). Burning turns them into carbon dioxide and water vapour, plus the heat that melts more wax. So a candle is a small machine that feeds itself, until the wax runs out.

The yellow light comes from tiny bits of hot carbon glowing in the flame, which is the same soot you can catch on a cold spoon. Blow a candle out and you can see a thread of white smoke: that is wax vapour that has not burned. Bring a match close to the smoke and the flame jumps down it to the wick!

> **Try it with an adult:** hold a cold spoon in the flame for two seconds. The black mark is carbon from the wax.
