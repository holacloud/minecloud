# Archelon

![Archelon](mob:archelon)

The biggest turtle there has ever been: **3 blocks long**, with a great head that ends in a **hooked beak**,
two front flippers like oars and a shell that was not hard plates but **leathery skin over a frame of
bone**. It is an ancient relative of the [turtle](28). It rows through the water without a hurry and, since it
was a reptile, now and then it comes up to **put its head out and breathe**. It died out millions of years
ago: in MineCloud you only see one if you find its fossil and bring it back to life.

## What it was

A **sea turtle**, from the time of the dinosaurs. It lived in the open sea and, as the sea turtles of today,
it breathed **air** and could not pull its head or its flippers into its shell.

## How to get one

1. **Its fossil.** It lies far down, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:fossil_rock_rare)
   `fossil_rock_rare`: a dark rock with a three-toed footprint. Break it with a [pickaxe](68): which animal is
   inside depends on the place, and it may be a ![](item:fossil_archelon) `fossil_archelon`. See [Fossils](262).
2. **Water.** It is a sea animal: the revival machine has to stand next to **60 blocks of water** joined
   together, or more: a lake, the sea, or a pool that you dig and fill with a [bucket](111).
3. **The [revival machine](264).** With its fossil inside it takes 45 seconds. The Archelon appears in the water.

The server keeps a revived Archelon: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back.

## The pool it needs

With 60 blocks of water it comes back to life, but to swim at its ease it wants more:

- It is **3 blocks long** and about **3.5 from flipper tip to flipper tip**. In a pool of less than about
  8 × 8 blocks it can hardly go forwards: it turns slowly on the spot and hangs still at times.
- It needs water **2 blocks deep** at the least, and better 3.
- Leave **air over the water**. With a roof right on the surface it cannot put its head out. It does not drown
  for that, but you miss the best of it.
- The walls may be [glass](51): it does not go through it, and you can watch it swim from outside.

## Behaviour

- **It swims in long curves**, a little above mid water, at three quarters of a block a second.
- **It rows with its flippers**, up and down like wings: the two big ones in front, and the two small ones
  at the back a little after them.
- **It rests**: now and then it hangs still in the water for a moment.
- **It comes up to breathe** about every 45 to 80 seconds: it rises until its shell touches the surface,
  **lifts its head out of the water** for some 3 seconds, its beak half open, and dives again. You will see
  the rings on the water and hear it puff.
- **It looks where it is going**: it slows down and turns before it reaches a wall, the bed or the shore, and
  it never leaves the water.
- **It does not go further** than about 6 blocks from where it was revived.
- **It is peaceful**: it does not bite you even if you swim beside it.
- **If its water goes away**, it lies on the ground, waiting. It does not die: as soon as the water is back,
  it swims again.

There are **2 colours**: slate blue with pale flecks, like the leatherback turtle of today, and olive brown.

## The real science

- **When and where.** It lived about **75 million years ago**, in the time of the dinosaurs, in a warm,
  shallow sea that then covered the middle of **North America** from north to south. Its bones are found
  today far from any coast, in the United States.
- **Its name** means "ruler turtle" in Greek. The first one was found in 1895.
- **Size.** The biggest skeleton is **4.6 metres** long, and about 4 metres from flipper tip to flipper tip.
  Alive it must have weighed more than 2,000 kilos, as much as a big car. The biggest turtle of today, the
  leatherback, is about 2 metres long.
- **A shell of leather.** Most turtles carry a solid box of bone covered with plates of horn. Archelon's
  shell was only a **frame of bones**, like the ribs of an umbrella, with thick leathery skin over it. It was
  much lighter that way: good for swimming, and not so good as armour.
- **The beak.** No teeth: a bill of horn with a hook at the end. Scientists think it ate animals with a
  shell, squid and jellyfish, but nobody has found its food.
- **It breathed air.** It had lungs, like every turtle, so it had to come up to the surface.
- **Eggs on the beach.** Sea turtles cannot lay their eggs in the water. Archelon very probably dragged
  itself out on to a beach to bury its eggs in the sand, as they do today.
- **A turtle that got away.** One of the skeletons lacks a hind flipper. Scientists think that a big hunter
  of that sea bit it off when the turtle was young, and that it lived on for many years.
- **Why it died out.** Nobody knows for sure. The sea it lived in grew smaller and cooler, and Archelon was
  gone some millions of years before the dinosaurs.
- **Who is left of its family.** Nobody: its branch of the turtles left no descendants. The leatherback
  looks like it, but it is a cousin, not a grandchild.
