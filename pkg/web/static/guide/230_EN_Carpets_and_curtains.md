# Carpets and curtains

![Red carpet](item:red_carpet)

Cloth for the house, in the same **16 colours** as [wool](58).

| | In-game name | Id | Made from |
|---|---|---|---|
| ![](item:red_carpet) | Red Carpet (and 15 more) | `red_carpet` | 2 wool of that colour (makes 3) |
| ![](item:red_curtain) | Red Curtain (and 15 more) | `red_curtain` | 3 wool of that colour (makes 2) |

- A **carpet** lies flat on the floor, thin enough to walk over without noticing.
- A **curtain** hangs at the back of its block, against the wall you click (click a floor and it looks at you). **Right click** draws it to one side, and again to close it. You can walk through it, like the cloth it is.
- It opens towards the **half you click** when you place it, as you see it: click the right half of the face and the cloth gathers on the right; the left half, on the left.
- For a tall window, **stack curtains** one on another, facing the same way: a right click on any of them draws the whole strip, up to **10** curtains, and all end the same way, open or closed, as the one you clicked. Colours may differ, and each one keeps its own side. A gap, another block or a curtain facing another way ends the strip. Everybody sees the whole strip change at once; if one of them was broken meanwhile, none changes.
