# Turtle

![Turtle](mob:turtle)

A patient turtle that plods along the beaches and sets off on long swims under water. If you startle it, it
hides in its shell.

## Where it lives

- On **sandy beaches** by the water, on the **plains**, in **forests** and in the **desert**. Not on the rocky
  hills.
- In groups of **1 to 2**. By day and by night.

## Behaviour

- **It lives between land and water:** it spends a while on the beach (20 to 45 seconds) and then goes for a swim
  (30 to 60 seconds). And round again.
- **On land** it walks very slowly and often stops to rest.
- **Under water** it sweeps its front flippers like wings and explores the bottom.
- **It comes up for air:** every so often it rises to the surface and ripples appear on the water.
- **It hides:** if you come within 3 blocks while it is on land, it draws its head and flippers into its shell
  and keeps still. In the water, instead, it swims away fast.

## How to interact

- **Hitting it:** it has **12 health** and falls in **3 blows** bare-handed (2 with a stone sword or better, see
  [Swords](71)); defeated, it drops 1 `shell`. Until then it hides for about 5 seconds; if it is in the water, it
  swims away. Each hit also has a **15 %**
  chance of dropping a `shell` ![Shell](item:shell) (at most one a minute per turtle).
- **Taming it:** not possible.
- **Baits:** `bait_fish` and `bait_worms` draw it in little by little (see [Baits and traps](4)).
- **Cage:** it **does** fit in the `cage_trap`.
- **Egg:** `turtle_egg`, or the command `/mob turtle`.

## Tips

- A `shell` is ground with `sand` into 4 `limestone` (see [Shell](82)).
- Shells also come up when fishing with a rod: 12 % of catches.
- Look for turtles on the sand along the shore: sand is a must for them to appear.

## Trivia

- Real sea turtles come back to lay their eggs on the **same beach where they hatched**, even after travelling
  thousands of kilometres.
- They can stay under water for hours while they sleep.
- The shell is not a house it can leave: it is joined to its ribs and spine.
- Unlike many land tortoises, sea turtles cannot fully pull in their heads. The one in the game does shrink its
  head down very small.
