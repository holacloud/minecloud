# Pickaxes

![Iron pickaxe](item:iron_pickaxe)

The miner's tool. With a pickaxe in your hand, **stone**, **ores**, **bricks** and every rocky block break much
faster.

| | Pickaxe | Id | Speed on its material |
|---|---|---|---|
| ![](item:wood_pickaxe) | Wood Pickaxe | `wood_pickaxe` | ×1.45 |
| ![](item:stone_pickaxe) | Stone Pickaxe | `stone_pickaxe` | ×1.9 |
| ![](item:iron_pickaxe) | Iron Pickaxe | `iron_pickaxe` | ×2.6 |
| ![](item:gold_pickaxe) | Gold Pickaxe | `gold_pickaxe` | **×3.2** |

## How to get them

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| `wood_pickaxe` | 3 `planks` + 1 `wood` |
| `stone_pickaxe` | 3 `cobblestone` + 1 `wood` |
| `iron_pickaxe` | 3 `iron_ore` + 1 `wood` |
| `gold_pickaxe` | 3 `gold_ore` + 1 `wood` |

## How to use them

Select it and mine with a left click, as usual. **It never wears out.**

**Where it really helps:** stone and cobblestone, [ores](53), [bricks](57), [fine stones and metals](59),
obsidian, [element blocks](60) and stone slabs. Because of the way the game sorts blocks, glass, cactus, the grass
block, beds, signs and other blocks with no material of their own also count as "stone".

| Block | Bare hand | Wood | Stone | Iron | Gold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stone | 0.9 s | 0.62 s | 0.47 s | 0.35 s | 0.28 s |
| Iron ore | 1.1 s | 0.76 s | 0.58 s | 0.42 s | 0.34 s |
| Obsidian | 3.5 s | 2.41 s | 1.84 s | 1.35 s | 1.09 s |

**On non-rocky blocks** (dirt, wood, leaves, wool…) it helps less: ×1 for wood, ×1.05 for stone, ×1.43 for iron
and ×1.76 for gold.

## Tips

- The first thing worth crafting is a **stone pickaxe**: cobblestone is plentiful and it nearly doubles your speed.
- Save your gold for a **gold pickaxe**: it is the fastest in the game.
- With a gold pickaxe, even obsidian falls in just over a second.

## Trivia

- In real life gold is **very soft**: a gold pickaxe would bend on the first blow. In the game it is the best!
- The builders of Stonehenge dug with picks made of **red deer antlers**.
- Pickaxes have barely changed shape in thousands of years: a pointed head on a long handle.
