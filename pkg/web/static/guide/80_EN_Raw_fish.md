# Raw fish

![Raw fish](item:raw_fish)

A silvery fish fresh out of the water. You can eat it raw in a pinch, but it is worth far more **cooked**, as
**bait**, or to **make friends** on four legs.

## How to get it

| How | What you get |
|---|---|
| ![Fish](mob:fish) Strike a [fish school](26) | **20 %** per strike to catch a fish: "You caught a fish!". The `raw_fish` floats on the water. |
| [Fishing rod](94) | **72 %** of catches; it goes straight into your inventory |

## Eating (G key)

| Health | Effect |
|---|---|
| **+1** | — |

## What it is for

| Recipe | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| [Cooked fish](81) `cooked_fish` (+6 health) | **1** `raw_fish` + 1 `coal_ore` |
| 2 [fish baits](93) `bait_fish` | **1** `raw_fish` |

**Taming** ([Taming and breeding](3)):

| Animal | Chance per try |
|---|---|
| ![Dog](mob:dog) [Dog](24) | 1/2 |
| ![Cat](mob:cat) [Cat](25) | 1/2 |
| ![Duck](mob:duck) [Duck](18) | 1/3 |

## Tips

- **Cook it whenever you can:** raw gives +1, cooked gives +6 for a single coal.
- One fish becomes **2 fish baits**, which draw cats, turtles, eagles and crabs, and make fish bite sooner on the
  rod.
- Dogs and cats are tamed on the first try half of the time: a few fish are enough.

## Trivia

- When you fish with a rod next to a school, sometimes one of those fish vanishes from the water: it is the one that
  took the hook.
- In Japan raw fish is a delicacy (sashimi), but in the game it is worth 6 times less than cooked.
