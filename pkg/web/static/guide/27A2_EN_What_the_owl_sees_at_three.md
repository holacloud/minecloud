# What the owl sees at three

> You found a hidden page. No index lists it. If you are reading this, you solved the owl's riddle… or the owl
> told you.

![Owl](mob:owl)

Three in the morning. The deepest hour of the night. The foxes have stolen what they could, the rabbits sleep
underground and, high in a tree, two round eyes miss nothing.

This is what the owl knows and does not tell.

## By day it pretends to sleep

- It sleeps on a **treetop**, puffed up with its eyes shut.
- But if you come within **7 blocks** without sneaking, it **opens one eye** and watches you.
- Come too close and it flies off to another tree: by day within **1.8 blocks**, at night within **3.2**.
  **Sneaking** halves that distance.
- Break the leaves it sleeps on and it leaves.

## The head that almost turns around

It turns its head **135 degrees to each side**: 270 in all. No further. If you stay **behind** it for more than
a second and a half, it shuffles round on its branch until it faces you. There is no hiding behind an owl that
is awake.

## How it hunts

1. Every **0.8 seconds** (1.6 in the rain) it looks for prey within **22 blocks**, and never more than 6 blocks
   above it.
2. Only **bats on the wing** (never a hanging one) and **rabbits in the open** (never one down its burrow) will
   do. Tamed animals do not interest it.
3. It picks the closest, **locks its gaze** for 1 to 2 seconds with a bob of the head… and swoops.
4. If the prey gets more than **26 blocks** away while it stares, it lets it go.
5. With the prey in its talons it flies back to a branch and swallows it.

## The invisible mark

When an owl swoops, it leaves a **mark** on its prey: "this one is mine". No other owl will chase a marked
prey. That is why **two owls never hunt the same rabbit**. If the hunter dies or misses, the mark is wiped and
the prey is free again… for the next one.

## If you strike it

It screeches, gulps down whatever it was carrying, wipes its mark and flies off to another tree. For
**20 seconds** it does not hunt. If you want to save a rabbit, now you know how.

## When it hoots

It hoots every 10 to 30 seconds (much less in the rain). **Awake** owls within **40 blocks** answer. While it
stares at prey it **falls silent**: if an owl suddenly stops hooting, it is about to swoop.

> The old tales say the owl does not hunt at three: at three it **counts**. It counts the sleeping rabbits, the
> flying bats and the lights that should not be there.
