# Iodine tincture

![Iodine tincture](item:iodine_tincture)

A flask of dissolved **iodine**, dark brown: an antiseptic for small wounds.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:iodine_tincture) | Iodine Tincture | `iodine_tincture` |

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *Iodine atoms always go in pairs; in a flask, they heal wounds.*

## What it is for

| Use (G key) | Effect |
|---|---|
| **+4** health | you get the `glass` back |

At full health it is not used.

## Trivia

- Iodine atoms never go alone: they pair up as I₂.
- Tincture of iodine has disinfected wounds since the 19th century.

## Solution

If you would rather discover it yourself, stop reading here.

| Reaction | Kind | Product |
|---|---|---|
| `2 I → I₂ (iodine tincture)` | compound | 1 `iodine_tincture` (uses 1 `glass`) |
