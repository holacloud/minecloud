# Bricks

![Brick](item:brick)

Bricks are the stuff real houses are made of: classic **red brick**, grey **stone bricks** and **sixteen coloured
bricks** for decorating.

## What they are for

They are **building** blocks: no special effect, but sturdy, handsome and cheap.

- Breaking them drops the same brick.
- A [pickaxe](68) breaks them much faster (they all count as stone).
- `brick` and `stone_bricks` can be cut into [slabs](56): 1 block = 2 slabs.

## Tips

- One red brick and a little sand give **4 coloured ones**: the cheapest way to fill a facade with colour.
- Mix stone bricks with stone [slabs](56) to make castle battlements.
- Combine colours to make **mosaics**, flags or giant letters.

## Trivia

- The oldest known bricks are about **9,000 years** old: they were mud dried in the sun.
- The Romans stamped their bricks with the seal of the workshop or the legion that made them.
- A modern brick weighs 2 or 3 kilos; a house can take more than 10,000 of them.
