# Giant mole

![Giant mole](mob:mole)

A huge, velvety grey burrower with giant **pink claws**, tiny eyes and a **pink star of tentacles** on its nose
that never stops wiggling. It is nearly always **underground**: what you will see is the mound moving along on
top.

## Where it lives

- On the **plains** and in **forests**, under soft ground: grass, dirt or sand.
- Always **alone**. There are at most **2 moles** at a time.
- It keeps no hours: it does the same by day and by night.

## Behaviour

- **Underground** it cannot be seen or struck. All you see is a **bulge of earth** travelling along the surface,
  rumbling and spitting clods, leaving a **trail of little molehills** behind.
- Now and then it **bursts out** of a fresh mound, waddles about blindly **sniffing the air** with its star nose,
  **scrabbles** flinging dirt behind and digs back down. It only digs into soft ground.
- **It feels vibrations:** if you **sprint** (or gallop on a horse), **jump** or **land heavily** near it, it
  notices up to 12–16 blocks away. **Sneaking** or being in water does not shake the ground.
- Then the bulge **speeds** towards where you stepped, stops, **shakes and rumbles**… and the mole **bursts out
  of the ground** right there. If you are still standing on it, **it bowls you over**: 2 damage and a hard shove.
- The first time, the chat warns you: "The ground rumbles... something felt your footsteps (sneak to go
  unnoticed)".
- The mounds and clods **never change the world**: they settle and sink away by themselves after 40–60 seconds.

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit it (above ground) | It squeaks and dives underground, away from you. Underground it cannot be hit. |
| Defeat it | **3 hits**, always while it is out. It leaves 1–2 ![fur](item:fur) `fur`. |
| Tame it | You can't: it lives underground. |
| `iron_shears` | 1 `fur` every 120 s, if you catch it above ground. |
| Bait | It is listed with `bait_worms`, but it follows its own behaviour and hardly ever comes. |
| Cage | It fits, but you will only catch it if it walks in while it is out. |
| Egg | `mole_egg`: it bursts out of the ground right away, so you can see it. |
| Command | `/mob mole` |

## Tips

- **Lure it on purpose:** sprint or jump on a meadow, then stand still to one side. When the bulge shakes, **step
  aside**: it will burst out right where you stepped, and you will have a few seconds to hit it.
- **To leave it in peace,** cross plains and forests sneaking.
- If you see **fresh molehills**, a mole is nearby.

## Trivia

Read between the lines… or rather, **where they begin**: you might find something beneath your feet.

- Measuring about 1.3 blocks long, it is a giant next to a real mole, which fits in your hand.
- 0 blocks of the world are changed as it passes: its mounds and clods are just scenery and sink away on their
  own.
- Like the real **star-nosed mole**, it has a pink star on its nose; the real one has 22 tentacles to "feel" the
  world, the game's has 10.
- Even with tiny eyes, real moles find their way by touch and by **vibrations in the ground**, just like this
  one.

And one more: the huge claws of real moles work like shovels, and they can dig several metres of tunnel in a
single hour.
