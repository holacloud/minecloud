# Sivatherium

![Sivatherium](mob:sivatherium)

A **giraffe built like a moose**: a short, thick neck, a heavy body on sturdy legs, and **four horns**, two
small ones over the eyes and two big ones behind, broad and flat. It is an ancient relative of the
[giraffe](22), and a peaceful grazer. It is an [extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself, it has to
be brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Look for its fossil.** It lies far down, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:fossil_rock_rare)
   `fossil_rock_rare`: a dark rock with a three-toed footprint. The page on [fossils](262) says how deep to dig.
2. **Break the block** with a [pickaxe](68). Which animal is inside depends on the place: if it was a
   Sivatherium, you take ![](item:fossil_sivatherium) `fossil_sivatherium` with you.
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45 seconds**. It is
   a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived Sivatherium: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back.

## Behaviour

- **It strolls slowly** near its place, with long, heavy steps.
- It is a grazer: when it stands still, **it lowers its neck to eat**.
- It turns its neck to look around and flicks its ears.
- It is **peaceful**: it comes up to nobody and attacks nobody. If you hit it, it gives a start.

It stands **2.8 blocks** tall to the tips of its horns: a [giraffe](22) is taller, but no heavier.
There is one colour only: brown with the faint blotches of its family, and pale legs with two dark bands above
the knee.

## Tips

- It is as hard to tame as a giraffe (1 in 4): take a good pile of leaves. Any of the four kinds will do
  (see [Wood and leaves](54)).
- Put it next to a giraffe to see what a difference a neck makes. In the [Bestiary](5), the sheet of both
  shows them together under the title **Evolution**.

## The real Sivatherium

- **When and where.** It lived in **India** and in **Africa**, from about **5 million** to about **1 million
  years ago**.
- **Its name** means "beast of Shiva", after a god of India. Its first bones were found in the Siwalik Hills,
  at the foot of the Himalaya.
- **Size.** It stood about **2.2 metres** at the shoulder and about **3 metres** to the top of its horns.
  Scientists think it weighed more than **1,000 kilos**: as much as a big giraffe of today, in a much
  shorter body.
- **The horns.** Like a giraffe's, they were bone covered in skin, and they are called **ossicones**. It had
  two pairs: small pointed ones over the eyes, and behind them two big flat ones with points, a little like
  the antlers of a moose. A moose sheds its antlers every year; ossicones are for life.
- **What it ate.** Leaves from bushes and trees and, it seems, grass as well. With its short neck it could
  reach the ground without spreading its legs, as a giraffe has to.
- **Why it died out.** Nobody knows for sure. The climate grew drier, forests turned into open grassland, and
  what there was to eat changed.
- **Is it the giraffe's grandfather?** Not quite. The giraffe family used to be big, with many shapes; today
  only two are left, the giraffe and the okapi. Sivatherium belonged to another branch of that family, which
  left no descendants: a great-uncle rather than a grandfather.
- **Nobody knows its colour.** The blotches and the banded legs of the game are a guess, borrowed from the
  giraffe and the okapi.
