# Leather

![Leather](item:leather)

A tanned hide, supple and tough. It is the base of clothing: the **warm coat**, the **scale mail** and the **meat
bait**.

## How to get it

**Loot:** the ![Cave monster](mob:cave_monster) [cave monster](21) leaves **1–3 leather** when you defeat it (it
takes 4 blows). Half of the time it also leaves `glow_dust`.

**Tanning fur** (C key, crafting):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `leather` | 2 `fur` + 1 `wood` |

Fur is trimmed with shears from many animals without hurting them: see [Fur](77).

## What it is for

| Recipe | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| [Warm coat](90) `warm_coat` | **3** `leather` + 2 `fur` |
| [Scale mail](91) `scale_mail` | 6 `scale` + **2** `leather` |
| 2 [meat baits](93) `bait_meat` | **1** `leather` + 1 `mushroom_brown` |

## Tips

- **Tanning is the peaceful way:** with `iron_shears` and a farm of goats or dogs you get leather without setting
  foot in a cave.
- A whole warm coat costs 3 leather + 2 fur = **8 fur and 3 wood** if everything comes from tanning.
- A **dog** that follows you attacks cave monsters, but if the dog finishes one off there is **no loot**: it only
  counts when you defeat it.

## Trivia

- In real life, "vegetable" tanning uses the **tannins in tree bark**, such as oak. That is why the game's recipe
  asks for wood.
- The game has leather loot ready for pigs, giraffes, horses, camels, goats and bats, but today those animals cannot
  be defeated: they shrug off any blow.
