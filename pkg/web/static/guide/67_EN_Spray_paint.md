# Spray paint

![Spray paint](item:spray_paint)

A can of **fluorescent paint** for graffiti on the faces of blocks. The paint glows, can be seen from afar… and
**is gone the next day**.

| In-game name | Id |
|---|---|
| Spray Paint | `spray_paint` |

## How to get it

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `spray_paint` | 1 `sand` + 1 `flower_red` + 1 `coal_ore` |

## How to use it

1. Select the can.
2. Press **F** to choose the colour. There are three, all fluorescent:

| Colour | In-game name |
|---|---|
| green | `green` (the starting one) |
| pink | `pink` |
| blue | `blue` |

3. **Right click** a block: it paints a coloured square on **the face** you aim at. You can paint each of a block's
   six faces separately.

**One can = 30 seconds of paint.** The first spray **uses up a can** and opens a 30 s window in which you paint as
much as you like. With the can in your hand, the block inspector shows the colour and the seconds left.

## How long it lasts

- Paint lasts **one game day** (about 4 real minutes): it fades when the clock comes back round to the same hour the
  next day. If someone sleeps in a [bed](63), the clock jumps ahead and that moment comes sooner.
- Painting the same face again **replaces** the old colour.
- If the **block is broken**, its paint is gone.
- Online, everyone sees your graffiti and the server remembers who made it.

## Tips

- Plan before you press: use the 30 s to paint a whole picture in one go.
- Mark your way back in a cave by painting the walls: they glow even in the dark.
- For a message that lasts, use a [sign](64) or [coloured wool](58).

## Trivia

- Spray paint was invented by Edward Seymour in 1949 to paint radiators; the idea came from his wife, Bonnie.
- Fluorescent colours seem to glow because they turn ultraviolet light, which we cannot see, into visible light.
