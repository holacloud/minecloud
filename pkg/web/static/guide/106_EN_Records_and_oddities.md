# Records and oddities

Fun facts taken straight from the game's insides. Some are useful; others are just for showing off.

## The numbers of the world

- **31 species** of creatures. See [Bestiary](5).
- **36 element blocks**, from **hydrogen** to **lead**, and all made the same way: one block of stone and one
  of coal. See [Element blocks](60).
- **20 different ways of moving** for the mobs. See [How mobs think](104).
- A **full day lasts 4 minutes**: 2 of day and 2 of night.
- The world is **256 blocks tall** and split into pieces of **16 × 16**. See [How the world is born](99).
- There are never more than **64 mobs** around you at once.

## Toughness

- The toughest is the ![Golem](mob:storm_golem) [storm golem](43): **5 blows** bare-handed. And on top of that, it heals
  when it calls the lightning down onto itself.
- Next comes the [cave monster](21), with 4 blows.
- Peaceful animals take **3 blows** bare-handed; with a stone [sword](71) or better, 2. The frailest are bees: they fall at
  the first blow. Fish, firefly clouds and wisps have no health bar.

## Crowds

- **Fish:** schools of 5 to 8 and up to **32 fish** at once. No species is as numerous.
- **Bats:** colonies of 3 to 6 and up to 12 at once.
- **Bees:** 5 to 7 per hive.

## Oddities

- **The owl turns its head 270 degrees**: 135 to each side. Stand right behind it for a while and it shuffles
  round on its branch. See [Owl](46).
- **The lizard drops its tail** when struck. The tail keeps wriggling on the ground for a few seconds, and the
  lizard grows it back **within a minute**. See [Lizard](30).
- **Thunder takes its time** depending on distance: the sound travels about 34 blocks per second. Count the
  seconds between the flash and the thunder and you will know how far away the bolt fell.
- **No sound is recorded:** they are all synthesized on the spot. The highest is the bat's (4200 Hz) and the
  deepest the shadow's (70 Hz). See [Sounds of the world](103).
- **Rabbits fear eagles**, even though in MineCloud eagles only go fishing.
- **Vultures come to anything defeated**… even a wisp you have just caught.
- **On 15 May 2026** the game got **104 changes in a single day**. See [History of MineCloud](102).
