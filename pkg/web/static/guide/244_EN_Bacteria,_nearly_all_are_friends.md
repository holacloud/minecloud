# Bacteria: nearly all are friends

![Soil bacteria](mob:soil_bacteria)

They are the smallest things your microscope can show. You can tell them apart from **400×**, with careful focus, and at **1000×** you see them close up. A bacterium has no nucleus: it is one small cell with its DNA loose inside it, and bacteria were here billions of years before anything else.

Many people think of them as germs. In fact **only a small share of them can make anyone ill**, and without the rest there would be no life on Earth as we know it.

## What they do for us

- **Rot.** With the fungi, soil bacteria break down whatever dies and give its food back to the plants. Without them the world would be buried in dead leaves. A teaspoon of good soil can hold as many of them as there are people on Earth.
- **Air for plants.** Plants need nitrogen and can't take it from the air. Some bacteria can, and they make it available. That is what [fertilizer](122) imitates.
- **Food.** **Lactobacillus** turns the sugar of milk into acid, which curdles it. That is how we get yoghurt and cheese, and it is why they keep better than milk.
- **Oxygen.** **Cyanobacteria** make food from light. Their ancestors filled the air with oxygen more than two billion years ago, and one of them moved into another cell and stayed: the [chloroplasts](242) of every plant are their descendants. See the paler, larger bead in the string? That cell takes in nitrogen.
- **Inside you.** You have about as many bacterial cells as your own, mostly in the gut, where they help with your food.
- **Medicines.** Many antibiotics were found in soil bacteria.

## Two that are not bacteria

These look similar at a glance, but they are **fungi**, with a nucleus, like you. They are much closer to you than to a bacterium.

![Yeast](mob:yeast)

**Yeast.** On ![](item:flower_red) flowers and in ![](item:honeycomb) honeycomb. Small ovals, often with a smaller one growing out of them: this is **budding**, how it multiplies. It feeds on sugar and gives off gas, and these bubbles are what make bread rise.

![Mushroom spores](mob:mushroom_spore)

**Spores.** From ![](item:mushroom_red) mushrooms. A mushroom is only the fruit of a fungus, whose main body is threads underground. Each one can release billions of these. They do the work of seeds, with only one cell: the wind takes them, and a few land somewhere good.

## Science and game

**True:** their shapes (rods, chains and strings of beads), how yeast buds, and that a good microscope at 400× does just reach them; to see them well, people use the oil objective, at 1000×.

**Simplified in the game:**

- Bacteria are drawn a little larger than true scale, and more clearly: real ones are nearly transparent, and usually need a stain.
- In the game no microbe ever harms you, and water is safe. **In real life some do make people ill**, and this is why we wash our hands, boil water we are not sure of and cook food.
- A real soil sample holds thousands of species, most of which nobody has named.
- Milk in the game shows only lactobacillus. Real raw milk holds others.
