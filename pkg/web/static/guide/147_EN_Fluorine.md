## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize F Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_f` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

It takes part in **1** reaction you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *One calcium with two fluorines makes purple crystals.*

## In the real world

- It is the **most reactive** element of all: it reacts with almost anything.
- The fluoride in toothpaste hardens tooth enamel.
- Non-stick pans are coated with a fluorine plastic, Teflon.
