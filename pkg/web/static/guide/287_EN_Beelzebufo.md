# Beelzebufo

![Beelzebufo](mob:beelzebufo)

The **devil frog**: as big as a beach ball, with a head that is half of it, a mouth as wide as its body, a
skull of bone like a helmet and a little horn over each eye. It is an ancient relative of the [frog](27).
Leave it alone and it only hops about. **Hit it and it jumps at you and bites.** It is an
[extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself, it has to be brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Look for its fossil.** It lies far down, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:fossil_rock_rare)
   `fossil_rock_rare`: a dark rock with a three-toed footprint. The page on [fossils](262) says how deep to dig.
2. **Break the block** with a [pickaxe](68). Which animal is inside depends on the place: if it was a
   Beelzebufo, you take ![](item:fossil_beelzebufo) `fossil_beelzebufo` with you.
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45 seconds**. It is
   a land animal: it needs no water, though it likes a pond.

The server keeps a revived Beelzebufo: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back. Each
player's game makes it hop by itself, so two players may see it a few blocks apart, always near its home.

## Behaviour

- **It sits and waits**, and hops a little way every few seconds. It does not stray more than about
  **8 blocks** from its home: further than that, it hops back.
- **It keeps its distance.** If you come within 3.5 blocks, it hops away, back to the water if it has found
  some nearby.
- **It swims** with its eyes out of the water, kicking its hind legs.
- **It croaks** at night and when it rains: two low notes, and its throat swells.
- It is **neutral**: it attacks nobody who has not attacked it.
- **If you hit it, it bites back.** First it hops away. Where it lands it turns round, **opens its mouth
  wide** and growls for 0.6 seconds. Then **it jumps at where you are**, up to 3 blocks, and if its mouth
  comes down within 0.9 blocks of you it bites: **2 damage**.
- It only bites whoever hit it, and only that player sees it. If you are more than 6 blocks away when it has
  turned round, it lets it be. In the water it does not bite back.

It is about **1 block** long and as wide, and a little over half a block tall. There are **2 colours**: moss
green with dark blotches, and the brown of dead leaves.

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit it | **16 health**: 4 hits bare-handed, 3 with a stone or an iron [sword](71), 2 with a golden one. Every hit that does not defeat it makes it bite back, unless it was at it already. |
| Defeat it | It leaves nothing. |
| Tame it | You can't. |
| Bait | None. |
| Cage | It does not fit in the cage. |
| Egg | `beelzebufo_egg` |
| Command | `/mob beelzebufo` |

## Tips

- **Step aside when it opens its mouth.** It jumps at where you were when it took off, not at where you are
  now: two steps to one side and it lands on nothing.
- With a [shield](252) in your left hand, and facing it, its bite does half the damage.
- Dig it a pond next to its home: sooner or later it hops in, and you will see it swim.
- Put it next to a frog to see the difference in size. In the [Bestiary](5), the sheet of both shows them
  together under the title **Evolution**.

## The real Beelzebufo

- **When and where.** It lived about **70 million years ago** on the island of **Madagascar**, in the time
  of the last dinosaurs.
- **Its name** joins Beelzebub, an old name of the devil, and *bufo*, which is "toad" in Latin: the devil
  toad. Scientists gave it that name in 2008.
- **Size.** The first bones made scientists think of a frog more than **40 centimetres** long that weighed
  over **4 kilos**. With more bones, later, most of them turned out to be smaller. It is still one of the
  biggest frogs there have ever been. The biggest alive today, the goliath frog of Africa, reaches
  32 centimetres. The one in the game is more than twice as big as the real one, so that you can see it well.
- **A helmet for a head.** The bones of its skull were thick and rough, and joined into a shield.
- **Its bite.** Scientists measured how hard its living cousins bite, the horned frogs of South America, and
  worked out that a big Beelzebufo bit about as hard as a wolf.
- **What it ate.** It sat still, hidden, until something walked by, and swallowed whatever fitted in its
  mouth: lizards, small mammals, other frogs. Perhaps even a dinosaur just out of its egg.
- **The horns.** The horned frogs of today have a point of skin over each eye, and the game gives them to
  Beelzebufo too. Nobody knows if it had them: skin does not turn into a fossil.
- **A puzzle.** It looks very much like frogs that live on the other side of the world, in South America.
  Long ago Madagascar, India, Antarctica and South America were joined in one great land. Scientists still
  argue about whether Beelzebufo is truly a close relative of those frogs.
- **Why it died out.** Nobody knows. Its bones are in rocks from the last few million years before the
  asteroid that ended the time of the dinosaurs.
