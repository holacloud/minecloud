# Windows

![Glass pane](item:glass_pane)

A **glass pane** is a thin sheet of glass for making windows; **iron bars** are its grille version. Both join up
by themselves with whatever is next to them.

| | Item | Id | Break |
|---|---|---|---|
| ![](item:glass_pane) | Glass Pane | `glass_pane` | 0.2 s |
| ![](item:iron_bars) | Iron Bars | `iron_bars` | 0.8 s |

## How to get them

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| **8** `glass_pane` | **3** `glass` |
| **8** `iron_bars` | **3** `iron_ore` |

Glass comes from sand: see [Sand, cactus and glass](51).

## How to use them

- Place them with a right click like any block.
- **They join up by themselves:** each pane reaches out to the panes, bars, glass and solid blocks next to it. In
  a gap between two walls it becomes a window from side to side.
- A **lone** pane makes a cross; with a single neighbour, a straight sheet across the block.
- They are thin (an eighth of a block), but **you cannot walk through them**.
- You see through glass, and between the bars. Sunlight and [torch](62) light go through both.
- Remove a neighbour and the pane pulls back towards the ones it has left.

## Tips

- A row of panes in a [brick](57) wall looks like a real window, frame included.
- Bars are good for pens and cages you want to see into, and animals do not get through them.
- A [door](197) in the middle with panes at the sides makes a shop window.

## Trivia

- Cheap flat window glass came with float glass (1950s): molten glass is poured onto a bath of liquid tin and
  sets perfectly smooth.
- Before that, windows were made of blown and flattened glass, which is why old windows have waves and bubbles.
