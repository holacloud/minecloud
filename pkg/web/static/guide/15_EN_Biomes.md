# Biomes

The world is split into four **biomes**, depending on the heat, the moisture and the height of each area.
The borders between them are smooth: the land blends little by little.

## Plains

- **Grass** meadows with gentle hills and **tall grass**.
- In some meadows yellow and red **flowers** cluster together.
- Scattered trees: oak, birch, acacia and maple.
- **Living here:** sheep, pigs, **horses**, ducks, dogs, cats, eagles, foxes, giant moles, rabbits and, in
  smaller numbers, owls. There are bees, fireflies and bats.

## Forests

- Dense trees of **eight kinds**: oak, spruce, birch, jungle, dark oak, cherry, maple and willow.
- **Red and brown mushrooms** grow on the ground, with some tall grass and the odd flower.
- **Living here:** pigs, cats, **macaws**, dogs, spiders, **foxes** (more than on the plains), giant moles
  and rabbits. This is where most **owls**, **bees** and **bats** live, and there are fireflies too.

## Rocks

- Mountains and ridges of **stone** and **cobblestone**, the highest land in the world, with very little
  soil.
- Only spruces grow, with some tall grass and brown mushrooms.
- There are no lakes or beaches.
- **Living here:** **goats**, eagles, sheep, lizards, scorpions, spiders, the **cave monster**, vultures and
  snakes. Bats hang under the overhangs.

## Desert

- **Sand** with low dunes and **cacti** 2 to 4 blocks tall. Not a single tree.
- **Living here:** **camels**, lizards, scorpions, giraffes, ducks, **vultures** and **snakes**. Now and then,
  sand-coloured rabbits.
- No bats: they have nowhere to hang from.

## Water, lakes and beaches

- The sea and the lakes all reach the same level. Lakes form in low areas, never in the rocks.
- Next to the water, outside the rocks, there are **sandy beaches**.
- **Schools of fish** swim in the water.
- **Frogs** live on green banks; **turtles** and **crabs**, on sandy beaches.

## Underground

- Below a few blocks of soil (just one in the rocks) the **stone** begins.
- Stone holds **coal** at any depth; **iron** and **gold** only deep down, gold even deeper. See [Ores](53).
- At the very bottom lies **bedrock**, which cannot be broken.
