# Goat

![Goat](mob:goat)

A nimble climber with a beard and curved horns that leaps up two-block ledges and always seeks the highest
point. Every now and then it lowers its head and **rams** you.

## Where it lives

- **Rocky hills:** it is the most common animal there.
- In herds of **2 to 4**. By day and by night.

## Behaviour

- **It climbs:** it leaps onto ledges up to **2 blocks** high and drops down slopes of up to 6. When it lands you
  hear the clack of its hooves.
- **It seeks high ground:** when choosing where to go, it prefers uphill paths.
- It often stops, grazes with its head down, looks around and flicks its ears.
- **It rams:** if you are between 3 and 7 blocks away and at its height, every now and then:
  1. It lowers its head, stares at you and **paws the ground** with one hoof.
  2. It **charges** at you at full speed.
  3. If it reaches you, it deals **1 damage** and **knocks** you far back.
- Afterwards it needs a good while (15 to 35 seconds) before it can ram again.
- **If you hit it, it butts back:** it paws the ground for half a second and charges.

## How to interact

- **Hitting it:** it has **12 health** and falls in **3 blows** bare-handed (2 with a stone sword or better, see
  [Swords](71)); while it holds out, it will ram you. Defeated, it drops 1 `leather` and 1 `fur`.
- **Resources** (see [Mob resources](2)):

| How | Held item | Gives | Wait |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trim | ![Shears](item:iron_shears) `iron_shears` | 1 `fur` | 120 s |
| Milk | ![Glass](item:glass) `glass` (used up) | 1 `milk` | 60 s |

- **Taming it** (see [Taming and breeding](3)):

| Food | Chance per try |
|---|---|
| ![Leaves](item:leaves) `leaves`, ![Tall grass](item:tall_grass) `tall_grass` | 1/3 |

- A tamed goat **never rams** its owner and follows you, leaping over the rocks.
- **Bait:** it comes to `bait_seeds` (see [Baits and traps](4)).
- **Cage:** it **does** fit in the `cage_trap`.
- **Egg:** `goat_egg`, or the command `/mob goat`.

## Tips

- Careful near cliff edges: a ram knocks you back and the fall can hurt. The `feather_cape` softens fall damage.
- If a goat is above or below you (more than a block and a half higher or lower), it cannot ram you.
- Tame it before milking and trimming it: that way it will not ram you while you work.
- Only the goat and the [Camel](29) give milk.

## Trivia

- There are **2 coats**: creamy white with dark horns, and brown with a dark stripe along its back.
- Real ibexes climb almost vertical walls, and even dam walls to lick the salt off the stones!
- Their pupils are **rectangular**: they give them a very wide view to spot predators coming.
- Goats were among the first animals to be domesticated, about 10,000 years ago.
