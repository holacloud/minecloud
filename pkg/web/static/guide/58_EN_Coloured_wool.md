Break time: **0.35 s** by hand. Breaking it drops the same wool.

## How to get it

**Crafting it** (C key). Every wool uses the same recipe; you pick the colour in the list:

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| **4** wool of one colour | 2 `leaves` + 1 `flower_red` |

Yes: even blue or black wool is dyed with a **red flower**.

**White wool from animals:**

| Where | How much |
|---|---|
| Shearing a ![Sheep](mob:sheep) [sheep](17) with `iron_shears` (right click) | 1–3 `white_wool`; it grows back in 150 s |
| Defeating a ![Spider](mob:spider) [spider](20) | 30 % chance of 1 `white_wool` |

## What it is for

| Recipe | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `bug_net` | 1 `wood` + **2** `white_wool` |
| 1 `feather_cape` | 6 `feather` + **2** `white_wool` |
| 1 `bed` (feather) | 4 `feather` + **2** `white_wool` + 3 `planks` |

Coloured wool is **decorative**: rugs, flags, awnings, pixel art…

## Tips

- [Iron shears](73) break wool almost instantly (0.13 s).
- For recipes, **white** wool from sheep is free: do not spend red flowers on it.
- With 16 colours you can draw on the ground and admire it from the top of a tower.

## Trivia

- Sheep's wool contains **lanolin**, a natural grease that makes it water-repellent.
- The red of many old fabrics came from **cochineal**, a tiny insect.
- Roman emperors' purple came from sea snails: it took thousands of them to dye one toga.
