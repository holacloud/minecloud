# Measuring tape

![Measuring tape](item:measuring_tape)

A steel tape rolled up in a yellow case. It **measures** buildings: mark two corners and it tells you the size of
the box between them. It is the first step to make a [blueprint](191).

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:measuring_tape) | Measuring Tape | `measuring_tape` |

## How to get it

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `measuring_tape` (*Craft Measuring Tape*) | 1 `iron_ore` + 1 `planks` |

Iron ore comes from veins in the stone: see [Ores](53).

## How to use it

1. Hold it and **right click** a block: that is **corner A**.
2. While you look for the other corner, a yellow box stretches **from A to the block you aim at**, with its size on
   top.
3. **Right click** the **opposite corner** (B). The chat tells you the size, for example **7 × 4 × 9**, and how many
   blocks are inside.
4. A third click starts a new measurement. A right click at **the sky** (no block) clears it.

Sizes go **width × height × depth**, counting the corner blocks: from one block to the one next to it is 2.

## Limits

- At most **16 blocks** per side. If you go over, the box turns **red**.
- At most **2048 blocks** inside (air, water, crops and bedrock do not count).

With the box measured, an **empty blueprint** draws it: see [Blueprints](191).

## Tips

- **Measure twice, cut once:** before drawing, look at the box from outside so you do not leave the roof out.
- **How big is your house?** Measure it even if you do not copy it: the chat tells you how many blocks you used.

## Trivia

- The **metre** was defined in 1791 as one ten-millionth of the distance from the North Pole to the equator.
- Pocket tape measures have a **curved** tape: that keeps it straight in the air without folding.
