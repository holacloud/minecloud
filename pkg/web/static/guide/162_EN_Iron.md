# Iron (Fe)

![Iron block](item:element_fe)

Element number **26** of the periodic table.

| | |
|---|---|
| Symbol | **Fe** |
| Atomic number | 26 |
| Group | 8 |
| Period | 4 |
| Family | transition metal |
| In-game name | Iron Element Block |
| Id | `element_fe` |

## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize FE Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_fe` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

The ore, `iron_ore`, is [found in the world](248), and is another item: the [foundry](235) turns it into `iron_block`, not into this block.

## In the lab

Reactions the lab knows from the start:

- [Steel](120): `Fe + C → steel`

It takes part in **4** reactions you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *They say iron rusts when it meets oxygen: two of iron for three of oxygen.*
- *The same corundum turns blue with a little iron and titanium.*
- *"Fool's gold" is iron with two of sulfur.*
- *Iron that falls from the sky brings nickel: one and one.*

## In the real world

- It is the **most used metal** in the world.
- The Earth's core is mostly iron and nickel.
- The iron in your blood (in haemoglobin) carries oxygen around your body. Its symbol comes from the Latin *ferrum*.
