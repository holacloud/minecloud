# Grass and dirt

![Grass block](item:grass)

The ground of the plains and forests: a layer of **grass** on top, **dirt** underneath and, scattered over it,
**tall grass** swaying in the wind.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:grass) | Grass Block | `grass` |
| ![](item:dirt) | Dirt | `dirt` |
| ![](item:tall_grass) | Tall Grass | `tall_grass` |

## Where to find them

- **Grass:** the surface of plains and forests, always above the water level.
- **Dirt:** the 3 or 4 layers under the grass. Below that, stone begins. The rocky lands have no dirt (their
  surface is stone and cobblestone), and the desert and beaches have sand.
- **Tall grass:** grows on grass, mostly in the plains; also in forests and, rarely, in the rocky lands.

You start the game with **24 grass and 24 dirt**.

## How to get them

Break them with a left click: each one drops **itself** (grass stays grass).

| Block | Break time (bare hand) | Bonus |
|---|---|---|
| `grass` | 0.35 s | 8 %: a random [sapling](55) |
| `dirt` | 0.45 s | — |
| `tall_grass` | 0.12 s | 35 %: [wheat seeds](108); 3.5 %: a random [mob egg](74) |

## What they are for

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 2 `brick` | **2** `dirt` + 1 `sand` |
| 1 sapling (`*_sapling`) | 2 leaves of that tree + **1** `dirt` |
| 3 `bait_worms` | **2** `dirt` |
| 4 `bait_seeds` | **2** `tall_grass` |

- **Tall grass** is not solid: you walk through it. It is food to tame sheep, ducks, camels, goats, horses and
  rabbits (see [Taming and breeding](3)).
- Grass and dirt are solid and perfect for filling, shaping hills or plugging holes.
- **Right click** grass or dirt with a [hoe](72) to till it into farmland, where wheat grows. See
  [Growing wheat](107).

## Tips

- To **dig fast** use a [shovel](69): it speeds up dirt, sand and grass.
- Looking for saplings? Break **grass blocks**: roughly one in twelve gives a sapling.
- Looking for eggs? Mow **tall grass**: it is instant, and now and then an egg of any creature drops.

## Trivia

- A handful of fertile soil holds more microorganisms than there are people on the whole planet.
- Bamboo, [wheat](109) and maize are **grasses**, from the same family as your lawn.
- **Game quirk:** the grass block counts as "stone" for tools, so a pickaxe breaks it fast too, just like a shovel
  or a hoe.
