# Glyptodon

![Glyptodon](mob:glyptodon)

A **giant armadillo** the size of a small car: a great dome of a **shell** made of hundreds of bony plates, a
little armoured cap on its head, short strong legs and a **tail in rings of bone**. It stands **1.4 blocks**
tall and is nearly **4 blocks** long with its tail. It is peaceful, and once tamed **you can ride it**: slowly,
but inside its armour. It is an [extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself, it has to be brought back to
life.

## How to get one

1. **Look for its fossil.** It lies far down, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:fossil_rock_rare)
   `fossil_rock_rare`: a dark rock with a three-toed footprint. The page on [fossils](262) says how deep to dig.
2. **Break the block** with a [pickaxe](68). Which animal is inside depends on the place: if it was a
   glyptodon, you take ![](item:fossil_glyptodon) `fossil_glyptodon` with you.
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45 seconds**. It is
   a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived glyptodon: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back.

## Behaviour

- **It plods** near its place, very slowly, swinging its ringed tail.
- It is a grazer: when it stands still, **it lowers its head to eat**.
- It is **peaceful**: it comes up to nobody and attacks nobody. If you hit it, it gives a start.

There is one colour only: a sandy brown shell with a pale plate in each cell of the pattern, and dark skin on
the head and legs.

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit it | **40 health**: 10 hits bare-handed, 7 with a stone [sword](71), 6 with an iron one, 5 with a golden one. |
| Defeat it | It leaves **2 to 4** ![leather](item:leather) `leather`. |
| Tame it | Yes, with `tall_grass`, `wheat` or `leaves`, with **1 in 3** per try. Then it follows you. See [Taming and breeding](3). |
| Ride it | **Right click** on a tamed one, with an empty hand or any block that is not its food. **Shift** gets off. A revived one has to be tamed first: only its owner rides it. See [Riding](7). |
| Breed | Two tamed ones in love have a baby, which is too young to ride until it grows up. |
| Bait | None. |
| Cage | It is too big for the cage. |
| Egg | `glyptodon_egg` |
| Command | `/mob glyptodon` |

## Riding it

| | Glyptodon | [Horse](34) |
|---|---|---|
| Your eyes, above the ground | **2.7 blocks** | 2.3 blocks |
| Speed, blocks per second | **5** | 9 |
| Galloping (double tap **W**) | **6.3** | 11.25 |
| Jump (**Space**) | about **1 block** | about 2.7 blocks |

- You sit on the top of its shell. It is slow and low, but **its armour covers you**: while you ride it, the
  blows of animals do **half the damage** (bites, horns, stings...). Falls, poison and lightning hurt as usual.
- It walks up 1-block steps by itself, as a horse does, but it cannot jump any higher.
- **It needs 3 free blocks of height**: under a low roof you cannot get on, and riding into one stops you.
- Other players see you riding it.

## Tips

- `tall_grass` is everywhere: take a good handful and offer it one at a time until it stays with you.
- Do not carry its food in your hand when you want to get on: with `tall_grass`, `wheat` or `leaves` the right
  click feeds it.
- It is the mount to take into a dark cave or across a land full of hostile animals. To get anywhere fast, take
  a horse.

## The real Glyptodon

- **When and where.** It lived in the grasslands of **South America** from about **3 million** to about
  **10,000 years ago**. Close relatives of it also reached North America.
- **Its name** means "carved tooth": its teeth had grooves along them.
- **Size.** It was about **3 metres** long and **1.5 metres** tall, and weighed between **1 and 2 tonnes**: as
  much as a small car.
- **The shell.** It was one piece, made of **more than a thousand** bony plates joined together, each a few
  centimetres thick, with a pattern of rosettes on each. Unlike an armadillo, it could not roll up: its head had
  a cap of bone and its tail was covered in rings of bone. Its legs were short and very strong, to carry all
  that weight.
- **What it ate.** Grass and other plants that grow low near the ground.
- **Its family.** It was a relative of the **armadillos** of today: a study of the DNA kept in the shell of one of
  its cousins showed that the glyptodonts were a branch of the armadillo family that grew enormous.
- **Why it died out.** It disappeared at the end of the Ice Age, when the climate changed and people arrived in
  South America. Scientists have found glyptodont bones with cut marks made by people's tools.
