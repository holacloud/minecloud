# Baits and traps

## Recipes

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 4 `bait_seeds` | 2 `tall_grass` |
| 2 `bait_seeds` | 1 `wheat_seeds` |
| 3 `bait_worms` | 2 `dirt` |
| 2 `bait_meat` | `leather` + `mushroom_brown` |
| 2 `bait_fish` | `raw_fish` |
| 2 `bait_honey` | `honeycomb` |
| `fishing_rod` | 2 `wood` + `iron_ore` |
| `bug_net` | `wood` + 2 `white_wool` |
| `cage_trap` | 4 `planks` + 2 `iron_ore` |

The `firefly_jar` is not crafted: you get it by catching fireflies with the net.

## Baits

With a bait in your hand, right click the ground to leave a small pile.

- It lasts **2 minutes** and draws the animals that like it within **16 blocks**.
- They come over and eat with their heads down, and the pile shrinks.
- Afterwards they stay around for 25 to 40 seconds.

| Bait | Draws |
|---|---|
| `bait_seeds` | sheep, duck, pig, giraffe, macaw, camel, goat, horse |
| `bait_worms` | duck, pig, frog, turtle, lizard, scorpion (and fish, but only on the fishing hook) |
| `bait_meat` | dog, cat, spider, cave monster |
| `bait_fish` | cat, turtle, crab |
| `bait_honey` | bees (they sip the bait instead of flowers) |

Wild animals with a will of their own (fox, rabbit, owl, vulture, eagle, snake, mole) ignore baits. Pets that
follow you ignore them too.

## Cage trap (`cage_trap`)

1. Right click the ground to set it down. The door faces you.
2. With a bait in your hand, right click the empty cage to put the bait inside.
3. When an animal walks in to eat, the door snaps shut and it is caught.
4. Right click the cage or the caught animal to get **that animal's egg** and the cage back.

If the cage is empty and you are not holding a bait, a right click picks it up.

**Fit inside:** sheep, duck, pig, dog, cat, spider, frog, turtle, lizard, scorpion, goat, crab, fox, rabbit, snake
and mole.

**Do not fit:** large animals, flying ones and fish.

**Never caught:** tamed animals and ridden ones. Frogs, lizards and turtles are nudged right to the middle
of a baited cage.

Cages, with their animal inside, are kept when the page reloads. They only exist in your browser.

## Fishing rod (`fishing_rod`)

1. Right click aiming at water (up to 9 blocks away) to cast. You will see the line and the bobber.
2. Wait for a bite: the bobber dips, splashes and makes a sound.
3. You have **1.1 seconds** to reel in with another right click.

| Catch | Chance |
|---|---|
| `raw_fish` | 72 % |
| `shell` | 12 % |
| Junk (`leaves`, `wood`, `dirt` or `tall_grass`) | the rest |

**For quicker bites:**

- Carry `bait_worms` or `bait_fish` in your inventory. One is used up per cast.
- Fish near a school of fish. The fish swim over.

The line breaks if you switch items or walk away.

## Bug net (`bug_net`)

Right click a small creature close by (up to 4.5 blocks) with the net:

| Creature | You get |
|---|---|
| Fireflies | `firefly_jar` (the cloud loses fireflies and vanishes when few are left) |
| Bee | `bee_egg` |
| Bat | `bat_egg` |
| Wisp | `glow_dust` |

## Firefly jar (`firefly_jar`)

- Right click the ground to set it down. It is a lamp: the fireflies blink and glow inside, with bloom in Ultra.
- Right click the jar: you pick it up.
- Right click it with the net: you set the fireflies free.

Jars that are set down are kept when the page reloads.
