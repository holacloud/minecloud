# Mammoth

![Mammoth](mob:mammoth)

The **woolly elephant of the Ice Age**: a shaggy brown coat, a high domed head, small ears, a trunk that sways
and two **long tusks** that curl outwards, up and back in. It stands **3.3 blocks** tall, twice as tall as a
[horse](34). It grazes in peace, but **if you hit it, it trumpets and charges**. It is an
[extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself, it has to be brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Its skeleton, in three pieces.** You need the skull, the spine and the tail: `fossil_mammoth_skull`,
   `fossil_mammoth_spine` and `fossil_mammoth_tail`. They come out of the three blocks of a **skeleton**
   (`fossil_bones_skull`, `fossil_bones_spine` and `fossil_bones_tail`), which lie in a straight line inside the
   stone, deep down. The three blocks of a skeleton always belong to one animal, and the mammoth is the one
   you will find most often: its weight against the other skeletons is 6, and none of the others has more than
   2. The page on [fossils](262) says how deep to dig and how to find the other two pieces once you have one.
2. **Break the blocks** with a [pickaxe](68).
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264), with **the three pieces** inside. It takes **45
   seconds**. It is a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived mammoth: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back.

## Behaviour

- **It strolls** near its place, slowly, swaying its trunk from side to side.
- It is a grazer: when it stands still, **it lowers its head to eat**.
- It turns its head to look around and flicks its small ears.
- It is **neutral**: it attacks nobody who has not attacked it.
- **If you hit it, it charges.** It trumpets, turns on you, **lowers its tusks** and paws the ground for
  1 second. Then **it runs in a straight line** at where you were: about 4 blocks, at 6 blocks a second. If it
  gets within 2.6 blocks of you, its tusks do **4 damage** and throw you back and up. After that it stands still
  for a second and a half.
- It only charges whoever hit it, and only that player sees the charge.
- It does not jump off cliffs, and it stops **before its head** reaches a wall, a fence or a drop: it looks
  1.6 blocks ahead of its middle.

There is one colour only: dark brown, with ginger tips in its hair, a long fringe along its flanks and ivory
tusks.

## Tips

- **Step aside as soon as it lowers its tusks.** It runs at where you were when it set off, not at where you
  are now: one long step to the side and it goes past.
- Its tusks reach far: stay more than 3 blocks from its middle while it runs.
- A fence between the two of you stops it.
- With a [shield](252) in your left hand, and facing it, the tusks do half the damage.
- It is very big: in a forest its head and tusks go through the leaves. Bring it back in an open place.

## The real mammoth

- **When and where.** The woolly mammoth lived in the cold steppes of **Europe, Asia and North America**, from
  about **400,000 years ago**. Most of them died out about **10,000 years ago**, at the end of the last Ice
  Age, but a few lived on a small island in the Arctic, **Wrangel Island**, until about **4,000 years ago**:
  when the pyramids of Egypt were already standing.
- **Size.** It stood about **3 metres** at the shoulder, as tall as an African elephant of today, and weighed
  around **6 tonnes**. Its tusks could be **4 metres** long.
- **Made for the cold.** Hair up to **90 centimetres** long over a soft woolly undercoat, a thick layer of fat
  under the skin, and **small ears and a short tail**: big ears, like those of an African elephant, lose heat.
- **What it ate.** Grass and other low plants of the steppe, many kilos every day. It used its tusks to sweep
  away the snow and find the grass under it.
- **We know it very well.** Mammoths have been found **frozen** in the ground of Siberia, with their skin and
  hair: a baby found in 2007 still had milk in its stomach. And the people of the Ice Age painted and carved
  them on the walls of caves, in France and in Spain.
- **Why it died out.** The climate grew warmer, and the cold steppe where it found its food shrank into
  forests and bogs. People hunted it too. Scientists still argue about how much each counted.
- **Its family.** It is not the elephant's grandfather but its cousin. Its closest living relative is the
  **Asian elephant**. There are no elephants in MineCloud, so it has no living animal next to it in the
  [Bestiary](5).
