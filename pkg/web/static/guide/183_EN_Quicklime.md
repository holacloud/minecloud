## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *One calcium with a single oxygen: the builders' lime.*

Or in the [foundry](235), as a lime kiln does: 1 `limestone` gives **2** `quicklime` in 15 s.

## What it is for

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 6 `stone_bricks` (*Lay Stone Bricks with Lime Mortar*) | 1 `quicklime` + 4 `cobblestone` |

## Trivia

- Quicklime is made by heating limestone: it gives off CO₂ and calcium oxide is left.
- With water it gets very hot (that is why it is called "quick", alive) and becomes slaked lime.
- The Romans built with lime mortar; mixed with volcanic ash, their concrete has lasted two thousand years.

## Solution

If you would rather discover it yourself, stop reading here.

| Reaction | Kind | Product |
|---|---|---|
| `Ca + O → CaO (quicklime)` | compound | 2 `quicklime` |
