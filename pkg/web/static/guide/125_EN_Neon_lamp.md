# Neon lamp

![Neon lamp](item:neon_lamp)

A block with a zigzag neon tube that **shines by itself** in red-orange, day and night.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:neon_lamp) | Neon Lamp | `neon_lamp` |

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *Alone, inside some glass, it glows red-orange.*

## What it is for

- Place it like any block. Breaking: **0.3 s**; it drops the lamp itself.
- It shines in every [graphics mode](14); in **Ultra** it also glows (bloom).
- **It does not light up its surroundings**: you need a [torch](62) for that.

## Trivia

- A neon lamp burns nothing: electricity excites the gas atoms, which give the energy back as light.
- The Frenchman Georges Claude showed off neon lamps in Paris in **1910**.
