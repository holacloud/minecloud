# Ant farm

![Ant farm](item:ant_farm)

A narrow glass box full of soil, on a wooden stand. Put a queen and a few workers inside and you will see them
**dig tunnels and chambers**, the queen **lay eggs** and the young turn into new ants. All behind the glass.

| | Name in the game | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:ant_farm) | Ant Farm | `ant_farm` |
| ![](item:ant_colony_jar) | Ant Colony Jar | `ant_colony_jar` |

## How to get it

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `ant_farm` | 3 `glass` + 2 `dirt` + 2 `planks` |

Ants are not crafted: you catch them with the [net](95) at an [anthill](135). Each catch gives you an
`ant_colony_jar` with **a young queen and five workers**.

## Step by step

1. With the farm in your hand, **right click** on top of a block. It is placed facing you.
2. **Right click the farm** with the `ant_colony_jar`. The queen and five workers move in.
3. **Feed them:** right click the farm with food.
4. Wait, and watch through the glass.

## What happens inside

- The workers **dig**: the tunnel goes down from the surface, bends, branches and widens into **chambers**. The
  soil they bring up piles up on top, next to the entrance. The more workers, the faster they dig.
- The first chamber, about a third of the way down, is the **queen's**. The next ones (up to 6 chambers) are for
  the **young** or for **food**.
- While they have food, the queen lays **an egg every 35–55 seconds** (up to 10 young at a time).
- Each one goes through **three stages**: **egg** (50 s, a white dot) → **larva** (70 s, cream) → **pupa** (60 s,
  tan) → **new worker**. About **3 minutes** in all. If you are nearby, the chat tells you: "A new worker ant
  hatched in your ant farm".
- There is room for up to **30 workers** and about 90 stretches of tunnel; when the soil is full, they stop
  digging.

## Food

| Food | How much it fills |
|---|---|
| `bread`, `honeycomb` | a lot (80 %) |
| `cheese`, `bait_honey` | 60 % |
| `raw_fish`, `cooked_fish`, `bait_meat` | 50 % |
| `wheat`, `bait_seeds`, `bait_worms`, `bait_fish` | 40 % |
| `mushroom_red`, `mushroom_brown` | 35 % |
| `wheat_seeds` | 30 % |
| `flower_red`, `flower_yellow` | 25 % |

- The store goes down little by little, faster the more workers there are. With a few ants, half a store lasts
  more than ten minutes.
- **Hungry** ants come to no harm, but the queen stops laying, the larvae stop growing and they dig much more
  slowly.

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Right click with an empty hand | It tells you how they are doing: queen, workers, eggs, larvae, pupae, tunnels (%) and food ("well fed", "running low", "hungry"). |
| Right click with an `ant_colony_jar` | With no queen, the queen and 5 workers move in. With one already, 5 more workers move in and **the second queen leaves**: this colony accepts only one queen. |
| Right click with food | The ants carry it down to a chamber. If they already have plenty, it is not used up. |
| **Shift** + right click with an empty hand | You pick up the farm. If it had a queen, you also get an `ant_colony_jar` back; the tunnels are lost. |
| Right click the ground with an `ant_colony_jar` | You **set the ants free** on grass, dirt or sand: the queen founds a new anthill, which grows to 12 ants. |

## Good to know

- Farms are saved in **this browser**, like cages and jars. When you come back to the game, the ants have kept
  working while you were away (up to half an hour).
- The farm is not a block of the world: other players don't see it.

## Trivia

- An ant farm is also called a **formicarium**. The narrow glass ones became famous in the 1950s as an
  educational toy.
- In the wild, a new colony starts the same way: a **young queen** digs in alone after the mating flight and
  raises her first workers.
- Eggs, larvae and pupae are called the **brood**. Workers move them from chamber to chamber as the temperature
  and the damp change, and feed the larvae mouth to mouth.
- In many species, real ants **do not accept** strange queens: they recognise them by the colony's smell.
