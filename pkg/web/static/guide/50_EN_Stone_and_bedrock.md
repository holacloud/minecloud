# Stone and bedrock

![Stone](item:stone)

Under the dirt, the world is **stone**. Further down still, at the very bottom, lies **bedrock**, which nothing
can break.

| | In-game name | Id | Break (bare hand) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ![](item:stone) | Stone | `stone` | 0.9 s |
| ![](item:cobblestone) | Cobblestone | `cobblestone` | 1 s |
| ![](item:bedrock) | Bedrock | `bedrock` | **unbreakable** |

## Where to find them

- **Stone:** under the layers of dirt or sand, everywhere. It hides the [ores](53).
- **Cobblestone:** in the rocky lands (mountains), the surface mixes cobblestone and stone.
- **Bedrock:** the floor of the world, at height **y = −5** (and in half of the cells at y = −4).

## How to get them

- Breaking **stone** gives you **cobblestone**, not stone.
- **Cobblestone** drops cobblestone.
- **Bedrock** cannot be broken: the block inspector says "Unbreakable".
- You start with **24 cobblestone**.

## What they are for

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 2 `stone_bricks` | **2** `cobblestone` + 1 `brick` |
| 1 `sound_block` | 2 `planks` + **1** `cobblestone` + 1 `coal_ore` |
| Stone tools (`stone_*`) | **3** `cobblestone` (sword: 2) + 1 `wood` |
| 2 marble, basalt, slate, limestone, granite or concrete | **2** `cobblestone` + 1 `sand` |
| 2 `cobblestone_slab` | **1** `cobblestone` |

Several recipes ask for **smooth stone** (`stone`): the [metal blocks and obsidian](59), the
[element blocks](60) and the stone slab. But in this version **mining stone gives cobblestone and no recipe makes
`stone`**, so those recipes cannot be completed yet.

## Tips

- A [pickaxe](68) is the tool for stone: an iron one breaks stone in 0.35 s.
- If you fall below the bedrock (through a hole in the world) and pass y = −35, **you die in the void**.
- Cobblestone is the most plentiful building material: walls, towers and roads.

## Trivia

- In geology, *bedrock* is the solid rock that lies under the soil and loose sediments.
- Cobbled streets have been around since ancient Rome.
- The rock of the Earth's crust is made mostly of **oxygen and silicon**.
