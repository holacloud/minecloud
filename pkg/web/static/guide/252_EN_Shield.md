# Shield

![Shield](item:shield)

Five boards with a pointed foot, an iron rim, a boss with a copper stud on the outside and straps on the inside. There is no
block button: you carry it in the [left hand](251) and it stops by itself what is **in front of you**.

## How to get it

Crafted (C key):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `shield` | **4** `planks` + **2** `iron_ore` |

## How to use it

Select it and press **X** (or the **L** button in the inventory). **It only works in the left hand:** in the right or stored in
the inventory it does nothing. Press G with it in hand and the chat reminds you what it does. It does not wear out.

**Effect:** −50 % of the damage of each attack by a creature **in front of you**: up to 60° to each side of where you look,
height aside. What comes from a side or from behind gets through whole.

## What it stops and what it does not

| Damage | With a shield |
|---|---|
| ![Cave monster](mob:cave_monster) [Cave monster](21), [wandering shadow](37), [spider](20), [scorpion](31), [snake](42), bee, crab, goat, [giant mole](47), [Kubanochoerus](269) and the other [extinct animals](261) that attack (bites, charges, pecks and dives) | Half, from the front |
| ![Storm golem](mob:storm_golem) The bolt a [storm golem](43) throws at you | Half, from the front |
| Poison (the points that keep falling after the bite) | Nothing: the bite is reduced, the poison is not |
| Falls and the void | Nothing |
| Lightning from the sky | Nothing |
| [Electric eel](213) discharge | Nothing: it comes through the water and has no front |
| [Ant](135) bites | Nothing: they are on your legs, on every side |

## With armour

First the armour takes its points, with its minimum of 1, and then the shield takes half of what is left.

| Attack from the front | Nothing | Shield only | [Coat](90) + shield | [Mail](91) + shield |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 (cave monster) | 4 | 2 | 1.5 | 1 |
| 3 (shadow) | 3 | 1.5 | 1 | 0.5 |
| 2 (spider) | 2 | 1 | 0.5 | 0.5 |
| 1 (bee, crab) | 1 | 0.5 | 0.5 | 0.5 |

Health takes half points: the health bar shows them.

## Tips

- **Do not turn your back.** Against a [shadow](37), look at it: it slows it down, and you stop half of the blow.
- Sword in the right and shield in the left: the strike is that of the sword alone.
- Carry [antivenom](89) as well: a shield does not stop poison.

## Trivia

- The bulge in the middle of a shield is called the **boss**: it protected the hand gripping behind it.
