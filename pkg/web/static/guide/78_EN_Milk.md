# Milk

![Milk](item:milk)

A glass bottle full of fresh milk. You drink it to **cure poison**, it is the base of **cheese** and **antivenom**,
and cats love it.

## How to get it

**Milking:** with a `glass` in your hand, right click a goat or a camel. The glass is used up and the bottle of milk
goes **straight into your inventory**.

| Animal | Milk | Wait |
|---|---|---|
| ![Goat](mob:goat) [Goat](32) | 1 `milk` | 60 s per animal |
| ![Camel](mob:camel) [Camel](29) | 1 `milk` | 60 s per animal |

If the animal has no milk yet, the chat tells you and the glass is **not** used up. Glass is crafted from 3 `sand`
→ 2 `glass` ([Sand, cactus and glass](51)).

## Drinking (G key)

| Health | Effect |
|---|---|
| **+2** | **cures poison** and gives the `glass` back |

At full health and not poisoned you do not drink it ("You are already at full health").

## What it is for

| Recipe | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| [Cheese](88) `cheese` | **2** `milk` |
| [Antivenom](89) `antivenom` | 1 `venom_sac` + **1** `milk` |

**Taming:** milk is one of the foods of the ![Cat](mob:cat) [cat](25) (1 in 2 tries). See
[Taming and breeding](3).

## Tips

- **Against the venom** of ![Snake](mob:snake) snakes and ![Scorpion](mob:scorpion) scorpions: poison takes 1
  health every 1.5 s for 3 ticks. One milk stops it dead.
- Drinking milk **gives the bottle back**, but cheese and antivenom **keep it**: every cheese costs two glasses.
- A tamed goat or camel follows you: your dairy travels with you.

## Trivia

- **Camel** milk has been drunk in deserts for thousands of years, but in real life it is very hard to turn into
  cheese: it barely curdles. In the game, no problem.
- Goats were among the first animals ever domesticated, some 10,000 years ago.
