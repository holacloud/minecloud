# Kitchen and bath

![Oven](item:oven)

| | In-game name | Id | What it does | Made from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ![](item:workbench) | Workbench | `workbench` | right click opens the [crafting](11) panel | 4 oak planks, 1 wood |
| ![](item:oven) | Oven | `oven` | right click lights it, and it gives light | 6 cobblestone, 2 iron ore, 1 coal ore |
| ![](item:sink) | Sink | `sink` | decoration | 2 marble, 2 oak planks, 1 iron ore |
| ![](item:fridge) | Fridge | `fridge` | decoration, two blocks tall | 6 iron ore, 1 marble |
| ![](item:toilet) | Toilet | `toilet` | you can [sit](224) on it, of course | 3 marble |
| ![](item:bathtub) | Bathtub | `bathtub` | two blocks long, you can step in | 5 marble, 1 iron ore |

The oven does not cook anything: it is a piece of a kitchen, a warm glow when you light it, and a good lamp for a winter night. Your food you still cook as usual, and the fridge keeps nothing cold.

The bathtub lies away from you when you place it, so its taps end up on the far side.

White china is really clay baked very hot, until the grains melt together and it stops letting water through. That is why it is used for sinks and bathtubs. In the game they are made from [marble](59), which you refine from stone.
