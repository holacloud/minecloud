# Kitchen and bath

![Oven](item:oven)

| | In-game name | Id | What it does | Made from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ![](item:workbench) | Workbench | `workbench` | right click opens the [crafting](11) panel | 4 oak planks, 1 wood |

Right click opens the oven, the fridge and the chest freezer, as with a [chest](226): crouch to build against them. The [oven](233) cooks with coal or wood and lights by itself. The [fridge and the chest freezer](234) need 30 W each; without power they are plain cupboards. Food never spoils in this game, in them or anywhere else.

The bathtub lies away from you when you place it, so its taps end up on the far side.

White china is really clay baked very hot, until the grains melt together and it stops letting water through. That is why it is used for sinks and bathtubs. In the game they are made from [marble](59), which you refine from stone.
