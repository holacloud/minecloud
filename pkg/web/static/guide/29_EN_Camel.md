# Camel

![Camel](mob:camel)

A sturdy desert traveller with sand-coloured fur and a single hump. It grazes among the dunes and now and then
folds its long legs to lie down on the warm sand.

## Where it lives

- **Desert:** it is the most common animal there.
- In groups of **1 to 3**. By day and by night.

## Behaviour

- It walks calmly without straying far from where it was born.
- It is a **grazer**: when it stops, it lowers its head.
- **It lies down:** now and then it folds its legs under its body and rests on the ground for 10 to 25 seconds.
- It looks around, flicks its ears and breathes.
- If you hit it, it hops, gives a low sound and turns towards you. It never attacks.

## How to interact

- **Hitting it:** it has **12 health** and falls in **3 blows** bare-handed (2 with a stone sword or better, see
  [Swords](71)). Defeated, it drops **1 to 2** `leather` and, half the time, 1 `fur`.
- **Resources** (see [Mob resources](2)):

| How | Held item | Gives | Wait |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trim | ![Shears](item:iron_shears) `iron_shears` | 1 `fur` | 120 s |
| Milk | ![Glass](item:glass) `glass` (used up) | 1 `milk` | 60 s |

- **Taming it** (see [Taming and breeding](3)):

| Food | Chance per try |
|---|---|
| ![Cactus](item:cactus) `cactus`, ![Tall grass](item:tall_grass) `tall_grass` | 1/3 |

- **Bait:** it comes to `bait_seeds` (see [Baits and traps](4)).
- **Cage:** it does **not** fit: it is too big.
- **Egg:** `camel_egg`, or the command `/mob camel`.

## Tips

- The desert is full of cacti: break some and use them to tame it on the spot.
- `milk` cures poison. Scorpions live in the desert, so milking a camel can save you (see [Milk](78) and
  [Scorpion](31)).
- 2 `milk` make `cheese`, and `milk` + `venom_sac` make `antivenom`.
- Only the camel and the [Goat](32) give milk.

## Trivia

- A real camel's hump does not store water but **fat**, which works as an energy reserve.
- A thirsty camel can drink more than 100 litres of water in a few minutes.
- They have a double row of eyelashes and can close their nostrils to keep the sand out.
- The game's camel has a single hump, like a **dromedary**. Bactrian camels have two.
