# Element blocks

![Oxygen block](item:element_o)

A **periodic table to build with**: 36 blocks, one for each chosen chemical element, each with its own colour and
its **chemical symbol written on every face**.

## How to get them

They are all "synthesized" with the same recipe (C key); you pick the element in the list:

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 element block | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

In the recipe list they show up as *Synthesize H Block*, *Synthesize HE Block*… (with the symbol in capitals).

They need **smooth stone** (`stone`), and in this version mining stone gives cobblestone, so for now they cannot be
crafted (see [Stone and bedrock](50)).

[Fine stones and metals](59): *Iron Element Block*, *Copper Element Block* and *Gold Element Block*.

## What they are for

They are **building and learning** blocks: they have no chemical effect in the game.

- Break time: **0.9 s** by hand; 0.35 s with an iron [pickaxe](68). They drop themselves.
- The symbol reads well up close and from afar: ideal for giant signs and words.

## Tips

- **Spell words with symbols:** **F**-**I**-**S**-**H**, **B**-**O**-**O**-**K**, **S**-**P**-**O**-**O**-**N**,
  **Ca**-**Fe**… how many more can you make with these 36?
- Build a real **periodic table** on a wall: leave gaps for the missing elements.
- Put a [sign](64) next to each block with a fact about the element.

## Trivia

- **Hydrogen** (H) is the most abundant element in the universe: stars are made mostly of it.
- **Helium** (He) was first found in the light of the **Sun**, in 1868, before it was found on Earth; hence its
  name (*helios*, "sun").
- **Oxygen** (O) is the most abundant element in the Earth's crust, and **silicon** (Si) the second.
- **Mercury** (Hg) is the only metal that is **liquid** at room temperature.
- **Tungsten** (W) is the metal with the highest melting point: 3,422 °C. Its symbol comes from its other name,
  *wolfram*.
- Some symbols come from Latin: **Fe** (*ferrum*), **Cu** (*cuprum*), **Ag** (*argentum*), **Au** (*aurum*),
  **Pb** (*plumbum*, hence "plumber"), **Sn** (*stannum*), **Na** (*natrium*) and **K** (*kalium*).
- **Platinum** (Pt) got its name from Spanish *platina*, "little silver".
