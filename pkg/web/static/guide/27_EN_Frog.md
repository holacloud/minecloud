# Frog

![Frog](mob:frog)

A springy frog that lives along the shore. It moves in hops, swims at the surface and dives into the water as
soon as it is frightened. At night, it croaks.

## Where it lives

- On the **shores** of lakes and rivers (land with water 1 or 2 blocks away) on the **plains** and in **forests**.
- It does not live in the desert or on the rocky hills.
- In groups of **1 to 3**. By day and by night.

## Behaviour

- **It hops instead of walking:** a short jump every few seconds. It can climb a one-block step in a single hop.
- **It swims at the surface,** with its eyes above the water, kicking. When it finds a low bank, it jumps out.
- **It gets frightened:** if you come within about 3 blocks, it hops away very fast towards the water if there is
  some close by, or simply away from you.
- **It croaks at night and in the rain:** two low, buzzy notes, while its throat swells up hugely. It can be
  heard up to about 28 blocks away.
- If you hit it, it hops away with a short "croak".

## How to interact

- **Hitting it:** it has **12 health** and falls in **3 blows** bare-handed (2 with a stone sword or better, see
  [Swords](71)); otherwise it hops away from you. Defeated, there is a **15 %** chance it drops a `venom_sac`
  ![Venom sac](item:venom_sac).
- **Taming it:** not possible: it hops about as it pleases.
- **Bait:** `bait_worms` draws it in little by little (see [Baits and traps](4)).
- **Cage:** it **does** fit in the `cage_trap`.
- **Egg:** `frog_egg`, or the command `/mob frog`.

## Tips

- Looking for a lake? Listen at night: where frogs croak, there is water.
- To catch one, set the cage near the shore with `bait_worms` inside.
- If you want to see it swim, walk up slowly: it will dive into the water.

## Trivia

- There are **2 colours**: green with dark spots, and brown. The brown frog croaks in a slightly deeper voice.
- The [Fox](45) hunts it when it finds it on dry land: it crouches, freezes and pounces.
- Real frogs drink through their skin: they do not need to swallow water.
- Some frogs can jump more than 20 times their own body length.
- The vocal sac that swells when it croaks works like a loudspeaker that makes the sound louder.
