# Carbon (C)

![Carbon block](item:element_c)

Element number **6** of the periodic table.

| | |
|---|---|
| Symbol | **C** |
| Atomic number | 6 |
| Group | 14 |
| Period | 2 |
| Family | nonmetal |
| In-game name | Carbon Block |
| Id | `element_c` |

## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize C Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_c` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

Reactions the lab knows from the start:

- [Steel](120): `Fe + C → steel`

It takes part in **3** reactions you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *Shells and chalk hold calcium, carbon and plenty of oxygen.*
- *What you breathe out puts bubbles into a water flask.*
- *Sodium, hydrogen, carbon and three oxygens make bread rise.*

## In the real world

- It is the basis of **life**: every living thing is made of carbon molecules.
- **Diamond** and **graphite** are both pure carbon, with the atoms arranged differently.
- Pencil "lead" is not lead at all: it is graphite.
