# Soda water

![Soda water](item:soda_water)

A flask of water with **carbon dioxide** dissolved in it: tickling bubbles.

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *What you breathe out puts bubbles into a water flask.*

## What it is for

You need a [water flask](118) in your inventory.

| Drink (G key) | Effect |
|---|---|
| **+3** health | you get the `glass` back, with a good "fizz!" |

## Trivia

- In 1767 **Joseph Priestley** hung a bowl of water over a vat of fermenting beer and invented soda water.
- CO₂ dissolves better in **cold** water and **under pressure**; open the bottle and it escapes as bubbles.
- Solid CO₂ is **dry ice**: at −78.5 °C it turns from solid to gas without melting.

## Solution

If you would rather discover it yourself, stop reading here.

|---|---|---|
| `C + 2 O → CO₂ (soda water)` | compound | 1 `soda_water` (uses 1 `water_flask`) |
