# Steel tools

![Steel tools](item:steel_pickaxe)

Pickaxe, shovel, axe and sword forged from [steel ingots](120): a step above gold.

## How to get it

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| `steel_pickaxe`, `steel_shovel`, `steel_axe` | 3 `steel_ingot` + 1 `wood` |
| `steel_sword` | 2 `steel_ingot` + 1 `wood` |

## What it is for

| | Wood | Stone | Iron | Gold | **Steel** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Speed on its own material | ×1.45 | ×1.9 | ×2.6 | ×3.2 | **×3.6** |
| Speed on other blocks | ×1 | ×1.05 | ×1.43 | ×1.76 | **×1.98** |
| Sword damage | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | **9** |

With a steel pickaxe, stone breaks in **0.25 s** and obsidian in under a second. They never wear out. See [Pickaxes](68), [Shovels](69), [Axes](70) and [Swords](71).

## Trivia

- Damascus steel swords were famous for their wavy patterns, which came from the way the steel was forged.
- Steel is one of the most recycled materials in the world.
