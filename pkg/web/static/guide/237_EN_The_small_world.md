# The small world

![A tardigrade](mob:tardigrade)

A drop of pond water looks like nothing. Under a lens it is a crowded town: hunters, grazers, things that spin, things that jump, and bacteria too small to see at first. This part of the guide is about that town, and about how to look at it.

## Is any of this dangerous?

Not in the game: no microbe here hurts the player, and water is never dangerous. All they can do is amaze you.

## Animals you could lose on a fingertip

Two animals on the list get no page of their own, as there is less to do with them. They are worth a long look.

![A rotifer](mob:rotifer)

**Rotifers** are animals, with a brain, nerves, muscles and jaws you can watch chewing, and yet some single cells are bigger than they are. On their head they have two discs of beating hairs that look like turning wheels, which is where the name comes from: "wheel bearers". It is a trick of the eye, as nothing really turns. Some kinds can dry out and come back like [tardigrades](241), and in 2021 scientists revived rotifers from Siberian frozen ground, about 24,000 years old.

![A water flea](mob:water_flea)

The **water flea** (*Daphnia*) is a tiny crustacean, a relative of crabs, not of fleas. It rows with its big antennae, which makes it jump. Its body is so clear that you can see inside it: the gut full of green food, the eggs under its shell and a heart that beats several times a second. Scientists use it to study how clean water is.

## What is science here, and what is the game

**True:**

- All of these organisms exist, and they look and behave much as you see them here.
- Antonie van Leeuwenhoek was the first to describe tiny living things in lake water, in the 1670s, with lenses he ground himself. He called them "little animals". Few believed him until others saw them too.

**Simplified in the game:**

- The sizes on the slide are roughly true for the larger ones. The smallest are drawn a little larger, or they would be single dots.
- In the game each place holds a few species. In a real drop of pond water you might find dozens.
- A real slide does not show so many kinds so neatly: you have to search.
