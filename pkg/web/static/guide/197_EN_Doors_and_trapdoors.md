# Doors and trapdoors

![Oak door](item:oak_door)

A **door** closes a two-block-high gap and opens with a click. A **trapdoor** is a door lying down: it covers a
hole in the floor or the ceiling.

| **1** `iron_door` | **2** `iron_ore` |
| **2** `oak_trapdoor` | **3** `planks` |
| **1** `iron_trapdoor` | **2** `iron_ore` |

## Doors

- **Placing it:** right click the floor where you want it. It needs **two free spaces** of height and solid ground
  below; it takes both blocks.
- The panel goes on the side of the block **nearest to you**, facing you.
- **The hinges** go on the side (left or right) of the block you aimed at. If there is already a door next to it
  facing the same way, the new one hinges on the outside: that makes **double doors** that open to the sides.
- **Opening and closing:** right click either half. Every player sees it change, and the world saves it open or
  closed.
- Closed, the panel is thin but nobody gets through. Open, it swings on its hinge against one side of the gap: you
  walk straight through.
- **Crouching (Shift)**, a right click does not open it: it places the block you hold against it.
- Breaking either half breaks the whole door, and you get **one** door.
- The iron door opens by hand too. An [electric motor](204) touching it opens it when the motor switches on and
  closes it when it switches off (iron trapdoors too). One touching a [lift stop](218) is a lift's landing door: only the
  lift opens it.

## Trapdoors

- Against the **wall** of a block, the hinge goes on that wall. On a floor or a ceiling, the hinge goes on the side
  nearest to you.
- It sits at the **bottom** or the **top** of its space depending on where you click: on a ceiling or the upper
  half of a wall, it goes at the top.
- Closed at the bottom it is like a very thin slab: you can walk on it. A right click lifts it against its hinge,
  leaving the hole open. Watch your step on an open trapdoor!

## Tips

- **Mobs cannot open doors.** A closed door stops monsters and animals; close it behind you at night.
- Light goes through doors and trapdoors: the room does not go dark when you close them.
- An open trapdoor at the top of a [ladder](65) makes a lid for an attic.

## Trivia

- Hinges made of two leaves turning on a pin were already used in ancient Rome.
- On ships, the trapdoors that lead to the hold are called *hatches*.
