# Touch controls

On a **phone or tablet** MineCloud shows its own controls: a joystick, a few round buttons and a menu. They work
in **portrait and landscape**, and you can move, look and act at the same time with several fingers.

## Moving

- Put your **left thumb** anywhere on the lower left part of the screen: the **joystick** appears under it.
  **Up and down** walk forward and back; **left and right turn your body**. To look up and down, or turn
  quickly, drag on the screen with your right thumb.
- Push it **all the way forward** to **run**, or tap **» Run** (above the joystick) to keep running until you
  stop.
- **⇡ Jump** jumps; hold it in water to swim up. **Double tap** it in creative mode to **fly**: then hold
  **Up** to rise and **Down** to sink.
- **⇣ Sneak** turns sneaking **on and off** (it stays lit while on), so you will not fall off edges. While
  riding, it gets you **off** the horse.

## Looking and acting

The rest of the screen is the world:

| On the world | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Drag** a finger | look around |
| **Tap** | use: place the block, open a door, use the held item, feed or ride an animal, hit a mob |
| **Touch and hold still** | **mine** the block in the crosshair (a ring around it fills up); keep holding and drag to aim |

The round buttons on the right do exactly what the mouse does on a computer:

- **⛏ Mine**: hold to mine (it fills up as the block breaks) or to hit a mob.
- **✋ Use**: the right click: place, use, open.

Tap a slot of the **hotbar** at the bottom to pick that item.

## The top buttons

- **☰ Menu**: everything a keyboard opens: inventory, crafting, [guide](1), [bestiary](5), chat, blueprints,
  **Interact** (the F key: read a sign, sleep in a bed, play a sound block), eat, drop, camera view, photo
  mode, the player list, voice chat, full screen and the **settings**.
- **🎒** opens the **inventory**.
- **💬** opens the **chat** with the keyboard. Write and press **Send** (or the keyboard's send key);
  **✕** closes it. [Commands](12) such as `/guide 12` work just like on a computer.

Every panel scrolls with your finger and closes with its **✕** or **Close** button, or by tapping outside it.
When it closes, the controls come back.

## Tips

- If the view turns too fast or too slow, change **Look Sensitivity** in **☰ → Settings**.
- In **photo mode** every control hides: tap the screen to bring them back.
- With **Sneak** on, **Interact** works like Shift + F (it retunes a sound block).
