# Star chart

![Star chart](item:star_chart)

A sheet of tanned leather and a feather for a quill. This is where you draw the sky: every constellation you
watch through the [telescope](133) gets recorded, with its stars and lines.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:star_chart) | Star Chart | `star_chart` |

## How to get it

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `star_chart` (*Draw Star Chart*) | 1 `leather` + 1 `feather` |

See [Leather](76) and [Feather](75).

## How to use it

1. **Carry it in your inventory** (it does not need to be in your hand).
2. At night, watch a constellation through the [telescope](133). A **golden bar** fills on the card: keep the
   constellation in view for **a second and a half**.
3. The chat confirms it: *"Charted The Owl (1 / 8 constellations)"*.
4. To **open it**, hold it and **right click**. Escape, the *Close* button or a click outside close it.

Any star of the figure will do, including its named star.

## What it records

- The **8 constellations**: the ones you recorded are drawn with their lines and named stars; the rest are just
  grey dots and **???**.
- The **planets** Ferro and Aurum.
- The **rare events**: the comet and the red moon. Once recorded, the chart **works out how many nights** until
  they come back.
- That night's **moon phase**.

When it is complete, the chat congratulates you.

## Good to know

- Your records are kept **in your browser**: if you lose the chart, a new one keeps your notes.
- Without a chart, the telescope reminds you: *"Carry a star chart to record it"*.
- Some constellations **never set** (The Owl, The Hive); others only show on some nights. Completing the chart
  takes several nights.

## Trivia

- The oldest known star charts were painted thousands of years ago, in caves and on clay tablets.
- Sailors steered by the stars for centuries, with charts like this one and a sextant.
