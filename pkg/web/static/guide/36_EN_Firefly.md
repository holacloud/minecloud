# Firefly

![Firefly](mob:firefly)

At nightfall, **clouds of tiny lights** appear over the grass and the ponds, blinking on and off. These are the
fireflies: harmless, shy and very pretty.

## Where it lives

- On the **plains** and in **forests**, preferably on the **shore of a pond or river**.
- Each cloud holds **10 to 16 fireflies**.
- There are at most **4 clouds** at a time.
- They only glow at night, **from 19:00 to 5:30**. By day they are there, but you cannot see them.

## Behaviour

- The cloud **drifts slowly** around its spot, a block or two above the grass or the water.
- Each firefly **blinks on its own rhythm**, fading softly on and off.
- They are **shy**: if you step into the cloud, they move away from your body.
- Hit them and they **scatter** in a flash: they all light up at once and spread out, with a high tinkle.
- In **Ultra** mode their light has a halo (bloom).

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit the cloud | They cannot be defeated. They scatter, and there is a **30 %** chance of ![glow dust](item:glow_dust) `glow_dust` (20 s wait). |
| Net (`bug_net`) | You catch **4 fireflies** in a ![jar](item:firefly_jar) `firefly_jar`. When 7 or fewer are left, the cloud vanishes. By day they cannot be caught ("Fireflies only come out at night"). |
| Jar on the ground | Right click the ground: it is a **lamp** that blinks. Right click it with the net: you set them free and they become a cloud again. See [Firefly jar](97). |
| Tame or bait | No. |
| Egg | `firefly_egg` |
| Command | `/mob firefly` (summoned ones always glow, even by day, so you can see them). |

## Tips

- **Torches without coal:** `glow_dust` + `wood` = 4 `torch`.
- A big cloud fills **two or three jars** before it runs out.
- Jars that are set down are kept when the page reloads: perfect to light your house with a magical touch.

## Trivia

- The light of real fireflies is **cold**: nearly all the energy becomes light, not heat. They make it with a
  substance called **luciferin**.
- They blink to **find a mate**: each species has its own code of flashes.
- In some forests of Asia and America, thousands of fireflies **blink at the same time**, in sync.
- The game's [bats](38) chase them at night.
