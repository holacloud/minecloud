# The food chain

In MineCloud the animals do not just stroll about: some of them **hunt** others. Stand still and watch, and you
will see chases, ambushes and dashes for the burrow.

## Who hunts whom

| Hunter | Prey | How |
|---|---|---|
| ![Fox](mob:fox) [Fox](45) | ![Rabbit](mob:rabbit) rabbit, ![Frog](mob:frog) frog | On dry land. It crouches, creeps closer, freezes with its rump wiggling and pounces in an arc. Then it lies down to eat. |
| ![Owl](mob:owl) [Owl](46) | ![Bat](mob:bat) bat, ![Rabbit](mob:rabbit) rabbit | At night. Bats on the wing and rabbits in the open. It locks its gaze, bobs its head and swoops. |
| ![Eagle](mob:eagle) [Eagle](33) | ![Fish](mob:fish) fish | Fish less than 1.6 blocks under the surface and within 28 blocks. It dives and carries them off in its talons. |

Prey caught by animals **leaves no loot**: there is only loot when you defeat a creature yourself. See
[Mob resources](2).

## The ones that do not hunt

- ![Vulture](mob:vulture) **[Vulture](40)**: a scavenger. Whenever a creature is **defeated** nearby, the
  vultures that can see it come over, circle low and land to peck. To them it does not matter who defeated it:
  you, a fox or an owl. The fish carried off by an eagle, though, vanishes without anyone noticing.
  And if your health drops to **30 %** or less, the vultures start circling **over you**…
- ![Bee](mob:bee) **[Bees](35)**: they fly from the hive to the **flowers** nearby, sip for a while and come
  back. At night and in the rain they stay inside.
- ![Bat](mob:bat) **[Bats](38)**: at night they chase the clouds of ![Firefly](mob:firefly)
  **[fireflies](36)** near their colony.

## How prey hides

- **[Rabbit](44)**: lives with its family by a **burrow**. When a fox, an owl or a low-flying eagle shows up,
  one of them thumps an alarm and they all zig-zag to the hole. Inside, no hunter can see them. A while later,
  a head peeks out.
- **[Frog](27)**: jumps into the water. Foxes only catch frogs on dry land.
- **Bat**: hanging under its roof by day, no owl will touch it; it is only prey while flying.
- **[Fish](26)**: when the eagle snatches one, the whole school scatters.
- **[Lizard](30)**: drops its **tail**, which keeps wriggling on the ground while it runs away.
- **[Crab](39)** and **[turtle](28)**: one digs into the sand, the other hides in its shell.

## And you

You are part of the chain too: the [snake](42) waits buried for you, the [scorpion](31) chases you at night
and the [wandering shadow](37) stalks you in the dark. But at the very top of the chain stand you: only your
blows leave loot.
