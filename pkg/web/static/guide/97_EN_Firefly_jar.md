# Firefly jar

![Firefly jar](item:firefly_jar)

A glass jar with a wooden lid and a handful of **live fireflies** inside, blinking and glowing. It is a decorative
lamp for your nights.

## How to get it

**It is not crafted.** You get it by catching ![Fireflies](mob:firefly) [fireflies](36) with the
[bug net](95):

1. Craft a net (1 `wood` + 2 `white_wool`).
2. **At night**, get close to a firefly cloud, 4.5 blocks away at most.
3. Right click with the net: "Caught with the net: Firefly Jar".

Each sweep takes 4 fireflies out of the cloud. A cloud holds 10 to 16, so it gives **2 to 4 jars** before it is gone.
By day there are no fireflies to catch.

## How to use it

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| Right click the ground with the jar in your hand | You **set it** on the block. It sounds like glass. |
| Right click the placed jar | You **pick it up** into your inventory. |
| Right click the jar with the **net** | You **set the fireflies free**: the jar disappears and a cloud flies again on that spot. |

It goes on top of a block, out of the water, and not right next to another jar, a cage or a bait.

## How it looks

- **6 fireflies** flutter inside, each with its own blinking rhythm, and a yellow-green halo surrounds the jar.
- In **Ultra** mode, the fireflies shine so bright that the *bloom* effect gives them a glow.
- **They are kept:** placed jars are still there when the page reloads. They only exist in your browser.

## Tips

- Decorate paths, gardens and your front door: at night they can be seen from afar.
- **It does not scare wandering shadows:** its glow is a firefly's, not a fire's. To keep them away you need a real
  [torch](62).
- If you tire of a jar, set the fireflies free: they form a cloud again that you can catch once more.

## Trivia

- Catching fireflies in jars is a classic summer game in many countries; in Japan "firefly hunting" (*hotaru-gari*) is
  a whole tradition.
- Real fireflies do badly when shut in: the nice thing is to watch them a while and **let them go** the same night.
  In the game they last forever, but freeing them has its charm too.
- Each firefly species has its own flash code to find a mate.
