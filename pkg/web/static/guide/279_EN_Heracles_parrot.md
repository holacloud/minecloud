# Heracles parrot

![Heracles parrot](mob:heracles)

**The biggest parrot there has ever been**: nearly a metre tall, with a beak as deep as its face, and far too
heavy to fly. It is an ancient relative of the [macaw](23). It minds its own business, but **if you hit it,
it opens its beak and bites**. It is an [extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself, it has to be
brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Look for its fossil.** It lies far down, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:fossil_rock_rare)
   `fossil_rock_rare`: a dark rock with a three-toed footprint. The page on [fossils](262) says how deep to dig.
2. **Break the block** with a [pickaxe](68). Which animal is inside depends on the place: if it was a
   Heracles parrot, you take ![](item:fossil_heracles) `fossil_heracles` with you.
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45 seconds**. It is
   a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived Heracles parrot: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back.

## Behaviour

- **It strolls slowly** near its place, on the ground, and looks about. It never flies: its wings are too
  short to lift it.
- It is **neutral**: it attacks nobody who has not attacked it.
- **If you hit it, it warns you.** It screeches, turns on you and **opens its wings and its beak** for 0.6 seconds.
- Then **it bites**, if you are still within 1.9 blocks of it: **2 damage**. After that it stands still for
  a little over a second.
- It only bites whoever hit it, and only that player sees the warning.

There are **2 colours**: moss green with a yellowish belly, and brown with a red belly.

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit it | **16 health**: 4 hits bare-handed or with a wooden [sword](71), 3 with a stone or an iron one, 2 with a golden one. Every hit that does not defeat it makes it warn, unless it was warning already, and has a **40 %** chance of dropping 1 ![feather](item:feather) `feather` (at most one every 8 seconds). |
| Defeat it | It leaves **1 to 3** `feather`. |

## Tips

- **Step back as soon as it opens its beak.** From more than 1.9 blocks away the bite does not reach you.
- With a [shield](252) in your left hand, and facing it, the bite does half the damage.
- Leave it alone and it is a calm neighbour: it never starts anything.
- Put it next to a macaw: the macaw flutters over your head; the Heracles parrot comes up to your waist and
  never leaves the ground.
- In the [Bestiary](5), its sheet and the macaw's show the two together under the title **Evolution**.

## The real Heracles parrot

- **When and where.** It lived between about **19 and 16 million years ago** in the south of **New Zealand**,
  by a great lake with warm forest all around it, full of birds.
- **Its name.** Scientists call it *Heracles inexpectatus*: Heracles, after the strongest hero of the Greek
  myths, and "unexpected", because nobody expected a parrot like this. Its leg bones were found in 2008 and
  were first taken for those of an eagle. It was given its name in 2019.
- **Size.** It stood nearly **1 metre** tall and weighed about **7 kilos**: twice as much as the kakapo, the
  heaviest parrot alive today.
- **It did not fly.** That is what scientists think, from its weight. Like the kakapo, it would have walked
  and climbed.
- **What it ate.** Nobody knows for sure. With such a beak it could crack almost any fruit or seed, and some
  scientists think it may have eaten other animals too.
- **Islands make giants.** New Zealand had no mammals that hunt on the ground, so birds took their place:
  many grew big and stopped flying. The moa and the [dodo](265) are two more.
- **Its relatives.** The parrots of New Zealand: the kakapo, the kea and the kaka. Macaws are more distant
  cousins, from America; in the game the macaw stands for every parrot.
- **Why it died out.** Nobody knows. Long after its time New Zealand grew colder and its warm forests shrank.
- **Nobody knows its colour.** Only a few bones of it are known: the green of the game is that of the kakapo.
