# Night life

Night in MineCloud runs from **18:00 to 6:00**: half of the day. When the sun goes down, some animals go to
sleep and others come out. See [Day, night and weather](8).

## The ones that hide

- **[Rabbits](44)**: at dusk they turn in to their burrow. At dawn they come out livelier than ever.
- **[Bees](35)**: they shelter inside the hive (in the rain too).
- **Owls** sleep by day; **bats** hang under their roof. At night the roles swap.

## The ones that wake up

![Owl](mob:owl)

- **[Owls](46)**: they open their eyes, which glow faintly. They turn their head to follow you, hoot
  ("hoo, hoo-hoo") and nearby owls answer. They hunt bats and rabbits. In the rain they hoot less and hunt more
  slowly.
- **[Bats](38)**: they wake one by one and flit about in jerks, clicking to find their way. They chase
  fireflies. At dawn they go back to their roof.
- **[Fireflies](36)**: clouds of little lights blinking over the grass and ponds, from dusk to dawn.
- **[Foxes](45)**: their eyes shine, they call to each other with high yips and **steal** items lying on
  the ground, as long as you are not within 4.5 blocks. They run off with them in their mouth and, at dawn,
  stash them somewhere. Strike the fox and it drops what it carries.
- **[Frogs](27)**: they croak at night, and also when it rains.

## The scary ones

![Wandering shadow](mob:shadow)

- **[Wandering shadow](37)**: a legend. Very, very rarely, in the dead of night and under a clear sky, it
  rises 20–30 blocks away from those who wander far from home and from torches, and follows them. An omen
  comes first. Light undoes it.
- **[Wisp](41)**: some nights, a pale flame floating in the distance that tries to lead you into deep water or over
  the edge of a cliff. It never attacks: it only deceives.
- **[Scorpion](31)**: by day it only chases you if you pass within 3.5 blocks; **at night, from
  8 blocks**.

## Light in the night

**Torches** shine brighter at night: by day they glow at 60 % of their strength, at night at 100 %. A torch in
your hand keeps the shadows away. See [Light and darkness](105) and [Torch](62).

> Tip: put your things away before going out at night. A fox never forgives an item left on the ground.
