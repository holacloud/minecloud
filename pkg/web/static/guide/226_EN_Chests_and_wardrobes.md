# Chests and wardrobes

![Chest](item:chest)

These three **really store items**, and keep them for as long as the world lasts.

| | In-game name | Id | Slots | Made from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ![](item:barrel) | Barrel | `barrel` | 18 | 4 oak planks, 1 iron ore |
| ![](item:chest) | Chest | `chest` | 27 | 6 oak planks, 1 iron ore |
| ![](item:wardrobe) | Wardrobe | `wardrobe` | 45 | 8 dark oak planks, 1 iron ore |

Each slot holds up to 999 of one item. The wardrobe stands two blocks tall and needs the room.

## How to use them

**Right click** opens the panel, with what is inside at the top and your items below. Click or tap an item to move it. Above them you choose how much moves with each click: **x1**, **x10** or **All**. To close it, press **E** or **Esc**, click outside, or use its **✕**.

- **What is inside belongs to the world, not to you.** Everyone playing sees the same contents, and the moves of another player show up while you watch.
- **Breaking one drops everything it held**, for whoever broke it, so be careful with a full wardrobe. A pickaxe or a [robot](193), it is all the same: it lands on the ground, not in the next chest.
- A [motor ram](217) cannot move them, and [blueprints](191) copy the box but never what is in it.

## Also for books, but not for keeping things

| | In-game name | Id | Made from |
|---|---|---|---|
| ![](item:bookshelf) | Bookshelf | `bookshelf` | 4 oak planks, 2 white wool |
| ![](item:wall_shelf) | Wall Shelf | `wall_shelf` | 2 oak planks, 1 white wool (makes 2) |

The bookshelf is a full block, and the wall shelf hangs on the wall you click. Both are decoration: they hold nothing.
