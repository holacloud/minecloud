# Wood and leaves

![Oak wood](item:wood)

Trees give **logs** (wood), which are sawn into **planks**, and **leaves**. There are **nine kinds of tree**,
each with its own wood, planks and leaves.

In the game they are called *Oak Wood*, *Spruce Planks*, *Cherry Leaves* and so on. Cherry leaves are **pink** and
willow leaves a lighter green.

## Where they grow

| Biome | Trees |
|---|---|
| Forest | all nine kinds, in groves of the same species |
| Plains | oak, birch, acacia and maple, scattered |
| Rocky | spruce only |
| Desert | none |

Each species has its own outline: spruce is a **cone**, acacia has a **flat** crown, willow lets its branches
**hang**, and jungle and dark oak trees have very wide crowns.

## How to get them

| Block | Break (bare hand) | Drops |
|---|---|---|
| Logs | 0.8 s | the same log |
| Planks | 0.6 s | the same planks |
| Leaves | 0.2 s | the same leaves; **8 %** a random [sapling](55), otherwise **3.5 %** a random [egg](74) |

You start with **20 `wood`, 20 `planks` and 16 `leaves`** (oak).

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 4 planks of its kind | **1** log |
| 1 [sapling](55) of its kind | **2** leaves of its kind + 1 `dirt` |

## What they are for

**Oak** wood and leaves are what nearly every recipe asks for:

| Recipe | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 8 `ladder` | 1 `wood` |
| 4 `torch` | 1 `wood` + 1 `coal_ore` (or `glow_dust`) |
| 1 `sign` | 2 `planks` + 1 `wood` |
| 1 `bed` | 3 `planks` + 2 `leaves` + 1 `wood` |
| 1 `sound_block` | 2 `planks` + 1 `cobblestone` + 1 `coal_ore` |
| 4 coloured wool (`*_wool`) | 2 `leaves` + 1 `flower_red` |
| Every tool | 1 `wood` (+ 3 `planks` for wooden ones) |
| 1 `leather` | 2 `fur` + 1 `wood` |
| 1 `fishing_rod` / 1 `bug_net` / 1 `cage_trap` | 2 `wood` + … / 1 `wood` + … / 4 `planks` + … |

The other eight woods are mostly for **building with colour**. All planks can be cut into [slabs](56).

**Leaves as food:** giraffes eat acacia, oak, jungle and birch leaves; sheep and goats, oak; macaws, cherry (see
[Taming and breeding](3)).

## Tips

- An [axe](70) speeds up logs and planks; a [hoe](72) or the [shears](73), leaves.
- Leaves are solid but let light through: good for garden roofs.
- Owls perch on top of tree crowns: cut the leaves away and the owl flies off.

## Trivia

- **Birch** bark is white because of betulin, which reflects sunlight.
- It takes about 40 litres of **maple** sap to make 1 litre of maple syrup.
- **Willow** bark gave us salicin, the ancestor of aspirin.
- **Spruce** is used for the tops of violins and guitars because it resonates so well.
- In Japan, watching the **cherry** trees bloom is a festival: *hanami*.
