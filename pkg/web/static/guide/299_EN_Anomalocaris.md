# Anomalocaris

![Anomalocaris](mob:anomalocaris)

The **great hunter of the first seas full of animals**, more than 500 million years ago: a flat body with a
row of **swimming flaps** along each side, **two spiny arms** at the front to grab its prey, **eyes on
stalks**, a round mouth underneath and a fan at its tail. It is **1 block long**, and in its time that was
enormous. It nips whoever swims near it, and it **chases the trilobites** of its water. It is an
[extinct animal](261): in MineCloud you only see one if you find its fossil and bring it back to life.

## What it was

Not a fish, nor a shrimp, nor a squid: an animal of a group of its own, the **radiodonts**. They were early
relatives of the animals with jointed legs, the **arthropods**: insects, spiders, crabs and the
[trilobites](266) it lived with.

## How to get one

1. **Its fossil.** It lies far down, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:fossil_rock_rare)
   `fossil_rock_rare`: a dark rock with a three-toed footprint. Break it with a [pickaxe](68): which animal is
   inside depends on the place, and it may be a ![](item:fossil_anomalocaris) `fossil_anomalocaris`. See
   [Fossils](262).
2. **Water.** It is a sea animal: the revival machine has to stand next to **16 blocks of water** joined
   together, or more: a pond that you dig and fill with a [bucket](111) is enough.
3. **The [revival machine](264).** With its fossil inside it takes 45 seconds. The anomalocaris appears in
   the water.

The server keeps a revived anomalocaris: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back.

## The pool it needs

- It is **1 block long** and a little more than half a block across its flaps. A pool of about 5 × 5 blocks
  is plenty; in one of 3 × 3 it turns about a lot.
- Water **1 block deep** is enough; with 2 it goes up and down.
- The walls may be [glass](51): you can watch it from outside, dry and safe.

## Behaviour

- **It swims a little above the bed**, at under 1 block a second, its flaps beating in a **wave** from the
  front to the back, as the real one swam. Its spiny arms move as it goes.
- **It is hostile, in the water.** If you are in the water within **6 blocks** of it, it comes for you at
  **1.4 blocks a second**, and nips: **1 damage**, at most once every 1.2 seconds. After each nip it swims on
  past you for a moment, and turns back.
- **On the shore you are safe.** It never leaves the water, and it does not go for a player who is out of it.
- **The trilobites.** With nobody in the water near it, it goes after the [trilobites](266) of its water,
  up to 5 blocks away, and snaps at them. The trilobite gets away with a fright, or rolls into a ball. It
  does not hurt them, and after a snap it leaves them alone for a while.
- **It does not go further** than about 4 blocks from where it was revived, unless it is after somebody:
  then it follows up to about 10 blocks from there, and turns back.
- **If its water goes away**, it lies on the ground, waiting. It does not die: as soon as the water is back,
  it swims again.

There are **2 colours**: the red of most drawings of it, and a sandy brown. Nobody knows its real colour.

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit it | It shoots off away from you, at more than twice its speed, for a couple of seconds. Then, if you are still in the water, it comes back. |
| Defeat it | **8 health**: 2 hits bare-handed or with a wooden, stone or iron [sword](71), 1 with a golden one. It leaves nothing. |
| Tame it | You can't. |
| Bait | None interests it. |
| Cage | It does not go in a cage: it lives in the water. |
| Egg | `anomalocaris_egg`: use it next to water. |
| Command | `/mob anomalocaris`: it appears in the nearest water; if there is none near, the game tells you. |

## Tips

- Put it in a tank with a few [trilobites](266) and watch it hunt, from outside the water.
- Its nip is weak, but it does not give up: get out of the water and it forgets you.
- Look at it from above: the flaps, the eyes on their stalks and the fan of its tail. From below you see its
  round mouth and the spines of its arms.

## The real science

- **When and where.** In the **Cambrian**, about **520 to 500 million years ago**, when the seas first filled
  with animals of many shapes. The best fossils come from the Burgess Shale, in the mountains of Canada;
  others come from China and Australia.
- **Its name** means "strange shrimp". Its pieces were first taken for three different animals: an arm for
  the body of a shrimp, the mouth for a jellyfish and the body for a sponge. Only much later did scientists
  see that they were one animal.
- **How big was it?** Most were about **half a metre** long, and the biggest perhaps up to **a metre**. Next to
  the animals of its time, mostly a few centimetres long, it was a giant.
- **Its eyes.** Fossil eyes found in Australia had more than **16,000 lenses** each, made like the eyes of a
  fly or a dragonfly. It must have seen very well.
- **What it ate.** For a long time it was thought to crunch trilobites. Studies of its mouth say it was too
  soft for their hard shells, so it probably caught softer animals with its arms. In the game it only chases
  trilobites and snaps at them.
- **How it swam.** Its flaps beat one after another, in a wave along its body, as a ray's fins do today.
- **Why it died out.** Nobody knows. Its relatives, the radiodonts, lasted about a hundred million years more,
  and then vanished too.
