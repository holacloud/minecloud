# Lizard

![Lizard](mob:lizard)

A sun-loving reptile that basks on the stones, does push-ups and dashes off like lightning. If you hit it, it
drops its tail to escape.

## Where it lives

- **Desert** and **rocky hills**.
- In groups of **1 to 2**. By day and by night.

## Behaviour

- **It basks:** it keeps still for a while, turning its head and slowly swaying its tail.
- **It runs in bursts:** suddenly it dashes a very short way, its body and tail bending in an S, and stops again.
- **It does push-ups:** sometimes it bobs the front of its body up and down several times in a row.
- **It flees:** if you come within 4 blocks, it zig-zags away at full speed.
- It does not wander far from its spot: if it gets more than 8 blocks away, it comes back.

## How to interact

- **Hitting it:** it cannot be defeated. It **drops its tail**, which lies **wriggling on the ground** for a few
  seconds, and runs off. Its tail grows back in **1 minute**.
- **Scales:** each hit has a **35 %** chance of dropping a `scale` ![Scale](item:scale) (at most one every 10
  seconds per lizard).
- **Taming it:** not possible: it goes its own way in a hurry.
- **Bait:** `bait_worms` draws it in little by little (see [Baits and traps](4)).
- **Cage:** it **does** fit in the `cage_trap`.
- **Egg:** `lizard_egg`, or the command `/mob lizard`.

## Tips

- 6 `scale` and 2 `leather` make `scale_mail`, which takes 2 off every creature attack (see [Scale](83) and
  [Scale mail](91)).
- To get scales, use your reach: you can hit a mob from about 5 blocks away, and the lizard does not run until
  you are within 4. Aim and strike before getting any closer.
- Even without its tail, it still drops scales when hit.

## Trivia

- There are **2 colours**: sandy with brown bands, and greyish green with dark bands.
- Dropping the tail is called **autotomy**. Many real lizards do it: the tail keeps moving to distract the
  predator while the lizard gets away.
- The push-ups of real lizards are a message: they mark territory or court a mate.
- Lizards are cold-blooded: they bask to warm up so they can move fast.
