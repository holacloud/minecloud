# History of MineCloud

Every world has an origin. MineCloud's fits into a few very busy days spread across 2026.

## 2 March: the first stone

On **2 March 2026** MineCloud is born: a world of blocks in the browser and, that same day, the first big idea:
**syncing games** so people can play together.

## 15 May: the longest day

After two quiet months comes **15 May**, the most frantic day in its history: over **a hundred changes** in a
single day. That day brought almost everything that now feels like it was always there:

- The player's **hand**, **building**, the **inventory** and **crafting**.
- **RTX mode**, with real textures.
- The **day and night cycle**, **rain** and **water** you can swim in.
- **Sounds**, every one of them made up by the game itself (see [Sounds of the world](103)).
- **Chat**, **commands**, **signs**, **beds** and **proximity voice chat**.
- The **torch** and its light, and **dark caves**.
- **Health** and respawning.
- The first mobs: ![Sheep](mob:sheep) [sheep](17), ![Duck](mob:duck) [duck](18), ![Pig](mob:pig) [pig](19),
  ![Spider](mob:spider) [spider](20) and ![Cave monster](mob:cave_monster) [cave monster](21).

## 16 May – 7 June: the first friends

The next day brought the ![Giraffe](mob:giraffe) [giraffe](22), the ![Macaw](mob:macaw) [macaw](23) and the
![Dog](mob:dog) [dog](24), along with [sound blocks](66), [spray paint](67) and [ladders](65). On **2 June**
the ![Cat](mob:cat) [cat](25) showed up, and in the days after came the player appearance editor, **tools**,
**mob eggs** and **trees** of many woods.

Then… almost **five months of calm**.

## 28 October: the world is remade

The comeback was a big one:

- Characters, textures and terrain **remade**.
- Terrain began to be stored in columns and drawn only by its visible faces, with **corner shading**.
- **Torch light** began to be worked out block by block.
- The **terrain viewer** was born, able to show kilometres of world.
- The terrain generator was **translated into Go** and moved to the server. See [How the world is born](99).
- **Ultra mode** arrived, on top of RTX: photoreal water, lifelike animals and waves. See
  [Graphics modes](14).

## 29 October: the great migration

The **species registry** was created, the list that says what each mob is, and through that door eleven
species walked in at once: ![Fish](mob:fish) [fish](26), ![Frog](mob:frog) [frog](27),
![Turtle](mob:turtle) [turtle](28), ![Camel](mob:camel) [camel](29), ![Lizard](mob:lizard) [lizard](30),
![Scorpion](mob:scorpion) [scorpion](31), ![Goat](mob:goat) [goat](32), ![Eagle](mob:eagle) [eagle](33),
![Horse](mob:horse) [horse](34), ![Bee](mob:bee) [bee](35) and ![Firefly](mob:firefly) [firefly](36).

The horse brought something new: you can **ride** it, and other players see you gallop. See [Riding](7).

## 30 October: the night fills up

The creatures of the night and of the odd corners arrived: ![Shadow](mob:shadow) [wandering shadow](37),
![Bat](mob:bat) [bat](38), ![Crab](mob:crab) [crab](39), ![Vulture](mob:vulture) [vulture](40),
![Wisp](mob:wisp) [wisp](41), ![Snake](mob:snake) [snake](42), ![Golem](mob:storm_golem)
[storm golem](43), ![Rabbit](mob:rabbit) [rabbit](44), ![Fox](mob:fox) [fox](45), ![Owl](mob:owl) [owl](46)
and ![Mole](mob:mole) [giant mole](47).

So that so many creatures could fit without making a mess, the game learned to take **extensions**: each mob,
item or mechanic can live in its own file.

And on the same day: [mob resources](2), [taming and breeding](3), [baits and traps](4) and the
[bestiary](5), which records all 31 species.

## And then, this book

The last piece is the one in your hands: the **guide**, so that nobody gets lost in a world that already has
31 species and well over a hundred kinds of
block. Whatever comes next will be written on new pages.
