# Beneath your feet

> You found a hidden page. It was buried under a heap of freshly turned earth. Speak softly: someone is
> listening from below.

![Giant mole](mob:mole)

The **giant mole** can barely see. It does not need to: it **feels**. Every heavy step you take on the meadow
reaches it through the earth like a drum. This is what it hears, and how to keep it from hearing you.

## What it feels

Three kinds of **vibration** reach the mole, each up to a certain distance:

| What you do | How far it feels it |
|---|---|
| **Sprinting** (or riding a horse) | **16 blocks** |
| **Landing** after more than a third of a second in the air | **14 blocks** |
| **Jumping** | **12 blocks** |

It also only feels what happens within **6 blocks of height** from its tunnel.

**Walking makes no noise:** only going faster than about 5 blocks per second counts, and you walk at 4.5. The
trouble is sprinting… or riding, since a moving horse always goes faster than 5.

## How to go unnoticed

- **Sneak.** While sneaking you make no vibration at all: you can jump and land without it noticing.
- **Swim.** It does not feel you in the water either.
- The first time it notices you, the chat warns: *"The ground rumbles... something felt your footsteps (sneak
  to go unnoticed)"*.

## What it cannot get through

The mole only digs through soft ground: **grass, dirt and sand**. It cannot travel under:

- **Stone**, cobblestone or any rock.
- **Planks**, wood or brick.
- **Water**.

A **path of stone or planks** is a border the mole does not cross. A house with a plank floor is a perfect
refuge.

## When it comes out

- The bulge of earth races to where it felt the thump, **rumbles** louder and **shakes** for a moment.
- Then the mole **bursts out**. If you are still on top (within a block and a half), it bowls you over:
  **2** health and a shove.
- **Trick:** as soon as the bulge stops and shakes, **step aside**.
- After coming out it stays calm for about 10 seconds, sniffing the air.

## Striking it

- Underground **it cannot be struck**: all you see is the bulge.
- Above ground it has health for **3 blows**. Strike it and it squeaks and **dives** to flee. For about
  12 seconds it ignores your footsteps.
- Defeated, it leaves **1–2 fur**. See [Fur](77).

## It does not change the world

The mounds and clods it leaves **are not blocks**: they settle and sink away on their own after a while. Your
meadow stays as it was.

> Some swear that giant moles write. That their tunnels, seen from very high up, spell letters. Nobody has
> climbed high enough to check.
