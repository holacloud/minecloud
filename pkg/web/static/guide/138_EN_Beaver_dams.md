# Beaver dams

![Beaver](mob:beaver)

[Beavers](137) are the only creatures that **change the world**. The trees they fell and the dams they build are
ordinary blocks, broken and placed like yours, so **every player sees them**. This page explains how they choose,
and how to keep them away from what you care about.

## Which trees they fell

A beaver only gnaws a tree when **all** of these hold:

- It is **small**: a trunk of **2 to 5 blocks**, of any kind of wood, standing on grass or dirt, with leaves on
  top.
- It stands **alone**: no other wood right next to the trunk (no big trees, no branches, no log walls).
- It is near the water: within **12 blocks** of the beaver's river or lake, and within 10 blocks of the beaver.
- **Nothing built nearby:** within **3 blocks** around the tree (from one block below its foot up to 3 above its
  top) there is only natural terrain: grass, dirt, stone, sand, water, ores, cactus, plants, trunks and leaves.
  A single plank, brick, glass, torch, slab, wool, sign, farmland, cobblestone… and the tree is safe. Logs lying
  side by side (a log cabin) also count as a build.
- Each beaver fells **one tree every 3–4 minutes** at most, and all the beavers together **one a minute**.
- It only fells a tree when it has a building site that needs wood.

It checks everything again just before the tree falls. The trunk drops **no items**: the beaver keeps up to 4 logs
to build with. The leaves of that tree that no other trunk holds up (at most 80) fall a few at a time.

> **To protect a tree,** put any non-natural block within 3 blocks of it: a torch or a slab is enough.

## Dams

The beaver looks for a **narrow stretch of shallow water** near where it lives:

- **1 to 3 blocks wide**, with solid ground at both ends.
- **1 block deep** (water with ground right below), with water going on at both sides (a stream, not a puddle).
- With nothing built within 3 blocks, and no other beaver site within 10 blocks.

Across it, it lays **one log in the water** and **one block of mud (`dirt`) on top** for each block of width: at
most 6 blocks.

## Lodges

Two or three blocks up- or downstream from the dam (or, on a lake shore with no narrow stretch, right next to the
bank) it builds a **lodge**: **walls of logs** around one water block, one block above the water, and a **roof of
mud**. The water under the walls is the **underwater entrance**: when frightened, a beaver dives, swims in and
surfaces in the dry chamber inside, out of sight.

## Limits

- Each beaver places **12 blocks at most** (a dam and a lodge together take 11 at most).
- The family shares one site; sites are remembered in your browser, so beavers that come back carry on with the
  same dam instead of starting another.
- It never places a block where you are standing.
- **Water in MineCloud does not flow**, so the dam does not raise the water behind it: it is a home and a sign
  that beavers live here.
- Beavers live in each player's game (mobs are not shared), but the blocks they break and place are shared like
  anyone else's.

## Good to know

- You can **mine** a dam or a lodge like any other blocks, and get `wood` and `dirt`.
- A lodge is a good place to look for beavers: walk up to the water and you may hear a tail slap from inside.
