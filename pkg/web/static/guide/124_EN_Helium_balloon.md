# Helium balloon

![Helium balloon](item:helium_balloon)

A pink balloon full of **helium**. While you hold it you fall slowly, as if with a parachute.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:helium_balloon) | Helium Balloon | `helium_balloon` |

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *This gas combines with nothing, but alone in the flask it floats away.*

## What it is for

- Keep it **selected in your hand**: when you fall, you drift down at 2.2 blocks per second at most and **take no damage** when you land.
- In another slot it does nothing. It never runs out. The G key reminds you what it does.
- It does not slow you down while riding.

## Trivia

- A helium balloon rises because helium weighs about **seven times less** than the air it pushes aside.
- Your voice sounds squeaky with helium because sound travels almost three times faster in it. But **do not breathe it**: it takes the place of oxygen.
- Earth's helium comes from natural gas deposits and, once released, escapes into space.
