# Cave monster

![Cave monster](mob:cave_monster)

A huge bluish brute with pale horns and eyes that glow a soft green. It walks on two legs and has very long arms.
It is the toughest of the game's first creatures.

## Where it lives

- **Rocky hills**, always alone. Despite its name, it appears on the surface, among the rocks.
- It is dangerous **by day and by night**.

## Behaviour

- While it has not noticed you, it strolls slowly.
- If you are within **10 blocks**, it follows you at a slow pace...
- ...but it **stops about 4 blocks** away and stands there staring, as if keeping watch.
- It only strikes if you get very close (just over a block).
- It swings its long arms as it walks.

## How to interact

- **Its attack:** **4 damage** per blow, the strongest of the mobs of its time. With a `warm_coat` it drops to 3,
  and with `scale_mail` to 2.
- **Defeating it:** **4 hits**. After the first one its health bar appears.
- **Loot when defeated:**

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| ![Leather](item:leather) `leather` | 1 to 3 |
| ![Glow dust](item:glow_dust) `glow_dust` | 1 (50 %) |

- **Taming it:** not possible.
- **Bait:** it comes to `bait_meat` when it is not chasing you (see [Baits and traps](4)).
- **Cage:** it does **not** fit: it is too big.
- **Egg:** `cave_monster_egg`, or the command `/mob cave_monster`.

## Tips

- Since it stops 4 blocks away, you choose when to fight: step in, strike and step back.
- **Your dog defends you:** if a [Dog](24) that is your friend gets within 2 blocks of a cave monster, it bites
  it (4 damage less than every second) until it is gone. The message "Your dog defended you from the cave
  monster" appears. Mind you: if the dog defeats it, there is no loot.
- Its `leather` is the base of the `warm_coat` (see [Leather](76)), and `glow_dust` makes 4 torches from a single
  `wood`.

## Trivia

- It is one of the game's first five mobs, along with the sheep, the duck, the pig and the spider.
- The sound it makes when hit is one of the deepest of all the mobs.
- Real cave animals (like the olm, a European amphibian) tend to be pale and almost blind: this one, instead,
  has glowing eyes.
- It is one of the few mobs with arms: it walks on two legs, like a person.
