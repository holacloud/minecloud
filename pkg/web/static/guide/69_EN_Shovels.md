# Shovels

![Iron shovel](item:iron_shovel)

For digging. With a shovel, **dirt**, **sand** and the **grass block** vanish in a flash.

| | Shovel | Id | Speed on its material |
|---|---|---|---|
| ![](item:wood_shovel) | Wood Shovel | `wood_shovel` | ×1.45 |
| ![](item:stone_shovel) | Stone Shovel | `stone_shovel` | ×1.9 |
| ![](item:iron_shovel) | Iron Shovel | `iron_shovel` | ×2.6 |
| ![](item:gold_shovel) | Gold Shovel | `gold_shovel` | **×3.2** |

## How to get them

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| `wood_shovel` | 3 `planks` + 1 `wood` |
| `stone_shovel` | 3 `cobblestone` + 1 `wood` |
| `iron_shovel` | 3 `iron_ore` + 1 `wood` |
| `gold_shovel` | 3 `gold_ore` + 1 `wood` |

## How to use them

Select it and dig with a left click. **It never wears out.** It speeds up only three blocks: `dirt`, `sand` and
`grass`.

| Block | Bare hand | Wood | Stone | Iron | Gold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grass | 0.35 s | 0.24 s | 0.18 s | 0.13 s | 0.11 s |
| Sand | 0.4 s | 0.28 s | 0.21 s | 0.15 s | 0.12 s |
| Dirt | 0.45 s | 0.31 s | 0.24 s | 0.17 s | 0.14 s |

**On other blocks** it helps less: ×1 for wood, ×1.05 for stone, ×1.43 for iron and ×1.76 for gold.

## Tips

- To dig a basement or a moat, a shovel is a must.
- In the desert, a stone shovel turns dunes into glass-making sand at full speed.
- Digging dirt with a shovel is the fastest way to gather `dirt` for [bricks](57) and worm bait.

## Trivia

- The first shovels were made from the **shoulder blades** of cattle or deer.
- The deepest hole ever dug, the Kola Superdeep Borehole in Russia, went down more than 12 kilometres.
