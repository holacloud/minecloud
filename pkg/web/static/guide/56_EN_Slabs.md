# Slabs

![Brick slab](item:brick_slab)

A slab is **half a block**: it has the same texture as its material, but only half the height. Use it for gentle
steps, roofs, benches and raised floors.

## How to get them

They are all crafted the same way (C key):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| **2** slabs | **1** block of their material |

One block makes two half blocks: nothing is wasted. Breaking a slab gives the slab back.

## How to use them

- Place them with a right click like any block; they always fill the **lower half** of their space.
- **You walk up them:** you can step onto a slab without jumping, because your steps clear up to half a block. A row
  of slabs and full blocks in turn makes a comfy staircase.
- Stone and brick slabs break faster with a [pickaxe](68); plank slabs, with an [axe](70). They take a little over
  half the time of their full block.

## Tips

- [Torch](62) light passes over slabs: they do not darken the room.
- A floor of half-height slabs looks great for porches and stages.
- The stone slab needs smooth stone (`stone`): mining gives cobblestone, so smelt it first (1 `cobblestone` +
  1 `coal_ore` → 2 `stone`). See [Stone and bedrock](50).

## Trivia

- In architecture, a *slab* is the flat concrete plate that forms the floors and ceilings of a building.
- The stairs of old cathedrals are worn down in the middle by centuries of footsteps.
