## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize AL Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_al` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

It takes part in **2** reactions you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *Corundum (two aluminium, three oxygen) turns red with a pinch of chromium.*
- *The same corundum turns blue with a little iron and titanium.*

## In the real world

- It is the **most abundant metal** in the Earth's crust.
- In the 19th century it was dearer than gold: Napoleon III kept his aluminium cutlery for his most important guests.
- Aluminium cans can be recycled again and again without losing quality.
