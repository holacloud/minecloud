## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize NA Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_na` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

Reactions the lab knows from the start:

- [Salt](119): `Na + Cl → NaCl (salt)`

It takes part in **1** reaction you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *Sodium, hydrogen, carbon and three oxygens make bread rise.*

## In the real world

- It is a metal so **soft** you can cut it with a knife, and it reacts with water.
- The old yellow street lamps were sodium vapour lamps.
- Its symbol comes from the Latin *natrium*.
