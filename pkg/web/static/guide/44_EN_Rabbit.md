# Rabbit

![Rabbit](mob:rabbit)

A timid hopper that lives with its family around a **burrow**. It nibbles the grass, sits up on its hind legs to
keep watch and, at the slightest danger, **sounds the alarm** and they all run to hide underground.

## Where it lives

- On the **plains** and in **forests**, and also in the **desert**, where they are sand coloured. Never on the
  rocky hills.
- In families of **2 to 4** around a burrow. There are at most **12 rabbits** at a time.
- They are **day** animals: at dusk they head home, at night they stay underground and **at dawn** they come out
  livelier than ever. In the rain they often turn in too.

## The burrow

- It is a **dark hole** with a heap of dug-out earth around it and a few blades of grass.
- It is not a world block: the game just draws it there, and it goes away with the last rabbit living in it.
- If you **dig away the ground** under it, it collapses, and any rabbits inside burst out and run off to find a
  new home.

## Behaviour

- It moves in **short hops**. It stops to **nibble the grass**, **sits up** with its ears swivelling, or
  **washes its face** with its paws.
- **Its nose never stops twitching**, faster when it sniffs the air or eats.
- **If it sees you** within 11 blocks, it sits up and stares. If it gets used to you, it goes back to eating.
- **If you come closer** than 5.5 blocks (1.8 when sneaking), or a [fox](45), an [owl](46) or a low-flying
  [eagle](33) shows up, **it thumps the ground** and **the whole family bolts** for the burrow in zig-zag hops,
  white tails up.
- A while later, a head **peeks out of the hole** to look around. If the coast is clear, they come out again, one
  by one.

There are **5 colours**: brown (the most common), grey, white (with red eyes), black-and-white (Dutch) and sandy
(only in the desert). Each family is usually one colour.

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit it | It cannot be defeated. It squeaks, is bowled over and runs off with its whole family. |
| Tame it | You can't: it is far too skittish. |
| `iron_shears` | 1 ![fur](item:fur) `fur` every 120 s, if you manage to get close (sneaking). |
| Bait | It is listed with `bait_seeds`, but it follows its own behaviour and hardly ever comes. |
| Cage | It fits, but you will only catch it if it hops in by chance. |
| Egg | `rabbit_egg` |
| Command | `/mob rabbit`: a family appears with its burrow a few blocks ahead. It needs grass, dirt or sand. |

## Tips

- **Sneak** to see them up close: that way they only run if you get within 1.8 blocks.
- The best time to watch them is **early in the morning**.
- To see them come out, step away from the burrow and wait quietly.

## Trivia

- Real rabbits also **thump the ground** with their hind feet to warn the others of danger.
- Their eyes, on the sides of the head, let them see almost all the way round without turning.
- The white tail they flash as they run is a **signal** for the rest of the family to follow.
- They are **prey** to many animals: in the game, to the [fox](45) and the [owl](46). Rabbits caught by a predator
  leave nothing behind.
- Real burrows are networks of tunnels with several exits, called **warrens**.
