# Cage trap

![Cage trap](item:cage_trap)

A wooden cage with iron bars and a door that snaps shut. It catches **small animals alive** and gives them to you as
an **egg**, so you can release them wherever you like.

## How to get it

It is crafted (C key):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `cage_trap` | 4 `planks` + 2 `iron_ore` |

The cage **is not used up**: you get it back when you collect the catch.

## Step by step

1. **Set it:** with the cage in your hand, right click the top of a block. The **door faces you**, open. It cannot go
   in water, without room above, or right next to another cage.
2. **Bait it:** with a [bait](93) in your hand, right click the empty cage. The bait sits in the middle ("… set inside
   the cage"). Only one fits.
3. **Wait:** the animals that fit and like that bait, within 16 blocks, walk in to eat.
4. **Snap!** As soon as an animal steps on the middle of the cage, the door slams shut, the bait vanishes and the chat
   says: "A … is caught in your cage trap!". If you are nearby, you hear the click.
5. **Collect:** right click the cage or the animal. You get **that animal's egg** and **the cage**.

**Picking up an empty cage:** right click without a bait in your hand. If it held an untouched bait, you get that
back too.

## Which bait to use

| Bait | Draws into the cage |
|---|---|
| `bait_seeds` | sheep, duck, pig, goat, rabbit |
| `bait_worms` | duck, pig, frog, turtle, lizard, scorpion, mole |
| `bait_meat` | dog, cat, spider, fox, snake |
| `bait_fish` | cat, turtle, crab |

## Details

- **Bait is not needed to catch:** any animal that fits and steps on the middle is caught. The bait just leads it
  there.
- **A caged animal is harmless:** a spider, scorpion or snake inside the cage no longer attacks you.
- **They are kept:** cages, with their animal inside too, are still there when the page reloads. The bait inside is
  not. They only exist in your browser: other players do not see them.
- If the caged animal disappears, the door opens again.
- **Your pets are safe:** the cage never catches a tamed animal or one being ridden, and a bait inside a cage
  does not draw pets in.
- **Frogs, lizards and turtles** hop, dash and swim their own way, but a bait inside the cage nudges them right to
  its middle, so they get caught too.

## Tips

- **Collector:** with a cage and baits you can get eggs of almost every small animal, the dangerous ones included.
- Set the cage with the door facing where the animals come from, and keep your distance.
- A caged snake or scorpion is the safest way to see one up close.

## Trivia

- Live-catch traps exist for real: they catch an animal without harming it, so it can be released far from the house
  or the vegetable patch.
- The bait stays inside until someone walks in; if nobody does, it spoils after 2 minutes like any bait.
