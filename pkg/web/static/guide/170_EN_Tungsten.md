## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize W Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_w` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

It takes part in **1** reaction you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *A filament of the hardest metal to melt, in glass full of a lazy gas, gives warm light.*

## In the real world

- It is the metal with the **highest melting point**: 3,422 °C.
- That is why it was used for light bulb filaments.
- Two Spanish brothers, the Elhuyars, isolated it in 1783. Its symbol comes from *Wolfram*.
