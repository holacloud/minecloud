# Wheat

![Wheat](item:wheat)

A golden sheaf of ripe ears, tied with twine. The harvest of your field.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:wheat) | Wheat | `wheat` |

## How to get it

Break a **ripe** wheat crop (*Ripe Wheat*, tall and golden): it drops **1 `wheat`** and **1–2 `wheat_seeds`**.
An unripe crop only gives its seed back. How to grow it: [Growing wheat](107).

It cannot be crafted, and no animal drops it.

## What it is for

| Use | How |
|---|---|
| ![](item:bread) [Bread](110) | **3** `wheat` → 1 `bread` (C key) |
| Taming and breeding | hold it and right click ![Sheep](mob:sheep) [sheep](17), ![Pig](mob:pig) [pigs](19), ![Camel](mob:camel) [camels](29), ![Goat](mob:goat) [goats](32) or ![Horse](mob:horse) [horses](34). See [Taming and breeding](3) |

Raw wheat is not food: bake it into bread first.

## Tips

- Three harvests make a loaf: sow at least three seeds at a time.
- For a horse, wheat works as well as tall grass, but with wheat in your hand the right click feeds the horse
  instead of mounting it (see [Riding](7)).

## Trivia

- Wheat covers more farmland than any other crop on Earth.
- Flour is ground wheat; the word *flour* comes from "flower", the finest part of the grain.
