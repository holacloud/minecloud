# Before dawn

> You found a hidden page. A shadow hissed it to you as the sun undid it. Keep it safe: shadows rarely speak.

![Wandering shadow](mob:shadow)

Just before dawn, when the night is at its most tired, the **wandering shadows** grow bolder. They know they
have little time left. This is what scares them and what does not.

## Where they are born

- Only at **night**. Every 10 seconds there is a chance that one appears, **20–30 blocks** away from you.
- **Never** over water or within **4 blocks** of a placed torch.
- There are at most **2** at once.

## Why they fear light

A shadow is, quite literally, a piece of darkness that walks. It dissolves into smoke as soon as one of these
three things happens:

| Light | Condition |
|---|---|
| The **sun** | When day comes (6:00) |
| A torch **in your hand** | If you are within **5 blocks** of it |
| A **placed** torch | Within **4 blocks** of it (checked about every half second) |

If you drive it off with light and you are within 16 blocks, there is a **50 %** chance it leaves **shadow
essence**. See [Shadow essence](86).

**What does not scare it:** the [firefly jar](97), the wisp or the moonlight. They glow, yes, but they are not
torches. Only real fire undoes it.

## A minute of grace

Shadows you **summon** (with an egg or `/mob`) last **60 seconds** in daylight before dissolving, so you can
get a good look at them. Wild ones, on the other hand, leave with the first ray of sun. Torches, though, undo
both alike.

## How it chases you

- It sees you from **24 blocks** and glides towards you unhurried: **slower than you walking**.
- **If you look straight at it from afar** (more than 7 blocks), **it stops** and stares back. As soon as you
  look away, it moves again.
- **If something blocks its way** for more than a second and a half, it **flickers**: it dissolves into smoke
  and reappears about **5 blocks** from you. Walls do not stop it for long.
- Within 14 blocks you will hear it **whisper**.
- Its claws take **3** health.

## If you would rather fight

It has health for **3 blows**. Each blow pushes it back a step in wisps of smoke.

> They say shadows are what is left of the night when the night goes away. That is why, before dawn, they
> look desperately for someone to follow. If one whispers something to you at sunrise… listen. It is the last
> thing it will say.
