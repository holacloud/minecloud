# Cheese

![Cheese](item:cheese)

A wedge of holey yellow cheese. It is **the most healing food** in the whole game.

## How to get it

It is crafted (C key):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `cheese` | **2** [Milk](78) `milk` |

Milk comes from ![Goat](mob:goat) [goats](32) and ![Camel](mob:camel) [camels](29), with a `glass` in your hand,
once a minute per animal.

## Eating (G key)

| Health | Effect |
|---|---|
| **+7** | — |

At full health you do not eat it.

## Cheese or milk?

| | Two milks drunk | One cheese |
|---|---|---|
| Health | 2 + 2 = **4** | **7** |
| Cures poison | yes | **no** |
| Bottles | **gives 2 `glass` back** | the bottles are lost |
| Space | 2 items | 1 item |

## Tips

- Make cheese when you have milk to spare: it heals almost twice as much and takes half the room.
- Always keep **some milk unmade**: cheese does not cure poison.
- Every cheese takes **two glasses** with it. Keep sand at hand to make more (3 `sand` → 2 `glass`).

## Trivia

- It takes about **10 litres of milk** to make a kilo of hard cheese.
- Cheese is one of the oldest prepared foods: remains over 3,000 years old have been found.
