# Fishing rod

![Fishing rod](item:fishing_rod)

A wooden rod with a line and a red bobber. It brings up **fish**, the odd **shell** and, now and then, junk.

## How to get it

It is crafted (C key):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `fishing_rod` | 2 `wood` + 1 `iron_ore` |

The rod **never wears out**: it lasts forever.

## Step by step

1. With the rod in your hand, **aim at water**, **9 blocks** away at most, with nothing solid in between. **Right
   click**: you cast. If there is no water in reach, the chat tells you.
2. The bobber flies in an arc and lands in the water with a splash. You see the line hanging from your hand.
3. **Wait.** Without bait, the bite comes **5 to 15 seconds** later. The water ripples now and then.
4. **A bite!** The bobber **dips**, splashes and you hear a low "plop".
5. You have **1.1 seconds** to reel in with another **right click**.

**If it gets away:** the bobber floats up again and another fish bites **3 to 11 seconds** later. No need to cast
again.

**If you reel in too early:** the line comes back empty ("Nothing on the hook") and you have to cast again.

**The line breaks** if you switch the item in your hand or walk more than about **16 blocks** from the bobber.

## What you catch

| Catch | Chance |
|---|---|
| ![](item:raw_fish) [Raw fish](80) `raw_fish` | **72 %** |
| ![](item:shell) [Shell](82) `shell` | **12 %** |
| Junk: `leaves`, `wood`, `dirt` or `tall_grass` | 16 % (4 % each) |

Your catch goes **straight into your inventory**.

## Quicker bites

| Trick | Effect |
|---|---|
| Carry `bait_worms` or `bait_fish` in your inventory | One goes on the hook when you cast (worms first): the wait drops to **55 %** (about 3–8 s). One is used up per cast. |
| Fish near a ![Fish](mob:fish) [fish school](26) (within 9 blocks of the bobber) | The fish **swim over** to the bobber and the wait runs **1.7 times faster**. |

Both tricks add up: with bait and next to a school, fish bite straight away.

## Tips

- Look for fish schools from the shore: they show as little silver fish under the water.
- At 3 `bait_worms` for every 2 `dirt`, worm bait is practically free.
- The rod is the easiest way to get [shells](82) for limestone.
- If you are about to click and unsure, wait: reeling in too early wastes the bait.

## Trivia

- When you land a fish next to a school, sometimes one fish of the school vanishes: that was the one that bit.
- Rod-and-line fishing is thousands of years old: people already fished like this in ancient Egypt and China.
- Real bobbers were made of cork or bird quills, so they would float and twitch at a bite.
