# Titanoboa

![Titanoboa](mob:titanoboa)

The biggest snake there has ever been: **7 blocks long** and as thick as a barrel. It is an ancient relative
of the [snake](42). It has **no venom**: it is slow and very strong, and **it comes for you**. It is an
[extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself, it has to be brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Look for its fossil.** It lies far down, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:fossil_rock_rare)
   `fossil_rock_rare`: a dark rock with a three-toed footprint. The page on [fossils](262) says how deep to dig.
2. **Break the block** with a [pickaxe](68). Which animal is inside depends on the place: if it was a
   Titanoboa, you take ![](item:fossil_titanoboa) `fossil_titanoboa` with you.
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45 seconds**. It is
   a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived Titanoboa: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back. Think
twice about where you put the machine: what comes out of it is a hunter.

## Behaviour

- **It appears coiled**, with its head up, and uncoils as it moves off.
- **Its body follows its head.** Every bit of it goes over the ground its head went over, in the waves of a
  snake: round a corner, up a step, between two trees.
- **It crawls slowly** near its place, at half a block a second, and stays within **8 blocks** of where it
  came back to life.
- **It is hostile.** It goes for the nearest player within **10 blocks**, at **1.3 blocks a second**: you walk
  more than three times as fast.
- **It rears before it bites.** When the player it is after is within 5 blocks, it lifts its head, opens its
  mouth a little and hisses.
- **The bite.** If its head gets within 1.2 blocks of you, it strikes: **4 damage**, at most once every
  1.2 seconds.
- **It lets go** when you are more than 15 blocks away from it, or more than 23 from its home. Then it goes
  back to its place.
- **One player at a time.** A revived one goes after the same player on everybody's screen: the server picks
  who. If that player dies, it leaves them alone for 10 seconds.
- **It flicks its tongue** to smell the air, more often while it hunts.

There are **2 colours**: olive green with black spots, like an anaconda, and sandy brown with dark saddles,
like a boa.

## Tips

- **Walk away.** It cannot catch a player who keeps walking, and it never goes further than 23 blocks from
  its home.
- **Mind its head, not its body.** Only the head bites: you can hit the tail while the head is busy turning
  round.
- **Do not let it corner you.** In a narrow tunnel there is nowhere to step aside.
- With a [shield](252) in your left hand, and facing it, its bite does half the damage.
- A fence stops it, as it stops any animal: build one round the machine before you start it.
- Put it next to a snake to see the difference in size. In the [Bestiary](5), the sheet of both shows them
  together under the title **Evolution**.

## The real Titanoboa

- **When and where.** It lived about **60 million years ago**, a few million years after the dinosaurs had
  died out, in what is now the north of **Colombia**. It was a hot, wet forest full of swamps and rivers then.
- **Where it was found.** In a huge open coal mine called Cerrejón. Its bones lay among those of giant
  turtles and of relatives of the crocodiles. Scientists told the world about it in 2009.
- **Its name** means "titanic boa".
- **Size.** About **13 metres** long, and more than **1,000 kilos**: as long as a bus and as heavy as a small
  car. The longest snakes alive today reach 6 or 7 metres. In the game it is 7 blocks, so that it fits.
- **How do they know, with no whole skeleton?** From the bones of its back. A snake has hundreds of them, all
  alike, and the bigger the snake, the bigger each bone: the scientists measured them against those of the
  snakes of today.
- **It did not bite to poison.** Like boas and anacondas, it wound itself round its prey and **squeezed**.
- **What it ate.** Mostly big fish, to judge by its skull and its teeth. And perhaps the crocodiles and the
  turtles that shared its swamp: nobody has found its dinner yet.
- **Why so big?** A snake does not make its own heat: it takes it from the air and the water. The hotter the
  place, the bigger a snake can grow. From the size of Titanoboa, scientists worked out that its forest was
  hotter than any forest of today: between 30 and 34 degrees on average, all year round.
- **Why it died out.** Nobody knows for sure. The climate changed many times after it, and no snake has been
  that big again.
- **Its family.** It was a boa, from the family of the boas and the anacondas that live today.
