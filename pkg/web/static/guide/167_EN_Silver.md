# Silver (Ag)

![Silver block](item:element_ag)

Element number **47** of the periodic table.

| | |
|---|---|
| Symbol | **Ag** |
| Atomic number | 47 |
| Group | 11 |
| Period | 5 |
| Family | transition metal |
| In-game name | Silver Block |
| Id | `element_ag` |

## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize AG Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_ag` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

It also comes from its ore, `silver_ore`, [found in the world](248). In the [foundry](235), 1 `silver_ore` gives **2** `element_ag`; by hand, 1 `silver_ore` + 1 `coal_ore` give 1. See [How metals are smelted](249).

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

It takes part in **1** reaction you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *Silver alone, spread on a glass, looks back at you.*

## In the real world

- It is the **best conductor** of electricity and heat of all metals.
- For centuries mirrors were made with silver.
- Argentina is named after silver (*argentum* in Latin).
