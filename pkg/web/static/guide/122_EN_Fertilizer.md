# Fertilizer

![Fertilizer](item:fertilizer)

A sack of fertilizer with **nitrogen** or **potassium**, what plants need most. It makes wheat shoot up at once.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:fertilizer) | Fertilizer | `fertilizer` |

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *A nitrogen surrounded by hydrogens smells strong... and plants love it.*
> *Potassium, joined to a halogen, also feeds the fields.*

## What it is for

- With fertilizer in your hand, **right click** growing wheat (see [Growing wheat](107)): it moves up **one stage** at once and one sack is used.
- Three sacks take a freshly sown crop all the way to ripe wheat.
- On ripe wheat, or on anything else, it does nothing (and is not used up).
- The server keeps tending the wheat from its new stage: the next one comes at the normal pace (50–90 s), and every player sees the change.

## Trivia

- Air is almost 78 % nitrogen, but plants cannot use it as it is: it has to be "fixed" into compounds such as ammonia.
- In 1909 Fritz Haber found how to make ammonia from the nitrogen in the air, and Carl Bosch scaled it up. These fertilizers are thought to help feed about half of humanity.
- **Potash** (potassium chloride) is mined from ancient salt beds; plants use it to manage their water.

## Solution

If you would rather discover it yourself, stop reading here.

| Reaction | Kind | Product |
|---|---|---|
| `N + 3 H → NH₃ (fertilizer)` | compound | 4 `fertilizer` |
| `K + Cl → KCl (fertilizer)` | compound | 4 `fertilizer` |
