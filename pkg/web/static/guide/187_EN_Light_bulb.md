# Light bulb

![Light bulb](item:light_bulb)

A block of warm light: a white-hot **tungsten filament** inside glass filled with argon.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:light_bulb) | Light Bulb | `light_bulb` |

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *A filament of the hardest metal to melt, in glass full of a lazy gas, gives warm light.*

## What it is for

- Place it like any block. Breaking: **0.2 s**.
- It shines by itself in every mode (with bloom in Ultra), but **does not light up** its surroundings: for that, the [torch](62).

## Trivia

- Edison showed his carbon-filament bulb in 1879; tungsten improved it some years later.
- Argon does not react with the hot filament and makes it last longer.
- Today almost every bulb is an LED, which uses far less energy.
