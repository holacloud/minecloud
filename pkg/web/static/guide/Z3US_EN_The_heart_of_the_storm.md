# The heart of the storm

> You found a hidden page. You read it on a stone that was still giving off sparks. Few dare to topple a giant
> in the middle of the rain.

![Storm golem](mob:storm_golem)

In the blackest part of the storm beats a heart of stone and lightning. The **storm golem** hates nobody: it
just walks while it rains, calls to the sky and listens to the thunder. But it has its habits.

## Child of the lightning

- It is only born **while it rains**, from a bolt that falls **20–30 blocks** away from you.
- There are never more than **2** at once.
- When the rain stops, its cracks go dark and it **crumbles** into rubble. Those summoned with an egg or `/mob`
  last **a minute** without rain.

## Lightning prefers metal

Every 9 to 18 seconds it raises its arms and **calls the lightning**. Where does it fall?

1. More than half the time (60 %) it first looks for an **iron, copper or gold** block within **12 blocks**.
   See [Fine stones and metals](59).
2. Among the ones it finds, it **prefers those open to the sky**: nothing solid in the 15 blocks above them.
3. The bolt hits the metal (the block **does not break**), throws orange sparks and the charge **arcs to the
   golem's fist**.
4. If there is no metal (or this time it does not look for any), the bolt falls **onto its own head**.

**Do not stand on the metal!** If you are on top of the block when the bolt strikes, it takes **2** health.

## It heals with its own lightning

When the bolt falls on its head, the golem **soaks up the charge** and **gets back 4 health**: exactly what one
of your bare-handed blows takes away. It has health for **5 blows** bare-handed, so a badly timed bolt costs you one more.

**Trick:** put an iron, copper or gold block **under the open sky** near where you are going to fight. It will
be your **lightning rod**: many of its bolts will land there instead of on its head.

## If you anger it

It is **neutral**: it does not attack you unless you strike it. But if you do, it turns on you for
**15 seconds** (which start again with every blow):

- It charges its fists with a crackle and a rising whine.
- It **takes aim**: a faint line shows where it will fire.
- It lets loose a **discharge** along that line: 2 or 3 health.
- **To dodge it**, step out of the line or get behind a block.

## Count the seconds

Thunder travels at about **34 blocks per second**. Count the seconds between the flash and the thunder and
multiply: you will know how far away the bolt fell (the delay is never more than 3 seconds).

## The prize

Toppled, it leaves **2–3 glow dust** and **1–2 iron ore**. And one thing more: a **mark carved** in its rubble.
If you are reading this, you have already seen it.

> They say every golem keeps in its chest a little piece of the world's first storm, and that is why it goes
> back to the sky when the rain ends. The mark in the rubble would be its signature: the name of the one who
> rules the storms.
