# Bat

![Bat](mob:bat)

By day, bats hang **upside down** under roofs, wrapped in their wings. At night they come out to fly in quick,
chaotic flutters, making high clicks.

## Where it lives

- Under any **roof**: the underside of a tree's canopy, a rock overhang, a tunnel or your own house. They need a
  solid block above and two free blocks below.
- In every biome **except the desert**. Forests have more than twice as many colonies.
- They live in colonies of **3 to 6**. There are at most **12 bats** at a time.
- They sleep **by day** and fly **at night**.

## Behaviour

- **Hanging:** they sway a little, turn their heads and now and then **stretch their wings**. If you are near,
  they sometimes squeak softly.
- **If you come within 3.5 blocks** (1.6 when sneaking), the whole colony nearby **bursts out in a panic**,
  dropping off the roof and fluttering about, some of them right past your head.
- **At dusk** they wake one by one and flit around the roost with sharp turns, changing course all the time.
- **Echolocation:** in flight they make very high **clicks**, faster when frightened.
- At night they sometimes **chase the [fireflies](36)**.
- **At dawn** they return to their roof. If you break the roof they hang from, they look for another.

There are **2 colours**: brown and dark grey.

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit it | It cannot be defeated. It frightens the whole colony, which flies off. |
| Net (`bug_net`) | You catch it and get a `bat_egg`. They are fast: aim well. |
| Tame, bait or cage | No. |
| Egg | `bat_egg`: a colony appears in flight and settles under the nearest roof (up to 10 blocks away). |
| Command | `/mob bat` |

## Tips

- To see them hanging, **sneak up**: that way you get much closer without waking them.
- Look under the big trees of the forests, or in tunnels and overhangs in the mountains.
- Want bats at home? Use a `bat_egg` inside: they will come to live under your roof.

## Trivia

- Bats are the **only mammals that truly fly**.
- They find their way by **echolocation**: they call out and listen to the echo to "see" in the dark. Most of
  those calls are too high for us to hear. The bat's tone in the game, **4200 Hz**, is the highest of all its
  animals.
- They sleep upside down because that way they can **take off** just by letting go.
- The [owl](46) hunts them in flight at night, but never the ones hanging.
