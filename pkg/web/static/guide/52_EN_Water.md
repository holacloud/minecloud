# Water

![Water](item:water)

Water fills the lakes, seas and shores of the world. You can swim in it, fish in it and place it like any other
block.

| Name | Id | Break |
|---|---|---|
| Water | `water` | 0.15 s, but breaking it **drops nothing** |

## Where to find it

Water covers everything below **sea level, y = 5**: lakes, coasts and the lowest valleys. There is no water in the
mountains.

## How to get it

You start the game with **12 water blocks**. Breaking water removes it, but you do not keep it.

To carry more, use a ![Bucket](item:bucket) [bucket](111) (3 `iron_ore`): right click water with the empty
bucket to scoop up that block and get a `water_bucket`; right click a block face with the water bucket to pour it
there and get the empty bucket back.

## How it works

- **Placing it:** right click, like any block (or pour it from a [bucket](111)). Water **does not flow**: it stays
  exactly where you put it.
- **Swimming:** in water you move slower (55 % speed). Hold **jump** to rise; otherwise you sink slowly.
- **Breaks falls:** water slows your fall; about 2 blocks deep stop even a very long fall. A pool at the foot of a
  tower saves your life!
- **You do not drown:** you can stay under water as long as you like.
- On horseback you swim even slower (35 %).
- Entering water splashes and makes ripples; in the RTX and Ultra [graphics modes](14) the waves move.

## Life in the water

- ![Fish](mob:fish) [Fish](26) swim in schools in deep water. A `fish_egg` can only be released in deep water.
- The [fishing rod](94) needs water within 9 blocks.
- Frogs, turtles and crabs live along the shores.

## Snow and ice

Water also comes **solid**. The [freezer](234) turns a flask of water into `ice`, and with ice you can craft two
building blocks (C key):

| | In-game name | Id | Made from | Break (bare hand) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ![](item:snow_block) | Snow Block | `snow_block` | 2 `ice` | 0.3 s |
| ![](item:packed_ice) | Packed Ice | `packed_ice` | 4 `ice` | 0.5 s |

They are placed like any block, and **neither melts** in the game, not even next to a torch.

Snow and ice are the same thing, solid water, but snow is made of flakes with lots of air between them: up to nine
parts of air to one of ice. That is why it is white (the air between the crystals scatters light in every
direction) and why it keeps the cold out so well: igloos are warm inside. Packed ice, with no air, is clear and
bluish.

## Tips

- Before jumping from very high up, place a couple of water blocks below.
- A shaft filled with water is a safe (if slow) way down into a mine without getting hurt.

## Trivia

- Water covers **71 %** of the Earth's surface, but nearly all of it is salty.
- Ice floats because water **expands** when it freezes, something very rare in nature.
- In the game, water is a plain block that never spills: you can build a waterfall that hangs still in the air.
