# Water

![Water](item:water)

Water fills the lakes, seas and shores of the world. You can swim in it, fish in it and place it like any other
block.

| Name | Id | Break |
|---|---|---|
| Water | `water` | 0.15 s, but it **drops nothing** |

## Where to find it

Water covers everything below **sea level, y = 5**: lakes, coasts and the lowest valleys. There is no water in the
mountains.

## How to get it

You start the game with **12 water blocks**. Breaking water removes it, but you do not keep it, so those 12 blocks
are all you will have: use them wisely.

## How it works

- **Placing it:** right click, like any block. Water **does not flow**: it stays exactly where you put it.
- **Swimming:** in water you move slower (55 % speed). Hold **jump** to rise; otherwise you sink slowly.
- **Breaks falls:** water slows your fall; about 2 blocks deep stop even a very long fall. A pool at the foot of a
  tower saves your life!
- **You do not drown:** you can stay under water as long as you like.
- On horseback you swim even slower (35 %).
- Entering water splashes and makes ripples; in the RTX and Ultra [graphics modes](14) the waves move.

## Life in the water

- ![Fish](mob:fish) [Fish](26) swim in schools in deep water. A `fish_egg` can only be released in deep water.
- The [fishing rod](94) needs water within 9 blocks.
- Frogs, turtles and crabs live along the shores.

## Tips

- Before jumping from very high up, place a couple of water blocks below.
- A shaft filled with water is a safe (if slow) way down into a mine without getting hurt.

## Trivia

- Water covers **71 %** of the Earth's surface, but nearly all of it is salty.
- Ice floats because water **expands** when it freezes, something very rare in nature.
- In the game, water is a plain block that never spills: you can build a waterfall that hangs still in the air.
