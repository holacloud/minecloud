# Zinc (Zn)

![Zinc block](item:element_zn)

Element number **30** of the periodic table.

| | |
|---|---|
| Symbol | **Zn** |
| Atomic number | 30 |
| Group | 12 |
| Period | 4 |
| Family | transition metal |
| In-game name | Zinc Block |
| Id | `element_zn` |

## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize ZN Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_zn` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

It also comes from its ore, `zinc_ore`, [found in the world](248). In the [foundry](235), 1 `zinc_ore` gives **2** `element_zn`; by hand, 1 `zinc_ore` + 1 `coal_ore` give 1. See [How metals are smelted](249).

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

It takes part in **1** reaction you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *Two parts copper and one part zinc give the golden metal of trumpets.*

## In the real world

- A layer of zinc protects steel from rust: that is **galvanised** steel.
- With copper it makes **brass**.
- Your body needs a little zinc to fight infections.
