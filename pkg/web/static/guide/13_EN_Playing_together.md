# Playing together

MineCloud is a shared world: everyone connected to the same server plays in the same world, at the same
time.

## What is shared

- **Blocks:** whatever someone breaks or builds, everyone sees, and it is saved on the server.
- **The time:** day and night are the same for everyone. If someone sleeps in a bed, dawn comes for
  everyone. See [Day, night and weather](8).
- **Signs, sound blocks and graffiti** (see below).

## What is yours alone

- **Animals and creatures** live in your browser: another player may see different animals in the same
  spot.
- Your **tamed pets**, your **cages** and your **firefly jars** are only saved in your browser. See
  [Taming and breeding](3) and [Baits and traps](4).
- Your **inventory**, your bed as respawn point and your settings.
- The **rain**.

**Exception:** if you ride a horse, others see you riding it. See [Riding](7).

## Other players

- Each player is a figure with a face and their **name above**. Clothing colours come from their identity,
  and anyone can change their look with **Edit Player** (in the pause menu or on the title screen).
- You hear the **footsteps** of nearby players.
- The **player list** shows who is online. Click a name to have the camera **follow** them; **Escape**
  brings you back.

## The chat

- **Enter** opens the chat. Everyone reads what you write.
- **Left click** a player to open the chat with `@name` already typed.
- The chat tells you when someone joins, leaves or dies.
- There are commands for floating messages, light markers, reactions and sounds: see
  [Chat commands](12).

## Signs

- Place a [sign](64) and write a message of up to **288 letters**. Everyone sees it and it is saved.
- Aim at a sign and press **F** to read it in full.
- While reading it you can **vote** with 👍 👎 ❤️ 😊 ⭐. Votes are counted for everyone, and the chat
  announces each vote.

## Sound blocks

- A [sound block](66) plays when you aim at it and press **F**. Players within about 18 blocks hear it.
- **Shift + F** changes the instrument and the note. There are 5 instruments: bell, drum, pluck, flute and
  bass.
- Its instrument and note are saved for everyone. Place several and play a tune!

## Spray graffiti

- With [spray paint](67) in your hand, **right click** a block face to paint it.
- **F** changes the colour: green, pink or blue.
- The first stroke uses up one can and gives you **30 seconds** to paint as much as you like.
- Everyone sees the graffiti, and it fades on its own after **one game day** (4 minutes), or when the block
  is broken.

## Voice chat

- Press the **Voice** button on screen to turn on your microphone (the browser will ask for permission).
- It is **proximity** chat: you hear others louder the closer they are, and beyond **18 blocks** you stop
  hearing them.
- Voices come from your left or right depending on where the other player is.
- Press the button again to turn it off.
