# Sand, cactus and glass

![Sand](item:sand)

Three desert blocks: the **sand** of dunes and beaches, the **cactus** that grows on it, and the **glass** that is
made from it.

| | In-game name | Id | Break (bare hand) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ![](item:sand) | Sand | `sand` | 0.4 s |
| ![](item:cactus) | Cactus | `cactus` | 0.45 s |
| ![](item:glass) | Glass | `glass` | 0.22 s |

## Where to find them

- **Sand:** the ground of the desert and of many beaches, by the water. You start with **20 sand**.
- **Cactus:** columns **2 to 4 blocks** tall in the desert.
- **Glass:** never found in the world; it is crafted.

All three drop themselves when broken.

## What they are for

- **Glass:** a solid, **see-through** block, ideal for windows, greenhouses and fish tanks. A glass block also works
  as a **bottle**:
  - Right click a goat or a camel holding glass: you **milk** it and the glass becomes `milk`.
  - `honeycomb` + `glass` = `honey_bottle`.
  - Drinking milk, honey or antivenom (G key) **gives the glass back**.
- **Cactus:** the ![Camel](mob:camel) [camel](29)'s favourite food for taming it. In the game it **does not
  prick**.
- **Sand:** good ground for beaches and desert gardens.

## Tips

- A [shovel](69) breaks sand almost instantly.
- [Torch](62) light goes through glass: you can light a room without letting anyone in.
- Always carry a few glass blocks if you are going to milk animals or collect honey.

## Trivia

- Real glass is made by melting **silica sand** at over 1,500 °C.
- When lightning strikes sand it melts it into glass tubes called **fulgurites**.
- The saguaro cactus can live for more than 150 years and grow over 12 metres tall.
