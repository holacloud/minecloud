## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize CR Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_cr` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

It takes part in **1** reaction you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *Corundum (two aluminium, three oxygen) turns red with a pinch of chromium.*

## In the real world

- The shiny **chrome** on taps and motorbikes is a very thin layer of chromium.
- It makes steel stainless, and gives rubies their red and emeralds their green.
- Its name comes from the Greek *chroma*, "colour".
