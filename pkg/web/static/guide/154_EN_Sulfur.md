## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize S Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_s` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

It takes part in **2** reactions you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *Copper, sulfur and four oxygens form deep blue crystals.*
- *"Fool's gold" is iron with two of sulfur.*

## In the real world

- It is yellow and appears around **volcanoes**.
- Pure sulfur hardly smells: the rotten egg smell comes from one of its compounds, hydrogen sulfide.
- It is used to toughen the rubber of tyres (vulcanisation).
