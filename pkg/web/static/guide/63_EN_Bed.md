# Bed

![Bed](item:bed)

A soft red bed. It does two very important things: it **chooses where you respawn** and it lets you **skip the
night**.

| In-game name | Id | Break |
|---|---|---|
| Bed | `bed` | 0.55 s |

## How to get it

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `bed` | 3 `planks` + 2 `leaves` + 1 `wood` |
| 1 `bed` (feather) | 4 `feather` + 2 `white_wool` + 3 `planks` |

Both give the same bed.

## Respawn point

- When you **place** a bed, your respawn point moves **on top of it**. The chat confirms it: "Respawn point updated
  to your bed".
- The **last** bed you placed is the one that counts.
- If you **break** that bed, the point is cleared ("Respawn point reset because your bed was removed"). Without a
  bed, you respawn at the last safe spot you stood on.
- The `/spawn` command takes you to your respawn point. See [Chat commands](12).

## Sleeping

1. Wait for **night** (from 18:00 to 6:00; `/time` tells you the hour).
2. Look at the bed and press **F**.
3. You wake up at **dawn**. When playing alone, the sky also clears.

In the daytime you only get "You can only sleep at night". You can sleep in **any** bed, not just yours.

**Playing with others:** when someone sleeps, **dawn comes for everyone** and the chat says who slept.

## Tips

- Place a bed **as soon as** you start exploring far away: if you die, you will not have to walk back.
- Before going down a deep mine, leave a bed at the entrance.
- Sleeping gets rid of shadows: at dawn they dissolve into smoke.

## Trivia

- People spend nearly **a third of their lives** asleep.
- Feather beds were a luxury for centuries; duvets are still filled with duck and goose down today.
- Dolphins sleep with **half their brain** at a time, so they do not forget to breathe.
