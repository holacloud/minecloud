# Ladder

![Ladder](item:ladder)

Wooden rungs that hang on a wall. With them you **go straight up and down** without jumping and without falling.

| In-game name | Id | Break |
|---|---|---|
| Ladder | `ladder` | 0.25 s |

## How to get it

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| **8** `ladder` | 1 `wood` |

A single log makes a ladder eight blocks tall.

## How to use it

- **Place it** with a right click on the **side** of a block: it sticks to that wall, facing you. It cannot go on a
  floor or a ceiling.
- It is not solid: you step "into" it to climb.
- **To go up:** walk forward or press jump (3.2 blocks per second).
- **To go down:** walk backwards or crouch (2.2 blocks per second).
- **If you press nothing, you stay put**, holding on, without falling. Ideal for a rest halfway up!
- You cannot climb on horseback.

## Tips

- Line a mine shaft with ladders to get in and out in seconds.
- Since you never fall while holding on, a ladder down a cliff is a safe way down.
- A watchtower with an inner ladder and a bed at the top makes a great shelter.

## Trivia

- Climbing to reach high places is very old: a cave painting in Spain (the Cuevas de la Araña, Valencia), about
  8,000 years old, shows someone hanging from ropes, climbing up to collect honey!
- Firefighters use extending ladders over 30 metres long.
