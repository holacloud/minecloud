# Eohippus

![Eohippus](mob:eohippus)

The **dawn horse**: the ancestor of the [horse](34), the size of a small dog. It has an arched back, a short
face and **toes instead of a hoof**. It is an [extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself, it has to be
brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Look for its fossil.** It lies far down, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:fossil_rock_rare)
   `fossil_rock_rare`: a dark rock with a three-toed footprint. The page on [fossils](262) says how deep to dig.
2. **Break the block** with a [pickaxe](68). Which animal is inside depends on the place: if it was an
   Eohippus, you take ![](item:fossil_eohippus) `fossil_eohippus` with you.
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45 seconds**. It is
   a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived Eohippus: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back.

## Behaviour

- **It strolls with quick little steps** near its place: a little lighter on its feet than a walking horse.
- It is a grazer: when it stands still, **it lowers its head to eat**.
- It looks around and flicks its ears.
- It is **peaceful and easily startled**: it comes up to nobody and attacks nobody. If you hit it, it gives a start.

There are **2 coats**: chestnut with pale spots and brown with dark stripes.

| What you do | What happens |

| Breed | Two tamed ones in love have a foal. |

| Command | `/mob eohippus` |

## Tips

- `tall_grass` is everywhere: take a good handful and offer it one at a time until it stays with you.
- Put it next to a horse to see how much they have changed in 50 million years.
- In the [Bestiary](5), its sheet and the horse's show the two together under the title **Evolution**.

## The real Eohippus

- **When and where.** It lived in **North America** between about **56 and 50 million years ago**. The climate
  was much warmer than today, and there were damp forests far to the north.
- **Its name** comes from Greek and means "dawn horse": it stands at the beginning of the story of horses.
- **Size.** It was less than half a metre tall at the shoulder: like a fox or a small dog, and it weighed a
  few kilos.
- **Toes.** It had **4 toes** on each front foot and **3** on each hind one. Each toe ended in a small hoof,
  and it stood on pads, as a dog does. The horse of today walks on **a single toe**: its hoof is the nail of
  the middle toe. In the game the toes are too small to be seen.
- **What it ate.** Soft leaves and the fruit of the forest. Its back teeth were low, no good for grass, which
  is tough and wears them down.
- **How it became a horse.** It did not vanish all at once: it changed. When the climate cooled and many
  forests gave way to grassland, its descendants did better by being bigger, with long legs for running, fewer
  toes and tall teeth for eating grass. It took about **50 million years**. And it was not a straight line but
  a tree with many branches: nearly all of them died out, and today only that of horses, donkeys and zebras is
  left.
- **A round trip.** Horses arose in North America and crossed from there to Asia. In America they died out
  about **10,000 years ago**, and came back by ship with the Spanish, around the year 1500.
- **Nobody knows its colour.** Fossils keep bones and teeth, not hair. The spots and stripes of the game are
  a guess: many small animals of the forest have them to hide.
