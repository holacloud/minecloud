# Axes

![Iron axe](item:iron_axe)

The woodcutter's tool. With an axe, **logs**, **planks** and **wooden slabs** are cut much faster.

| | Axe | Id | Speed on its material |
|---|---|---|---|
| ![](item:wood_axe) | Wood Axe | `wood_axe` | ×1.45 |
| ![](item:stone_axe) | Stone Axe | `stone_axe` | ×1.9 |
| ![](item:iron_axe) | Iron Axe | `iron_axe` | ×2.6 |
| ![](item:gold_axe) | Gold Axe | `gold_axe` | **×3.2** |

## How to get them

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| `wood_axe` | 3 `planks` + 1 `wood` |
| `stone_axe` | 3 `cobblestone` + 1 `wood` |
| `iron_axe` | 3 `iron_ore` + 1 `wood` |
| `gold_axe` | 3 `gold_ore` + 1 `wood` |

## How to use them

Select it and chop with a left click. **It never wears out.** It speeds up every log and plank of the nine woods
and the plank slabs (see [Wood and leaves](54)).

| Block | Bare hand | Wood | Stone | Iron | Gold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Log | 0.8 s | 0.55 s | 0.42 s | 0.31 s | 0.25 s |
| Planks | 0.6 s | 0.41 s | 0.32 s | 0.23 s | 0.19 s |

**On other blocks** it helps less: ×1 for wood, ×1.05 for stone, ×1.43 for iron and ×1.76 for gold. Leaves do not
count as wood: for them, better a [hoe](72) or the [shears](73).

## Tips

- With an axe and a few [saplings](55) you can run a **wood farm**: plant, chop, plant again.
- Taking a wooden house apart is twice as fast with an axe.
- The axe is no weapon: each blow to an animal does the same as your bare hand.

## Trivia

- Stone **hand axes** are over 1.7 million years old: one of humanity's oldest tools.
- Ötzi, the 5,300-year-old iceman, carried an axe of almost pure **copper**.
