# Fishing

![Fishing rod](item:fishing_rod)

With a [fishing rod](94) you can pull fish out of any lake, river or sea. It is crafted from 2 `wood` and 1
`iron_ore`.

## Step by step

1. Hold the `fishing_rod` and **right click aiming at water**, up to **9 blocks** away. The bobber flies
   out and lands on the water, tied to you by the line.
2. **Wait.** The bobber floats and makes small ripples. A fish takes **5 to 15 seconds** to bite.
3. **A bite!** The bobber dips, splashes and goes *plop*.
4. You have **1.1 seconds** to reel in with another **right click**.

If you miss it, no harm done: the bobber floats up again and another fish bites a few seconds later.

If you reel in before a bite, the line comes out empty ("Nothing on the hook").

## What you can catch

| Catch | Chance |
|---|---|
| ![](item:raw_fish) `raw_fish` | 72 % |
| ![](item:shell) `shell` | 12 % |
| Junk: `leaves`, `wood`, `dirt` or `tall_grass` | 16 % |

[Raw fish](80) can be eaten (+1 health) or cooked: `raw_fish` + `coal_ore` makes a [cooked fish](81) that
heals **+6**.

## For quicker bites

- **Bait in your inventory:** if you carry `bait_worms` or `bait_fish`, one is used up when you cast and
  the fish bites in almost **half the time**. See [Baits](93).
- **Near a school of fish:** [fish](26) within 9 blocks of the bobber swim over, and the wait runs **1.7
  times** faster.
- You can combine both.

When you land a fish near a school, it is sometimes one of the school's fish, and it disappears from the
water.

## The line breaks if...

- you switch the item in your hand, or
- you walk too far from the bobber.

## Other ways to get fish

- **Striking a school of fish:** each hit on a fish has a **20 %** chance to catch it ("You caught a
  fish!").
- **Fishing birds:** [eagles](33) circle high up and, when they spot a fish near the surface, dive and
  carry it off in their talons. If the water suddenly splashes, look up.
