# Mob eggs

![Sheep egg](item:sheep_egg)

Every creature in the game has **its own egg**, in the colour of its species. Place it and the creature appears
right there. Eggs are how you **bring animals home** or fill in your [Bestiary](5).

## How to get them

- **Mowing tall grass or breaking leaves:** **3.5 %** of the time an egg of a **random** species drops, any of
  the 31!
- **With a [cage trap](96):** set bait, wait for an animal to walk in and collect it: you get **its egg** and the
  cage back.
- **With the [bug net](95):** catch a bee or a bat and you take its egg.

See [Baits and traps](4).

## How to use them

With the egg in your hand, **right click** a block: the creature appears on top and the egg is used up. The chat
says, for example, "Sheep spawned".

Some eggs are special:

| Egg | What happens |
|---|---|
| ![](item:bee_egg) bee | a whole **colony** appears with its hive, in a nearby tree (or on the ground if there is none) |
| ![](item:bat_egg) bat | a **flock** of bats appears |
| ![](item:fish_egg) fish | a **school of fish**; only in deep water ("Fish eggs must be placed in deep water") |
| ![](item:wisp_egg) wisp | a wisp appears, floating |
| ![](item:owl_egg) owl | the owl looks for a perch nearby |
| ![](item:shadow_egg) shadow | it lasts **one minute** in daylight before dissolving |

**Careful!** Eggs of hostile creatures (spider, cave monster, scorpion, snake, shadow…) release **hostile**
creatures, which will attack you.

## Tips

- Want a farm? Catch a pair with cage traps, release them near home and [look after them](3) so they breed.
- If a rare egg drops from the leaves (a storm golem!), keep it for a special occasion.
- The `/mob <animal>` command also spawns creatures, but eggs can be stored and traded.

## Trivia

- In real life only some of these creatures lay eggs: birds, reptiles, fish, amphibians, insects, spiders… Sheep,
  dogs and bats are mammals and **do not lay eggs**.
- The largest egg of any living animal is the ostrich's: it weighs over 1 kilo.
- The only mammals that lay eggs are the platypus and the echidnas.
