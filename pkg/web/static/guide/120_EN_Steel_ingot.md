# Steel ingot

![Steel ingot](item:steel_ingot)

Iron with a little carbon: harder than iron and than gold.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:steel_ingot) | Steel Ingot | `steel_ingot` |

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), a reaction known from the start:

| Reaction | Kind | Product |
|---|---|---|
| `Fe + C → steel` | alloy | 2 `steel_ingot` |

## What it is for

Steel ingots forge the [steel tools](128), the best in the game:

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| `steel_pickaxe`, `steel_shovel`, `steel_axe` | 3 `steel_ingot` + 1 `wood` |
| `steel_sword` | 2 `steel_ingot` + 1 `wood` |

## Trivia

- Steel has **less than 2 %** carbon by mass. The carbon atoms slip in between the iron atoms and stop their layers from sliding: that is what makes it harder.
- With at least 10.5 % **chromium** it becomes **stainless**.
- In 1856 Henry Bessemer invented a furnace that made cheap steel in huge amounts.
