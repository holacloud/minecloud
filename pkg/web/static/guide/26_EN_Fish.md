# Fish

![Fish](mob:fish)

Fish swim in tight **schools** that move all at once. They flee when you come close and, now and then, leap out
of the water.

## Where it lives

- In **lakes, rivers and seas** of any biome, as long as the water is **at least 2 blocks deep**.
- Schools of **5 to 8** fish. By day and by night.
- At most there are about 32 fish around you at a time.

## Behaviour

- **They swim together:** the school picks a heading and they all follow it, without bumping into each other or
  breaking the surface.
- **They get startled:** if you come within 3 blocks, the whole school darts off the other way.
- **They leap:** now and then a calm fish near the surface jumps out and falls back with a splash.
- **Out of the water** (if you remove the water or release it on land) it lies on its side and flops about.
  After 12 seconds it is gone.
- The tail beats faster the quicker they swim, and the fins paddle.

## How to interact

- **Hitting it:** it startles the whole school. Each hit has a **20 %** chance of **catching** the fish: "You
  caught a fish!" appears and you get a `raw_fish` ![Raw fish](item:raw_fish).
- **Taming it:** not possible.
- **Fishing with a rod:** schools within 9 blocks of the bobber swim over and bite sooner. Carrying `bait_worms`
  or `bait_fish` in your inventory also speeds up the bite (see [Fishing](6) and [Fishing rod](94)).
- **Baits on the ground:** no use, because it does not leave the water.
- **Cage:** it does **not** fit.
- **Egg:** `fish_egg`. It must be placed in **deep water**: a school of 6 is born. The command `/mob fish` creates
  another school of 6 in the nearest deep water (within 10 blocks).

## Tips

- `raw_fish` is cooked with `coal_ore` into `cooked_fish`, which heals 6 (see [Cooked fish](81)).
- It is the favourite food for taming dogs, cats and ducks.
- To watch the fish leap, stand still on the shore: if you wade in, the school flees.

## Trivia

- There are **4 kinds**, and each school is of a single kind:
  - **trout:** olive back, silver belly, a pink stripe and dark speckles;
  - **koi:** white with orange patches;
  - **tang:** deep blue with a yellow tail;
  - **perch:** green-gold with dark bars and orange fins.
- The [Eagle](33) hunts them: when a fish swims near the surface, it dives and carries it off.
- Real fish swim in schools to confuse predators: it is hard to pick one out of so many.
- They have a "lateral line" along their body that feels the movement of the water, which is why they all turn
  at once.
