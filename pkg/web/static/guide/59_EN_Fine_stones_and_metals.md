# Fine stones and metals

![Marble](item:marble)

**Refined** blocks for elegant buildings: polished veined stones, riveted metal blocks and mysterious
**obsidian**, and a **magma rock** that glows.

None of these blocks appears in the world: they are crafted. But copper, iron and gold **do have ores**, which are found in the world and smelted: see [How to find metals](248) and [How they are smelted](249).

## Recipes (C key)

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 2 marble, basalt, slate, limestone, granite or concrete | 2 `cobblestone` + 1 `sand` |
| 2 copper, iron or gold blocks, or obsidian | 2 `stone` + 1 `sand` |
| **4** `limestone` | 1 `shell` + 1 `sand` |
| 2 `obsidian` | 1 `shadow_essence` + 2 `stone` |
| **4** `magma_rock` | 4 `basalt` + 1 [glow dust](85) |

The [foundry](235) also smelts 1 `iron_ore` into 1 `iron_block`, 1 `gold_ore` into 1 `gold_block`, and 1 `copper_ore` into **2** `copper_block`. By hand, 1 `copper_ore` + 1 `coal_ore` give 1 `copper_block`. For the [lab bench](117), each of the three metals turns into its [element block](60).

Polished stones come from cobblestone, which is plentiful. Metals and obsidian need **smooth stone** (`stone`):
mining stone only gives cobblestone, so smelt it first, 1 `cobblestone` + 1 `coal_ore` → 2 `stone` (see
[Stone and bedrock](50)).

## What they are for

They are **building** blocks with no special effect: palace floors, columns, statues, vaults… Breaking them drops
the same block.

The one exception is **magma rock**: it gives [light](105) of level **10**, with no electricity, and never burns
out. Basalt is lava that cooled fast; where it is still hot inside, the cracks glow like those of a lava flow that
has just stopped.

| Block | Bare hand | Iron pickaxe |
|---|---|---|
| All but obsidian and magma rock | 1 s | 0.38 s |
| Magma rock | 1.1 s | 0.42 s |
| **Obsidian** | **3.5 s** | 1.35 s |

Obsidian is the **hardest** breakable block in the game. For all of them, the right tool is the [pickaxe](68).

## Tips

- The **shell** recipe gives twice as much limestone: look for [crabs](39) on the beach or fish up shells.
- Obsidian is perfect for a shelter: it takes ages to break.
- Marble with granite columns looks like an ancient temple.

## Trivia

- **Marble** is limestone transformed by heat and pressure. Michelangelo's *David* is carved from Carrara marble.
- **Limestone** is made of the shells and skeletons of sea creatures: that is why the game makes it from shells.
- **Slate** was used for roofs and for school blackboards.
- **Basalt** is cooled lava; sometimes it forms hexagonal columns, as at the Giant's Causeway in Ireland.
- **Obsidian** is volcanic glass. Its edges are so fine that it has been used in scalpels.
- The dome of the Pantheon in Rome, made of unreinforced **concrete**, still stands after almost 2,000 years.
- **Copper** turns green over time: that is why the Statue of Liberty is green.

> Limestone can also be made **in the lab**: it is one of the reactions to discover in
> [Chemistry](115). Hint: shells and chalk hold calcium, carbon and plenty of oxygen.
