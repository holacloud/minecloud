# Kubanochoerus

![Kubanochoerus](mob:kubanochoerus)

A giant pig, as tall as a cow, with **a horn on its forehead** and a small one over each eye. It is an ancient
relative of the [pig](19). It minds its own business, but **if you hit it, it charges**. It is an
[extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself, it has to be brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Look for its fossil.** It lies far down, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:fossil_rock_rare)
   `fossil_rock_rare`: a dark rock with a three-toed footprint. The page on [fossils](262) says how deep to dig.
2. **Break the block** with a [pickaxe](68). Which animal is inside depends on the place: if it was a
   Kubanochoerus, you take ![](item:fossil_kubanochoerus) `fossil_kubanochoerus` with you.
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45 seconds**. It is
   a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived Kubanochoerus: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back.

## Behaviour

- **It strolls slowly** near its place and **roots**: when it stands still, it lowers its snout to the ground.
- It is **neutral**: it attacks nobody who has not attacked it.
- **If you hit it, it charges.** It snorts, turns on you, **lowers its head** and paws the ground with one leg
  for 0.7 seconds. Then **it runs in a straight line** at where you were: about 3 blocks, at 6 blocks a second.
  If it gets within 1.9 blocks of you, the horn does **3 damage** and throws you back. After that it stands
  still for nearly a second.
- It only charges whoever hit it, and only that player sees the charge.
- It does not jump off cliffs or go through fences: when something is in its way, it stops.

There is one colour only: dark brown, with a crest of black bristles over the shoulders and a paler belly.

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit it | **24 health**: 6 hits bare-handed, 4 with a stone or an iron [sword](71), 3 with a golden one. Every hit that does not defeat it makes it charge, unless it was charging already. |
| Defeat it | It leaves **2 to 4** ![leather](item:leather) `leather`. |
| Tame it | You can't. |
| Bait | None. |
| Cage | It is too big for the cage. |
| Egg | `kubanochoerus_egg` |
| Command | `/mob kubanochoerus` |

## Tips

- **Step aside as soon as it lowers its head.** It runs at where you were when it set off, not at where you are
  now: one long step to the side and it goes past.
- A fence between the two of you stops it.
- With a [shield](252) in your left hand, and facing it, the horn does half the damage.
- Do not hit it at the edge of a cliff: its shove is a strong one.
- Put it next to a pig to see the difference in size. In the [Bestiary](5), the sheet of both shows them
  together under the title **Evolution**.

## The real Kubanochoerus

- **When and where.** It lived in the **Miocene**, between about **17 and 10 million years ago**, from the
  Caucasus to China.
- **Its name** means "pig of the Kuban". The Kuban is a river of the Caucasus, and its first bones were found
  near it.
- **Size.** The biggest species, *Kubanochoerus gigas*, stood around **1.2 metres** at the shoulder and is
  thought to have weighed up to about **500 kilos**. It is one of the biggest pigs there have ever been. And it
  had long legs, for a pig.
- **The horn.** It grew from the forehead, and over each eye there was another, much smaller. Scientists think
  only the **males** had the big horn, and that they used it to **fight each other**, pushing head against
  head. In the game they all have it.
- **What it ate.** Probably plants, and maybe a bit of everything, as pigs do today.
- **Why it died out.** Nobody knows for sure. During its time the climate grew cooler and drier, and the
  forests and what there was to eat changed.
- **Is it the pig's grandfather?** Not quite. It belonged to the **same family** as the pig and the wild boar,
  but to a branch that left no descendants: a great-uncle rather than a grandfather.
