# Bread

![Bread](item:bread)

A crusty golden loaf, baked from the wheat of your own field.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:bread) | Bread | `bread` |

## How to get it

It is crafted (C key):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `bread` | **3** [Wheat](109) `wheat` |

Wheat is grown on farmland: see [Growing wheat](107).

## Eating (G key)

| Health | Effect |
|---|---|
| **+5** | — |

At full health you do not eat it.

## Bread or something else?

| Food | Health | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| `bread` | **5** | your field, any time, without hunting |
| `honey_bottle` | 5 | a beehive, and a `glass` that comes back |
| `cooked_fish` | 6 | a `raw_fish` (fishing) plus a `coal_ore` |
| `cheese` | 7 | two milks from goats or camels |

## Tips

- A field of nine crops gives three loaves per harvest, and plenty of seeds to sow it again.
- Bread needs no animals and no coal: it is the easiest food to make in large amounts.

## Trivia

- People have baked bread for at least **14,000 years**: the oldest crumbs were found in a fireplace in Jordan.
- The crust is brown because of the *Maillard reaction*, the same that browns toast and roasted meat.
