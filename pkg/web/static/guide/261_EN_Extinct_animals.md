# Extinct animals

An **extinct** animal is one with none left alive, anywhere in the world. We know what they were like from
their **fossils**: bones, teeth, shells and footprints that were buried and, over a very long time, turned to
stone. Some insects were trapped in resin, which is **amber** today.

You do not run into them in MineCloud either: none appears by itself, and its [egg](74) never comes out of
grass or leaves. To see one you have to find its fossil and bring it back to life.

The fossils are in the [deep rock](262) and in the [amber](263) under the forests, and they come back to life in the [revival machine](264).

## In the bestiary

In the [Bestiary](5) they have a section of their own, at the end of the panel: **Extinct animals**.

- **They are a secret.** Until you have seen one alive, its card is the same for all of them: a silhouette with
  a "?", with no name and no habitat. Its sheet only says: "An extinct animal. Find its fossil and bring it back to life to see it."
- **You discover them by seeing them up close**, like any other animal. You need not have revived it yourself:
  another player's counts.
- **Once discovered**, its card is like the rest, with its portrait, its description and the "Extinct" tag. Its
  **Habitat** is not a biome of the game: it says where and when it really lived.
- **They keep their own count**, next to the title of the section: how many you have "seen alive". They do not
  add to the counter at the top, and the notebook is complete without them.

## The tree of evolution

Some extinct animals are **ancestors**, or ancient relatives, of an animal that lives today. Then the sheet of
both shows the same thing under the title **Evolution**: the portrait of the extinct one, an arrow and the
portrait of the living one, with "Extinct" or "Alive today" under each.

- If you have not discovered one of the two yet, it has no name: with its "?" if it is the extinct one, and as a
  black silhouette if it is the living one.
- An animal of today may have several ancestors: they all show, side by side.
- Click a portrait to go to its card.

## Trivia

- Of all the species that have ever lived on Earth, **more than 99 in every 100** are thought to be extinct.
- Turning into a fossil is very rare: the animal has to be buried at once, in mud or sand, before it is eaten
  or falls apart. That is why most of them left no trace at all.
- Extinct does not always mean millions of years ago: some animals vanished only a few centuries ago, and
  because of people.
- The dinosaurs did not go away completely: **birds are dinosaurs** that are still alive.
