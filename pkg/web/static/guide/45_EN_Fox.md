# Fox

![Fox](mob:fox)

A clever, wary hunter with a bushy, white-tipped tail. By day it hunts rabbits and frogs; at night it **yips to
other foxes** and **steals whatever it finds lying on the ground**.

## Where it lives

- Mostly in **forests**, and also on the **plains**.
- Alone or in pairs. There are at most **4 foxes** at a time.
- Active by day and by night. At night **its eyes shine**.

## Behaviour

- **Quiet life:** it trots about, stops to **sniff the ground**, looks around and sometimes **sits** with its
  tail curled round its feet and **yawns**.
- **Hunting:** if it spots a [rabbit](44) or a [frog](27) on dry land (within 14 blocks), it creeps up **low** and
  slow, **freezes with its rump wiggling**… and **pounces** in a high arc onto the prey. Then it trots off with it
  in its mouth, lies down and eats it. Afterwards it rests a good while before hunting again.
- **At night** it gives a run of **high yips** now and then, and nearby foxes answer.
- **Night thief:** at night, if there are items lying on the ground within 12 blocks (and more than 4.5 from you),
  it goes for them and **carries them off in its mouth**. At dawn it stashes them somewhere and leaves them there.
- **Wary of you:** if you come within 6 blocks (2.2 when sneaking), or walk towards it quickly, it runs. If it is
  carrying something, it runs even sooner.
- **But if you sneak** and stay within 8 blocks, it sits and **watches you with its head tilted**.
- It doesn't like water: if it falls in, it swims for the bank.

There are **4 coats**: red, ginger, arctic (white) and silver. All with a white tail tip and dark "socks".

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit it | **It drops whatever it is carrying**, yelps and bounds away. For a while it won't steal. |
| Defeat it | **3 hits** bare-handed (2 with a stone [sword](71) or better). It leaves 1–2 ![fur](item:fur) `fur`. |
| Tame it | You can't: it goes its own way. |
| `iron_shears` | 1 `fur` every 120 s, if you manage to get close (sneaking). |
| Bait | It is listed with `bait_meat`, but it follows its own behaviour and hardly ever comes. |
| Cage | It fits, but you will only catch it if it wanders in by chance. |
| Egg | `fox_egg` |
| Command | `/mob fox` |

## Tips

- **Don't leave things lying around at night.** If a fox takes something, hit it and it will drop it.
- Robbed and can't find it? Search the area in the morning: at dawn it leaves what it stole on the ground.
- If you crouch and keep still near it, you can watch it hunt.

## Trivia

- The fox's pounce is called *mousing*: real foxes use it to land on mice hidden under the snow.
- Real foxes also **hide food** for later, and sometimes run off with shoes, gloves and balls.
- Their high, repeated yip is a way to talk to other foxes, especially at night.
- The eyes of many night animals shine in the light thanks to a layer that reflects light inside the eye.
- The arctic fox is white to blend in with the snow.
- When the fox makes a catch, nearby [vultures](40) come to the prey.
