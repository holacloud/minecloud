# Meteoric iron

![Meteoric iron](item:meteoric_iron)

An alloy of **iron and nickel**, like that of metal meteorites.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:meteoric_iron) | Meteoric Iron | `meteoric_iron` |

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *Iron that falls from the sky brings nickel: one and one.*

## What it is for

It is a **collectible**: the G key tells you what it is.

## Trivia

- Before people could smelt iron, some got it from meteorites: **Tutankhamun's** dagger is meteoric iron.
- The largest meteorite ever found, Hoba (Namibia), weighs about 60 tonnes.
- Cut and polished, it shows criss-cross patterns, Widmanstätten figures, that only form by cooling over millions of years.
