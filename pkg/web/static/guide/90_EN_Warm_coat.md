# Warm coat

![Warm coat](item:warm_coat)

A leather coat with a fur collar and cuffs. It **softens creature attacks**: every blow takes 1 less health.

## How to get it

It is crafted (C key):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `warm_coat` | **3** [Leather](76) `leather` + **2** [Fur](77) `fur` |

All of it can be had without a fight: fur with `iron_shears`, and leather by tanning fur (2 `fur` + 1 `wood`).

## How to use it

**There is nothing to put on:** just **carry it in your inventory**. If you select it and press G, the chat reminds
you what it does.

**Effect:** −1 damage on every creature attack, **but never below 1**. Blows of 1 damage still take 1.

| Attacker | Without coat | With coat |
|---|---|---|
| ![Cave monster](mob:cave_monster) [Cave monster](21) | 4 | **3** |
| ![Wandering shadow](mob:shadow) [Wandering shadow](37) | 3 | **2** |
| ![Storm golem](mob:storm_golem) [Storm golem](43) (punch) | 2–3 | **1–2** |
| ![Spider](mob:spider) [Spider](20) | 2 | **1** |
| ![Giant mole](mob:mole) [Giant mole](47) | 2 | **1** |
| Bee, crab, goat, scorpion, snake | 1 | 1 |

**It does not protect from:** poison, falls, lightning or the void.

## Coat and mail together

If you carry the coat and the [scale mail](91) at once, **only the better one counts** (the mail, −2). They do not
add up: with the mail on you, the coat is spare.

## Tips

- It is the first garment you can make: **it costs 8 fur and 3 wood** if you tan all the leather.
- Ideal for caves: against the cave monster it saves you a quarter of every blow.
- It goes along with the [feather cape](92) just fine: one guards against creatures, the other against falls.

## Trivia

- Hide and fur coats are among humanity's oldest garments: Ice Age hunters already wore them.
- It is called "warm", but there is no cold in the game: its warmth is for the heart only.
