# Hell ant

![Hell ant](mob:hell_ant)

An ant of the age of the dinosaurs with **a horn on its head** and **two jaws like scythes that closed
upwards**, against the horn. It is an ancient relative of the [ant](135), and **it is a hunter: it runs at you
and bites**, though it is small and weak. It is an [extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself, it has
to be brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Look for amber.** It is under the **forest**, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:amber_rock)
   `amber_rock`: stone with honey-coloured drops that glow a little in the dark. The page on [amber](263) says
   how deep to dig.
2. **Break the block** with a [pickaxe](68). Which insect is inside depends on the place: if it was a hell
   ant, you take ![](item:fossil_hell_ant) `fossil_hell_ant` with you: a piece of amber with the ant in it.
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45 seconds**. It is
   a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived hell ant: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back.

It is **one big ant**, not an anthill: it has no colony, no queen and no trails.

## Behaviour

- While nobody is near, **it strolls** around its place and works its jaws up and down.
- **It is hostile, by day and by night.** When a player comes within **6 blocks**, it goes after them: the
  nearest one, and the same one for everybody who is watching.
- **It runs at 1.4 blocks a second.** You walk at 4.5: you can always get away on foot.
- When it is just over a block from you **it bites**: its scythes snap shut upwards. **1 damage**, at most
  once every 1.2 seconds. You have 20 health: that is 20 bites.
- **It lets go** when you are more than 9 blocks from it, or more than 17 blocks from its home. Then it walks
  back. It never goes further from its home than that.
- If it defeats you, it leaves you alone for 10 seconds.

There are **2 colours**: reddish brown, and black with amber legs.

## Tips

- It is the weakest hunter among the extinct animals: two blows with your bare hand are enough.
- It is small and low: look down, and aim well.
- **Walk away and it gives up.** It does not leave the surroundings of its home.
- With a [shield](252) in your left hand, and facing it, its bite does half the damage.
- Look at its head from the side while it walks: the horn above, the two scythes below.
- In the [Bestiary](5), its sheet and the ant's show the two together under the title **Evolution**.

## The real hell ant

- **When and where.** Hell ants lived in the Cretaceous, the last age of the dinosaurs. The best known come
  from the amber of **Myanmar**, in Asia, which is about **99 million years** old. Others have turned up in
  amber from France and from Canada. Their family lasted some 20 million years.
- **The name.** The first one was called *Haidomyrmex*: "ant of Hades", the underworld of the Greek myths.
  That is why they are all called hell ants.
- **Size.** Tiny, like many ants of today: less than **1 centimetre**. In the game it is some fifty times
  bigger, so that you can see its head.
- **Jaws the wrong way round.** Every ant alive today, and there are more than 14,000 kinds, shuts its jaws
  **sideways**, like a pair of pliers. Hell ants shut theirs **upwards**. Each jaw was a scythe that pointed
  up, towards a horn that grew out of the forehead.
- **Caught in the act.** How did it use them? In 2020 scientists showed a piece of amber with a hell ant that
  still held its prey, a young relative of the cockroaches, **pinned between its jaws and its horn**. It is
  very rare for a fossil to show an animal doing something.
- **A horn with metal.** In one kind, the pad at the end of the horn was hardened with metal, so that it would
  not wear out. Many insects of today do the same with the tips of their jaws.
- **Why it died out.** No hell ant is known from after the great extinction that ended the dinosaurs,
  **66 million years** ago. Nobody knows why they went and other ants stayed.
- **Is it the ant's grandmother?** No. Hell ants were one of the first branches of the family tree of the
  ants, and it left no descendants. The ants of today come from another branch. All of them have the same
  great-grandparents: wasps.
- **In amber.** [Amber](263) keeps an insect whole, with its legs, its feelers and even its hairs. That is why
  we know the shape of its jaws so well. Its colour is not so sure: the red of the game is a guess.
