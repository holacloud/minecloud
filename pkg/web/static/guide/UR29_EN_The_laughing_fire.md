# The laughing fire

> You found a hidden page. A flame whispered it as it died. Few make it this far: most end up at the bottom of
> a lake, following a light.

![Wisp](mob:wisp)

The old folk call it the **will-o'-the-wisp**. It does not burn, bite or strike. It only **deceives**. And it
laughs.

## Where it is born

- Only at **night**. Every 12 seconds there is a chance that one appears, **25–40 blocks** away from you.
- It likes to be born near **water**, on a **shore** or near a **drop** of 4 blocks or more. If it finds
  nothing like that, it sometimes appears on flat ground.
- There are never more than **2** at once.
- At dawn it fades away, and also if you get more than 72 blocks away. Those summoned with an egg or `/mob`
  linger for a minute in daylight, so you can see them.

## How it picks its trap

When you come within about 11 blocks, the wisp **looks ahead**, away from you, in a wide fan and up to
**28 blocks** away. It looks for two things:

- **Deep water**, 2 blocks or more.
- **A cliff**, a drop of 4 blocks or more. Treetops are no good to it.

Of all the dangerous spots it finds, it prefers the **most dangerous**, the **closest** and the one **most
straight** ahead. It hovers right above it: over the water, or over the void at the edge of the cliff.

If you fall for it (into the water, or more than 2.5 blocks below the edge), it **giggles**, goes out and
reappears 14–20 blocks away, somewhere else. For 10 seconds it leaves you alone. The first time, the chat says:
*"Somewhere, a wisp giggles"*.

## It gets tired

This is its great secret:

- If you stay within **6 blocks**, it flees at full speed: **5 blocks per second**, almost as fast as you
  sprinting (6 is your top speed).
- That sprint only lasts **5 to 8 seconds**. Then it **tires** for 2.5 seconds and barely moves at 2.6 blocks
  per second.
- That is your moment: **sprint** (double tap W) and **touch it**, from a little over a block away. Or strike
  it.

When you catch it, it bursts into sparks and leaves **glow dust** (sometimes two). See [Glow dust](85).

## What it does not do

- **It never hurts you.** All the danger is yours to bring: the water and the fall.
- It has no health bar: a single touch is enough.

## Little details

- Its chimes follow a **pentatonic scale**. See [Sounds of the world](103).
- Now and then it **goes out and reappears** further ahead, like a blink.
- There is only **one light** for all wisps: it shines on the one closest to you.
- Its glow dust, with a little wood, makes 4 torches. And the torch is what other creatures of the night fear
  most…

> Some say wisps are the laughter of those who got lost following them. Others say they are just fireflies
> with bad intentions. Nobody has asked one.
