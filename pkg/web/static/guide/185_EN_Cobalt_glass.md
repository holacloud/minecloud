# Cobalt glass

![Cobalt glass](item:cobalt_glass)

**Deep blue** glass, tinted with a little cobalt.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:cobalt_glass) | Cobalt Glass | `cobalt_glass` |

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *Glass (Si + 2 O) turns deep blue with a touch of cobalt.*

## What it is for

- Place it like any block. It is see-through, like [glass](51), but blue. Breaking: **0.22 s**.
- Ideal for stained glass, aquariums and coloured windows.

## Trivia

- The Egyptians were tinting glass blue with cobalt over 3,000 years ago.
- The blue of Ming dynasty Chinese porcelain is cobalt blue.

## Solution

If you would rather discover it yourself, stop reading here.
