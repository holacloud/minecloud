# Scorpion

![Scorpion](mob:scorpion)

A desert stalker that hides by day and hunts by night. It attacks by lunging its curled tail forward, and its
sting **poisons** you.

## Where it lives

- **Desert** and **rocky hills**, always alone, and **not often**.
- It is there by day and by night, but it is **only dangerous at night** (18:00 to 6:00).

## Behaviour

- **By day** it wanders quietly and only attacks if you nearly step on it (within about three and a half blocks).
- **At night** it comes for you as soon as you are within **8 blocks**.
- As it walks, it **opens and closes its pincers** all the time.
- When it attacks, it **lunges its tail** with the sting over its body.

## How to interact

- **Its attack:** **1 damage** plus **poison**: 3 more points of damage, one every second and a half. The message
  "You were poisoned by a scorpion" appears. Armour does not reduce these 1-point hits.
- **Curing the poison:** drink `milk` (G key) or, better, `antivenom`, which also protects you from poison for
  **3 minutes** (see [Antivenom](89)).
- **Defeating it:** **2 hits** bare-handed or with a wooden sword; 1 with a stone sword or better (see [Swords](71)). After the first one its health bar appears.
- **Loot when defeated:**

| Item | Chance |
|---|---|
| ![Venom sac](item:venom_sac) `venom_sac` | always |
| ![Shell](item:shell) `shell` | 25 % |

- **Taming it:** not possible.
- **Bait:** `bait_worms` draws it when it is not chasing you (see [Baits and traps](4)).
- **Cage:** it **does** fit in the `cage_trap`. Once caught it is no longer dangerous.
- **Egg:** `scorpion_egg`, or the command `/mob scorpion`.

## Tips

- If you cross the desert at night, carry `milk` or `antivenom`.
- It is the best source of `venom_sac`: it always drops one. With `milk` you turn it into `antivenom`. The camels
  of the same desert give the milk (see [Camel](29)).
- By day it is almost harmless: the best time to hunt or catch it.

## Trivia

- There are **2 colours**: sandy yellow and black.
- Real scorpions **glow blue-green** under ultraviolet light, and nobody knows exactly why.
- They are arachnids, cousins of spiders (see [Spider](20)): they have eight legs, plus two pincers.
- Some scorpions can go a whole year without eating.
- The sound it makes when hit is one of the highest in the game.
