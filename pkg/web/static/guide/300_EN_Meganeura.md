# Meganeura

![Meganeura](mob:meganeura)

A **giant cousin of the dragonflies**, as big as a hawk: two pairs of **long clear wings**, a round head that
is nearly all **eyes**, and a long thin abdomen. It lived in the swampy forests of 300 million years ago, long
before the first dinosaur. It is peaceful: it **hurts nobody**. It is an [extinct animal](261): it never
appears by itself, it has to be brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Its fossil.** It lies far down, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:fossil_rock_rare)
   `fossil_rock_rare`: a dark rock with a three-toed footprint. Break it with a [pickaxe](68): which animal is
   inside depends on the place, and it may be a ![](item:fossil_meganeura) `fossil_meganeura`. See
   [Fossils](262).
2. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45
   seconds**. It is a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived meganeura: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back.

## Behaviour

- **It flies low**, 1.5 blocks above the ground, from one place to another near its home, at 1.4 blocks a
  second. Its four wings never stop while it flies, the hind pair a little behind the front one, and from
  close by you hear them rustle.
- **It lands.** When it has had nowhere to go for 4 seconds, it comes down to the ground and rests with its
  **wings spread flat**, as dragonflies do: a bee or a beetle folds them, a dragonfly cannot. It takes off
  again as soon as it moves on.
- It is **peaceful**: it comes up to nobody and it never bites. If you hit it, it takes off and stays in the
  air for a while.

Its wings are **0.7 blocks** from tip to tip. There are **2 colours**: a bronze body with green eyes, and a
steel blue body with brown eyes. The wings of both are clear, with dark veins and a dark spot near each tip.

## Tips

- Wait for it to land to look at it closely: the veins of its wings, its huge eyes, the six legs it perched
  and caught other insects with.
- Its wings are as wide as your arms are long: put it next to a [bee](35) to see what "giant insect" means.

## The real meganeura

- **When and where.** It lived about **300 million years ago**, in the **Carboniferous**, the age of the
  great swampy forests whose trees turned into coal. Its fossils were found in **France**.
- **How big.** Its wings were about **70 centimetres** from tip to tip, as wide as a hawk's, and its body some
  30 centimetres long. It is one of the biggest insects that have ever lived. A cousin of it that lived a
  little later, in North America, was just as big.
- **Not quite a dragonfly.** It belonged to the **griffinflies**, the older cousins of the dragonflies. It
  looked like one and lived like one: a hunter of the air.
- **What it ate.** Other insects, which it caught in flight with its spiny legs, as dragonflies do today.
- **Why were insects so big then?** The air of the Carboniferous had **much more oxygen** than today's:
  about a third of it, against a fifth now. An insect has no lungs: the air goes into its body through little
  tubes, and with so much oxygen in the air a big body could still get enough. And there were no birds yet to
  hunt them. Scientists still argue how much each of the two mattered.
- **Why it died out.** Nobody knows for sure. After the Carboniferous the oxygen of the air went down, which
  was bad news for giant insects. The last griffinflies vanished about 250 million years ago, in the greatest
  extinction there has ever been.
- **Its living cousins.** The dragonflies and damselflies of today, which still hunt in flight over ponds and
  rivers. The biggest of them have wings less than 20 centimetres across.
