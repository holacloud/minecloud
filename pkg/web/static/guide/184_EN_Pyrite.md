# Pyrite

![Pyrite](item:pyrite)

Shiny golden cubes of **iron disulfide**: "fool's gold".

| | In-game name | Id |

| ![](item:pyrite) | Pyrite | `pyrite` |

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *"Fool's gold" is iron with two of sulfur.*

## What it is for

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 4 `torch` (*Strike Pyrite Sparks into Torches*) | 1 `pyrite` + 1 `wood` |

Torches without coal.

## Trivia

- Many gold prospectors got excited about pyrite... and were disappointed.
- In prehistory people struck pyrite against flint to make sparks and light fires; its name comes from the Greek for "fire".
- In Navajún, Spain, pyrite forms almost perfect cubes, as if machine-made.

## Solution

If you would rather discover it yourself, stop reading here.

| Reaction | Kind | Product |
|---|---|---|
| `Fe + 2 S → FeS₂ (pyrite)` | compound | 1 `pyrite` |
