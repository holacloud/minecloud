# Bug net

![Bug net](item:bug_net)

A wool net on a wooden stick. It catches **small flying creatures** without hurting them: fireflies, bees, bats…
and even a wisp.

## How to get it

It is crafted (C key):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `bug_net` | 1 `wood` + 2 `white_wool` |

White wool comes from shearing sheep, or it is crafted (2 `leaves` + 1 `flower_red` → 4). The net **never wears
out**.

## Step by step

1. With the net in your hand, get close: the creature must be **4.5 blocks** away at most.
2. Aim at it and **right click**. You do not need sharp aim: the net catches the nearest creature close to the
   crosshair.
3. If you miss, you hear a "swish" through the air. Try again.

## What it catches

| Creature | You get | Details |
|---|---|---|
| ![Fireflies](mob:firefly) [Fireflies](36) | ![](item:firefly_jar) `firefly_jar` | **Only at night** (by day: "Fireflies only come out at night"). Each catch takes 4 fireflies out of the cloud; with 7 or fewer left, you take the whole cloud. |
| ![Bee](mob:bee) [Bee](35) | `bee_egg` | Netting bees **does not anger** the colony. |
| ![Bat](mob:bat) [Bat](38) | `bat_egg` | They are very fast: wait for them to fly close. |
| ![Wisp](mob:wisp) [Wisp](41) | 1 `glow_dust` | Straight into your inventory. |

Eggs are placed like any other [mob egg](74) to release the animal wherever you like.

## Setting fireflies free

**Right click with the net** on a [firefly jar](97) set on the ground: the jar disappears and a firefly cloud flies
again on that spot.

## Tips

- **One cloud gives several jars:** a cloud holds 10 to 16 fireflies and each sweep takes 4, so it gives **2 to 4
  jars** before it is gone.
- **The wisp** is better touched on the run: it bursts and leaves 1 glow dust, sometimes 2. With the net it is always
  1, but you do not have to catch up with it completely: being within 4.5 blocks is enough.
- Swatting the firefly cloud can also give you `glow_dust` (30 %), but the net gives you the whole jar.

## Trivia

- Entomologists (insect scientists) have used fine-mesh nets almost exactly like this one for over two hundred years.
- The net is no use against fish or large animals: that is what the [rod](94) and the [cage trap](96) are for.
