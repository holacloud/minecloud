# Argon lamp

![Argon lamp](item:argon_lamp)

Like the neon lamp, but **blue**: argon with a drop of mercury.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:argon_lamp) | Argon Lamp | `argon_lamp` |

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *Argon with a drop of the liquid metal, in glass, glows blue.*

## What it is for

- Place it like any block. Breaking: **0.3 s**.
- It shines in every graphics mode (with bloom in Ultra), but **does not light up** its surroundings.

## Trivia

- Most "neon" signs that are not red hold argon with a little mercury, which gives off blue and ultraviolet light.
- For other colours, the tube is coated inside with fluorescent powders.

## Solution

If you would rather discover it yourself, stop reading here.

| Reaction | Kind | Product |
|---|---|---|
| `Ar + Hg → argon lamp` | noble gas | 4 `argon_lamp` (uses 1 `glass`) |
