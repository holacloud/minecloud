# Vulture

![Vulture](mob:vulture)

A big, dark scavenger with a **bald pink head** sunk into a ruff of white feathers. It circles patiently over the
desert and the rocks… and it always knows **where someone has fallen**.

## Where it lives

- In the **desert** and on **rocky hills and mountains**.
- It appears in groups of **1 to 3**, already flying together on the same thermal, stacked one above another.
- There are at most **4 vultures** at a time.
- Day and night, with no fixed hours.

## Behaviour

- **It soars in wide, slow circles** high above the ground, wings held in a shallow V with the tip feathers spread
  like fingers. It only flaps to climb.
- Now and then it **comes down to perch** on the highest rock or cactus, hunched, its head sunk into the ruff. If
  you come within 4.5 blocks, it leaves.
- **Carrion:** when an animal or creature dies within 56 blocks, **every vulture that sees it comes**. They
  circle down over the spot, land around it and **peck and squabble**, hissing and spreading their wings to cover
  the food. If it happens in the desert or the rocks, up to two more arrive from beyond the horizon.
- **Ill omen:** if your health drops to **30 % or less** (6 of 20), the vultures nearby **gather over your head**
  and circle until you recover. The chat warns you: "Vultures are circling above you".
- It grunts and hisses; **it never attacks**.

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit it | **12 health**: 3 blows bare-handed, 2 with a stone sword or better ([Swords](71)); defeated, it drops **2 to 3** `feather`. Otherwise it hisses and flies off higher. There is a **40 %** chance it drops a ![feather](item:feather) `feather` (8 s wait). |
| Wait nearby | Vultures drop a feather on their own now and then, if you are within 24 blocks. |
| Tame it | You can't. |
| Bait | It is listed with `bait_meat`, but it flies its own way and in practice does not come down for it. |
| Cage or net | No. |
| Egg | `vulture_egg` |
| Command | `/mob vulture` |

## Tips

- **If you see vultures circling over you, heal now:** eat something with the G key. See [Health, food and
  dangers](9).
- A ring of vultures in the distance means something has just died there.
- They are a good source of feathers: follow a group that has landed to feed and hit them gently.

## Trivia

- Real vultures have **featherless heads** to keep clean when they reach into a carcass.
- A group of vultures circling on a thermal is called a *kettle*, because they look like they are boiling.
- The pale bar under the model's wings recalls the **griffon vulture**, common in southern Europe.
- They have a bad reputation, but they do a huge job: they **clean up the countryside** and stop diseases from
  spreading.
- When the [fox](45) catches a rabbit or you topple a [storm golem](43), the vultures find out too.
