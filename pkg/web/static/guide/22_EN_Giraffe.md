# Giraffe

![Giraffe](mob:giraffe)

A spotted giant with a very long neck that towers over everything. It is calm, goes about alone and can be seen
from far away.

## Where it lives

- **Desert**, always **alone**. It is not one of the most common animals: spotting one is a small prize.
- By day and by night.

## Behaviour

- It strolls slowly across the dry lands without wandering far from where it was born.
- It is a **grazer**: when it stops, it lowers its head as if eating.
- It looks around, flicks its ears and breathes slowly.
- If you hit it, it hops and turns towards you. It never attacks.

## How to interact

- **Hitting it:** it has **12 health** and falls in **3 blows** bare-handed (2 with a stone sword or better, see
  [Swords](71)). Defeated, it drops **1 to 2** `leather` ![Leather](item:leather).
- **Resources:** only its leather, when defeated.
- **Taming it** (see [Taming and breeding](3)):

| Food | Chance per try |
|---|---|
| ![Acacia leaves](item:acacia_leaves) `acacia_leaves`, ![Leaves](item:leaves) `leaves`, ![Jungle leaves](item:jungle_leaves) `jungle_leaves`, ![Birch leaves](item:birch_leaves) `birch_leaves` | 1/4 |

- **Bait:** it comes to `bait_seeds` (see [Baits and traps](4)).
- **Cage:** it does **not** fit: it is too big.
- **Egg:** `giraffe_egg`, or the command `/mob giraffe`.

## Tips

- No trees grow in the desert: bring the leaves from home. Acacia and birch grow on the plains, and jungle trees
  in the forest (see [Wood and leaves](54)).
- It is one of the hardest to tame (1 in 4): take a good pile of leaves.
- Since it does not fit in a cage, to have giraffes at home tame one and lead it back, or get a `giraffe_egg`.

## Trivia

- The giraffe is the tallest animal in the world: it can be more than 5 metres tall.
- It has **seven vertebrae in its neck**, the same as you, only much, much longer.
- A giraffe's "horns" are called **ossicones** and are covered in skin and hair. On the game's model they are the
  two orange stubs on its head.
- Its tongue is almost half a metre long and dark, so it does not get sunburnt.
- No two real giraffes have the same spots, just like our fingerprints.
