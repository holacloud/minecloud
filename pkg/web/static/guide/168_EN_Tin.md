# Tin (Sn)

![Tin block](item:element_sn)

Element number **50** of the periodic table.

| | |
|---|---|
| Symbol | **Sn** |
| Atomic number | 50 |
| Group | 14 |
| Period | 5 |
| Family | other metal |
| In-game name | Tin Block |
| Id | `element_sn` |

## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize SN Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_sn` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

It also comes from its ore, `tin_ore`, [found in the world](248). In the [foundry](235), 1 `tin_ore` gives **2** `element_sn`; by hand, 1 `tin_ore` + 1 `coal_ore` give 1. See [How metals are smelted](249).

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

It takes part in **1** reaction you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *5,000 years ago someone melted plenty of copper with a little tin...*

## In the real world

- "Tin cans" are steel coated with a layer of tin.
- With copper it makes **bronze**.
- Bend a bar of tin and it crackles: the "tin cry".
