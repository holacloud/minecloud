# Spider

![Spider](mob:spider)

A huge, dark, red-eyed spider that rushes at anyone who comes near. It hits hard, but goes down fast.

## Where it lives

- **Forests** and **rocky hills**, always alone, and **not often**: there are never more than 4 wild
  hostiles around you (spiders, cave monsters, scorpions and snakes together). See [How mobs think](104).
- It is dangerous **by day and by night**: it does not wait for dark.

## Behaviour

- While it has not noticed you, it strolls slowly like any other animal.
- If you are within **16 blocks**, it comes straight for you, much faster than it wanders.
- Once it reaches you, it bites every second or so.
- Its eight legs sweep from side to side as it walks.

## How to interact

- **Its attack:** **2 damage** per bite (your maximum health is 20). With a `warm_coat` or `scale_mail` it drops
  to 1.
- **Defeating it:** **2 hits** bare-handed or with most swords; 1 with a gold one (see [Swords](71)). After the first hit its health bar appears above it.
- **Loot when defeated:**

| Item | Chance |
|---|---|
| ![Venom sac](item:venom_sac) `venom_sac` | 50 % |
| ![White wool](item:white_wool) `white_wool` | 30 % |

- **Taming it:** not possible.
- **Bait:** it comes to `bait_meat` when it is not chasing you (see [Baits and traps](4)).
- **Cage:** it **does** fit in the `cage_trap`. Once caught it is no longer dangerous.
- **Egg:** `spider_egg`, or the command `/mob spider`.

## Tips

- Two quick hits are enough: do not run, strike before it bites you several times.
- The `venom_sac` makes `antivenom` with `milk` (see [Antivenom](89)).
- Need wool and have no sheep? Spiders sometimes drop `white_wool`.
- To take a live spider home, catch it with a cage and `bait_meat`: it gives you its egg.

## Trivia

- Real spider silk is, thread for thread, stronger than steel. That is why this spider drops wool.
- Most spiders have **eight eyes**; this one has several red dots on its face.
- Spiders are not insects: they are arachnids, like scorpions (see [Scorpion](31)).
- It was one of the game's first five mobs.

*Arrived in the game on 15 May 2026.*
