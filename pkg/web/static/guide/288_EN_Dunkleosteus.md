# Dunkleosteus

![Dunkleosteus](mob:dunkleosteus)

An **armoured fish**, **3.5 blocks long**: its head and its shoulders are a helmet of bone plates, and in its
mouth it has **no teeth at all**, but bony **blades** that sharpen each other every time it shuts. It is an
ancient relative of the [fish](26), and it is a hunter: **it bites whoever swims in its water**. On the shore
you are safe. It died out hundreds of millions of years ago: in MineCloud you only see one if you find its
fossil and bring it back to life.

## What it was

A **fish**, but not like those of today: one of the armoured fishes, which wore their bones on the outside,
as a box round the head and the chest. Behind the armour its body was soft, with a skeleton of gristle like
a shark's. It breathed with **gills**: it never comes up for air.

## How to get one

1. **Its fossil.** It lies far down, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:fossil_rock_rare)
   `fossil_rock_rare`: a dark rock with a three-toed footprint. Break it with a [pickaxe](68): which animal is
   inside depends on the place, and it may be a ![](item:fossil_dunkleosteus) `fossil_dunkleosteus`. See
   [Fossils](262).
2. **Water.** It is a sea animal: the revival machine has to stand next to **60 blocks of water** joined
   together, or more: a lake, the sea, or a pool that you dig and fill with a [bucket](111).
3. **The [revival machine](264).** With its fossil inside it takes 45 seconds. The Dunkleosteus appears in the
   water.

The server keeps a revived Dunkleosteus: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back.
Think twice about which water you put it in: after that, it is its water.

## The pool it needs

With 60 blocks of water it comes back to life, but to swim at its ease it wants more:

- It is **3.5 blocks long** and about 1 block tall. In a pool of less than about 8 × 6 blocks it can hardly
  go forwards: it turns slowly on the spot and hangs still at times.
- It needs water **2 blocks deep** at the least, and better 3.
- The walls may be [glass](51): it does not go through it, and you can watch it from outside, dry and safe.

## Behaviour

- **It swims in long curves**, a little below mid water, at nearly 1 block a second, driving itself with
  its tail.
- **It is hostile, in the water.** If you are in the water within **9 blocks** of it, it comes for you at
  **2 blocks a second**. You swim a little faster than that.
- **The bite.** When its mouth reaches you it bites: **4 damage**, at most once every 1.2 seconds.
- **On the shore you are safe.** It never leaves the water, and it does not go for a player who is out of
  it. Standing in water up to your knees counts as being in it.
- **It does not go further** than about 6 blocks from where it was revived, unless it is after somebody:
  then it follows up to about 15 blocks from there, and turns back.
- **It looks where it is going**: it slows down and turns before it reaches a wall, the bed or the shore.
- **If its water goes away**, it lies on the ground, waiting. It does not die: as soon as the water is back,
  it swims again.

There are **2 colours**: steel blue with a helmet the colour of old bone, and olive with a rusty helmet.

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit it | It shoots off away from you, at more than twice its speed, for a couple of seconds. Then, if you are still in the water, it comes back. |
| Defeat it | **30 health**: 8 hits bare-handed, 5 with a stone or an iron [sword](71), 4 with a golden one. It leaves **2 or 3** ![raw fish](item:raw_fish) `raw_fish`, which float up. |
| Tame it | You can't. |
| Bait | None interests it. |
| Cage | It does not fit. |
| Egg | `dunkleosteus_egg`: use it next to water. |
| Command | `/mob dunkleosteus`: it appears in the nearest water where it fits; if there is none near, the game tells you. |

## Tips

- **Get out of the water.** One step on to the shore and it forgets you.
- **Hit it from the edge**, with your feet dry: it cannot answer.
- With a [shield](252) in your left hand, and facing it, its bite does half the damage.
- Do not go swimming in a lake where somebody has left one. Look for the helmet first.
- Put it in a tank next to a fish to see the difference in size. In the [Bestiary](5), the sheet of both
  shows them together under the title **Evolution**.

## The real science

- **When it lived.** In the **Devonian**, the "Age of Fishes", between about **380 and 360 million years
  ago**. That is more than 100 million years before the first dinosaur.
- **Where.** In seas all over the world. The best fossils come from the United States, and others from
  Morocco and from Europe.
- **Its name** means "Dunkle's bone". David Dunkle was a scientist who studied fossil fish at a museum in the
  United States.
- **Armour on the outside.** The plates of its head and those of its chest were joined by a hinge at the
  neck. So it could lift its skull while it dropped its jaw, and open its mouth very wide.
- **Blades, not teeth.** The edges of its jaw bones were as sharp as a knife. They rubbed against each other
  like the two halves of a pair of scissors, and so they **sharpened themselves** all its life.
- **Its bite** was one of the strongest of any fish that has ever lived: enough to cut through the armour of
  other armoured fishes. And it was fast: it opened its mouth in a fiftieth of a second, which sucked the
  water in, and its prey with it.
- **How big was it?** Only its armour turned into fossil: the rest was gristle, which rots away. So nobody
  has seen its body or its tail, and they are drawn by looking at its smaller relatives. Old books say it was
  6 to 9 metres long. A study of 2023 says about 3.5 to 4 metres, and very stout, more like a tuna than like
  a shark. The one in the game is 3.5 blocks.
- **What it ate.** Whatever it wanted: sharks, other armoured fishes, and even its own kind. Armour of
  Dunkleosteus has been found with the marks of the blades of another Dunkleosteus.
- **Why it died out.** At the end of the Devonian something went very wrong in the seas, and a great many
  animals vanished. Every armoured fish went with them, about 359 million years ago.
- **Who is left of its family.** Nobody. But the armoured fishes were among the first animals with **jaws**.
  Every animal with a jaw today, you too, comes from fishes of that time.
