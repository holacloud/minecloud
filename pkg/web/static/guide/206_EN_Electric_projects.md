# Electric projects

![Electric lamp](item:electric_lamp)

Five builds to learn by doing. Measure each one with the [multimeter](205).

## 1. The first lamp (easy)

You need: 1 [hand crank](201), 1 [electric lamp](204) and, later, 1 [battery](203).

1. Put the lamp right next to the crank (touching face to face).
2. Right click the crank: the lamp lights up for 4 seconds.
3. Turn it several times in a row: how long can you keep it lit?
4. Now put a battery between the crank and the lamp. Each turn gives 100 W for 4 s: the lamp takes 10 W and the
   other 90 W go into the battery (360 J). After one turn the lamp stays lit for about **36 seconds** more!

**What you learn:** a generator and a consumer that touch already form a network, and a battery keeps the energy for
later.

## 2. Night lights (easy)

You need: 1 [solar panel](201), 1 battery, 1 lamp and a few wires.

1. Put the panel in the open (nothing above it) and join it with wire to the battery and the lamp.
2. In the daytime the panel lights the lamp and charges the battery; at night the battery keeps the lamp lit.

**The maths:** a panel collects about **3000 J** in a day. The lamp is lit day and night: 10 W × 240 s = **2400 J** a
day, so there is a little to spare. At night the battery has to give 10 W × 120 s = **1200 J**, and it holds 3000 J.

**Challenge:** you want 3 lamps. How many panels do you need (3 × 2400 J a day)? And how many batteries for the night
(3 × 1200 J)? (Answer: 3 panels and 2 batteries.)

## 3. Windmill farm (medium)

You need: 3 [wind turbines](201), wire posts, wires and 1 battery.

1. Find the highest hill (or build a tower). Leave 2 free blocks around each turbine for the blades.
2. Join them with posts and wires down to the battery at the foot of the hill.
3. Put one more turbine at the foot of the hill, on its own network, and compare them with the multimeter.

**What you learn:** the wind is stronger up high, and the power grows with the cube of the speed: at the foot of the
hill a turbine may give 20–40 W, and at the top often 75 W or more.

## 4. The water mill (medium)

You need: 1 [water wheel](201), 1 [motor](204), several lamps and 1 [iron door](197).

1. Put the wheel at the edge of a lake so that three of its sides touch the water: 105 W.
2. Join it with wire to a motor (50 W) and a lamp (10 W): 60 W.
3. Put an iron door touching the motor: when the motor starts, the door opens by itself.
4. Add lamps one by one and watch the **demand**. With five lamps there are 100 W; with the sixth, 110 W is more than
   105 W: blackout!

**What you learn:** water is a steady source, day and night. And when demand is bigger than generation, everything
goes off.

## 5. The night greenhouse (hard)

You need: [grow lamps](204), a water wheel or solar panels, batteries, glass, farmland and wheat seeds.

1. Build a greenhouse with a glass roof and sow [wheat](107).
2. Hang grow lamps from the ceiling, at most 4 blocks above the wheat.
3. Power them with a water wheel (105 W runs three grow lamps), or with panels on the roof and batteries.

**What you learn:** plants grow with light, and electric light can help them. Time how long the wheat takes with
and without lamps.
