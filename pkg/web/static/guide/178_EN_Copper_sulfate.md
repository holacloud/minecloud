## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *Copper, sulfur and four oxygens form deep blue crystals.*

## What it is for

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 2 `blue_wool` (*Dye Wool with Copper Sulfate*) | 1 `copper_sulfate` + 2 `white_wool` |

## Trivia

- Growing copper sulfate crystals is a classic school experiment (with gloves: it is not food).
- The blue comes from water molecules bound to the copper; heat it and it loses the water and turns white.

## Solution

If you would rather discover it yourself, stop reading here.

| Reaction | Kind | Product |
|---|---|---|
| `Cu + S + 4 O → CuSO₄ (copper sulfate)` | compound | 2 `copper_sulfate` |
