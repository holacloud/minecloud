# Triceratops

![Triceratops](mob:triceratops)

A **dinosaur that eats plants**, as long as six blocks in a row, on four strong legs. Its head is enormous: a
**beak** like a parrot's, a short **horn on its nose**, two long **horns over its eyes** and a great **frill**
of bone behind them. It stands **2.7 blocks** tall at the top of its frill. It grazes in peace, but **if you hit
it, it lowers its horns and charges**. It is an [extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself, it has to be
brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Look for its fossil.** It lies far down, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:fossil_rock_rare)
   `fossil_rock_rare`: a dark rock with a three-toed footprint. The page on [fossils](262) says how deep to dig.
2. **Break the block** with a [pickaxe](68). Which animal is inside depends on the place: if it was a
   triceratops, you take ![](item:fossil_triceratops) `fossil_triceratops` with you.
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45 seconds**. It is
   a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived triceratops: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back.

## Behaviour

- **It strolls** slowly near its place, swinging its heavy tail.
- It is a grazer: when it stands still, **it lowers its head to crop the plants**.
- It is **neutral**: it attacks nobody who has not attacked it.
- **If you hit it, it charges.** It bellows, turns on you, **lowers its horns** and paws the ground for 1 second.
  Then **it runs in a straight line** at where you were: about 4 blocks, at 6.5 blocks a second. If it gets
  within 3 blocks of you, its horns do **4 damage** and throw you back. After that it stands still for a second
  and a half.
- It only charges whoever hit it, and only that player sees the charge.
- It does not jump off cliffs, and it stops **before its head** reaches a wall, a fence or a drop: it looks
  2.4 blocks ahead of its middle.

There is one colour only: olive green with dark scales and a pale belly, a brown frill with an orange rim, and
pale horns.

## Tips

- **Step aside as soon as it lowers its horns.** It runs at where you were when it set off, not at where you are
  now: one long step to the side and it goes past.
- Its horns are long: stay more than 3 blocks from its middle while it runs.
- A fence between the two of you stops it.
- With a [shield](252) in your left hand, and facing it, the horns do half the damage.
- It is six blocks long: in a forest its head and tail go through the leaves and trunks. Bring it back in an
  open place.

## The real Triceratops

- **When and where.** It lived in the west of **North America**, between about **68 and 66 million years ago**:
  it was one of the very last dinosaurs.
- **Its name** means "face with three horns".
- **Size.** It was about **8 to 9 metres** long and weighed between **6 and 12 tonnes**, as much as a big
  elephant or more. Its skull alone could be **more than 2 metres** long: one of the biggest heads of any
  animal that has ever walked on land. In the game it is smaller, so that it fits.
- **What it ate.** Plants: ferns, palms and other low plants. It cut them with its beak and chopped them with
  rows of hundreds of teeth at the back of its mouth, which were replaced as they wore out.
- **The horns and the frill.** Scientists think it used them to show off to others of its kind and to fight
  them, pushing head against head, and also to defend itself. Bones of Triceratops have been found with the
  tooth marks of a **Tyrannosaurus**, and horns broken and healed again.
- **Why it died out.** About **66 million years ago** a huge rock from space, an asteroid, crashed into the
  Earth, where Mexico is today. The dust darkened the sky for years, the plants withered, and the dinosaurs
  died out, all but one branch: the **birds**.
- **Its family.** Birds come from the dinosaurs, but from another branch, that of Tyrannosaurus. Triceratops
  left no descendants: no animal alive today comes from it.
