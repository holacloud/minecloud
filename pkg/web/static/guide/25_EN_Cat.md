# Cat

![Cat](mob:cat)

A small, striped cat with a collar and green eyes. It is independent: if you walk up calmly, it may decide you
are acceptable, and then it follows you and purrs.

## Where it lives

- **Forests** (more often) and **plains**, in groups of **1 to 3**.
- By day and by night.

## Behaviour

- **It accepts you on its own:** if you come within **7 blocks** of a cat, "A tiny cat decided you are
  acceptable" appears.
  - Only one cat at a time can adopt you this way.
- **It follows you**, and when it reaches you it does not stop in front of you: it **circles around you**.
- **It purrs:** when it is within 3 blocks of you, a soft purr is heard every few seconds.
- While it follows you it **never despawns**.
- Other cats wander, look around and move their ears and tail.
- If you hit it, it hops, gives a high meow and turns towards you. It never scratches.

## How to interact

- **Hitting it:** it has **12 health** and falls in **3 blows** bare-handed (2 with a stone sword or better, see
  [Swords](71)). Defeated, it drops 1 `fur` ![Fur](item:fur).
- **Trimming its fur:** right click with `iron_shears`: it drops 1 `fur` ![Fur](item:fur). It grows back in **120
  seconds**.
- **Taming it** (see [Taming and breeding](3)):

| Food | Chance per try |
|---|---|
| ![Raw fish](item:raw_fish) `raw_fish`, ![Milk](item:milk) `milk`, ![Brown mushroom](item:mushroom_brown) `mushroom_brown` | **1/2** |

- **Baits:** it comes to `bait_fish` and `bait_meat` (see [Baits and traps](4)).
- **Cage:** it **does** fit in the `cage_trap`.
- **Egg:** `cat_egg`, or the command `/mob cat`.

## Tips

- As with the dog, the spontaneous friendship is lost when the page reloads. **Tame it** to keep it, make it sit
  with an empty-handed right click and breed kittens.
- It is the only animal that can be tamed with `milk` (from goats or camels, see [Milk](78)).
- You can have a dog friend and a cat friend at the same time: both follow you.

## Trivia

- There are **3 coats**, each with its own collar:
  - orange tabby with a pink collar,
  - grey-brown tabby with a blue collar,
  - creamy white with a yellow collar.
- Real cats also purr to calm themselves, not only when they are happy.
- A cat spends about two thirds of its life asleep.
- Its whiskers are roughly as wide as its body: they tell it whether it fits through a gap.
- It was the last animal of the first batch: it arrived more than two weeks after the dog.
