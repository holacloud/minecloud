# Sign

![Sign](item:sign)

A wooden **post with a board** on which you can **write a message** for passers-by. Other players can read it and
**react with emojis**.

| In-game name | Id | Break |
|---|---|---|
| Sign | `sign` | 0.35 s |

## How to get it

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `sign` | 2 `planks` + 1 `wood` |

## How to use it

1. **Place it** with a right click. On the ground it stands on its post, **looking at you**. On the side of a block
   it hangs against it as a wall board, with no post. A box opens: "Write the sign message (up to 288 characters)".
2. Write your message, up to **288 characters**, and accept. If you leave it empty or cancel, the sign is not
   placed.
3. **To read it**, **right click** the sign (tap it on a touch screen), or look at it and press **F**: the reader
   opens with the full text and its votes. To build against a sign instead, sneak while you click.

The board is always **one block wide**, the same size, and stays in its own cell. Short texts get big letters. What
does not fit ends in **three dots**: click the sign to read the rest. A post's board reads the right way round
**from both sides**, and it can be read at night too. You can walk past a post, and through a wall board.

If the text has ` // ` in it, the board only shows what comes before it, and the reader all of it: handy for saying
the same thing in two languages.

When you aim at a sign, the block inspector already shows the start of the message.

## Reactions

The reader has five buttons with counters:

| 👍 | 👎 | ❤️ | 😊 | ⭐ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| like | dislike | love it | funny | awesome! |

Each click adds a vote. When playing online, votes are **saved on the server**, everyone sees the same count and the
chat announces who voted and what.

## Tips

- Mark paths: "← Mine", "Sheep farm →".
- Leave a **guest book** at your house and see how many ⭐ it gets.
- Put signs next to your [element blocks](60) or your animals to share fun facts.
- To change the text, break the sign and place it again (the votes are lost).

## Trivia

- The Romans painted adverts and even election slogans on the walls of Pompeii; many survive today.
- 288 characters is more than an old tweet, which had 140.
