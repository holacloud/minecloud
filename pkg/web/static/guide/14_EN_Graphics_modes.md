# Graphics modes

MineCloud has three graphics levels. The higher, the prettier... and the more work for your computer.

| Mode | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Classic** | simple textures and basic lighting, no shadows. The lightest |
| **RTX** | soft shadows, real textures on the main blocks, better lighting and ripples on the water |
| **Ultra** | everything in RTX, plus a realistic sky, **bloom**, sun rays and film effects |

## RTX mode

- Turn it on with the **Toggle RTX** button in the pause menu (**Escape**), with the **/rtx** command, or
  by pressing **R three times in a row** (within about a second).
- Blocks cast **shadows**; without RTX there are no shadows.
- Water shows **ripples** when something touches it.

## Ultra mode

- Turn it on with the **Toggle Ultra** button in the pause menu. The first time it downloads a little extra
  code.
- Turning Ultra on also turns RTX on. Turning RTX off also turns Ultra off.
- What it adds:
  - an **atmospheric sky** that lights the materials,
  - **bloom**: whatever glows shines, like firefly jars or owls' eyes,
  - a **crisp sun** with a soft halo and **sun rays** shafting through the air, without a dazzling sky,
  - a film look: colour grading, vignette and grain,
  - very detailed **crisp shadows**,
  - finer textures, **wind** in the leaves and moving water.

The game **remembers** the mode you chose for next time.

## Pause menu settings

| Setting | What for |
|---|---|
| Look Sensitivity | mouse speed when looking around (0.5 to 2.5) |
| Master Volume | overall volume |
| Render Distance | how much world loads around you (2 to 7); lower it if the game runs slowly |
| FOV | field of view (55 to 100) |
| Toggle Fullscreen | full screen |

## The camera

**V** switches the view, in this order:

1. first person (the usual),
2. third person **from the front**: you see your own face,
3. third person **from behind**,
4. and back to first person.

## Photo mode

**F2** hides the whole interface (hotbar, chat, health, compass, crosshair...) for clean screenshots. Press
**F2** again to bring it back.

**Trick:** combine Ultra, third-person view (**V**) and photo mode (**F2**) at sunset.
