# Scale

![Scale](item:scale)

A hard green reptile scale. Six of them, sewn onto leather, make the **scale mail**, the best protection in the
game.

## How to get it

| Source | How | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| ![Lizard](mob:lizard) [Lizard](30) | Strike it (without killing it): **35 %** per strike, with a **10 s** wait per lizard | 1 |
| ![Lizard](mob:lizard) [Lizard](30) | Defeat it (3 blows) | **1–2** |
| ![Snake](mob:snake) [Snake](42) | Defeat it (3 blows) | **1–2** |

The snake also leaves a `venom_sac` 60 % of the time. But it bites and **poisons**: carry `milk` or `antivenom`.

## What it is for

| Recipe | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| [Scale mail](91) `scale_mail` | **6** `scale` + 2 `leather` |

## Tips

- The **lizard** is the easiest source: it does no harm. But when struck it **drops its tail** and dashes off, so
  chase it for the next strike (10 s later).
- Do the maths: each defeated lizard leaves 1.5 scales on average, and sometimes sheds another while you chase it:
  about **4 lizards** give the 6 scales of a mail.

## Trivia

- Many real lizards drop their tail to escape a predator (it is called **autotomy**) and then grow a new one.
- Reptile scales are made of keratin, just like bird feathers.
- At first lizards could not be defeated, and their loot scales were out of reach. Now they fall in 3 blows.
