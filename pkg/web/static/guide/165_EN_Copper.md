Element number **29** of the periodic table.

| | |
|---|---|
| Symbol | **Cu** |
| Atomic number | 29 |
| Group | 11 |
| Period | 4 |
| Family | transition metal |
| In-game name | Copper Element Block |
| Id | `element_cu` |

## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize CU Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_cu` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

It takes part in **3** reactions you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *5,000 years ago someone melted plenty of copper with a little tin...*
- *Copper, sulfur and four oxygens form deep blue crystals.*
- *Two parts copper and one part zinc give the golden metal of trumpets.*

## In the real world

- It was one of the **first metals** humans used.
- It conducts electricity very well: almost every wire is copper.
- The Statue of Liberty is copper: the green is its patina. The symbol comes from the Latin *cuprum*, after Cyprus.
