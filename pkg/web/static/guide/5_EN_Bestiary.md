# Bestiary

A field guide to everything alive in the world: **35 animals and creatures** and **16 microscopic organisms**, 51 cards in all. Everything it says is in the language of the game.

- **Open:** the **B** key, or the "Bestiary (B)" button in the pause menu.
- **Close:** Escape, B, the "Close" button or a click outside the panel.

## Discovering species

An animal or creature is discovered when you see it up close: within 12 blocks while looking towards it, or within 2.5
blocks even if you are not looking. A new discovery shows "New bestiary entry: …" with a soft chord. Defeating
a creature you had not met yet also discovers it.

## What it shows

- **A grid of cards:**
  - Discovered species show a colour portrait made from their own model.
  - Missing ones appear as a black silhouette with "???" and their habitat. Their sheet gives a hint of where to find them.
  - The progress of all the cards is shown at the top, for example "12 / 51 discovered".
- **A sheet for each animal or creature:**
  - Its model, turning in 3D.
  - Tags for temperament (Passive, Friendly, Neutral or Hostile) and activity ("Active: Day", "Active: Night"…).
  - A description of how it behaves and how to deal with it.
  - Its **Habitat**.
  - The tables of the other mechanics, with their limits:
    - **Diet:** what tames it, see [Taming and breeding](3). Only those that can be tamed show it.
    - **Drops:** what resources it gives, see [Mob resources](2).
    - **Lured by:** the baits that bring it over, see [Baits and traps](4). Only those that walk to a bait show it.
    - **Hook bait:** on fish, what goes on the [fishing rod](94) to make them bite sooner.
  - **Encounters**, **Defeated** and **First seen**.

Only creatures defeated by the player count as defeated, not those hunted by other animals. Your progress is
saved in the browser.

## Under the microscope

The 16 organisms of [the small world](237) are cards like the rest, but you cannot run into them: they are only found through a [microscope](238). They go in the bestiary when you identify one, with a click, and the new entry notice shows then too.

Their sheet shows their drawing, alive, the power they are seen from ("Seen from 40×"), their **Real size**, their **Group**, where they **Live** and how many times they were **Identified**, with a button to their page of the guide. They have no temperament, no diet and no drops, and nobody defeats them.

## The complete notebook

The counter at the top adds up **all** the cards, the 51. But the notebook counts as complete with **the 35 animals and creatures**, without the organisms. When you discover the last one, the chat says: "Your bestiary is complete! Something is written on its last page...", and a code for the guide appears next to the counter. That is why you can see it with the counter at "35 / 51 discovered".

- Identifying organisms neither brings that notice forward nor holds it back: they are a collection of their own.
- One of the 35 never appears by itself: the [copper golem](215) has to be built, or hatched from its [egg](74).
