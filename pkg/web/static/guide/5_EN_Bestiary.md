# Bestiary

A field guide to every species in the world (31).

- **Open:** the **B** key, or the "Bestiary (B)" button in the pause menu.
- **Close:** Escape, B, the Close button or a click outside the panel.

## Discovering species

A species is discovered when you see it up close: within 12 blocks while looking towards it, or within 2.5
blocks even if you are not looking. A new discovery shows "New bestiary entry: …" with a soft chord. Defeating
a creature you had not met yet also discovers it.

## What it shows

- **A grid of cards:**
  - Discovered species show a colour portrait made from their own model.
  - Missing ones appear as a black silhouette with "???" and a hint of where to find them.
  - Your progress is shown at the top, for example "12 / 31 discovered".
- **A sheet for each species:**
  - Its model, turning in 3D.
  - A description of how it behaves and how to deal with it.
  - Tags for temperament (passive, neutral or hostile) and activity (day or night).
  - Its habitat.
  - The tables of the other mechanics: **Diet** (what tames it, see [Taming and breeding](3)), **Drops** (what
    resources it gives, see [Mob resources](2)) and **Lured by** (which baits draw it, see [Baits and traps](4)).
  - How many you have met and defeated, and the date of the first sighting.

Only creatures defeated by the player count as defeated, not those hunted by other animals. Your progress is
saved in the browser.

## Under the microscope

The 16 organisms of [the small world](237) are cards like the rest, but you cannot run into them. They only go in the bestiary when you identify one in a [microscope](238), with a click. Their sheet shows their drawing, alive, and their real size; they have no temperament, no diet and no drops, and nobody defeats them. They do not count for the complete notebook of the world's creatures.
