# Owl

![Owl](mob:owl)

A silent night hunter that perches high up in the trees. By day it sleeps puffed up like a ball; at night its big
eyes glow and **it turns its head to follow you** wherever you go.

## Where it lives

- In **forests** and, less often, on the **plains**, always **on top of a tree**, on the leaves. Now and then it
  moves to another tree.
- Always **alone**. There are at most **3 owls** at a time.
- It sleeps **by day** and is awake **at night**.

## Behaviour

- **By day** it sleeps with its eyes shut and its feathers puffed up. If you pass nearby (within 7 blocks, and
  not sneaking), **it opens one eye** to keep watch on you.
- **At night** its eyes **glow** (with a halo in Ultra mode) and the pupils widen in the dark.
- **The head:** it follows you with its gaze **up to 135° either way**, in quick jerks, like real owls. Now and
  then it **tilts its head** curiously or bobs it up and down to judge the distance. If you go behind it, its head
  whips across to the other side, and if you stay there, **it shuffles round** on its perch.
- **It hoots** now and then ("hoo… hoo-hoo-hoooo" or a two-tone "hoo-hoo"), and nearby owls answer. It hoots
  less in the rain.
- **It hunts** [bats](38) in flight and [rabbits](44) in the open: it locks its gaze on the prey, bobs its head
  and **swoops silently** with its talons forward. It carries the catch back to its perch and swallows it whole.
- **If you come too close** (3.2 blocks at night, 1.8 by day, half that when sneaking), it flies off to another
  tree. It also leaves if you break the leaves it stands on.
- It flies almost **without a sound**.

There are **3 kinds**: a brown horned owl with feather tufts (orange eyes), a grey owl (yellow eyes) and a snowy
owl (white, pale yellow eyes).

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit it | **It screeches** and flies to another tree. There is a **40 %** chance it drops a ![feather](item:feather) `feather` (8 s wait). |
| Defeat it | **3 hits.** It leaves 1–3 `feather`. |
| Wait nearby | It drops a feather on its own now and then, if you are within 24 blocks. |
| Tame it | You can't: it is a wild hunter. |
| Bait | It is listed with `bait_meat`, but it follows its own behaviour and does not come. |
| Cage or net | No. |
| Egg | `owl_egg`: it appears in the air and glides to the nearest treetop (or to the ground, if there are no trees). |
| Command | `/mob owl` |

## Tips

- To see one up close, **go by day and sneak**: asleep, it lets you get much closer.
- At night, look for two **glowing eyes** high up in the trees. If you hear hooting, another owl nearby is
  answering.
- Its feathers make the `feather_cape` and the feather bed. See [Mob resources](2).

## Trivia

- Real owls cannot move their eyes: that is why they **turn their heads up to 270°** in all, 135° either way,
  just like the one in the game.
- They fly silently thanks to comb-like edges on their feathers, which break up the noise of the air.
- They swallow their prey whole and later cough up a little ball of bones and fur, called a **pellet**.
- When an owl answers another, in the game it always uses the short hoot: **"hoo-hoo", two notes**.
- **The owl's riddle.** *At three in the night the owl turns its head as far as it can, from one side to the
  other: keep the first two figures of those degrees. Add the first letter of the alphabet, the one that opens
  every book. End with the notes of the hoot an owl answers another with. Type those four signs into the guide
  and you will see what the owl sees at three.*
