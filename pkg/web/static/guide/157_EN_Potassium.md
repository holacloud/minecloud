Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize K Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_k` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

It takes part in **1** reaction you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *Potassium, joined to a halogen, also feeds the fields.*

## In the real world

- **Bananas** are famous for their potassium, which your nerves and muscles need.
- It reacts with water with a lilac flame.
- Its symbol comes from the Latin *kalium*.
