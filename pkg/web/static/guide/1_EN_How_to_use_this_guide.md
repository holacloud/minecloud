# How to use this guide

This guide is a book inside the game. You can open it at any time while you play.

## Opening and closing

- **H** opens the book **at the page where you left it**, even after a reload.
- **H**, **Escape**, the **✕** button or a click outside the book close it.
- The pause menu (**Escape**) also has a **Guide (H)** button.

## Turning pages

- The **◀ ▶** buttons at the bottom, or the **← → arrow keys**, go to the previous or next page.
- They only walk through the public (numbered) pages, skipping numbers that do not exist yet.
- The **Contents** button takes you back to [page 0](0).
- Coloured words are **links**: click them to jump to that page.

## Going to a given page

- Type a number in the **Go to** box and press **Enter**.
- In the chat, type **/guide 12** to open page 12 directly. Just **/guide** opens the book where you left
  it.

> If you ask for a page that does not exist yet, the book tells you so. Page numbers never change: it may
> be written in a future version.

## Language

The **ES** and **EN** buttons at the top switch the language. The book **remembers** your choice; the first
time it follows your browser's language.

If a page is not translated yet, it is shown in the other language with a notice.

## Printing the guide

Rather read it on paper? The **🖨 Print** button at the bottom opens the **printable guide** in a new tab: a
book with a cover, contents and the pictures of mobs and items, with every numbered page.

- Pick the paper: **A5** (pocket-book size; also on A4 with "2 pages per sheet") or **A4**.
- Wait until it is laid out, then press **Print** (or save it as a PDF), with margins set to "None".
- **Ink saver** prints on a white background; turn it off for parchment.
- The **clear font** is easier to read on paper; the **pixel font** looks more like this book.
- On paper the only numbers are the ones **at the foot of each sheet**: the contents and the links (→ p. 12)
  point to them. At the top of each sheet, a small "In-game page 34" tells you which page it is here:
  **/guide 34**.

Secret pages are never printed: those you have to find by playing.

## Secret pages

Some pages have no number, but a **code of letters and digits** (up to 8). No index lists them and the
arrows never reach them.

- To read one, type its code in **Go to** or use **/guide CODE** in the chat.
- The hints are hidden around the book... and around the world. Read carefully and keep an eye on what
  happens around you.
