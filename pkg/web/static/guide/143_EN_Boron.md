## How to get it

Like every [element block](60), it is synthesised with the C key (*Synthesize B Block*):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `element_b` | 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore` |

Each block is **one atom** at the [lab bench](117).

## In the lab

It takes part in **1** reaction you have to discover by mixing in the flask. Hints:

- *Two silicons, with boron on one side and phosphorus on the other, turn light into electricity.*

## In the real world

- Boron makes the **borosilicate** glass of labs and some oven dishes, which survives sudden changes of temperature.
- Borax, a boron compound, is what makes slime stretchy.
- Plants need a tiny amount of boron to grow.
