# Swords

![Iron sword](item:iron_sword)

A gleaming sword to feel like a knight. Be warned: in this version of the game it is **mostly for show**.

## How to get them

They are the cheapest tools: they need **2** of the material instead of 3.

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| `wood_sword` | 2 `planks` + 1 `wood` |
| `stone_sword` | 2 `cobblestone` + 1 `wood` |
| `iron_sword` | 2 `iron_ore` + 1 `wood` |
| `gold_sword` | 2 `gold_ore` + 1 `wood` |

## What it does (and does not do)

- **No extra damage.** Each blow to a creature that can be defeated takes **4 health**, sword or no sword: a
  ![Spider](mob:spider) [spider](20) falls in 2 blows and a ![Cave monster](mob:cave_monster)
  [cave monster](21) in 4.
- **It is not the tool for any block.** Even so, like any tool, metal ones speed up all mining a little: ×1 for
  wood, ×1.05 for stone, ×1.43 for iron and ×1.76 for gold.
- **It shows.** Online, other players see in your hand whatever you have selected: a gold sword looks impressive.
- It never wears out.

## Tips

- For real speed, a [pickaxe](68), [shovel](69) or [axe](70) of the same metal is better.
- A gold sword is a handy "all-rounder": it speeds up any block ×1.76.
- To defend yourself from shadows, a [torch](62) does more than a sword.

## Trivia

- Japanese swords (*katanas*) were forged by folding the steel many times to make it strong and flexible.
- Toledo, in Spain, was famous for centuries for its steel swords.
- Real gold swords were only ceremonial: gold is far too soft for fighting.
