# Antivenom

![Antivenom](item:antivenom)

A bottle of pale green liquid. It cures poison and also makes you **immune to poison for 3 minutes**.

## How to get it

It is crafted (C key):

| Result | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 1 `antivenom` | 1 [Venom sac](84) `venom_sac` + 1 [Milk](78) `milk` |

| Venom sac from… | Chance when defeated |
|---|---|
| ![Scorpion](mob:scorpion) [Scorpion](31) | 100 % |
| ![Snake](mob:snake) [Snake](42) | 60 % |
| ![Spider](mob:spider) [Spider](20) | 50 % |

## Drinking (G key)

| Health | Effect |
|---|---|
| **+1** | **cures poison** and gives **3 minutes of immunity** to poison; gives the `glass` back |

Unlike milk, **you can drink it at full health and without poison**: that way you are protected before you get
bitten. The chat confirms it: "venom will not harm you for 3 minutes".

## How the immunity works

- For 3 minutes, the venom of ![Snake](mob:snake) snakes and ![Scorpion](mob:scorpion) scorpions takes no health:
  it is cut short as soon as it starts.
- **The bite still hurts:** you take the blow itself (1 damage) anyway. You are only spared the 3 poison damage that
  follow.
- If you drink another antivenom, the 3 minutes start again from that moment; they do not add up.

## Tips

- **Drink it right before heading** into the desert at night or into snake country.
- Each antivenom saves up to 3 health per bite: against a scorpion that bites you several times, it shows.
- Carry a milk as well: it is cheaper for curing a one-off poisoning.

## Trivia

- The first serum against snakebite was made by **Albert Calmette** in the 1890s, at the Pasteur Institute.
- In the game, venom is cured with venom itself: the idea behind vaccines and serums.
