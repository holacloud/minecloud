# Horse

![Horse](mob:horse)

A calm animal that grazes in herds on the plains. It is the **only mount** in the game: climb on and you will
cross the map at a gallop.

## Where it lives

- On the **plains** (the `plains` biome) and, less often, in **forests**.
- It appears in herds of **2 to 4** horses.
- Day or night, it keeps no hours.

## Behaviour

- It ambles slowly about its spot and **lowers its head to graze** when it stands still.
- It breathes, looks around and flicks its ears, like the other farm animals.
- When you climb on, it **neighs**. At a gallop you hear the **clop of the hooves**, faster the faster it goes.
- In the air it **tucks its legs in**, and its gait changes with the speed, from a trot to a gallop.

There are **4 coats**:

| Coat | Looks like |
|---|---|
| Bay | dark brown with a black mane and a white blaze on the face |
| Chestnut | tan with a flaxen mane, light socks and a white blaze |
| Black | black from head to hoof |
| Dapple grey | light grey with spots, darker mane and legs |

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Right click with an empty hand | **You ride it.** Space jumps, Shift gets off. See [Riding](7). |
| Hit it | It cannot be defeated: it gives a start and turns towards you. |
| Tame it | Yes: `tall_grass`, `flower_yellow` or `honeycomb`, with a **1 in 4** chance per try. See [Taming and breeding](3). |
| Make it sit (tamed) | **Shift + right click**, because a plain click rides it. |
| Breed | Two tamed horses in love have a foal, which cannot be ridden until it grows up. |
| `iron_shears` | 1 ![fur](item:fur) `fur` every 120 s. |
| Bait | `bait_seeds`: it comes over to eat. |
| Cage or net | It is too big for the cage. |
| Egg | `horse_egg` |
| Command | `/mob horse` |

## In the saddle

- It gallops at **9 blocks per second** (twice your walking speed), and a bit more if you sprint.
- **It hops up one-block steps by itself**, without you having to jump.
- Its jump is much higher than yours: about **twice** as high.
- Other players see you on your horse, with its coat.
- If there is no room for horse and rider (a low ceiling), you cannot ride it.

## Tips

- **Tame one before a long trip:** if you make it sit when you get off, it waits where you left it, and it does
  not despawn when you walk away.
- Carry `tall_grass`: it tames it, puts it in love and makes `bait_seeds`.
- Careful galloping across plains and forests: the [giant mole](47) feels the heavy hoofbeats and comes to see
  what is going on.

## Trivia

- Real horses can sleep **standing up**, thanks to a way of "locking" their knees.
- Their eyes, on the sides of the head, see almost all the way round, except right behind them and right in front
  of the nose.
- The white stripe on the face is called a **blaze**, and white lower legs are **socks**.
- A grey horse is born dark and turns grey as it gets older.
