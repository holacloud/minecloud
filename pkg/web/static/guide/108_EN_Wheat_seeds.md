# Wheat seeds

![Wheat seeds](item:wheat_seeds)

A handful of pale grains. Sown on farmland, each one grows into a wheat plant.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:wheat_seeds) | Wheat Seeds | `wheat_seeds` |

## How to get them

| Source | Seeds |
|---|---|
| Breaking ![](item:tall_grass) `tall_grass` | 1, **35 %** of the time (the tall grass drops too) |
| Breaking a ripe wheat crop | **1–2** (plus 1 `wheat`) |
| Breaking an unripe wheat crop | 1 (the seed you sowed) |

They cannot be crafted.

## What they are for

- **Sowing:** right click ![](item:farmland) farmland (grass or dirt tilled with a [hoe](72)) with nothing on top.
  See [Growing wheat](107).
- **Seed bait:** 1 `wheat_seeds` → 2 `bait_seeds` (C key). See [Baits](93).
- **Ducks:** they tame, feed and put in love ![Duck](mob:duck) [ducks](18). See [Taming and breeding](3).

Seeds are not food for you: G does nothing with them.

## Tips

- Your first seeds come from tall grass: mow a meadow and you will have a pocketful in no time.
- Do not turn all your seeds into bait: keep a few to sow, since each harvest gives more back.

## Trivia

- A wheat "seed" is really a whole fruit, called a *caryopsis*: the seed and the fruit wall are fused together.
- Stored cool and dry, wheat grains can still sprout after many years.
