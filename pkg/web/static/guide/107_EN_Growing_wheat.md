# Growing wheat

![Farmland](item:farmland)

With a hoe, a handful of seeds and a little patience you can grow your own wheat field, and bake bread from it.

## Step by step

1. **Get seeds.** Mow tall grass (`tall_grass`): **35 %** of the time it also drops
   ![](item:wheat_seeds) [wheat seeds](108).
2. **Till the soil.** Hold any [hoe](72) and **right click** a grass or dirt block that has nothing on top. It
   turns into ![](item:farmland) **farmland** (`farmland`), dark soil with furrows.
3. **Sow.** Hold the seeds and **right click** the farmland. A green sprout appears on it.
4. **Wait.** The wheat grows by itself through four stages.
5. **Harvest.** Break the ripe, golden wheat with a left click: you get ![](item:wheat) [wheat](109) and 1–2 seeds
   to sow again.
6. **Bake.** 3 `wheat` → 1 ![](item:bread) [bread](110) (C key), which restores **5 health** (G key).

## The four stages

| Stage | Looks like | If you break it |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Sprout | short green shoots | 1 `wheat_seeds` |
| 2. Young | taller green stalks with leaves | 1 `wheat_seeds` |
| 3. Earing | green-yellow ears | 1 `wheat_seeds` |
| 4. **Ripe** (*Ripe Wheat*) | tall and **golden** | **1 `wheat` + 1–2 `wheat_seeds`** |

Each stage takes **between 50 and 90 seconds**, so a field ripens in **2½ to 4½ minutes**: about one in-game
day (a day lasts 4 minutes). It grows by day and by night, and also while you are away.

## Good to know

- **Everyone sees the same field.** The server makes the wheat grow, so all players see each stage at the same
  time, and a field keeps growing even when nobody is near it.
- **Farmland** breaks in 0.45 s and gives **dirt** back; a [shovel](69) speeds it up. If you break the farmland
  under a crop, the crop pops off too and drops what it would drop.
- Wheat only grows on farmland: if the soil under it stops being farmland, it stops growing.
- The crop is not solid: you walk through it, like tall grass. It breaks in 0.1 s.
- Seeds only go on farmland. Right clicking anything else with them just reminds you to till first.

## Feeding animals

`wheat` tames, feeds and puts in love ![Sheep](mob:sheep) [sheep](17), ![Pig](mob:pig) [pigs](19),
![Camel](mob:camel) [camels](29), ![Goat](mob:goat) [goats](32) and ![Horse](mob:horse) [horses](34). Seeds do the
same for ![Duck](mob:duck) [ducks](18). See [Taming and breeding](3).

## Tips

- One ripe crop gives 1–2 seeds back, so **each harvest lets you sow more**: start small and the field grows on
  its own.
- Till a whole row first and sow it in one go: a row of wheat ripens at about the same time.
- Keep a few seeds in your pocket when you go out: mowing tall grass on the way refills them.

## Trivia

- Wheat was one of the first plants ever farmed, about **10,000 years ago** in the Fertile Crescent.
- An ear of wheat holds 30 to 50 grains.
