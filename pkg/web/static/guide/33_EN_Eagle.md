# Eagle

![Eagle](mob:eagle)

A huge bird of prey with brown plumage, a **white head and tail** and a golden beak. It spends nearly all its time
in the sky, circling slowly over the mountains, and only comes down to perch on the highest point around or to
fish.

## Where it lives

- **Rocky hills and mountains** (the `rocky` biome), its favourite ground, and also the **plains**.
- It appears alone, already flying high, when its part of the map is generated.
- There are at most **2 eagles** at a time.
- It keeps no hours: it flies the same by day and by night.

## Behaviour

- **It soars in circles** on rising warm air, banking into every turn. It only flaps to climb, take off or land;
  the rest of the time it glides.
- Every so often it **perches on the highest spot** it can find nearby (never over water). There it folds its
  wings and turns its head to watch the land.
- **Fishing:** when it spots a fish swimming near the surface (less than 28 blocks away), it **stoops** on it,
  thrusts its talons forward at the last moment, splashes and carries it off. Then it flies to a perch and eats
  it, holding it down under its talons.
- **Screech:** now and then it gives a shrill cry you can hear up to about 48 blocks away.
- If you come within 5 blocks while it is perched, it **takes off** screeching and goes to circle far away from
  you.

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit it | It cannot be defeated. It screeches, takes off and moves away. There is a **50 %** chance it drops a ![feather](item:feather) `feather` (8 s wait). |
| Tame it | You can't: it is a wild bird of prey. |
| Bait | It is listed with `bait_fish`, but it flies its own way and in practice does not come down for it. |
| Cage or net | It does not fit in a cage and the net cannot catch it. |
| Egg | `eagle_egg` |
| Command | `/mob eagle` |

## Tips

- **Feathers without harm:** eagles are the best source of `feather` (50 % per hit). Collect 6 for the
  `feather_cape` ([Feather cape](92)).
- To watch it fish, stand still near a school of [fish](26) in a mountain lake.
- To see one up close, find its perch: it is usually the highest rock around. Move slowly, because it startles at
  5 blocks.

## Trivia

- The model looks like the **bald eagle**, with its white head, yellow eyes and hooked golden beak.
- Real eagles spend hardly any energy in the air: they ride **thermals**, columns of warm air rising from the
  ground, just as the one in the game does.
- The dive is called a *stoop*; some birds of prey go faster than 300 km/h doing it.
- [Rabbits](44) fear it: when an eagle flies low nearby or dives, the whole family runs for the burrow.
- The fish it takes really leaves its school: you can see it hanging from the talons as the eagle flies.
