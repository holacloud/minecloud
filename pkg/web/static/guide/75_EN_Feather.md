# Feather

![Feather](item:feather)

A light white bird feather. It is the material of the **feather cape** and the **feather bed**, and you can almost
always get it **without harming anyone**.

## How to get it

**Strike a bird** (without killing it): now and then it drops a feather. Each bird has a wait before it can drop
another one.

| Bird | Chance per strike | Wait |
|---|---|---|
| ![Duck](mob:duck) [Duck](18) | 40 % | 8 s |
| ![Macaw](mob:macaw) [Macaw](23) | 40 % | 8 s |
| ![Owl](mob:owl) [Owl](46) | 40 % | 8 s |
| ![Vulture](mob:vulture) [Vulture](40) | 40 % | 8 s |
| ![Eagle](mob:eagle) [Eagle](33) | **50 %** | 8 s |

**Moulting:** ducks, macaws, owls and vultures within **24 blocks** of you drop a feather on their own now and
then (on average, one every 6 or 7 minutes per bird). Just walk near a flock.

**Loot:** the ![Owl](mob:owl) owl, if you defeat it (3 blows), leaves **1–3 feathers**.

## What it is for

| Recipe | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| [Feather cape](92) `feather_cape` | **6** `feather` + 2 `white_wool` |
| Feather [bed](63) `bed` | **4** `feather` + 2 `white_wool` + 3 `planks` |

A feather cannot be eaten and is not used for taming.

## Tips

- A **pond with ducks** is the best farm: tap them gently every 8 s and pick up what falls.
- The **eagle** is the most generous (50 %), but after the blow it takes to the air.

## Trivia

- Real feathers are made of **keratin**, like your hair and nails.
- Real birds also **moult**: they swap old feathers for new ones once or twice a year.
- For centuries people wrote with sharpened goose quills, which is why a fountain pen's tip is still called a nib
  and "pen" comes from the Latin *penna*, feather.
- Vultures cannot be defeated in the game, but they do shed feathers when struck.
