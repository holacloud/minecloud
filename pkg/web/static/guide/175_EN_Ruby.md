# Ruby

![Ruby](item:ruby)

A red gem: **corundum** (aluminium oxide) with a pinch of chromium.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:ruby) | Ruby | `ruby` |

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *Corundum (two aluminium, three oxygen) turns red with a pinch of chromium.*

## What it is for

It is a **collectible**: the G key tells you what it is. Keep it, or show it to your friends.

## Trivia

- Pure corundum is colourless; 1 % chromium is enough for the red.
- It is the hardest mineral after diamond (9 on the Mohs scale).
- The first laser, in 1960, worked with a ruby crystal.

## Solution

If you would rather discover it yourself, stop reading here.

| Reaction | Kind | Product |
|---|---|---|
| `2 Al + 3 O + Cr → Al₂O₃ : Cr (ruby)` | coloured crystal | 1 `ruby` |
