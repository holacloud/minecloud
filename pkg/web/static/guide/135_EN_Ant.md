# Ant

![Ant](mob:ant)

Ants live in **colonies** around an **anthill**: a mound of soil with the entrance on top. They are tiny, but they
are never alone: by day you will see **lines of ants** going to and from the anthill carrying crumbs of food.

## Where it lives

- On the **plains**, in **forests** and in **deserts**, on grass, dirt or sand. There are none in rocky areas.
- There are three kinds of anthill:

| Anthill | Ants | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Mound of **pine needles** | red, wood ants | mostly in forests |
| Mound of **soil** | black, garden ants | mostly on the plains |
| Mound of **sand** | red, harvester ants | in the desert |

- Each colony has **8 to 20 ants**. There are at most **4 anthills** around you at a time.
- They come out **by day** in fair weather. **At night and in the rain** they all go home and stay inside.

## Behaviour

- They leave the anthill **one at a time**; up to **14** can be out at once.
- Some are **scouts**: they wander off looking for food up to about **13 blocks** away. If they pass near food,
  they take a **crumb** (carried on their back, in the colour of the food) and go back home.
- Back home they lay a **scent trail** (a pheromone) that **recruits** other ants. The others follow that way
  **in single file**, and every trip with food makes the trail stronger. Look closely and the way shows as a
  **line of faint dots** on the ground: the busier, the clearer.
- If nobody walks it (the food is gone), the trail **fades** by itself in a couple of minutes.
- Food they look for:
  - **Flowers** (`flower_red`, `flower_yellow`), **mushrooms** and **wheat** that is growing or ripe.
  - **Food lying on the ground**: seeds, wheat, bread, honeycomb, cheese, fish, mushrooms, flowers and baits.
  - **Bait piles** of seeds, honey or meat ([Baits and traps](4)). These they do eat: each crumb is a little bit of
    the pile.
- They take steps in their stride: they climb up the face of the block, like real ones.
- **If you disturb them** (step on the anthill or strike it, or an ant), up to **12 ants** run out to your feet
  for about **10 seconds**. While two or more are on you, they **bite**: **1 damage every 3 seconds**. Get more
  than 14 blocks away and they go back home.
- The colony **replaces** its ants: a new one is born every 20 s while they are calm.

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Step on the anthill | "You stepped on an anthill! The ants swarm out": they come out to bite you. |
| Hit an ant | One blow squashes it ("You squashed an ant") and it counts in the [Bestiary](5). It drops nothing. The rest of the colony gets angry. |
| Hit the anthill | The mound shakes and the colony comes out to bite you. Blows cannot destroy it. |
| Break the block under it or place one on it | The anthill disappears and the ants scatter. |
| Net (`bug_net`) | Aim at the anthill or the ants: you get an ![jar](item:ant_colony_jar) `ant_colony_jar` with **a young queen and five workers**. The colony needs at least 6 ants, and it does not get angry. |
| Bait | `bait_seeds`, `bait_honey` or `bait_meat`: within 13 blocks, they find it and open a trail to it. |
| Tame them | You can't: it is a colony. But you can keep them in an [ant farm](136). |
| Cage | No: they slip out between the bars. |
| Egg | `ant_egg` on grass, dirt or sand: an anthill appears with its colony. |
| Command | `/mob ant` |

## Tips

- **To watch an ant trail:** put down a pile of `bait_seeds` or drop some bread a few blocks from the anthill, by
  day, and wait. First a scout comes; then the whole line.
- **Don't stand on the anthill:** the ants won't calm down while you are still there.
- To start an [ant farm](136), catch ants with the net from a big colony.

## Trivia

- A colony works as a **superorganism**: each ant does little on its own, but together they find food, build
  and defend the nest as if they were a single animal.
- Ants are organised in **castes**: the **queen** lays all the eggs; the **workers** (all female) find food, look
  after the young and dig; the **males** only appear for the mating flight.
- They talk mostly with **pheromones**, scent signals. The trail to food grows stronger with every trip and
  evaporates if nobody uses it: that way the colony always "chooses" the nearest, richest food.
- Their body has **three parts** (head, thorax and abdomen), **six legs** and **elbowed antennae**, with a bend in
  the middle. They walk on a **tripod**: three legs on the ground and three in the air, taking turns.
- Wood ants thatch their anthill with **pine needles**; desert harvester ants store **seeds** in underground
  granaries.
- More than **14,000 species** of ants are known, and a black garden ant queen lived for almost **29 years** in a
  laboratory.
