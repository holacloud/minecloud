# Lithium battery

![Lithium battery](item:battery)

A rechargeable cell of **lithium cobalt oxide**, like the one in your phone.

| | In-game name | Id |
|---|---|---|
| ![](item:battery) | Lithium Battery | `battery` |

## How to get it

At the [lab bench](117), with a reaction you have to **discover**: it shows as "???" in the list until you mix the right atoms in the flask. Hint:

> *Lithium, cobalt and two oxygens store your phone's energy.*

## What it is for

For now it is a **collectible**: keep it for when machines and electricity arrive.

## Trivia

- John Goodenough found lithium cobalt oxide as a battery material in 1980.
- Lithium-ion batteries won the 2019 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for Goodenough, Whittingham and Yoshino.

If you would rather discover it yourself, stop reading here.
