# Venom sac

![Venom sac](item:venom_sac)

A little purple pouch full of venom, with a green drop hanging from it. On its own it is useless, but mixed with
milk it becomes **antivenom**.

## How to get it

Only as **loot**, by defeating venomous or dangerous creatures:

| Mob | Blows to defeat it | Chance |
|---|---|---|
| ![Scorpion](mob:scorpion) [Scorpion](31) | 2 | **100 %** |
| ![Snake](mob:snake) [Snake](42) | 3 | 60 % |
| ![Spider](mob:spider) [Spider](20) | 2 | 50 % |

The scorpion is the sure source, but it only comes out at night. Scorpions and snakes **poison** when they bite: 1
damage and then 1 health every 1.5 s, 3 times.

## What it is for

| Recipe | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| [Antivenom](89) `antivenom` | **1** `venom_sac` + 1 `milk` |

## Tips

- **First sac, first milk:** go scorpion hunting with a `milk` on you. With the first sac and another milk you
  already have an antivenom, which protects you for 3 minutes.
- Spiders also give `white_wool` (30 %), handy for the [feather cape](92) or the [bug net](95).

## Trivia

- Real antivenoms are made roughly like this: a horse or a sheep is given a little venom, its body makes
  **antibodies**, and the serum is prepared from them.
- The game's loot list includes a venom sac (15 %) for defeating a frog, but frogs cannot be defeated: they always
  hop away.
