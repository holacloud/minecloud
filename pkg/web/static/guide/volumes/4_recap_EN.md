# Before you start

This volume is about fossils, the machine that brings extinct animals back to life, those animals one by one, the
portal network and the big buildings. This chapter reminds you of what volumes 1 to 3 told, in case you do not have them
at hand. Every idea comes with the number of the topic that tells the whole story.

## Digging

- How deep you are is your height, the **y**: the water is at y = 5 ([Water](52)) and bedrock, the floor nothing
  can break, at y = −14 ([Stone and bedrock](50)). The world has four biomes: plains, forests, rocks and desert
  ([Biomes](15)).
- A ![](item:iron_pickaxe) [pickaxe](68) breaks stone much quicker: 3 `cobblestone`, 3 `iron_ore` or
  3 `gold_ore`, plus 1 `wood`. The gold one is the quickest. A [shovel](69) does the same for dirt and sand.

## Electricity, in a few lines

- Power is measured in **watts** (W): 1 W is 1 joule every second ([Electricity](200)).
- Electric blocks that touch **face to face** make a network. `copper_wire` takes it further: 12 wires from
  1 `copper_block` ([Wires and networks](202)).
- The [generators](201): a hand crank gives 100 W for 4 seconds with every turn you give it with a right click; a
  water wheel, 35 W for every side touching water, up to 105 W; a solar panel, 40 W when the sun is high. The
  [radioisotope generator](220) gives 80 W all the time, day and night.
- A full [battery](203) stores 3 kJ and gives up to 200 W.
- **All or nothing:** if a network cannot feed every [consumer](204), they all switch off.
- A **signal** comes from a lever, which stays on, or from a push button, which lasts 1.5 seconds
  ([Switches](207)). A [sensor](208) or a [logic gate](209) that is on gives a signal too.

## Water

- Water **does not flow**: it stays exactly where you put it, and under it you never drown ([Water](52)).
- The ![](item:bucket) [bucket](111) is made from 3 `iron_ore` and carries **one block** of water: right click
  water to fill it, right click the face of a block to pour it.
- 3 `sand` give 2 `glass` ([Sand, cactus and glass](51)): with glass walls you can watch what swims inside.

## Taming, breeding and riding

- **Taming:** with the food it likes in your hand, right click the animal. Each try uses one up: hearts when it
  works, grey smoke when it fails. A tamed animal follows you, listens only to you, and the server keeps it
  ([Taming and breeding](3)).
- **Breeding:** feed two tamed adults of the same kind, within 8 blocks of each other, and a **baby** will be born,
  which grows up in about 5 minutes.
- **Riding:** right click with an empty hand or with any block that is not its food, and **Shift** to get off.
  Double tap **W** to gallop and **Space** to jump ([Riding](7)). The [horse](34) goes 9 blocks a second.
- The `cage_trap` catches small animals and gives you their egg ([Baits and traps](4)). With an egg in your hand,
  right click a block and the animal appears on it ([Mob eggs](74)).

## The bestiary

**B** opens it. An animal is discovered when you see it up close: within 12 blocks while looking at it, or within
2.5 blocks even if you are not ([Bestiary](5)).

## Health and danger

- You have 20 health points, and they only come back when you eat, with **G**. A `warm_coat` in your inventory
  takes 1 damage off every blow of a creature, and a `scale_mail` 2 ([Health, food and dangers](9)).
- A [sword](71) is made from 2 of a material + 1 `wood`. Bare-handed you take 4 health a blow; with a sword, 5 for
  wood, 6 for stone, 7 for iron and 8 for gold.
- The ![](item:shield) [shield](252) is made from 4 `planks` + 2 `iron_ore`. Move it to your
  [left hand](251) with **X**: the blows of a creature in front of you do half the damage. It does not stop falls or
  poison.

## For travelling and for the buildings

- A [plot](260) is a piece of the world, 17 × 17 blocks around its marker: only you and whoever you invite can
  build inside. `/home` takes you there, once every 30 seconds, and `/spawn` to your bed
  ([Chat commands](12)).
- The sun **rises in the east**, stands **in the south** at noon and sets in the **west**. The compass at the top
  of the screen says which way you are looking ([Day, night and weather](8)).
- A [chest](226) opens with a **right click**, and every player sees what it keeps.
- [Lights without electricity](228) shine by themselves, by day and by night: a candle gives a light of 9, a
  lantern 13 and a chandelier 14.
- [Stained glass](229) is coloured glass, and what you see through it takes its colour. A [mirror](232) is silver
  on glass: come close and you will see yourself.
- [Furniture](223) with a front faces you when you place it.
