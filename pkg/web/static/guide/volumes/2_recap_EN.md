# Before you start

This volume is about science: farming, the weather, chemistry and the elements, genetics, the stars, ants and
beavers, blueprints and the robot. This chapter reminds you of what volume 1 told, in case you do not have it at hand. Every idea comes
with the number of the topic that tells the whole story: type it in the game after `/guide` ([Chat commands](12)).

## Breaking and placing blocks

- **Breaking:** hold the **left mouse button** on a block, up to 4.5 blocks away ([Mining and building](10)).
- **Placing:** pick the block in the hotbar (keys **1–8** or the mouse wheel) and **right click** the face where
  you want it. **E** opens the full inventory ([Mining and building](10)).
- **Tools:** the ![](item:iron_pickaxe) [pickaxe](68) is for stone and ores, the [shovel](69) for dirt and sand,
  and the [axe](70) for wood. The [hoe](72) also **tills**: right click grass or dirt and it turns into farmland,
  ready for seeds.
- They are made from 3 of their material (`planks`, `cobblestone`, `iron_ore` or `gold_ore`) and 1 `wood`
  ([Crafting](11)), and they **never wear out** ([Pickaxes](68)).

## Crafting

There is no crafting table: everything is made from the **recipes panel**, wherever you are ([Crafting](11)).

1. Press **C**.
2. Type in the search box the name of what you want, or a tag such as `#chemistry` or `#science`.
3. Click the card: you see the ingredients and how many of each you have.
4. Press **Craft**.

Almost every lab item, instrument and machine in this volume is made this way.

## Stone, coal and metals

Chemistry begins underground.

- Breaking stone gives you **cobblestone**. **Smooth stone** (`stone`) comes from the *Smelt Stone* recipe:
  1 `cobblestone` + 1 `coal_ore` make 2 `stone` ([Stone and bedrock](50)).
- Ores lie in veins inside the stone. ![](item:coal_ore) Coal, iron and gold go into recipes just as they are
  ([Ores](53)).
- Copper, iron and gold blocks are made from 2 `stone` + 1 `sand`, and you get 2. Copper also comes from its
  ore: 1 `copper_ore` + 1 `coal_ore` make 1 `copper_block` ([Fine stones and metals](59)).
- The **36 element blocks** are all made the same way: 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore`. In the list they appear as
  *Synthesize H Block*, *Synthesize HE Block*... Each one has its chemical symbol on its faces ([Element
  blocks](60)). In the lab of this volume, **every block is one atom**.

## Sand, glass and water

- **3 `sand` make 2 `glass`**. Glass works as a **bottle**: with it you milk a goat or a camel, and drinking milk
  or honey gives it back ([Sand, cactus and glass](51)).
- Water **does not flow**: it stays exactly where you put it. The sea is at height **y = 5**. You never drown
  under water; hold **jump** to rise ([Water](52)). In this volume you will learn to carry it in a bucket.

## Day, night and rain

All of this is in [Day, night and weather](8):

- A day lasts **4 real minutes**. Night runs from 18:00 to 6:00, about 2 minutes. It is the same time for
  everybody, and **/time** tells you.
- The sun **rises in the east**, stands **in the south** at noon and sets in the **west**. The compass at the top
  of the screen says which way you are looking.
- Rain is rare: it rains on **one day in twenty**, a single shower, at the same time for everybody. The wind
  blows harder before the rain and while it rains.

The weather station measures all of that, and astronomy looks at the sky of those nights.

## Tamed animals

Genetics works with your animals and their young ([Taming and breeding](3)):

- **Taming:** hold the food the animal likes and right click it. Each try uses up one; when it works, hearts
  appear.
- **Breeding:** feed a tamed adult. It falls **in love** for 20 seconds. If another one of its species is in love
  within 8 blocks, a **baby** is born. Each parent rests 2 minutes before breeding again.
- **The baby** is born half size and grows up in about 5 minutes. It inherits its coat colour, its size and its
  speed from its parents: one gene from each.
- **Baits:** a little pile of bait lasts 2 minutes and draws the animals that like it from up to 16 blocks. Ants
  open a trail to a bait near their nest, and the **bug net** on an anthill gives you an `ant_colony_jar`
  ([Baits and traps](4)).

## Health and food

You have **20 health points**, and they do not come back by themselves: you have to eat. Select the food in the
hotbar and press **G** ([Health, food and dangers](9)). Some things from the lab are also drunk with **G**.
