# What is new

Topics of earlier volumes that have changed since they were printed:

- [Sand, cactus and glass](51): sandstone, plain and cut, made from sand, and why it shows layers.
- [Water](52): two blocks made from ice, the snow block and packed ice, and why snow is white.
- [Slabs](56): the sandstone slab and the six glazed tile slabs.
- [Bricks](57): glazed tiles in six colours, with their stairs and slabs, and the metal that colours each glaze.
- [Fine stones and metals](59): magma rock, basalt with cracks that give light of level 10.
- [Light and darkness](105): magma rock among the other lights.
- [Astronomy](132): the sundial and the astrolabe, read with a right click.
- [Quicklime](183): white and red lime stucco, and the lime cycle that turns back into stone.
