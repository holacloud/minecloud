# Before you start

This volume is about building and machines: doors and fences, electricity and logic, furniture, the workshop, the
microscope and its microbes, and plots. This chapter reminds you of what volumes 1 and 2 told, in case you
do not have them at hand. Every idea comes with the number of the topic that tells the whole story.

## Every day

- **C** opens the recipes panel, wherever you are. The search box understands tags: try `#electric`,
  `#furniture` or `#microscope` ([Crafting](11)).
- Hold the **left mouse button** to break and **right click** to place, up to 4.5 blocks away ([Mining and
  building](10)). A ![](item:iron_pickaxe) [pickaxe](68) is quicker on stone and ores, and an [axe](70) on wood.
- How deep you are is your height, the **y**: the sea is at y = 5 ([Water](52)) and bedrock, the floor nothing
  can break, at y = −14 ([Stone and bedrock](50)). The world has four biomes: plains, forests, rocks and desert
  ([Biomes](15)).
- You have 20 health points, and they only come back when you eat, with **G**. A `warm_coat` or a `scale_mail`
  in your inventory takes damage off every blow of a creature ([Health, food and dangers](9)). A [sword](71)
  takes 4 health a blow bare-handed, and up to 8 with a gold one.

## Wood, stone, sand and metal

Most recipes in this volume start from these.

- 1 `wood` gives 4 `planks` ([Crafting](11)), and 3 `sand` give 2 `glass` ([Sand, cactus and glass](51)).
- Breaking stone gives `cobblestone`. 1 `cobblestone` + 1 `coal_ore` make 2 smooth `stone` ([Stone and
  bedrock](50)).
- Ores lie in veins inside the stone. ![](item:coal_ore) Coal, iron and gold go into recipes just as they are
  ([Ores](53)).
- Copper, iron and gold blocks: 2 `stone` + 1 `sand` give 2 of them. By hand, 1 `copper_ore` + 1 `coal_ore`
  give 1 `copper_block` ([Fine stones and metals](59)).
- The 36 element blocks are all made the same way, 1 `stone` + 1 `coal_ore`, and carry their chemical symbol on
  every face ([Element blocks](60)). The one this volume uses most is **silicon**, `element_si`: solar panels,
  logic gates, the copper golem and the radioisotope generator all take it ([Silicon](152)).

## Water and light

- Water **does not flow**: it stays exactly where you put it, and under it you never drown ([Water](52)).
- The ![](item:bucket) [bucket](111) is made from 3 `iron_ore` and carries one block of water: right click water
  to fill it, right click the face of a block to pour it. The water wheel and the eel tank both
  need it.
- A ![](item:torch) torch gives light of level **14**, which drops one level with every block it spreads ([Light
  and darkness](105)). 1 `wood` + 1 `coal_ore` make 4 of them ([Torch](62)). The microscope sees nothing without
  light.

## The lab

- The [lab bench](117) is made from 4 `planks` + 2 `glass` + 1 `iron_ore`, and a right click opens it.
- Every element block is **one atom**. You put atoms in the flask and, when the mix matches a reaction
  **exactly**, you press **React**. There are 30 reactions: 4 are known from the start (water, salt, glass and
  steel), and the rest are discovered by mixing just the right atoms ([Chemistry](115)). The 36 elements sit in
  their places on the [periodic table](116).
- What this volume asks of the lab:
  - the `water_flask`: 2 H + O, and it uses 1 `glass` ([Water flask](118));
  - the [light bulb](187), the ![](item:iodine_tincture) [iodine tincture](186) and [cobalt glass](185): three
    reactions to discover, whose topics give a hint;
  - for twice as many solar panels or batteries, the [solar cell](177) and the [lithium battery](189), two more
    reactions to discover.
- The [thermometer](113), made from 2 `glass` + 1 `element_hg`, goes into the incubator.

## Weather, wheat and robots

- A day lasts **4 real minutes**, and it rains on one day in twenty ([Day, night and weather](8)). The
  [weather station](112) measures the temperature and the wind and knows in advance whether it will rain: wind
  turbines and sensors depend on that weather.
- Wheat grows on farmland: right click grass or dirt with a hoe and sow the seeds that tall grass drops ([Growing
  wheat](107)).
- The [programmable robot](193) digs and builds by itself, following a program you snap together. It is yours
  only: other players do not see it, and it is kept in your browser. [Blueprints](191) measure a building and
  let you build it again, block by block.

## The bestiary

**B** opens it. An animal is discovered when you see it up close: within 12 blocks while looking at it, or within
2.5 blocks even if you are not ([Bestiary](5)). The microbes you identify under the microscope go in as cards too.
