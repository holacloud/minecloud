# Dog

![Dog](mob:dog)

A light brown dog with a red collar that, as soon as it sees you up close, decides you are its best friend. It
follows you everywhere and even defends you.

## Where it lives

- **Plains** and **forests**, in groups of **1 to 3**. It is not one of the most common animals.
- By day and by night.

## Behaviour

- **It makes friends on its own:** if you come within **10 blocks** of a dog, the message "An adorable dog is now
  your friend" appears.
  - Only one dog at a time can be your friend this way.
- **It follows you** at a brisk pace and stops about 2 blocks away.
- While it follows you it **never despawns**, however far you both go from where it was born.
- **It defends you** from the [Cave monster](21): if one is within 2 blocks of your dog, it bites it again and
  again (4 damage each time) until it is gone. "Your dog defended you from the cave monster" appears.
- Other dogs wander like any animal, looking around and moving their ears and tail.
- If you hit it, it hops, gives a bark and turns towards you. It never bites.

## How to interact

- **Hitting it:** it has **12 health** and falls in **3 blows** bare-handed (2 with a stone sword or better, see
  [Swords](71)). Defeated, it drops 1 `fur` ![Fur](item:fur). If it was your dog friend, you lose your companion.
- **Trimming its fur:** right click with `iron_shears`: it drops 1 `fur` ![Fur](item:fur). It grows back in **120
  seconds**.
- **Taming it** (see [Taming and breeding](3)):

| Food | Chance per try |
|---|---|
| ![Raw fish](item:raw_fish) `raw_fish`, ![Brown mushroom](item:mushroom_brown) `mushroom_brown` | **1/2** |

- **Bait:** it comes to `bait_meat` (see [Baits and traps](4)).
- **Cage:** it **does** fit in the `cage_trap`.
- **Egg:** `dog_egg`, or the command `/mob dog`.

## Tips

- The spontaneous friendship is not kept when the page reloads. **Tame it** to make it truly yours: it is saved,
  it can sit with an empty-handed right click and it can have puppies.
- Together with the cat, it is the easiest animal to tame: 1 try in 2.
- If you head for the rocky hills, take the dog: it will help you with cave monsters. But whatever the dog
  defeats leaves no loot.

## Trivia

- Real dogs have a sense of smell tens of thousands of times keener than ours.
- The dog was the first animal humans domesticated, more than 15,000 years ago.
- Dogs wag their tails more to one side or the other depending on whether they feel happy or nervous.
- In the game there is only one dog: light brown fur, dark ears and a red collar.
