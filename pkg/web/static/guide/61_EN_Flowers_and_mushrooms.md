# Flowers and mushrooms

![Red flower](item:flower_red)

Small plants that grow on grass. They are **food**, **dye**, **bait** and many animals' favourite treat.

| | Plant | In-game name | Id | Eaten (G key) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ![](item:flower_red) | Red flower | Red Flower | `flower_red` | not edible |
| ![](item:flower_yellow) | Yellow flower | Yellow Flower | `flower_yellow` | **+1** health |
| ![](item:mushroom_red) | Red mushroom | Red Mushroom | `mushroom_red` | **+3** health |
| ![](item:mushroom_brown) | Brown mushroom | Brown Mushroom | `mushroom_brown` | **+4** health |

## Where they grow

| Biome | What you will find |
|---|---|
| Forest | mostly red and brown **mushrooms**; a few flowers |
| Plains | **meadows** of red and yellow flowers, in patches |
| Rocky | scattered brown mushrooms |

## How to get them

They break instantly (0.08–0.1 s) and drop the same plant. They are not solid: you walk through them.

## What they are for

| Recipe (C key) | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| 4 wool of any colour (`*_wool`) | 2 `leaves` + **1** `flower_red` |
| 1 `spray_paint` | 1 `sand` + **1** `flower_red` + 1 `coal_ore` |
| 2 `bait_meat` | 1 `leather` + **1** `mushroom_brown` |

## Tips

- The **brown mushroom** is the best wild food: +4 health. A forest is a pantry.
- Plant flowers near a hive and watch the bees come and go.
- Keep **red flowers**: they make wool of **any** colour.

## Trivia

- The red mushroom with white spots looks like the **fly agaric** (*Amanita muscaria*), which is truly
  **poisonous**: in real life, never eat a mushroom you do not know!
- Fungi are more closely related to **animals** than to plants.
- The largest known living thing is a fungus in Oregon (USA) spreading across almost 10 km² underground.
- Bees see ultraviolet: many flowers have "landing strips" we cannot see.
