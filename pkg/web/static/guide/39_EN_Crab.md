# Crab

![Crab](mob:crab)

A shore dweller that **walks sideways** across the sand, swivels its eyes on their stalks and clacks its
pincers. It is shy, but if you hit it, **it pinches you**.

## Where it lives

- On **sandy beaches** next to water, in any biome.
- It appears in groups of **2 to 4**. There are at most **8 crabs** at a time.
- Active by day and by night.

## Behaviour

- **It walks sideways** in short bursts, its body always square to the way it goes. It often shuffles back the
  way it came.
- When it stands still it **waves a claw**, **picks food** off the ground and brings it to its mouth, or turns a
  little on the spot.
- **Its eyes move each on its own**, and blink by folding down.
- **It clacks its pincers** now and then, and its eight legs patter as it runs.
- It wades into **shallow water** (up to 1.6 blocks deep), walking on the bottom and blowing bubbles, but never
  into the deep.
- **If you come near** (within 4 blocks, 1.8 when sneaking), it runs off, towards the water if it lies that way,
  and on soft ground (sand, dirt, grass, gravel, clay or snow) **it buries itself** until only its eyes stick
  out. It comes back up 8 to 18 seconds later, once you have moved away.

There are **3 colours**: red, orange and sandy.

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Hit it | It rears up on its back legs with its claws open, faces you and **pinches you** if you stay within 1.5 blocks: **1 damage** and a shove. Then it runs off and buries itself. |
| Defeat it | **3 hits** bare-handed (2 with a stone [sword](71) or better). It leaves 1–2 ![shell](item:shell) `shell`. |
| Tame it | You can't: it goes its own way. |
| Bait | `bait_fish`: it comes over to eat. |
| Cage | **Yes**: put `bait_fish` inside and wait. |
| Egg | `crab_egg` |
| Command | `/mob crab` |

## Tips

- **Sneak up** to see it close without it burying itself.
- To catch one, set a `cage_trap` on the sand with `bait_fish` inside. See [Baits and traps](4).
- `shell` with `sand` makes 4 `limestone`. See [Mob resources](2).
- If it has buried itself, step back a little: as soon as you leave, it comes out.

## Trivia

- Real crabs walk sideways because their legs bend more easily to the sides than forwards.
- Their stalked eyes can **fold down** for protection, just like the game's crab does when you hit it.
- They have **ten legs**: eight for walking and two turned into claws.
- Many crabs bury themselves in the sand to hide from gulls and other predators.
- Clacking claws is a way to warn or scare off other crabs.
