# Electric eel

![Electric eel](mob:eel)

A **long** fish (almost two blocks) with a **dark olive** back, a **yellowish** belly and an orange throat, a broad,
flat head and **tiny eyes**. Under its belly runs a **long fin** that ripples like a ribbon. It looks calm, but
**don't touch it**: it is a living battery.

## Where it lives

- On the **bottom of deep lakes and rivers**: water at least **2 blocks** deep, in any biome.
- **Solitary** and **rare**: there are at most **2** at a time.
- It turns up **more often at night**, although it is active at all hours.

## Behaviour

- It **swims slowly along the bottom**, changing course now and then, and sometimes **lies still** on the bottom
  with only its fin rippling.
- **It comes up for air:** about once a minute it **rises to the surface**, gulps a mouthful of air (ripples, a
  splash and a *gulp*) and goes back down. Real electric eels **breathe air**!
- **Electric discharge:** if you **touch** it, **swim into** it or **strike** it, it discharges:
  - **Blue arcs** crackle through the water around it, with an electric **crackle**.
  - If you are **in the water** within **3 blocks**, you lose **2 health**.
  - If you are **out of the water** but **right next to it** (within 1.6 blocks), you lose **1**.
  - It needs about **3.5 seconds** to recharge before the next one.
  - **Fish** nearby are stunned and the school **bolts**.
- After a discharge it **swims away** for a few seconds.
- In the **Ultra** graphics mode a very faint **blue flicker** runs along its body.
- **Out of water** it wriggles on the ground; if there is water next to it, it flips back in. It lasts a minute out
  of the water.

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Touch it or swim into it | Discharge: 2 damage if you are in the water nearby, 1 if you are out of it right next to it. |
| Hit it | Discharge (if it is charged) and it swims off. |
| Defeat it | **3 hits** bare-handed (2 with a stone [sword](71) or better). It leaves 1–2 ![raw fish](item:raw_fish) `raw_fish`. |
| Right click with an ![bucket](item:bucket) **empty bucket** | **You catch it** with its water: you get an ![eel bucket](item:eel_bucket) `eel_bucket`. Aiming near its body, within 4.5 blocks, is enough. |
| Tame it | You can't, but you can keep it in an [eel tank](214). |
| Bait and cage | No: it never leaves the water. |
| Egg | `eel_egg` (it must go into deep water) |
| Command | `/mob eel` (needs deep water nearby) |

## Tips

- To catch it, come **from outside the water**: on the bank it only reaches you if you are right next to it, and
  even then it takes 1, not 2.
- A school of fish shooting off for no reason? There may be an eel on the bottom.
- When you see it **coming up for air**, it is a good moment to catch it: it is near the surface.
- In a [tank](214) it is a **25 W generator** that works day and night.

## Did you know?

- **It is not an eel.** The electric eel (*Electrophorus electricus*) is a **knifefish** from the rivers of South
  America (the Amazon and the Orinoco), closer to catfish than to true eels. It swims mostly with its **anal fin**,
  the long ribbon under its belly.
- It can discharge up to **860 volts**, the record for any animal (the species *Electrophorus voltai*, described in
  2019). A home socket in Europe gives 230 V.
- The electricity comes from thousands of cells called **electrocytes**, which fill most of its body. Each one gives
  only about **0.15 V**, but they are **stacked in a row**, like the batteries in a torch: thousands of them added
  together give hundreds of volts. It is the same idea as the game's [batteries](203).
- It uses **weak** discharges to find its way and "see" in muddy water, and **strong** ones to hunt and defend
  itself: they stun fish instantly. Sometimes it **leaps out of the water** and presses its chin against an
  attacker so that the shock is not lost in the water.
- **It breathes air:** it takes most of its oxygen through its mouth, which is full of blood vessels. It comes up
  every few minutes, and if it cannot, it drowns.
- It can be **more than 2 metres** long and weigh about 20 kg.
- In 1800 **Alexander von Humboldt** watched eels in Venezuela fight off horses wading into their water. That same
  year **Alessandro Volta** invented the **electric pile** by stacking metal discs, and called it an "artificial
  electric organ", because electric fish had inspired it.
