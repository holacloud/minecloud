# Pig

![Pig](mob:pig)

A round, pink pig that roots around in forests and meadows. It is peaceful, easily startled and very easy to
find.

## Where it lives

- **Plains** and **forests:** in both it is one of the most common animals.
- By day and by night, in groups of **1 to 3**.

## Behaviour

- It strolls slowly near where it was born, stopping every few steps.
- It is a **grazer**: when it stands still, it lowers its snout to the ground.
- It looks around, flicks its ears and breathes.
- If you hit it, it hops, gives a low grunt and turns towards you.

## How to interact

- **Hitting it:** it cannot be hurt or defeated.
- **Resources:** none for now: it cannot be sheared or milked.
- **Taming it** (see [Taming and breeding](3)):

| Food | Chance per try |
|---|---|
| ![Brown mushroom](item:mushroom_brown) `mushroom_brown`, ![Red mushroom](item:mushroom_red) `mushroom_red`, ![Honeycomb](item:honeycomb) `honeycomb` | 1/3 |

- **Baits:** it comes to `bait_seeds` and `bait_worms` (see [Baits and traps](4)).
- **Cage:** it **does** fit in the `cage_trap`.
- **Egg:** `pig_egg`, or the command `/mob pig`.

## Tips

- Mushrooms grow in the forest, right where it lives: take a few along to tame it on the spot.
- If you have no mushrooms, a `honeycomb` from a beehive works too.
- A cage with `bait_worms` in a forest draws pigs; catch one and you take its egg home.

## Trivia

- Real pigs barely sweat: they roll in mud to cool down.
- Their sense of smell is so keen that they are used to find truffles underground. That is why it likes
  mushrooms!
- Pigs are very clever: in lab tests they learn tricks faster than many dogs.
- In the game there is only one colour: pink, with darker little spots on its back.
