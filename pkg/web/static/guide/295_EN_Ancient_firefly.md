# Ancient firefly

![Ancient firefly](mob:ancient_firefly)

**An early firefly**: a soft beetle of the age of the dinosaurs that already carried **a lantern at the tip
of its belly**. It is an ancient relative of the [firefly](36), it harms nobody, and **at night its light
blinks on and off**. It is an [extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself, it has to be brought back to
life.

## How to get one

1. **Look for amber.** It is under the **forest**, inside the stone, in the block ![](item:amber_rock)
   `amber_rock`: stone with honey-coloured drops that glow a little in the dark. The page on [amber](263) says
   how deep to dig.
2. **Break the block** with a [pickaxe](68). Which insect is inside depends on the place: if it was an ancient
   firefly, you take ![](item:fossil_ancient_firefly) `fossil_ancient_firefly` with you: a piece of amber with
   the beetle in it.
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45 seconds**. It is
   a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived ancient firefly: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back.

It is **one beetle** with a body, big enough to look at: not a cloud of lights like the fireflies of today.

## Behaviour

- **It flies low**, a little under 1 block above the ground, from one place to another near its home, with
  its wing covers lifted and its wings beating.
- **It lands.** When it has had nowhere to go for 5 seconds, it comes down to the ground and shuts its wing
  covers over its back. It takes off again as soon as it moves on.
- **At night it lights up.** From **18:00 to 6:00** its lantern glows softly all the time, and every 2.5 to
  4.5 seconds it **flares** for nearly a second. Each firefly keeps its own rhythm. The light comes on little
  by little at dusk and goes out at dawn.
- By day the lantern is there, pale yellow and unlit.
- It is **peaceful**: it comes up to nobody and attacks nobody. If you hit it, it takes off and stays in the
  air for a while.

There is one colour only: a dark body, an orange shield over its head and dark wing covers with a pale edge.

## Tips

- **Revive it and wait for the night** (see [Day, night and weather](8)): by day it is just a small dark
  beetle.
- Its light shows from far away, but it lights nothing around it: you still need your torch.
- Wait for it to land to see how a beetle shuts its wing covers.
- It is small: look for it at the height of your knees, near the machine that revived it.
- In the [Bestiary](5), its sheet and the firefly's show the two together under the title **Evolution**.

## The real ancient firefly

- **When and where.** It lived about **99 million years ago**, in the Cretaceous, the last age of the
  dinosaurs. It was found in the amber of **Myanmar**, in Asia.
- **A beetle, not a fly.** Fireflies are beetles with a soft body. Like every beetle, they have two hard wing
  covers, and under them the two wings they fly with.
- **The lantern.** In the amber you can still see its light organ, near the tip of the belly, very much like
  that of a firefly of today. So fireflies have been lighting up the night for at least 99 million years: the
  dinosaurs saw them.
- **How the light is made.** Inside the lantern two substances meet with the oxygen of the air, and light
  comes out. It is a **cold** light: nearly all the energy turns into light and hardly any into heat. A light
  bulb cannot do that.
- **What the light is for.** The grown-ups of today blink to find a mate, and each kind has its own rhythm.
  The larvae glow too, and that is a warning: "I taste bad". Scientists think the warning came first, and the
  love messages later.
- **Size.** Small, like the fireflies of today: less than 2 centimetres. In the game it is many times bigger,
  so that you can see it.
- **Did it die out?** That kind, yes. But its family is still here: there are more than 2,000 kinds of
  fireflies today.
- **In amber.** [Amber](263) keeps an insect whole, even one as soft as this. Its colour is not so sure: the
  orange shield of the game is borrowed from the fireflies of today.
