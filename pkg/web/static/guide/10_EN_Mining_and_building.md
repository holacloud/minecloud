# Mining and building

## Mining

- **Hold the left mouse button** on a block, up to **4.5 blocks** away.
- Cracks appear, chips fly and your hand strikes. When the cracks cover it, the block breaks.
- If you let go or look at another block, you start over.

Each block takes its own time with an empty hand:

| Block | Time |
|---|---|
| Mushrooms, flowers, `tall_grass`, `torch` | 0.1 s |
| `leaves` | 0.2 s |
| `grass`, `sand`, `dirt` | 0.35–0.45 s |
| `wood`, `planks` | 0.6–0.8 s |
| `stone`, `cobblestone` | 0.9–1 s |
| Ores | 1.05–1.15 s |
| `obsidian` | 3.5 s |
| `bedrock` | unbreakable |

## Tools

With the right tool in your hand, you mine faster:

| Tool | Good for |
|---|---|
| ![](item:iron_pickaxe) [Pickaxe](68) | stone, ores, bricks, element blocks |
| ![](item:iron_shovel) [Shovel](69) | dirt, sand, grass |
| ![](item:iron_axe) [Axe](70) | logs and planks |
| ![](item:iron_hoe) [Hoe](72) | leaves and grass |
| ![](item:iron_shears) [Shears](73) | leaves and wool |

| Material | Right block | Other block |
|---|---|---|
| Wood | ×1.45 | ×1 |
| Stone | ×1.9 | ×1.05 |
| Iron | ×2.6 | ×1.43 |
| Gold | ×3.2 | ×1.76 |

For example, a gold pickaxe breaks stone in about 0.3 s. The [sword](71) does not speed up mining.

## What blocks drop

- Every broken block drops **the same block**, except `stone`, which gives `cobblestone` (smelt it back into
  `stone` with coal: see [Stone and bedrock](50)).
- Water and bedrock drop nothing (water can be carried in a [bucket](111)).
- **Falling blocks:** if you break a block above your eyes, it falls to the ground before you can pick it
  up.
- **Magnet:** items within 3.4 blocks fly to you by themselves.
- **Surprises:** breaking grass or leaves sometimes gives a [sapling](55) (8 %), and tall grass or leaves,
  very rarely, a [mob egg](74) (3.5 %).
- **Q** drops one unit of the item in your hand.

## Building

- Pick a block in the hotbar (keys **1–8** or the **mouse wheel**) and **right click** the face where you
  want it, up to 4.5 blocks away.
- **E** opens the full inventory.
- You cannot place a solid block where you are standing.
- Some items do something special when placed: the [bed](63) sets your respawn point, the [sign](64) asks
  for a text, a [sapling](55) becomes a tree and an [egg](74) hatches its animal.

## Slabs and steps

- [Slabs](56) take half a block. **You walk up them**, without jumping.
- To climb a full block you need to **jump** (Space).

## Ladders

- A [ladder](65) only sticks to the **sides** of a block, not to the floor or the ceiling.
- Walk into it to grab on.
- **W** or **Space** climbs up; **S** or **Shift** climbs down. If you press nothing, you stay put.

## Crouching

**Shift** makes you crouch: you move slower (less than half speed) and **never walk off an edge**, ideal for
building next to a cliff. Many animals are also less skittish if you approach crouching.
