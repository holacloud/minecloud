# Dodo

![Dodo](mob:dodo)

A fat pigeon, nearly a metre tall, that **could not fly** and **was afraid of nobody**. Instead of running
away, it walks up to look at you. It is an [extinct animal](261): it never appears by itself, it has to be
brought back to life.

## How to get one

1. **Look for its bones.** They are not in the rock, but near the surface, in the block ![](item:old_bones)
   `old_bones`: under the sand of a beach and, now and then, under the grass of a plain. Take a [shovel](69).
   The page on [fossils](262) says where to dig.
2. **Break the block.** If a dodo was inside, you take ![](item:fossil_dodo) `fossil_dodo` with you: some dodo bones.
3. **Bring it back to life** in the [revival machine](264). **One piece** is enough and it takes **45 seconds**. It is
   a land animal: it needs no water.

The server keeps a revived dodo: every player sees it, and it is still there when you come back.

## Behaviour

- **It strolls slowly**, swaying, and **pecks at the ground** when it stands still.
- **It is trusting.** If you stand within **6 blocks**, it drops what it was doing and **walks up to you**. It
  stops 1.7 blocks away, lifts its head to look at you, tilts it and now and then **coos**: two low notes.
- With several players about, it goes to the **nearest** one.
- It does not stray from home: it only comes to somebody within **8 blocks** of the place where it lives.
- **It never runs away**, not even if you hit it: it gives a start and stays there looking at you.
- It moves its tiny wings a little; they are no use for flying.

There are **2 colours**: blue-grey and brown-grey.

## How to interact

| What you do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Come near | It comes to your side. No bait is needed. |
| Hit it | **12 health**: 3 hits bare-handed, 2 with a stone [sword](71) or better. Each hit has a **40 %** chance of dropping 1 ![feather](item:feather) `feather` (at most one every 8 seconds). |
| Defeat it | It leaves **1 or 2** `feather`. And it is extinct again. |
| Tame it | Yes: `wheat_seeds`, `wheat`, `mushroom_brown` or `flower_red`, with **1 in 2** per try. Then it follows you everywhere and sits when you ask. See [Taming and breeding](3). |
| Breed | Two tamed dodos in love have a chick. |
| Bait | None. |
| Cage | **Yes**, it fits in the `cage_trap`, if it walks in. |
| Egg | `dodo_egg` |
| Command | `/mob dodo` |

## Tips

- To see it up close, do nothing: stand still and wait for it to arrive.
- To take it far away, **tame it**: while it is nobody's, it does not leave the surroundings of its home.
- `wheat_seeds` drop when you break `tall_grass`: the easiest thing to get for taming it.
- To get it into a cage without bait, set the `cage_trap` between the dodo and you, about 3 blocks from
  you: it will walk to you in a straight line and step inside. See [Baits and traps](4).

## The real dodo

- **Where and when.** It lived only on **Mauritius**, an island in the Indian Ocean, east of Madagascar. The
  first sailors who described it arrived in **1598**. The last time anybody told of seeing one was around
  **1662**: in less than a hundred years there was none left.
- **What it was like.** It stood about **1 metre** tall and weighed between **10 and 18 kilos**, like a big
  turkey. It had grey or brownish feathers, very small wings, a tuft of curly feathers for a tail, yellow legs
  and a huge hooked beak.
- **It was a pigeon.** Its living relatives are pigeons and doves. The closest is the **Nicobar pigeon**,
  which does fly. Its ancestors flew to the island. Nothing there ate them, and the food was on the ground:
  over time they grew big and stopped flying.
- **What it ate.** Fallen fruit, seeds and probably roots. It swallowed stones, which helped it grind its food
  in the gizzard, as hens do.
- **Why it was not afraid.** There had never been hunters on its island, so it did not know how to flee. It
  happens to many animals of islands with no predators, and it is called **island tameness**.
- **Why it died out.** Sailors hunted it, but the worst came on the ships: **pigs, rats, dogs and monkeys**,
  which ate its eggs and its chicks. The dodo nested on the ground and, by the accounts of those who saw it,
  laid **a single egg**. Its forests were cut down as well.
- **What is left.** No museum has a whole stuffed dodo: only bones and, in Oxford, a head and a foot. That is
  why in the game it turns up as recent bones and not as a fossil in the rock.
- For a time some people believed the dodo was a tale. Today it is the symbol of the animals that vanish
  because of people, and it is in *Alice's Adventures in Wonderland*.
